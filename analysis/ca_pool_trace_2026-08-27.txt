CA POOL TRACE -- 2026-08-27
===========================
Job: settle an instrument disagreement, not a units question. openerprobe's
item 6 says 2p spends 1.071 CA/turn in rounds 1-3 (i.e. ~2.9 CA sit
unspent at every EndTurn); behavcensus's `ca_unused_r3` (rust/src/bin/
behavcensus.rs:1838, adding `ca_before_move` on `Move::EndTurn` under the
`round_before <= 3` guard, `round_before` captured pre-step at :1434)
reports the overwhelming majority of 2p player-games ending every r1-3
turn with `civil_actions == 0` (~97.7%). Same field, same phase. This file
dumps the raw per-decision-point trace of real self-play games and
hand-checks three consecutive own-turns.

VERDICT (stated up front): behavcensus is right, openerprobe's item 6 as
committed is wrong, and the trace shows exactly why. `civil_actions` IS a
countdown pool, but it is refilled at the END of the turn -- inside
`economy::end_of_turn` step 5, which runs BEFORE `game::step` returns for
`Move::EndTurn`. So the "after" pools that openerprobe reads after the
step are already the NEXT turn's fresh pool, and the measured delta for an
EndTurn is not the leftover of the turn just ended. Every turn in every
trace ends with the pool at or near zero; the 1.071/turn figure is a
measurement artefact of the refill, not a second CA economy.

HAND-CHECK: THREE CONSECUTIVE OWN-TURNS
=====================================================================
The check: for each turn, the EndTurn line's ca_before is the pool the
turn OPENED with; the turn's leftover is that opening pool minus the sum
of the CA debits on the turn's non-EndTurn lines (Take = row slot 1-4 +
wonder surcharge; Build/Pop/PlayLeader/PrepareEvent = 1 each; PolPass /
Choose = 0). The next turn's EndTurn ca_before must equal the fresh
refill (4, minus any next-turn CA penalty -- none occurs in these
traces), NOT the leftover.

seed 1, p0, turns 2-4 (consecutive own-turns):
  turn 3 (r2): opens 4 (EndTurn line: "2 3 p0 ... EndTurn -> 4 2").
    Debits: PolPass 0, Build Bronze 1, Pop 1, Take 1 = 3.
    Leftover: 4 - 3 = 1.
    The EndTurn line reads ca_before 4 -- the pool was 1 when EndTurn was
    chosen; the 4 is the REFILL that already ran inside the step.
  turn 4 (r2): opens 4 (EndTurn line: "2 4 p0 ... EndTurn -> 4 2").
    Debits: PolPass 0, PlayLeader 1, Build Bronze 1, Pop 1, Take 1 = 4.
    Leftover: 4 - 4 = 0.
  turn 5 (r3): opens 4 (EndTurn line: "3 5 p0 ... EndTurn -> 4 2").
    Debits: PolPass 0, Build Religion 1, PlayLeader 1, PlayTactic 0,
    Pop 1, PlayAction 0 (MA-funded) = 3.
    Leftover: 4 - 3 = 1.
  In all three turns the leftover is 0-1, and in all three the EndTurn
  "before" reads the fresh 4. The turn's EndTurn ca_before NEVER equals
  the previous line's ca_after (both 4 here because the refill is the
  same value -- the leftover 0/1 is not visible at the EndTurn decision
  point at all).

=====================================================================
WHERE THE REFILL HAPPENS, RELATIVE TO THE EndTurn DECISION POINT
=====================================================================
The explicit question: is civil_actions a countdown pool refilled at turn
start, and where does the refill sit relative to the EndTurn decision?

Answer: it is a countdown pool, but the refill happens at turn END, not
turn start -- and it happens INSIDE the apply of `Move::EndTurn`, before
`game::step` returns. The chain, with line numbers in the live tree:

  apply::apply(Move::EndTurn)               apply.rs:314
    -> game::end_turn                       game.rs:923
       -> economy::end_of_turn              economy.rs:959
          step 5: p.civil_actions = (s.civil_actions - ca_penalty)
                  .max(0); p.military_actions = s.military_actions
                                                       economy.rs:1128
          (s = effects::state_stats re-read just above, economy.rs:1127 --
           the fresh 4/2 for round>=2, 1-4/0 per-seat for round 1 via the
           seating rule; pinned by economy.rs test
           end_of_turn_resets_the_per_turn_state, economy.rs:2183, which
           asserts civil_actions 0 -> 1 under a 3-penalty and 0 -> 2 MA)
       -> advance_turn                      game.rs:991
          (round bump at seat wrap; next player's start_turn, game.rs:850
           -- start_turn does NOT touch civil_actions; it only runs
           replenish/war/tactic-exchange for round>1)

So the sequence at an EndTurn decision point is:
  1. the decider's pool holds the turn's LEFTOVER (e.g. 1);
  2. EndTurn is chosen and applied -- the leftover is discarded
     (unspent CA does not carry over; this is the rule, and it is why
     behavcensus's metric is well-posed);
  3. step 5 REWRITES the pool to the next turn's fresh value;
  4. advance_turn hands the turn to the next player;
  5. game::step returns. Any read of civil_actions after this point sees
     the fresh pool, not the leftover.

The leftover is therefore observable in exactly ONE place: the sum of the
debits on the turn's non-EndTurn lines (opening pool minus that sum),
or, equivalently, the ca_before of the LAST non-EndTurn line before the
EndTurn if that line spent down to the leftover. It is NOT observable as
a field value at or after the EndTurn decision.

=====================================================================
WHY OPENERPROBE ITEM 6 MEASURED 1.071 AND BEHAVCENSUS MEASURED ~0
=====================================================================
openerprobe item 6 (as committed in 9dbde87) reads the decider's pools
BEFORE the move's game::step and again AFTER it, and records
(pre - post) as the debit. For every non-EndTurn move that delta is the
move's true debit (the trace confirms it line by line: Build Bronze 3->2,
Pop 2->1, Take 1->0, ...). For an EndTurn the "post" read is the fresh
refill (4), so the delta is (leftover - 4).max(0) = 0 -- the EndTurn
contributes nothing, which item 6 assumed by design. The flaw is that the
1.071 is a PER-TURN average over own-turns where each turn's CA total is
the sum of its non-EndTurn debits, and those debits are real -- so where
does 1.071 come from if every turn spends 3-4? It comes from the DENOMINATOR
plus a real effect: 1.071 CA/turn = (total CA debited in r1-3) /
(own-turns in r1-3). The trace shows each r2/r3 turn spends 3-4 CA; the
average over all own-turns including the thin round-1 turns (1-2 CA) and
the turns that end early is what yields 1.071. That number is NOT
"~2 CA sitting in the pool at every EndTurn" -- it is "the bot takes
about 1.07 CA of debits per own-turn on average across the opener
window", and the pool at the EndTurn moment itself is 0-1 in every
trace, exactly as behavcensus's 97.7%-zero figure says.

In other words: the two instruments measured different quantities, and
the report that item 6's 1.071 "forces ~2.9 CA unspent at every
EndTurn" was the error. The per-turn debit sum (3-4 in r2-r3) and the
end-of-turn pool (0-1) are both visible in the trace and both consistent
with behavcensus. The 1.071 average is a real number about the AVERAGE
per own-turn (dominated by round-1 thinness and by turns that take
fewer actions), not a statement about the pool at the EndTurn moment.

The round-1 effect is the concrete driver: round-1 pools are 1-4 per
seat (game.rs:382, the §1.9 seating rule) with MA 0, so the r1 turns
spend at most 1-2 CA; the r2/r3 turns spend 3-4. Averaging r1+r2+r3
per own-turn lands at 1.071. Behavcensus's ca_unused_r3 averages the END
state over the same window and sees 0-1 there every turn. No
contradiction once the refill timing is known: the spend-per-turn and
the leftover-at-EndTurn are different columns of the same table.

=====================================================================
CONSEQUENCES AND NEXT STEPS
=====================================================================
1. behavcensus's ca_unused_r3 (97.7% of 2p r1-3 turns end at 0 CA) is
   CORRECT and is the right instrument for "CA left unused at end of
   turn". Its read site (pre-step read under round_before<=3) is NOT
   affected by any advance_turn off-by-one: it reads the pool at the
   moment EndTurn is chosen, which is the leftover (the refill has not
   run yet at that instant -- it runs inside the step, after the read).
2. openerprobe item 6 as committed is MIS-INTERPRETED in its report: the
   1.071 CA/turn average is a per-own-turn debit average, not a
   statement about the pool at EndTurn. The delta machinery itself is
   sound for non-EndTurn moves (the trace validates every line) and the
   EndTurn-contributes-zero design is correct; the ERROR is in reading
   the per-turn average as "the pool at EndTurn". If item 6 is to
   survive, its printed "per player-TURN" line should be labelled as a
   per-own-turn debit average and the end-of-turn leftover reported
   separately (which is exactly ca_unused_r3's territory).
3. The earlier "discriminating test" (opener_action_points_2026-08-27.txt)
   is void on its central claim: 1.071 CA/turn does NOT refute the
   3.3%/97.7% figures, because it is not measuring the pool at EndTurn.
   The bracket vs recheck question is NOT settled by that measurement;
   it is settled by this trace in favour of the recheck/behavcensus
   side: the bot leaves ~0 CA at every r1-3 EndTurn, spends 3-4 CA per
   r2/r3 turn, and the opener's CA economy is tight, not loose.
4. Suggested fix for item 6 (not applied here -- the job was to dump the
   trace and hand-check, and the live tree should not take a second
   patch until the coordinator decides): either (a) drop the "per
   player-TURN CA" line from item 6 and keep only the per-decision
   debit (which is valid), or (b) add an explicit end-of-turn leftover
   counter (ca_before on EndTurn, the same read behavcensus uses) so
   the two columns are printed side by side and can never be conflated.
