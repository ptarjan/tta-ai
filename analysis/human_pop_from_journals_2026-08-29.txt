# Human population growth, re-derived from the journals alone (2p)

## Why this exists

`analysis/population_curve_bot_vs_human_2026-08-28.txt` reported a human
population curve whose producer was reverted before committing and exists in no
git revision, so its expression and its denominator can never be read. Its human
population column also violates a conservation law (see that file's REVIEWER
block). This report rebuilds the human side from the one source that can be
re-run and re-checked: the BGO journals.

## Method

Same pipeline as `analysis/human_midgame_actions_2026-08-28.txt`. The 632
2-player games in `analysis/guard_ids_882.txt` (selected as exactly 2 distinct
`player_colour` values). Journals are TSV, `date / player_colour / age / round /
text`; round is column 4 as a base-10 int; the action verb is the leading word of
`text` after the colour word, regex `^\w+\s+(\w+)\b`.

DENOMINATOR, stated explicitly: **player-rounds**, the count of distinct
(player_colour, round) pairs, = **1264 for every round 1..17** (632 games x 2
seats). No 2p game ends before round 18, so nothing is dropped and n is flat by
construction. This is the quantity the withdrawn curve could not be shown to use.

CROSS-CHECK, PASS: the per-round `increases` means for r9-r16 reproduce the
published census exactly (0.272 / 0.332 / 0.381 / 0.460 / 0.479 / 0.521 / 0.542 /
0.672).

## (b) Worker removals: negligible

All 260 distinct verb tokens were listed and each candidate read with its full
line. Only two verbs remove a worker from the acting player's own board:

    disbands   398 total   "Orange disbands Warrior"
    annexes      1 total   "Purple annexes Inhabited Territory"

Per-round mean for `disbands`: r1-2 .000, r3 .001, r4 .000, r5-6 .001, r7 .007,
r8 .015, r9 .013, r10 .013, r11 .016, r12 .025, r13 .041, r14 .043, r15 .040,
r16 .036, r17 .062. Cumulative through r17: **0.315**, plus 0.001 for `annexes`.

Excluded, each with its full line as evidence: `destroys` ("Orange destroys
Philosophy", cards), `destroy` ("Terrorists destroy a Purple Movies", event),
`casualties` ("Raid casualties 1 Bread & Circuses; 1 Opera", buildings on the
victim's line), `loses` ("Purple loses Developed Territory (I)", a result line
for the opponent's workers), `revolutions`, `defeat` ("concedes defeat"), `dies`
("Alexander dies... Orange gets 1 yellow token" - returns a token TO the bank,
the opposite direction), `enlists` ("Barbarossa enlists a Warrior" - ADDS one).

## (c) The result, and what it does to the damage figure

Net board-worker gain per human player, gross adds minus removals:

    through r9    4.040 - 0.038 = 4.002
    through r17   8.089 - 0.316 = 7.773
    r9 -> r17 net gain           = 3.771

The withdrawn curve has the human population rising **+7.21** over exactly that
span. The journals pay for **+3.77**. The ratio is **1.91**.

The curve's absolute level fails the same way. It puts the human at 13.92 workers
at r17; the journals supply only 7.77 net gains over the whole game to that
point, so reaching 13.92 needs about six workers on the board at setup. The
engine starts each player with `yellow_bank: 18` and `workers_free: 1`
(`rust/src/game.rs:268-270`). Six is not supported.

CORRECTED FIGURE. A human player holds roughly 9-11 workers at r17 against the
bot's 8.25. **The mid-game population gap is on the order of +1 to +3, not
+5.67.** The peel is probably real; it was roughly half to a fifth of what was
published. Anything sized off +5.67 needs re-sizing.

The bank column of the withdrawn curve is untouched by this and remains
consistent with these counts.

## Reproducing

Read-only over `sources/bgo/journals/*.tsv` filtered by
`analysis/guard_ids_882.txt`; no build, no engine, no self-play. Count the
`increases` verb per (colour, round), divide by 1264, and cumulate.
