INTERPRETATION
==============

The human baseline shows a strong preference for spending the full civil-action
grant in round 1 at 2 players:
  - Seat 0 (1 CA grant): 93.5% spend exactly 1 CA
  - Seat 1 (2 CA grant): 89.7% spend exactly 2 CA

Almost no player leaves civil actions unspent:
  - Seat 0: 0.0% unspent
  - Seat 1: 0.6% unspent

The mean overspend is minimal — most excess is just 1 extra action, and
happens in ~8% of seat-1 turns and ~6% of seat-0 turns.

COMPARISON TO BOT BASELINES
===========================

Human mean (all seats): 1.601 CA/turn
Bot current champion: 0.689 CA/turn
Bot older version: 1.161 CA/turn

The human baseline is 2.3x higher than the current bot champion and
1.4x higher than the older bot version. This suggests the current bot
is significantly under-spending civil actions in round 1 relative to
human play, and may be leaving value on the table.

CAVEATS
=======

1. This analysis uses only the journal text "uses X civil action" pattern.
   The pattern appears consistent across the corpus.

2. Analysis is limited to round-1 data extractable from journal timestamps
   and round numbers. No per-turn or within-action breakdown is available.

3. Player skill level is mixed — BGO games span Emperor, King, and lower
   difficulty levels. No stratification by level was performed.

4. Seat position may have been inferred from order of appearance in
   journal rather than explicit seat metadata. The pattern is consistent
   with a player-order-based assignment.
