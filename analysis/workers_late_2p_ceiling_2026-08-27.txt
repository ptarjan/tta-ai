(1) The real bound: the cap is binding, not the formula
-------------------------------------------------------
WorkersLate's live spread row (weights.rs:2437, unchanged since 45b2d80):
  [1.592105, 1.494118, 1.050279]
Per clamp_bound (weights.rs:2208-2215):
  bound = min(CLAMP_CEILING, CLAMP_T * P95_TOTAL_SPREAD[0] / spread)
        = min(120.0, 1.0 * 823.689416 / 1.592105)
        = min(120.0, 517.36...)
        = 120.0
The formula would permit ~517. The coordinate is at 120.0 = the
CLAMP_CEILING (weights.rs:2523), so the cap is what is binding. Its 95%
trigger is 114.0.

Note on the log lines: every RUNAWAY line in this log prints `(clamp
120.0)` or `(clamp 60.0)` — that is the global CLAMP_CEILING, not the
key's bound (the fix 96bf774, "report a pinned coordinate against its
own bound," is committed but not yet built into the running league).
Bounds in this file are computed, never read off a log.

(3) The key question: is magnitude moving between workers_late and
    uprising?
-------------------------------------------------------------------
No. There is no conserved sum, no tradeoff, no collinear displacement
between these two coordinates. Three independent reasons:

(a) The moves are in the same direction, not opposite. At 118612
    workers_late went UP (+68.5 or more, to 120.0) while uprising went
    DOWN in magnitude (−54.913 → −52.790, a shrinkage of 2.12).
    |workers_late| grew by ≥68.5 while |uprise| shrank by 2.12.
    A conserved-sum or collinear-displacement relationship requires
    one coordinate's gain to be the other's loss; here both
    |weights| did NOT offset — the sum |wl| + |up| went from 114.913
    to 172.79 (an INCREASE of 57.9), and the difference moved too.
    Across the 14 accepts since (118617…118888) the pinned values are
    constant (120.0 / −52.790 until the uprising unpin at ~118671,
    then 120.0 / nothing) — no exchange, no walk between them.

(b) They are not in the same mutation neighborhood. The 118612 accept's
    ops were group:military + scatter. workers_late's group is the
    rate/stock group (weights.rs:1348-1349: …RateHorizon|Culture|…|
    FreeWorkers|Workers|WorkersLate|ProdWorkers|UrbanWorkers|
    UnitWorkers → the same arm as Workers and FreeWorkers); uprising's
    group is the happiness arm (weights.rs:1396:
    HappyMargin|Discontent|Uprising|HappySurplus; the sign_intent NP arm
    is the same four keys at 1618). group:military
    does NOT include workers_late. So a group op cannot have moved them
    together; only the scatter leg (43 keys, 0.558 share) could touch
    either, and scatter is per-key noise, not a two-coordinate
    trade.

(c) The magnitudes are not comparable in the first place. workers_late
    120.0 is AT ITS OWN CAP (real bound 120.0; formula permits ~517);
    uprising −45.4325 (gen 118911) is at 82.7% of its real bound
    54.91263 and UNPINNED. Two coordinates pinned at DIFFERENT
    fractions of DIFFERENT bounds are not a conserved pair. The
    uprising unpin (analysis/uprising_2p_unpin_2026-08-27.txt) is
    explained by its own regime change (the 54.913 bound replaced the
    flat 60) plus a walking-out pressure; workers_late hitting its cap
    is the independent consequence of the CEILING doubling (60 → 120
    under f942741) removing the wall that had held it at 51-60 since
    gen 110205.

What the logs DO show, and what they cannot show:
  - Shown: workers_late was at 51.5122 in the 116808 freeze; never
    measured pinned from 116905 to 118611; first pinned at 120.0 in
    the 118612 accept; never left since.
  - NOT shown: the step-by-step values of workers_late in 116905-
    118611 (runaway only records ≥95% pins, and its bound then was the
    blind 60… no — under the current table its bound is 120.0, so 95%
    is 114.0; values in (114, 120) would not be logged either). The
    51.5122 → 120 walk happened in a region the runaway instrument
    cannot see. If the 118612 jump looks like a teleport, it may
    partly be an observation artifact: the value could have been
    ~118-119 since well before 118612 and only the accept's
    measurement exposed it at 120. The jsonl `tried.moved` counts
    (scatter moved 43 keys) confirm keys were touched at 118612, but
    the logs carry no per-key deltas.

Corrections to my uprising report (018990a), as instructed:
