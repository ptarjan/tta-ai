THREE DEMOTIONS -- they fail the report's OWN stated rule
---------------------------------------------------------
The report's practical rule is "at least TWO lineages large AND the lineages
disagree in sign". Three entries do not meet it:

  special_techs   only ONE arm is large (+50.879 at 4p). 2p -0.106 and 3p -0.650
                  are both under the floor. A single large arm against two
                  near-zero arms is the report's own NOISE shape.
  happy_margin    same: only -1.647 at 4p clears the floor. 2p has drifted to
                  +0.737, under it.
  rival_desire    two large arms, but they AGREE (+10.65, +9.41). The only
                  "flip" is the near-zero 2p arm at -0.233 -- again the report's
                  own NOISE definition, and it is flagged in the report itself
                  ("both large arms agree").

rival_desire has a second, harder disqualification. It is not a linear-eval
coordinate at all: row.rs:323 reads

    let desire = w.get(WeightKey::RivalDesire).clamp(0.0, 1.0);

so it is a Class A hyperparameter clamped to [0,1]. A sign_intent on it would
not mean "more is never worse"; and the clamp makes the 2p value of -0.233
behaviourally identical to 0.0, so the 2p/3p "sign disagreement" is really
off-versus-on, not a disagreement about direction. Do not put it back on a
sign_intent list.

The other six are all raw emitted coordinates, verified at
features.rs:840 (FreeWorkers), :971/:972 (BestFarm/BestMine), :966 (AuctionBid),
:1109 (RivalCultureRate), so a sign_intent is at least the right SHAPE of
question for them.
