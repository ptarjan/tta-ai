Q3 — cause or coincidence
-------------------------
Cause, and the mechanism is legible in the logs. Three independent
supports:

(a) The unpin is the DIRECT consequence of the regime change. The whole
    54.913-bounded era runs gens ~115243-118887 (the 823.689416/15.0
    table). Under the OLD flat rail the same weight sat at −58.693…
    −54.913 against 60.0 — 91-98% pinned. The measured table cut the
    effective trigger for THIS key from 57.0 to 52.167 while the weight
    was still walking at −54.9. The key did not suddenly start behaving
    differently; the line it was already standing on moved under its
    feet. That the first step OFF the bound (−52.790 at 118612) is a
    normal mutation step (0.116 sigma, group:military+scatter — neither
    op is happiness-scoped) and that the walk then continued to −45.43
    without any rail event in between says the coordinate is under
    genuine pressure: it wants to be more negative than its measured
    bound permits. A coincidence would not produce a monotonic walk out
    of the pin.

(b) The window's other moves are the same pressure from adjacent keys,
    not independent noise. The 118519 group:rivals accept pinned
    rival_mean_culture exactly at its measured bound (27.456) and drove
    rival_free_ca to its blind bound (−60.0) in one step. The 118612
    group:military+scatter accept stepped uprising −2.12 AND pinned
    workers_late at the 120.0 ceiling in the same generation. Both
    accepts were broad-group moves at the window's moderate sigmas;
    the pinned keys are the coordinates those groups price. The
    happiness/rival/military/economy groups are overlapping neighborhoods
    in the weight vector, so a group op that wins on one coordinate
    drags its neighbors — and the neighbors that are already near their
    bounds are exactly the ones that show up in the runaway list.

(c) The two-step-and-revert structure (118309 → revert 118390) is the
    selection working as designed, not a glitch: a rescale:row+scatter
    accept at −54.197 held for 81 gens, then a kick+scatter accept
    re-pinned it to −54.913. The pin is a RESTORING condition under this
    rail — the weight returns to the bound whenever a move off it is not
    good enough to win. The −52.790 step at 118612 was the first off-pin
    move that won AND was never reverted, and it was the first one that
    crossed 52.167.

What is NOT going on:
  - No clamp-repair event in this window. The log's only three
    clamp-repair lines are at 105000/105001 (the 300.449105/33.0
    era) and 106636. Since the current table landed, no coordinate has
    been repaired — the gate is being respected at load and at
    mutation, not violated and patched.
  - No oscillation. 217 clean gens off the pin, 13 accepts, zero
    re-entries.
  - No restart confounding. The last "resumed from" line is 116903
    (resume at gen 118374, inside the pinned plateau); the jsonl is
    contiguous through the window.

Residual question (for whoever owns the rail, not for this trace):
the current 54.913 bound for uprising@2p is measured (spread 15.0), but
the weight is now WALKING THROUGH the bound, not resting on it. That is
the first time under any regime that the pin has been shed rather than
held — either the coordinate genuinely wants a larger magnitude (the
rail is too tight for a real signal) or the pressure is an artifact of
the neighbors being pinned (a pinned rival_mean_culture and a
ceiling-pinned workers_late both shift what the other coordinates see).
Distinguishing those two needs the per-feature contributions, which the
climb logs do not carry; the jsonl `runaway` list only says which keys
are at 95%+, not why.
