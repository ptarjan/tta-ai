ADDITIVE ARENA PROBE OF TWO HAND-INVENTORY KEYS
2026-08-31/09-01, 2 players, frozen champion, binary at d522218

WHAT THIS MEASURES
------------------
The five civil-hand columns promoted in d522218 all default to exactly 0.0 on
every champion. That is the precondition named in memory/tta-probe-additively-
not-absolutely.md: a fixed-c weight probe is only interpretable against a zero
baseline, because +c and -c are then symmetric excursions from the live policy
rather than perturbations of an already-fitted value.

Two of the five were probed, the two with the largest measured within-decision
spread ratios from analysis/handfeat_features_2026-08-31.txt:

  hand_specialtech_count      spread ratio 1.496296   mean spread 0.3092
  hand_resource_cost_total    spread ratio 1.134221   mean spread 3.7807

c was scaled per key so each induces a score swing comparable to the live
HandCivil key (spread ratio 0.615241):

  c_specialtech = 0.619594
  c_resource    = 0.050673

Baseline is the frozen 2p champion (181 keys) with the 5 new columns appended
at 0.0 => 186 keys, /tmp/handprobe/baseline_186.json. Every probe file differs
from it in exactly one key. All runs: rust/target/release/arena
--games 2400 --players 2, except the null at --games 1200.

THE NULL IS MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED
---------------------------------
Run A is baseline against a byte-identical opponent: 50.0% +/- 0.0, rho -1.00,
deff 0.00. The arena pairs each deal with the seats swapped, so seat bias
cancels by construction and the null for this configuration really is 0.500.
This matters because memory/tta-forced-slack-arena-loses.md records seat 0
alone being worth +1.8pp in a differently-configured arena. Here it is not.

Per memory/tta-common-opponent-needs-headroom.md, an exact 50.00 +/- 0.00 can
also mean the instrument is measuring nothing. It does not mean that here: the
four probe runs against the same baseline return rho -0.39..-0.53 and deff
0.47..0.61, i.e. real per-deal variation survives the pairing as soon as the
two sides actually differ. The zero variance in run A is the correct answer to
"identical weights", not a dead harness.

READING
-------
Both keys are ANTISYMMETRIC: the two signs land on opposite sides of the null.
That is the signature of a gradient. A column that could not decide would leave
both arms inside the interval; a column being merely damaged by perturbation
would push both arms the same way. Neither happened.

  hand_specialtech_count   gradient sign POSITIVE
      -c is decisive (44.5%, p=0.0000, lead -7.1 +/- 1.4).
      +c is NOT (51.3%, p=0.0893, interval straddles the null).
      Established: the direction. NOT established: that this magnitude of c
      is an improvement.

  hand_resource_cost_total gradient sign NEGATIVE
      Both arms clear. +c 46.3% (p=0.0000) and -c 53.3% (p=0.0000), and the
      -c arm BEATS the frozen champion by +3.3pp with the interval clear of
      the null.

Direction of both is one coherent statement: prefer special-tech cards in
hand, penalise total resource cost sitting in hand. Cheap playable cards are
worth holding; cards that cannot be afforded are dead weight. That is
consistent with memory/tta-bot-underbuys-and-wastes-cards.md (the bot takes
17.5 cards and plays 61%, humans 26.9 and 78%) -- a hand-cost penalty is
pressure toward not accumulating unplayable cards.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
----------------
+3.3pp against a FROZEN champion at a hand-tuned c is not a promotion. It is
evidence the column is reachable and signed, which is exactly the thing both
offline screens could not tell us. memory/tta-two-screens-disagree.md is
explicit that clearing both screens is necessary and not sufficient, and the
REL family is the standing counterexample: it won the R2 screen, landed at
78acfbc, and ablated to 50.1% +/- 0.3 (c69b111). The verdict that counts is
whether a climb starting from these keys at 0.0 finds and keeps a non-zero
value for them, judged by ablation.

Note also that HAND is the quadrant tta-two-screens-disagree.md described as
"moves most, predicts least" (median spread 0.5321, R2 total +0.002732) --
the family that looked worse on the screen that REL won.
