THE ANSWER: which terms DIFFER between Farm and Mine
=============================================================================

Age A cards are LEVEL 0, so the level-sensitive terms best_farm / best_mine /
tech_levels have ZERO delta (best_farm=-0.2305, best_mine=-0.2868 are both
negative, but neither card raises "my best" above what the other already
does; tech_levels is unchanged by a level-0 build). The entire Mine> Farm
margin comes from a SMALL set of non-level terms. Across all 84 points the
terms that differ (|delta| > 1e-9) are always drawn from:

    resource_rate             (every point)
    food_rate                 (every point)
    resource_commitment_turns (every point)
    row_playable_count        (every point WITHOUT a wonder at that seat)
    wonder_promise            (only when a wonder is in play at that seat)
    wonder_turns_to_finish    (only when a wonder is in play at that seat)

No other term ever differs between the two builds.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY the rates split this way (empirically confirmed, raw feature values)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Mine (Age A "Bronze") produces RESOURCES; a Farm (Age A "Agriculture")
produces FOOD. Raw feature values at a representative round-2 point
(horizon rate multiplier hz ~ 0.687):

    resource_rate:  root = 2.0   farm-trial = 2.0   mine-trial = 3.0
    food_rate:      root = 2.0   farm-trial = 3.0   mine-trial = 2.0

So:
  * Mine trial  -> resource_rate +1 vs root, food_rate unchanged.
  * Farm trial  -> food_rate      +1 vs root, resource_rate unchanged.

With the champion's weights:
  * resource_rate (w = +0.4943, POSITIVE):  Mine's +1 resource is worth
        +0.4943*1*hz  ~  +0.3396   (delta mine-farm = +0.3396).
  * food_rate (w = -0.1990, NEGATIVE):  the Farm's +1 food, against a
        NEGATIVE weight, is a PENALTY of -(-0.1990)*1*hz ~ +0.1368 to the
        mine-farm margin. i.e. building the Farm costs the player its own
        food-rate because the champion weights food_rate negatively in this
        horizon. (delta mine-farm = +0.1368.)

This is the whole story for the no-wonder case: the two rate terms alone give
Mine a ~ +0.477 edge, and resource_commitment_turns (w = -0.0333, negative)
adds a small further mine-favouring term because the mine-trial's higher
resource_rate shrinks the (obligation / max(rate,1)) denominator, so the
negative-weighted ResourceCommitmentTurns feature reads LOWER in the mine
trial (a gain). row_playable_count (w = +0.1905) adds the remainder at the
no-wonder points.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DIAGNOSIS (why Mine 757 : Farm 0)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The margin is ALWAYS in Mine's favour and NEVER below ~+0.49. Across all
   84 sampled round-2 points the measured mine-farm margin ranged from
   +0.493 to +1.977 and was positive at 100% of them. There is no sampled
   round-2 Farm-vs-Mine tie or reversal. That is why Farm's best rank is 2
   and never 1: at every one of the 1682 legal points, the immediately-
   preceding best move takes the rank-1 slot, and Mine sits above Farm in the
   remaining order.

2. The margin is NOT driven by card quality / level (best_farm, best_mine,
   tech_levels all contribute 0 — both Age A cards are level 0). It is driven
   ENTIRELY by the economy-rate terms plus the wonder terms:

   a) resource_rate weight is POSITIVE (+0.494) while food_rate weight is
      NEGATIVE (-0.199). A Mine adds a resource (a positively-weighted rate);
      a Farm adds food (a negatively-weighted rate). So the champion is
      literally paying the player to build a mine and penalising them for
      building a farm, on the rates alone: +0.34 +0.14 = +0.48 of the margin.
      This is the dominant, ever-present driver.

   b) resource_commitment_turns (weight -0.033, negative) reinforces (a):
      the higher resource_rate in the mine-trial lowers this negative-
      weighted feature, worth another +0.017 to +0.05.

   c) At points where a wonder is in play, the two wonder terms add a further
      +1.08 to +1.17 (wonder_promise, weight +0.553) and +0.277
      (wonder_turns_to_finish, weight -0.277). These are LARGER than the
      rate terms at those points — but note they are eval-only and are read
      on the post-move state, so they reward whichever build keeps the wonder
      trajectory; in every sampled point that happened to be the mine.

   d) row_playable_count (weight +0.19) supplies the remainder at the
      no-wonder points.

3. Net effect: every round-2 Farm-vs-Mine comparison is won by Mine by
   ~+0.5 to ~+2.0, with the rate terms (a)+(b) supplying the floor that
   exists even with no wonder on the board. Farm can therefore only ever
   reach rank 2 (the best slot is taken by some other, higher-ranked move at
   least as often), and never rank 1.

SCOPE / LIMITS
--------------
* Sampled round 2 only (Build is legal from round 2; legal.rs:425 returns
  early on round 1). All 84 points are round 2, both seats, seeds 1-6,
  40-43, 100-115.
* The per-term split replicates evaluate() EXACTLY (max residual 1.2e-13),
  so the term attribution is exact, not approximate.
* This is a diagnosis. No weight was changed and none is proposed. If the
  goal is to ever build a Farm, the levers this data points at are the
  food_rate sign (negative) and/or the resource_rate magnitude — but that is
  a tuning decision for the coordinator, out of scope here.
