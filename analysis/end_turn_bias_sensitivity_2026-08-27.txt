end_turn_bias sensitivity — does it still control the 2p pass rate?
============================================================
Verdict: YES, it controls it strongly and monotonically. The
coordinate is NOT inert. With the champion frozen at gen 119049
(end_turn_bias = -2.9570330419228377, live re-read at freeze time)
and only end_turn_bias varied, EndTurn% of opener decisions sweeps
23.3% -> 75.2% over [-60, +10], with no plateau anywhere in the
range.

Mechanics of the table (important for reading it)
------------------------------------------------------------
- The ABSOLUTE number of EndTurn choices is IDENTICAL across all
  six runs: 360 every time. What end_turn_bias changes is how many
  OTHER decisions get made before a player ends the turn: at -60
  the bot plays on into 1545 total decision points (1185 more
  actions), at +10 it stops at 479 (234 fewer than at -3). The
  pass rate is therefore 360/total — the knob rescales the
  denominator, and it does so monotonically with no knee.
- The action mix reshuffles, not just the count: at +10 Urban
  climbs to 21.5% and Mine collapses to 1.0%, while Pop goes to
  0.0% — the bot that refuses to pass also stops popping in the
  opener. Farm is 0.0% everywhere (no farm-building happens in
  rounds 1-3 at any setting).
- -3 is nearly the frozen value (-2.957): 50.5% EndTurn, close to
  the 48.6% the coordinator quoted for the live champion, so the
  probe reproduces the defect's scale.

Reading
------------------------------------------------------------
- The trace (analysis/end_turn_bias_trace_2026-08-27.txt) found
  the 2p coordinate walked from a -60.0 pin to -2.957 — i.e. back
  to its un-trained default of -3.0. This table says that walk
  was not cosmetic: at -60 the same bot ends only 23.3% of
  opener decisions, at the current -2.957 it ends ~50%. The
  relaxation toward default roughly DOUBLED the pass rate.
- "Inert" is ruled out in both directions: raising the weight to
  0 or +10 pushes the pass rate to 63-75%; pushing it back toward
  its old pin (-60) would halve it. The defect is directly
  manipulable by this one coordinate.
- Caveat: 60 games per cell, seed 0; the 360-EndTurn constancy
  makes the denominator shifts structural rather than noise, but
  per-cell percentages carry sampling error of a few points at
  the small-n cells.
