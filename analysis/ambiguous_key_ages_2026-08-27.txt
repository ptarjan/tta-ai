  WHY the 2026-08-26 trio is OLD, not a date-rule artifact (the mechanism):
  The climb's mutation policy (rust/src/bin/climb.rs:418-490) is Scatter 45% of
  generations sampling 25% of the movable keys, Group 33%, Kick 10% sampling 60%,
  Rescale 12% -- so a single key is proposed for movement on roughly a quarter to
  a half of all generations. "Never mutated" over even 100 generations has
  probability on the order of 0.7^100: not a real possibility. And the sharper
  point: proposals do not persist, only ACCEPTED generations do -- the right unit
  of climb age is the ACCEPT count, not the generation count. since_accept read
  from the live champions on 2026-08-27: 2p 10, 3p 31, 4p 4 (accepts land every
  few dozen generations). The 2026-08-26 trio at 4p has roughly 2900 generations
  behind it -- on the order of a hundred accepted mutations, each touching a
  quarter to three fifths of the vector. Those keys have been moved by accepted
  mutations many times over and are still exactly 0.0. That is the gate, not
  youth. OLD is correct for them.
