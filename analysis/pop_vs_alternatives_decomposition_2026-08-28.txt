POP vs ALTERNATIVES score decomposition (diagnosis only — no weight change proposed)
2026-08-28, worker qwen3.8-27b, repo /Users/pt/tta-ai, rust crate `tta`
=============================================================================

TASK
----
At every own-turn decision point where Move::Pop (IncreasePopulation) was
LEGAL but another move outranked it, decompose winner_score - pop_score into
per-WeightKey w*delta terms. 2p, rounds 6-14, 200 games seeds 1..200, both
seats, frozen champion gen 121100. Same method as the farm_vs_military
decomposition (analysis/farm_vs_military_decomposition_2026-08-28.txt),
different target: here the loser is always Pop, and the winner is whatever
the bot actually chose instead.

WHY THIS IS THE DIRECT MEASUREMENT OF THE 76.9% DECLINE
-------------------------------------------------------
The military-curve report just refuted the "over-militarised" hypothesis:
the bot has LESS military than humans at every round (+22.9 human lead at
r20). The bot is not big, it is small. Population is the binding constraint
on everything (food, workers, the military curve, the whole economy), and the
prior pop report measured the bot declining a LEGAL Pop 76.9% of the time.
This decomposition is that decline, scored point by point: it answers "at
each moment the bot had Pop on the table and didn't take it, WHAT did it take
instead, and WHICH weight terms made the alternative score higher."

METHODOLOGY
-----------
Frozen champion: experiments/rust_champion_2p_frozen_2026-08-28.json
    "gen" field = 121100

Self-play mirror match, 2p, seeds 1..200, both seats, whole game (the probe
plays each game to completion so the round-6..14 windows are the real
mid-game). At each decision point that is the player's OWN turn (Move::EndTurn
among the legal moves) in rounds 6..14, where a legal Pop exists and some
OTHER move scores strictly higher, I:
  * score every legal candidate with the bot's own machinery —
    eval::candidate_features + eval::dot (the exact trial-and-evaluate loop
    rank_moves uses: same root, same RivalContext, same determinize, same
    EndTurnBias handling),
  * take the argmax (the bot's real choice) and Pop,
  * for every WeightKey report the per-move term
        delta(k) = w[k] * (f_win[k] - f_pop[k])
    so that  sum_k delta(k)  ==  winner_score - pop_score  (the HARD
    REQUIREMENT).

  decline is the sum of many small positive terms (the rate keys above all),
  not one oversized weight.

  THE ONE-LINE ANSWER: no single key dominates, but if one must be named,
  it is the RATE group — culture_rate (w=+60.08) and workers_late
  (w=+119.74) — whose combined +23.4 of mean contribution is the whole mean
  margin. Reducing their joint size (or, equivalently, raising the value
  the bot places on the worker Pop itself) is the lever; a single-key tweak
  of ~+1.85 on row_last_copy (or ~-1.87 on food_stock) flips the median
  decision, but that is a point-specific fix, not a structural one.

=============================================================================
THE DIAGNOSIS (why the bot declines a legal Pop 76.9% of the time)
=============================================================================
1. The decline is REAL and UNIVERSAL at the score level: 3609 legal-Pop
   points, margin positive at 100% of them, reconciling to 8.2e-13. The bot
   is not declining Pop for a rules reason — its own evaluator scores the
   alternative higher every time.

2. The dominant driver is RATE, not the military terms. culture_rate
   (+15.2) and workers_late (+8.2) — both with the two largest weights in
   the vector (60 and 120) — supply the entire mean margin. The champion
   learned to over-price growing a RATE (culture production, late-game
   worker count) relative to taking a one-off population token, so a Take
   or Develop that adds rate beats a Pop that adds a worker-and-food-spend.

3. The WINNER mix confirms the behavioural picture: 28.9% Take, 20.8%
   WonderStep, 14.2% PlayAction. The bot declines Pop to take cards and fund
   its wonder — the card-taking/wonder-first habit, exactly the mechanism
   the population-curve and food-gates reports diagnosed (bot runs
   near-food-starved, keeps population gated, defers to card-taking). The
   military terms (strength / strength_rel_early) are present but cancel to
   ~+0.7 of the mean margin — the bot is NOT over-militarising its way out
   of Pop; it is rate-chasing and wonder-chasing.

4. Half the declines are CLOSE (margin < 5 at 48%): the median decision
   flips on a small single-weight change (~+1.85 on row_last_copy or
   -1.87 on food_stock at the median point; 0.1-0.9 per round), which means
   the 76.9% is not a hard-coded refusal — it is a shallow, many-
   term preference that a modest re-weight of the RATE group (or of the
   worker Pop's own value) would move substantially. But because the flip
   key differs by round, the structural fix is the RATE weights
   (culture_rate / workers_late), not any one small term.

SCOPE / LIMITS
--------------
* 2p self-play, seeds 1..200, both seats, rounds 6-14. 3609 points.
* The per-term split replicates the bot's own candidate scoring EXACTLY
  (max residual 8.2e-13), so the attribution is exact.
* "Pop" = Move::Pop (the food-paying IncreasePopulation). PopFree (a card
  grants a free pop) is a distinct move and is NOT the decline measured
  here — a PopFree is never "declined" in the same sense.
* This is a diagnosis. No weight was changed and none is proposed. The
  levers the data points at are the RATE group (culture_rate +60.08,
  workers_late +119.74) and the worker-Pop value — a tuning decision for
  the coordinator, out of scope here.
