FARM vs MINE LEAF-EVAL ARITHMETIC, BY HAND (2026-08-28)

Question: at all three player counts the bot builds a farm ZERO times in the
opener (2p 0/741, 3p 0/1851, 4p 0/2432 legal chances) while the mine ranks 1
at the median. Valuation defect, not legality. What does the leaf eval
actually score for "build the Age-A Farm" vs "build the Age-A Mine", from
the LIVE 2p champion, no code run?

3. WHERE THE FARM IS KILLED (and the mine survives)
---------------------------------------------------
Both builds are NEGATIVE in this champion (the free_workers -3.41 and
tech_levels -1.93 terms dominate; that is the "never build in the opener"
part, and it is shared). But the mine is ~0.88 to ~1.27 LESS negative,
entirely from four terms:

  resource_rate  +0.4025   vs   food_rate   -0.1839   (+0.5863)
  resource_stock -1.2889   vs   food_stock  -1.8866   (+0.5977)
  resource_gap   +0.8867   vs   food_gap    +0.5160   (+0.3707)
  best_mine     -0.1947   vs   best_farm   -0.3105   (+0.1159)
                                                        SUM +1.2705 (the
                                                        rest is rounding on
                                                        the famine row)

So the leaf eval prices the mine's +1 resource as a GAIN (rate +0.40,
gap-closure +0.89) and the farm's +1 food as a LOSS (rate -0.18,
stock-heavy -1.89). The food side is penalized ~1.27 more than the
resource side, and that is exactly the farm-vs-mine gap: the eval
ranks "build Bronze" above "build Agriculture" by ~1.27 on every legal
chance, so the farm goes 0/N.

Is that the defect? The resource side of the arithmetic is defensible:
resources pay for everything (builds, development), so a positive
resource_rate and a positive resource_gap-closure are the right shape.
The food side is where the champion disagrees with the rules: a +1 food
rate in an opening where food is needed for population is being priced
NEGATIVE (food_rate -0.1839), and the food stock is being priced so
heavily (food_stock +0.9433, doubled by the cost) that spending 2 food to
build a farm costs 1.89 while the +1 food rate only refunds 0.18. The
fitter has found "food is a liability, resources are an asset" at 2p --
a reading in which the mine is the only rational production build and the
farm is never worth taking. That is a valuation defect in the food
coordinates, not in the farm/mine pair: both buildings are scored by the
same machinery, and the farm inherits the negative food price.

One caveat: food_rate and friends are NET of consumption/corruption
(features.rs:711-716) and hinge on the projected banks, so part of the
negative food_rate may be a real opening fact (low population, low
consumption, food stock sitting idle). But a NEGATIVE rate price means the
eval prefers NOT to produce food, which no opening position justifies:
population is the engine of every later action. At minimum the food_rate
sign is the coordinate to look at first.

COORDINATOR CORRECTION (2026-08-28): TWO DELTAS ABOVE ARE ZERO
================================================================

Two rows in both totals do not fire on an Age-A build. Neither is a
judgement call; both follow from the level arithmetic.

1. best_farm / best_mine delta is 0, not +1.
   features.rs:214-215 keeps a LEVEL, not a count:
       CardType::Farm if lv > best.farm => best.farm = lv
   CardId::level() maps Age A to 0 (cards.rs:45-48), and best.farm is a u8
   initialised to 0. So "no farm" and "the Age-A farm" are the SAME
   coordinate value, and building Agriculture cannot move it. The
   comparison `lv > best.farm` is 0 > 0, false.

2. tech_levels delta is 0, not +1.
   tech_levels (features.rs:193, 230-242) SUMS the level of each tech card
   in the tableau. A Move::Build places a worker on a tech already in play;
   it develops no card. And even on the reading where the card is counted,
   Age A contributes lv = 0. Zero either way.

WHAT SURVIVES AND WHAT DOES NOT.

The verdict survives: the gap is the resource pair, the mine's +1 resource
is priced as a gain and the farm's +1 food as a loss, and the farm inherits
a negative food price rather than carrying a farm-specific defect.

The supporting claim "both builds are NEGATIVE, and that is the 0/N part"
does NOT survive. Both are slightly POSITIVE once the two spurious rows
come out. The 0/N is therefore not an absolute-value failure but a RANKING
failure -- consistent with the observed behaviour, where the mine ranks 1
at the median and the farm never ranks above 3. Nothing needs both builds
to be negative for the farm never to be chosen; it only needs everything
else to outrank it.

WHY THIS MATTERED MOST AT 4p. The correction is small at 2p and decisive at
4p, where the live champion (gen 21875) reads best_farm +42.2362 against
best_mine -1.3881. Credited as a +1 delta, that single row would have made
the farm beat the mine by ~40 at 4p and falsified the whole verdict on the
arm with the most legal farm chances (0 of 2432). It is zero, so it does
not. The 4p asymmetry is the resource pair alone, resource_rate +3.9316
against food_rate +0.4375 -- the same story as 2p and 3p.

A STANDING CONSEQUENCE. best_farm and best_mine are blind to the Age-A
buildings entirely: they cannot distinguish "no farm" from "the starting
farm", and no opener build can move them. Any weight they carry is priced
on Age-I-and-later upgrades only. Note best_farm is also one of the eight
keys with a 0.0 live spread row, so its +42.2362 at 4p is drifting under
the blind rail rather than fitted -- do not read it as importance.
