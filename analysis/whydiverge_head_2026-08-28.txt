READING IT
----------
CultureRate has signed == abs at 2p, 3p and 4p: it never once pushed the other way. It is
a pure one-directional pull on every take decision where it fires.

HandPotential fires most often of anything at 2p (7146) but its signed total is barely
half its abs total, so it cuts both ways and its per-firing influence is small. Note the
name collides: the HandPotential FEATURE delta is identically zero on a take, while
eval.rs separately multiplies w.get(HandPotential) by cards::hand_potential(). Any claim
about this row must say which of the two it means.

FreeWorkers is the only top-10 key at 2p with a NEGATIVE signed total.

DO NOT FIT ON THIS. A divergence from a human is evidence to investigate, not an error to
minimise; fitting the leaf eval on human agreement is on the do-not-do list.
