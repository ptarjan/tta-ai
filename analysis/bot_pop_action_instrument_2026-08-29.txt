THE DENOMINATOR (the whole point)
=================================
Two populations, printed separately on purpose, NOT reconciled silently:

(1) PER-ROUND table: one row per round. n = the player-rounds that reached
    that round (from production_by_round[round].2, the same turn-start sample
    this file already takes per player-round). n FALLS as rounds go up because
    games end at different rounds. mean = total pop actions in that round / n.
    A cumulative mean over this falling-n population would be
    survivorship-biased, so it is NOT printed.

(2) COMPLETED-GAMES CUMULATIVE: the journals' 8.089-by-r17 / 2.98-by-r6 is
    per PLAYER over games that COMPLETE. So this is computed over
    completed_player_games only (no MOVE_CAP hit), summed to the round shown
    and divided by that completed count.

If the completed count differs from a per-round n, the two populations
differ, and the divergence is STATED in the printed note rather than
reconciled. In the sample below they are numerically equal (400) only because
every game in the sample completed with no cap hit and all reached round 17+;
the note still prints the distinction explicitly.
