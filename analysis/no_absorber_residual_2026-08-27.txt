THE "NO ABSORBER" RESIDUAL: CLOSED
==================================
2026-08-27. Follows analysis/pinned_key_absorbers_2026-08-27.txt (7936fbd),
which grouped the 11 effective proven snaps by WeightGroup and found 3 with no
sign-consistent nonzero sibling to absorb their signal: no_aggression@2p, and
military_action_gap at both 2p and 4p. If collinearity is the mechanism behind
the sign gate pinning a key, these three had no mechanism.

All three are now explained, and none of them is a gate victim.


1. military_action_gap@2p -- ESCAPED. Not a snap at all.
--------------------------------------------------------
  frozen (gen 116808) = 0        live (gen 116956) = -0.08091218942484857

It crossed off zero in 148 generations, the FOURTH observed escape from the
sign gate and by far the fastest (wonder_remaining took ~834). A key that
escapes in 148 generations was never pinned in any meaningful sense; it was
sitting at zero on the day the census happened to be taken. This is
tta-champion-file-is-live biting a static census.


2. military_action_gap@4p -- almost certainly the same, just on the slow arm.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  frozen (gen 21445) = 0         live (gen 21455) = 0

4p advanced only 10 generations since the freeze, against 148 at 2p. Its 2p twin
escaped, the two counts price the family similarly (military_action_surplus
+0.082 at 2p vs +0.057 at 4p), and the key simply has not been given comparable
climb time. Re-read it, do not theorise about it.


3. no_aggression@2p -- the one real residual, and the answer is that its true
   coefficient at 2 players is approximately zero.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  frozen (gen 116808) = 0        live (gen 116956) = 0    (still exactly 0
  after 148 more generations, unlike military_action_gap)

  cross-count live values:   2p  0        3p -0.030369      4p -4.235542
  (4p was -2.854 at the earlier census -- it is still growing more negative)

The feature is a plain binary indicator, features.rs:1057:
    f.set(WeightKey::NoAggression, if s.no_aggression { 1.0 } else { 0.0 });
so its spread row is 1.000000 at all three counts in every measurement (live
table, 40-game run and 160-game run all agree). It is not invisible and it is
not thinly sampled.

Its sign_intent is NonPositive with the reason "strips the holder's own
aggression and war moves" (weights.rs:1923, asserted in a test at :3202). That
is rules-correct and is not the problem.

The problem was never a missing absorber. The cross-count profile is monotone in
the number of opponents you could attack: 0 / -0.03 / -4.24. Losing your own
aggression and war moves is a real cost at 4 players, where there are three
targets; it is worth essentially nothing at 2 players, where there is one
opponent and the aggression options are thin to begin with. The true 2p
coefficient is ~0, the fit lands slightly positive on noise, and the gate rounds
it to exactly 0.

That is the BENIGN case. The gate is not destroying accumulated magnitude here,
because there is no magnitude to destroy. No absorber is needed, because there
is nothing to absorb.


VERDICT
-------
The sign gate has no unexplained victim among the 11 effective proven snaps:
8 have a sign-consistent absorber in their WeightGroup, 1 escaped in 148
generations, 1 is the same key on the slow arm, and the last has a true
coefficient of about zero at the count where it is pinned.

The general rule this yields: a pinned key with NO absorber AND a cross-count
profile that is MONOTONE in player count is a key whose true coefficient at that
count is ~0 -- read the rules for why the count matters and stop. Reserve
suspicion for a pinned key that the other lineages price strongly and
NON-monotonically, which is the shape that says information is being lost.
