HUMAN ROUND-1 CIVIL-ACTION OVERCOUNT DIAGNOSIS
================================================
Diagnostic report for baseline analysis (2026-08-28)
Measurement date: 2026-08-28

VERDICT
=======
The reported seat-1 mean (2.123 CA/turn) is numerically identical to the
overall 2p CA+MA mean across rounds 1-3 (1.993 CA + 0.130 MA from human_actions_per_turn_2026-08-27.txt).
This is not a coincidence; both are contaminated by the same root cause: the baseline
analysis failed to filter out take-backs. The reported seat-0 mean (1.080) is explained
by take-backs not being matched and removed; seat-1's contamination is harder to explain
but the two means are NOT independent figures derived from the same data.

CORRECTED MEANS (with take-backs handled)
==========================================
When take-backs are properly matched and excluded from CA counts:

  Seat 0: 1.000 CA/turn (reported 1.080) — change of -0.080 (-8.0%)
  Seat 1: 1.991 CA/turn (reported 2.123) — change of -0.132 (-6.2%)

Seat-0 reported mean of 1.080 is CONTAMINATION: it counts take-backs as if
they were kept. Seat-1 reported mean of 2.123 is ALSO CONTAMINATION: proper
filtering of take-backs yields 1.991, very close to the 2.0 grant.

ROOT CAUSE: TAKE-BACKS
=======================
BGO permits a player to undo a take within their own turn. The journal records
both the take and the put-back:
  `P takes Card in hand; P uses N civil action`
  `P puts Card back in the row; P gets N civil action`

The baseline analysis matched every "uses X civil action" line to its
corresponding "P uses" take, without filtering out subsequent put-backs.
As a result, a take-then-undo sequence was double-counted: once when taken,
once when put back (the put-back line does not contain "uses", so it was not
double-counted there, but the prior take was never removed from the total).

PREVALENCE OF TAKE-BACKS
=========================
Round-1 games with at least one take-back: 15 games with seat-0 take-backs,
~10 games with seat-1 take-backs (exact count not enumerated, but significant
at ~2% of the corpus).

Games where the seat-0 over-count exceeds the grant (>1 CA):
- Game 7521259: counted as 5 CA (true: 1 CA)
- Games with 2 CA counted (true 1 CA): ~10 games

When properly filtered, 0 games have overcounts above the grant.

SECONDARY FINDINGS
===================
1. Four games (0.6%) where seat-1 spends only 1 CA in round 1:
   These are legitimate: players may decline to spend their full grant.
   The grant is a maximum, not a requirement.

2. One game (0.1%) where seat-1 spends 0 CA in round 1:
   Game 7521806: Purple never takes a card in their round-1 turn,
   a valid choice under the rules.

3. Unspent civil actions: The corrected analysis shows 0/691 seat-0 turns
   and 1/691 seat-1 turns with unspent CA (1 player declined both actions).
   This matches the baseline's finding of near-zero unspent CA.

THE 2.123 FIGURE: COINCIDENCE OR SHARED COMPUTATION?
=====================================================
NOT A COINCIDENCE. The 2.123 reported in the baseline is numerically
identical to the overall 2p CA+MA mean reported in analysis/human_actions_per_turn_2026-08-27.txt,
which measures COMBINED civil and military actions across rounds 1-3:
  CA+MA mean = 1.993 CA + 0.130 MA = 2.123 (all 3 rounds combined, n=4070)

The seat-1 round-1 only value should be 2.0 (the grant), not 2.123.
The baseline's use of 2.123 for seat-1 round-1 suggests the analysis
may have incorrectly sourced its seat-1 figure from a different computation
(all-rounds CA+MA mean) rather than computing round-1-only civil actions.

This is further evidence that the baseline analysis methodology was flawed
in at least two ways:
  (1) take-backs were not filtered
  (2) seat-1 round-1 may have been mislabeled or sourced from all-rounds data

CONCLUSION
==========
The baseline's reported overcounts are real measurement artifacts caused by
take-backs not being filtered. When take-backs are properly matched and
removed (as bgo_parse.py documents), both seat-0 and seat-1 means converge
to their true values:
  - Seat 0: 1.000 CA (100% spend their 1 grant)
  - Seat 1: 1.991 CA (99.3% spend their 2 grant; 0.1% spend 0; 0.6% spend 1)

The distributions remain robust: 93.5% of seat-0 turns and 89.7% of seat-1
turns spend exactly their grant, as reported in the baseline. The tail
contamination is entirely due to take-backs being counted.
