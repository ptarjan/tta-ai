production_key_counterexamples_2026-08-27
==========================================

Job: does the CARD DATA contain a counterexample (something that can
DECREASE the quantity or make more of it a cost) for each of the seven
production keys, the same way Vast Territory's `blueTokens: -1` is one for
Colonies/HasColony? Read-only, mechanical. No weights.rs edit, no
sign_intent proposal.

Headline, said plainly and up front:

  * ONE of the seven keys has a card-data counterexample: RESOURCE_RATE.
    Acceptance of Supremacy (side B) prints `resourceProduction: -1`, which
    reaches `stats.resources` with a negative sign and therefore can drive
    `ResourceRate` negative. This does NOT touch FoodRate (the queued
    experiment's key) -- food is unaffected. See "RESOURCE_RATE" below.

  * FOOD_RATE has NO food counterexample in the card data. Nothing in the
    base-2015 card set prints a negative food production, removes food, or
    makes more food a cost. The queued food_rate change is not killed by a
    card-data counterexample. (See the nuance at the end about the
    consumption netting, which is a rulebook deduction, not a card.)

  * The other five keys (FoodStock, BestFarm, BestMine, ProdWorkers,
    FreeWorkers) also have NO counterexample in the card data.

PER-KEY VERDICTS
==========================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOOD_RATE  --  NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
What was searched: every `food` / `foodProduction` / `gainFood` / `foodAnd
orResources` value in all three data/*.json (exhaustive negative walk),
every `food:` in card_table.rs, the `EventBlock.food` / `food_and_or_
resources` / `lose_all_stored_food` / `choose_food` writers in events.rs,
and `effects.rs::compute`'s food accumulation.

Findings:
  * No card, wonder, leader, or event prints a negative food PRODUCTION.
    The only negative `happy`/`science`/`strength` productions (Communism
    -1 happy, Fundamentalism -2 science, Kremlin -1 happy) are on other
    statistics; nothing is `food: -N`.
  * The only food-removing mechanic in the data is `lose_all_stored_food`
    (a bool, `EventBlock`), and it targets STORED food (`p.food`), not the
    RATE. It never appears in a negative `food:` production field, and
    features.rs:713 reads `s.food` (the rate from `effects::compute`), not
    stored food, so it cannot reach this key.
  * The net `- consumption` at features.rs:713 is a RULEBOOK deduction
    (economy::consumption of the projected yellow bank), not a card effect.
    It is always non-negative and is already netted into the feature by
    design -- it is not a card-data counterexample and not a sign that a
    bigger number is a downside.

Verdict: NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA. (This is the key the queued
experiment targets; nothing in the card data licenses a negative food rate
the way Vast Territory licenses a negative blue token.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
RESOURCE_RATE  --  COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The quoted line (the Vast-Territory-shaped hit):

  data/cards_military_actions.json :: Acceptance of Supremacy ::
  "effects": { "noAttacksBetweenParties": true,
               "A": { "resourceProduction": 1 },
               "B": { "resourceProduction": -1 } },
  "text": "Neither player may attack the other. Player A gains +1 resource.
           Player B loses 1 resource."

Same fact in the engine's own table, card_table.rs:2572:

  Special::A(PactBlock { ..., resources: 1, ... }),
  Special::B(PactBlock { ..., resources: -1, ... }),

Where it lands in the feature:
  effects.rs:921   stats.resources += block.resources as i32;   (apply_pact_block)
  effects.rs:1517  stats.resources = stats.resources.max(0);    ("Limits on Ratings" clamp)
  features.rs:715  f.set(WeightKey::ResourceRate, f64::from(s.resources) + g(GainFeature::ResourceRate) - corruption);

So side B of a live Acceptance of Supremacy subtracts 1 from `s.resources`
and therefore from `ResourceRate`, before the `.max(0)` clamp. A player who
is a party B of this pact (and has little else) can read `s.resources <= 0`
-- the resource rate genuinely prices negative for that player, exactly the
Vast-Territory shape. Note the clamp means the STORED feature `s.resources`
is floored at 0, but the underlying `stats.resources` before the clamp is
negative, and any feature that reads it before/at that point (or that the
evaluator reconstructs) sees the negative. The card data licenses a
non-positive resource rate for a pact side.

Verdict: COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND -- Acceptance of Supremacy side B
`resourceProduction: -1` (data/cards_military_actions.json; card_table.rs:
2572), reaching `stats.resources` via effects.rs:921 and `ResourceRate` via
features.rs:715.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOOD_STOCK  --  NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
What was searched: same exhaustive negative walk (no negative `food:`
anywhere), the `lose_all_stored_food` event writers, and the feature setter
features.rs:736 (`p.food + pending` -- a STORED-food stock, floored at 0 by
the engine's own `p.food` type).

Findings: `p.food` is a non-negative stored-food count. `lose_all_stored_
food` (a bool, not a negative integer) sets it to 0 but never negative. No
card makes stored food a negative quantity. The feature is a stock, not a
rate; it cannot go negative from card data.

Verdict: NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEST_FARM  --  NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
What was searched: the `BestTypes.farm` computation in features.rs
`sweep_tableau` (features.rs:188 ff.), which takes `id.level()` of each
`CardType::Farm` in the tableau; the farm `production.food` values in
data/cards_civil.json (all positive: Agriculture 1, Irrigation 2, ...); and
card_table.rs farm definitions.

Findings: `BestFarm = max farm LEVEL in tableau` (features.rs:971, fed by
`best.farm` at features.rs:188 ff.). A farm's level is a non-negative
integer; a card's production can be negative on OTHER statistics but farm
production is `food: +N` only. Nothing in the card data makes a farm's
LEVEL negative or makes a higher best-farm a cost. (The negative
`production.food` values that DO exist are on Communism/Fundamentalism-
style effects and are not farm levels.)

Verdict: NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEST_MINE  --  NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
What was searched: the `BestTypes.mine` computation in `sweep_tableau`
(features.rs:188 ff., `CardType::Mine`), mine `production.resources` values
in data/cards_civil.json, and card_table.rs mine definitions.

Findings: `BestMine = max mine LEVEL in tableau` (features.rs:972, fed by
`best.mine`). Mine levels are non-negative; mine production is
`resources: +N` only in the data. Nothing makes a mine level negative or a
cost. (The single negative `resourceProduction: -1` is Acceptance of
Supremacy's PACT side B, not a mine card -- it changes a player's resource
RATE, not any mine's level or production print.)

Verdict: NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROD_WORKERS  --  NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
What was searched: the `prod_workers` accumulation in `sweep_tableau`
(features.rs:188 ff., `CardType::Farm | CardType::Mine` worker counts), the
feature setter features.rs:863, and every card that could REMOVE workers
from farm/mine slots (no such card in the data -- the only worker-movers are
population changes that add FREE workers, not remove staffed ones).

Findings: `ProdWorkers = sum of workers on farm+mine slots` (features.rs:
863, fed by `prod_workers` in `sweep_tableau`). Workers on a slot are a
non-negative count; `sweep_tableau` sums `slot.workers`, never a negative.
No card in the data decrements a staffed farm/mine worker or makes more
staffed production workers a cost.

Verdict: NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
FREE_WORKERS  --  NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
What was searched: `p.workers_free` (the field behind features.rs:840), the
only writer that decrements it (`apply.rs:881 do_build`, per features.rs's
own comment), and every card that could remove a free worker.

Findings: `FreeWorkers = p.workers_free + pending` (features.rs:840).
`p.workers_free` is a non-negative free-worker pool; it is decremented only
by BUILDING a slot (apply.rs:881 `p.workers_free -= 1`), which is a
conventional cost of an action, not a card-data counterexample of the
Vast-Territory kind (no card prints a negative worker value, removes free
workers, or makes more free workers a cost). No negative `workers:` appears
anywhere in the data or card_table.rs.

Verdict: NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND IN CARD DATA.
