Round-1 civil-action measurement, human BGO corpus (read-only; no bot runs, no build)
corpus: 1011 journals, 2525 player-games, all age-A round 1
method: every round-1 line matching '<Colour> takes <Card> in hand <Colour> uses <N> civil action'.
Round 1 contains ONLY takes: corpus-wide verb census for round 1 = takes 3923, 'puts X back in the row
... gets N civil action' refunds 257, plus phase/score/bookkeeping lines; 0 builds, 0 population, 0
develops, 0 political plays in any of the 2525 player-games. So 'actions by kind' = kinds of takes,
and CA spend = CA taken. Card taxonomy from data/: cards_wonders_leaders.json (16 wonders, 24 leaders),
cards_civil.json (54: 8 government, 12 special-tech, 4 farm, 4 mine, 12 urban, 10 military),
cards_military_actions.json (action cards). Journal prints 'Stockpile'; data names it 'Stock Pile'
(same card: Age A, type action, 'Gain 1 food and 1 resource') - matched, not a gap.

== 4. CHOICE vs AVAILABILITY: is the unused CA deliberate? ==
Method: seat = order of first logged r1 event (1 = first to act, 4 = last). Net spend = sum of
CA taken (refunds logged as 'puts X back in the row ... gets 1 civil action' are NOT subtracted;
245 of 257 r1 refunds sit immediately after the same player's take = undo/refund, so net spend
overstates true spend slightly; direction of the result below is unaffected).
The card row does not slide until all players have picked in age A, so late seats face a row
whose cheap (1-CA, positions 1-5) end has been partly consumed.

unused r1 CA by seat (conditional on net spend <=4, i.e. CA-gain games excluded):
  seat 1: n=1008 mean=2.93 min=0 q25=3 median=3 q75=3 max=3   wasting>=1: 1007/1008 (99.9%)  spent all 4: 1
  seat 2: n=1002 mean=1.90 min=0 q25=2 median=2 q75=2 max=3   wasting>=1: 982/1002 (98.0%)   spent all 4: 20
  seat 3: n=300  mean=0.98 min=0 q25=1 median=1 q75=1 max=2   wasting>=1: 290/300 (96.7%)    spent all 4: 10
  seat 4: n=162  mean=0.10 min=0 q25=0 median=0 q75=0 max=1   wasting>=1: 16/162 (9.9%)      spent all 4: 146
(4p games: 185 player-games at seat 4; the 3p corpus has no seat 4. Seat 3-4 n's differ by player count.)

net spend per player-game by seat:
  seat 1: n=1010 mean=1.08 median=1 max=5
  seat 2: n=1009 mean=2.12 median=2 max=6
  seat 3: n=319  mean=3.18 median=3 max=9
  seat 4: n=185  mean=4.25 median=4 max=11

CA band mix by seat (cheap end = 1-CA band = row positions 1-5):
  seat 1: cheap 1088 (100.0%)  n=1088
  seat 2: cheap 1359 (77.7%) expensive 390 (22.3%)  n=1749
  seat 3: cheap 373 (56.6%) expensive 286 (43.4%)   n=659
  seat 4: cheap 173 (40.5%) expensive 254 (59.5%)   n=427

VERDICT: the round-1 "waste" is BOTH, and it is seat-driven:
- EARLY seats (1-2) have a CHOICE: the row is fresh, every take they make is in the cheap 1-CA
  band (seat 1: 100% cheap; seat 2: 77.7%), yet they still leave a median 3 / 2 of 4 CAs
  unspent (99.9% / 98.0% waste at least one). That is a deliberate non-spend: with 4 CAs and a
  full cheap end, they could have taken 2-4 one-CA cards and chose not to.
- LATE seat 4 is AVAILABILITY-limited: 146/185 (78.9%) spend all 4 (median unused 0), and 59.5%
  of their takes are 2-3-CA band cards - the cheap end is consumed, so the row forces expensive
  takes and the CA budget runs out. Seat 4's "no waste" is the row, not restraint.
- The corpus-wide "median 2 unused / 91% waste >=1" mixes these: it is a choice statement for
  seats 1-3 (which hold 2327 of 2525 player-games) and an availability statement for seat 4.
  For weight work, the human baseline is "early seats deliberately bank CAs in r1; the last seat
  is budget-limited", NOT a uniform deliberate-banking claim.

== 5. HAND-CAP HYPOTHESIS TEST (coordinator-proposed) ==
Hypothesis: humans bank r1 CAs because 4 hand-card takes fill the civil hand to its cap
(= CA total = 4 under Despotism, RULES_SPEC §2.5/§6.7) and starve round 2; the escape route
is wonders (taken directly into play, §2.4, no hand space). Test: hand-card takes vs wonder
takes by seat, the rate of reaching the hand cap, and whether heavy r1 hand-card spend
predicts less r2 activity (pop+builds).
NOTE: the 257 "puts X back in the row" refunds are NOT netted from hand takes (they undo a
take and free a hand slot), so hand takes here are a slight OVERCOUNT; the conclusion is
unaffected because the cap-reach rate is ~0% either way.

r1 hand-card takes per player-game by seat:
  seat 1: n=1010 mean=0.69 median=1 max=5
  seat 2: n=1009 mean=1.30 median=1 max=6
  seat 3: n=319  mean=1.52 median=1 max=5
  seat 4: n=185  mean=1.73 median=2 max=4
r1 wonder takes per player-game by seat:
  seat 1: n=1010 mean=0.39 median=0 max=2
  seat 2: n=1009 mean=0.44 median=0 max=3
  seat 3: n=319  mean=0.55 median=1 max=3
  seat 4: n=185  mean=0.58 median=1 max=3
P(hand takes >=4, i.e. hand at the cap): seat 1: 2/1010 (0.2%), seat 2: 5/1009 (0.5%),
seat 3: 2/319 (0.6%), seat 4: 6/185 (3.2%). Corpus-wide 15/2523 (0.6%).
P(>=1 wonder taken): seat 1: 38.2%, seat 2: 41.8%, seat 3: 52.4%, seat 4: 56.2% (n rises with seat).
Among the 15 players who reached the hand cap: 3 also took a wonder (20%).

r2 activity (pop+builds, the actions the cap would starve) by r1 hand-take count:
  hand takes 0: n=558  mean=1.43 median=2
  hand takes 1: n=1256 mean=1.41 median=2
  hand takes 2: n=597  mean=1.40 median=1
  hand takes 3: n=97   mean=1.29 median=1
  hand takes 4: n=11   mean=1.18 median=1
Controlled by seat (r2 actions, r1 hand takes >=3 vs <=2):
  seat 1: n=4 vs n=1006, mean 1.50 vs 1.33 (no difference; n tiny)
  seat 2: n=33 vs n=976, mean 1.27 vs 1.47 (direction consistent with the hypothesis; n small)

RESULT: NOT SUPPORTED as stated.
- The restraint is NOT the hand cap: 99.4% of player-games never approach it (median 1 hand
  take; >=4 only 0.6%). Seat 1's median-3-unused is NOT "banking to avoid a full hand" -
  they take ~1 hand card and ~0.4 wonders and stop.
- The wonder escape route is not used for heavy spending: wonder-take probability RISES with
  seat (38%->56%), but only to a mean of 0.58; and only 20% of the 15 cap-reachers used one.
  Wonders are taken by late seats (who face the expensive row), not as a heavy-spend device.
- The predicted round-2 starvation is absent: r2 pop+builds mean 1.43->1.18 from 0 to 4
  r1 hand takes (monotone, but the >=3 groups are n=97/n=11), and within seats 1-2 the
  difference is 0.15-0.20 actions on small n. No starved round 2.
The hand cap is REAL (4 takes legal, 5th not) but is a LEGALITY bound, not a behaviour driver:
it constrains the max, which no one approaches. The seat-1/2 banking from section 4 stands
without this mechanism - the data show deliberate under-spend, and the hand cap does not explain it.
