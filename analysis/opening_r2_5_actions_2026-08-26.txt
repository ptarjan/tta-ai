Rounds 2-5 civil actions, first build/pop, and take CA bands, human BGO corpus (read-only; no bot runs, no build)
corpus: 1011 journals, 2525 player-games; rounds 1-5 measured (r1 included for continuity with the round-1 file)
CA-spend parsing: take = 'uses N civil action' clause (N = CA spent); build/pop/elect/discover/play-action = 1 CA each;
'plays event' = 0 CA; 'puts X back in the row' = refund, counted as a kind, NOT subtracted from spend;
standalone 'spends N resources/food' lines are resource costs, not CA. CA bands: 1 = row positions 1-5, 2 = 6-9,
3 = 10-13, 4+ = beyond 13 (CA-gain effects let takes cost 4+; see round-1 file).
Government changes in r1-5: 30 revolutions (30 in r2-5) + 63 peaceful changes = 93 of 2525 player-games
REVOLUTIONS ARE CA-OPAQUE: the journal logs the science cost, not how many CAs were available/spent,
and no CA-adjustment line follows. By rule a revolution consumes ALL available CAs (unused = 0 that round),
so revolution rounds are set to unused=0 in the tracked table. Peaceful changes are tracked: the new
government's civilActions (data/cards_civil.json) becomes the baseline; unspent CAs carry over by rule.
GOV CA totals: {'Communism': 7, 'Republic': 7, 'Fundamentalism': 6, 'Theocracy': 4, 'Despotism': 4, 'Monarchy': 5, 'Constitutional Monarchy': 6, 'Democracy': 7}
