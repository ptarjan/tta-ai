Human mid-game action census: per-player-round action-type counts, rounds 8-16

Source: sources/bgo/journals/ (TSV journals), 2-player games only.
2p selection: games in analysis/guard_ids_882.txt with exactly 2 distinct player_colour values = 632 games (3p=112, 4p=138 excluded).

Line format parsed (tab-separated, header row skipped):
  date <TAB> player_colour <TAB> age <TAB> round <TAB> text
  - ROUND NUMBER = column 4 ("round"), parsed as a base-10 integer; only rows with round in 8..16 counted.
  - ACTION TYPE = the first word of the text field (column 5) AFTER the leading player-colour word.
    The journal starts an action line as "<Colour> <verb> ..." (e.g. "Orange builds Bronze...",
    "Purple increases population...", "Green takes X in hand..."). The verb is extracted with the regex
    r"^\w+\s+(\w+)\b" on the text, i.e. the single word following the colour. Lines whose text does
    not start with a colour word are counted under <unparsed>.
  - EVERY action line within a player-round is counted (a player-round contains many lines; each is one action).
  - PLAYER-ROUND = one distinct (player_colour, round) pair per game. n per round = number of such pairs across all 632 games.
    Mean per cell = (total lines of that verb in that round) / (n player-rounds that round).

Table: rows = action type (ordered by total count, desc); columns = rounds 8..16.
Each cell = mean count of that action type per player-round in that round = total / n. n row = player-rounds.

================================================================================
REVIEWER VERIFICATION AND ONE CONTRADICTION - 2026-08-28
================================================================================

PARSING RULE CHECKED AND CORRECT. Journal header is
  date <TAB> player_colour <TAB> age <TAB> round <TAB> text
verified against sources/bgo/journals/7520707.tsv. Round is column 4, integer.
The verb is the word after the leading colour word. "Action Phase begins" ->
"Phase" and "No Discard Phase" -> "Discard" reproduce the two system rows, so
the rule is doing what it says.

PRIVACY CLEAN. Two suspicious tokens in the action-type column are CARD names,
not accounts: "Columbus"/"Territory" come from Christopher Columbus lines
("Christopher Columbus discovers I / Vast Territory") and "Sebastian" from
"Johannes Sebastian Bach...". No account name reaches this file. Note the
journals DO carry real account names on the round-1 "Game <name> ... created."
line; it is outside the r8-16 window and never counted.

KNOWN SMALL UNDERCOUNT. Lines whose text begins with a LEADER NAME instead of a
colour word are mis-bucketed under that name. Columbus 101 + Sebastian 44 +
Territory 3 = ~148 lines, against ~110k counted. It very slightly understates
"discovers" and "upgrades". Not material; do not re-run for it.

INTERNAL CONSISTENCY CHECK - PASSES. The five civil-action verbs sum to a
plausible civil-action count that rises the way a real TTA turn does:
  takes + builds + discovers + upgrades + increases
  r8  4.291    r12  5.206    r16  5.888
That is the right shape and magnitude for a mid-game civil-action allowance,
which independently confirms the all-lines re-run was the correct fix.

** THE CONTRADICTION. THIS CENSUS DOES NOT AGREE WITH THE POPULATION CURVE. **
population_curve_bot_vs_human_2026-08-28.txt has the HUMAN population rising
6.71 (r9) -> 13.92 (r17), +7.21 workers. This census counts human
increase-population actions over rounds 9-16 summing to only 3.66 per player.
An increase-population adds exactly one worker, so the curve claims very close
to TWICE the growth the journals contain.

The same table's yellow bank falls only 11.83 -> 6.33 (-5.50) over the same
span, and the bank must fall by at least as much as population rises (a token
comes out of it) PLUS 2 per age change. It does not. So the curve's human
population column and its human bank column cannot both be right either.

The near-exact factor of 2 is the shape of a per-game vs per-player-game
normalisation error. NOT YET DIAGNOSED - a definition read of both sides of
that report is out for review.

** DO NOT QUOTE THE +5.67 r17 POPULATION GAP, OR ANY ROW OF THAT CURVE. **

RESOLVED 2026-08-29 by `human_pop_from_journals_2026-08-29.txt`: the human
column was the broken side, and it is withdrawn. Re-derived from the journals
over 1264 player-rounds, humans take 8.089 population-increase actions and
lose 0.316 workers by the end of round 17, making the mid-game gap +1 to +3,
not +5.67. The census in THIS file is independent of that curve and stands.

METHOD NOTE: this census was commissioned as the human half of a comparison and
instead falsified the measurement it was meant to complement. A census of the
ACTIONS behind a curve is a conservation check on the curve. Run one before
trusting an accumulated quantity.
