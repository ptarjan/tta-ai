1. THE SAMPLING QUESTION THE RUN WAS LAUNCHED FOR: ANSWERED, AND THE ANSWER IS "NO".

So thin sampling is NOT why featspread emits zero rows. The hypothesis that

artifact of not observing the key fire.


2. WHAT THE RUN ACTUALLY FOUND: FEATSPREAD CANNOT SEE THE CREDIT CLASS.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
deep160 and the live table disagree about zero-ness on 67 cells, and the
disagreement is perfectly systematic in both directions.

19 keys are NONZERO in the live table and ZERO in featspread at every count
where the live table has a value. They are, exactly and without exception, the
credit class:

  ActionBoardCredit   AggressionBoardCredit  BonusCardCredit    BuildFreshCredit
  CardBoardCredit     CardBoardLeader        EventBoardCredit   FreeActionCredit
  GovBoardCredit      PactBoardCredit        RestrictedResourceCredit
  TacticBoardCredit   TacticReachCredit      TechBoardCredit    TerritoryCredit
  UnitStrengthCredit  UnitTechCredit         WarBoardCredit     WonderBoardCredit

That is 19 of the 20 credit keys; the 20th, CardRateCredit, is zero in both
because it is unreachable on every champion (see tta-card-rate-credit-is-dead).
No non-credit key appears in this set.

This is not a defect and not a sampling problem. It is why creditspread.rs
exists as a separate binary: a credit key never appears as a raw coordinate in
the dot product, so featspread's method has nothing to difference, and
creditspread measures it by firing slope instead (bound_from_slope,
firing_slopes). The live p95_candidate_spread table is therefore a MERGE of two
measurements -- featspread rows for ordinary keys, creditspread rows for the
credit class.

LANDING HAZARD, and the real reason this run mattered: any spread table
generated by featspread ALONE zeroes out all 19 credit keys. A zero row falls
back to CLAMP_BLIND, the LOOSEST rail, so landing such a table would silently
un-bound the entire credit class at all three player counts. The matched table
in featspread_matched_2026-08-27.txt has exactly this shape. It must not be
landed as-is; its credit rows have to be carried over from the live table or
re-measured with creditspread.
