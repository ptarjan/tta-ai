2p climb flatness autopsy -- 2026-08-15
========================================

QUESTION: 2p hill-climb ran 69,586 gens / accepted 8,807 mutants over ~9 days
but its win share vs the FIXED early reference champion_2p_gen1454_140key_2026-08-06
stayed flat (0.699 early window -> 0.642 late window, oscillating 0.47-0.70),
while 3p (0.365->0.815) and 4p (0.218->0.622) climbed against their own Aug-06
references. Why?

DATA CAVEAT (measured): rust_climb_2p.jsonl has 50,764 lines but gen numbers
run 1..69,599 with two large gaps in the logged gen sequence: 44852->57407
(12,555 missing) and 57436->63715 (6,279 missing). No rotated/backup log file
exists on disk to explain this (checked `ls -la logs/*climb*`). I could not
determine the cause (crash/relaunch losing lines? something not logged?) --
flagging as a data-quality caveat, not resolved. It does not appear to affect
the conclusion below since the effect (early-ref flatness) is visible
throughout the whole logged range, gaps included.

METHOD: read rust/src/bin/climb.rs (accept gate `challenge()` ~line 381,
`Config::null()` line 1330, defaults ~line 720), and rust_league.sh (identical
launch flags for all 3 arms: same --threads 2, same --max-games/--pool-k
defaults, same --accept-z 1.2816, only --players and --seed differ). Then
analyzed all three rust_climb_{2,3,4}p.jsonl with python3 (json per line;
fields per gen: accepted/vetoed/secs/since_accept/gauntlet[]/tried[]).

HYPOTHESES TESTED
------------------
H1 (gate too loose at 2p) -- RULED OUT.
Measured accept-rate: 2p 17.35% (8807/50764), 3p 15.01%, 4p 17.25% -- 2p is
NOT an outlier, if anything 3p (which climbed hardest) accepts LESS often.
Measured n games on accepted gens: median 72 (2p), 48 (3p), 72 (4p) -- similar.
Measured lo margin above null on accepted gens (null = 1/players, i.e. 0.5 /
0.333 / 0.25): median lo 0.508 (2p, margin +0.008), 0.343 (3p, +0.010), 0.257
(4p, +0.007) -- all three arms clear the gate by essentially the same thin
margin. The gate is not structurally looser for 2p.

H2 (2p is faster per gen, does a noisy random walk for longer) -- PARTIALLY
CONFIRMED as a contributing/enabling factor, not the cause of flatness itself.
Measured mean secs/gen: 2p 12.8s, 3p 21.4s, 4p 60.8s. 2p really does run far
more generations in the same wall time (69,586 vs 33,664 vs 12,982 gens over
the same ~9 days, roughly tracking the inverse of secs/gen). But H4 below
shows this extra volume of accepted mutants does NOT correlate with the
early-ref score moving up or down, so "more generations" alone does not
explain flatness -- it explains why 2p reached its plateau almost immediately
rather than explaining why the plateau exists.

H3 (2p incumbent churns too fast to consolidate, tiny since_accept) --
RULED OUT. Measured since_accept run-length (gens between accepts): median 4,
mean 5.97 (2p) vs median 4, mean 6.92 (3p) vs median 4, mean 6.11 (4p).
Statistically indistinguishable across arms.

H4 (accepting is anti-correlated with external reference score, i.e. climb.rs
is optimizing something that actively hurts external strength) -- RULED OUT.
Correlation between (# accepts in a ~50-gen window) and (change in gauntlet
share vs the early 2p ref over that same window), n=981 windows:
corr = -0.025 -- indistinguishable from zero. Accepting mutants is neither
helping nor hurting the early-ref score; the score's movement is pure noise
around a stable mean.

H5 (2p was already near its ceiling against this specific stale reference,
while 3p/4p had far more headroom) -- THIS IS THE ANSWER, and there is a
sharper, more precise version of it than "already good": the early-ref
metric is SATURATED, exactly like the already-established anchor-blindness
issue, just for the 2p gauntlet member specifically.

Measured (gauntlet share vs the FIXED EARLY 08-06 ref, over the full run):
  - 2p vs champion_2p_gen1454: slope of share-vs-gen ~ -0.00098 per 1000 gens
    (flat/negative), share ranges 0.47-0.95 across 981 samples, no trend.
  - 3p vs champion_3p_gen1384: slope +0.01312 per 1000 gens (climbing).
  - 4p vs champion_4p_gen448:  slope +0.02892 per 1000 gens (climbing).

But measured against the LATER, less-stale 08-08 reference
(champion_2p_gen19554_140key_2026-08-08, frozen only ~3 days after the run
started rather than from generation 1454), the SAME 2p arm shows a real,
substantial climb:
  gen 19600: share 0.50  ->  gen 69550: share 0.90 (622 samples),
  slope +0.00578 per 1000 gens, clearly positive and much larger in absolute
  terms than 3p's climb against ITS late ref (+0.00942 but only 0.5->0.6ish)
  and comparable in shape to 4p's late-ref climb (+0.08467, 0.3->0.87).

So the 2p arm IS improving in real skill throughout the run -- the flatness
is confined to the one early, long-stale 08-06 reference. The reason 3p and
4p show clean climbs against THEIR 08-06 references while 2p does not is
that the 2p arm's gen-1454 snapshot (frozen 08-06) was already strong enough,
by the time the run's logged/measured window begins, that further real
improvement stops moving the win-share needle against it -- the same
mechanism as the anchor going blind once win rate saturates: with a 60-game
gauntlet sample, once the true skill gap is large, sampling noise (the
0.47-0.95 range) dominates whatever small residual signal remains, so the
score just oscillates around a plateau instead of trending.

This is NOT a code bug and NOT evidence the 2p climb has stalled. It is a
measurement artifact: the specific frozen opponent used for the "did 2p
improve" headline number is stale for 2p in exactly the way rust_league.sh's
own comments (lines ~55-70) already describe happening to the 3p arm's 08-06
reference on 2026-08-08 ("gen1384 was diagnosed as a bad optimum... when the
live 3p champion had already climbed out of it unaided").

BEST EXPLANATION (single sentence)
-----------------------------------
The 2p arm's Aug-06 frozen reference (gen1454) was already thoroughly beaten
by the time the reported measurement window starts, so the win-share metric
against it saturated (like the anchor) and now just reports sampling noise;
the arm is demonstrably still climbing in real strength, as shown by its
0.50->0.90 climb against the less-stale Aug-08 2p reference over the same
period.

RECOMMENDATION
--------------
No change needed in climb.rs's accept gate, sigma schedule, or n-schedule --
H1/H3/H4 rule out anything wrong there, and it is identical across arms by
construction (rust_league.sh:97-118 launches all three arms with the same
flags, differing only in --players/--seed and per-arm gauntlet-eval file).

The fix, if the 0.699->0.642 headline number is going to keep being reported,
is at the ANALYSIS layer, not climb.rs: stop treating the single Aug-06 early
gauntlet member as "the" progress metric for 2p. Concretely:
  1. Whatever script/dashboard computes the "win share vs fixed reference"
     summary should report the LATE (08-08) gauntlet member
     (champion_2p_gen19554_140key_2026-08-08) as primary for 2p, or better,
     report the trend slope across ALL gauntlet members rather than a single
     early/late window average of one member -- this is already logged, no
     climb.rs change required, just a different read of
     rust_climb_2p.jsonl's `gauntlet` array (see rust_league.sh:52-70 for the
     full list of gauntlet member names available: 2P/3P/4P x {early, late}
     + human + hybrid-search).
  2. Per rust_league.sh's own precedent (the 3p false-reseed-alarm comment,
     lines ~55-63), consider freezing a THIRD, more recent gauntlet cut
     (e.g. an 08-15 snapshot per arm) periodically so "is this arm still
     improving" always has a reference from roughly one week prior rather
     than one that is 9000+ generations stale for the fast arm (2p) and
     only ~1400 generations stale for the slow arm (4p) at the same wall-clock
     age -- the staleness-in-generations is arm-speed-dependent even though
     the staleness-in-wall-clock-time is not.

No climb.rs or rust_league.sh line needs to change for the climb itself to
keep working correctly; the recommendation is purely about which existing
logged numbers to read when judging "is 2p still climbing."
