WHAT EACH MODE REPORTS (one sentence each)
- EMISSIVE/EMIT MODE: p95, over FIRING decisions only, of spread_k =
  max - min of the key's candidate value at every real self-play decision the
  champion reaches. This is the quantity p95_candidate_spread splices into
  weights.rs and clamp_bound divides by.
- DECISIVE MODE: the same spread_k statistic (my report's p95_spread column is
  the same firing-only p95) PLUS mean_level = mean |candidate value| (the
  alive-vs-dead split) and swing/capable_frac vs the actual best-minus-runner-up
  margin. Its spread column is directly comparable to emit mode's p95_spread.
So the two numbers ARE comparable: same statistic, same seed (0), same game
count (40), same feature code (eval::candidate_features, freeze pinned to the
champion), different CHAMPION VECTORS. The modes measure the same thing; the
instruments agree perfectly when pointed at the same champion.

WHY THE VALUE JUMPS BETWEEN CHAMPIONS (mechanism, measurement only)
The key varies across candidates only when a candidate move changes some
rival's civil_actions or changes WHICH rival holds the max (the loop never
touches dc). A champion that frequently plays moves touching rival CA state
makes the key fire; a champion that does not, reads spread 0. The champion
lineages in question sit 3.5-4 weeks of climb apart and their rival-context
weights moved substantially (frozen_0827 4p: rival_science_rate 13.85 vs
preclimb 4p 11.47; rival_free_ca -0.24 vs -0.08). The monotone 0/2.0/4.0
pattern in the OLD row is a sample property of that old champion at that
moment, not a law of player count: the NEW row is 0/0/1.0 and the
postclimb_0826 row is 2.0/2.0/0.0. The table's own doc comment (weights.rs
lines 2097-2100) already says the rows are "a property of the CHAMPION as
much as of the game" and "rerunning after a few thousand generations of
climbing moves the noisier rows by tens of percent".

THE PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE FOR CLAMP_BOUNDS (weights.rs:2078-2086)

when spread <= 0.0. The stale row currently gives rival_free_ca a 3p bound of
CLAMP_T * 483.787396 / 2.0 and a 4p bound of CLAMP_T * 545.274556 / 4.0 --
TIGHTER than the 60.0 flat rail. My 2026-08-27 measurement says the current
champion reads 0.0 at 3p (and the frozen-0827 4p spread is 1.0, not 4.0).
