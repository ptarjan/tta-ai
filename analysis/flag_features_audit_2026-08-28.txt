FLAG-FEATURE AUDIT (2026-08-28)

Scope: every feature written in rust/src/bots/weighted/features.rs whose value
is a FLAG (0/1, bool cast, is_none/is_some test) or a SMALL FIXED STEP
(count_ones, saturating small integer) -- not a continuous quantity. Weights
from analysis/champion_freeze_2026-08-26/rust_champion_{2,3,4}p.json
(169 keys each). Question per top-|w| flag: is there a SEPARATE channel that
discriminates within the thing being flagged, and is that channel ALIVE
(non-zero) on the live champion? A flag with a dead discriminator is the same
defect class as Leader (verified: seat-0 round-1 split, +18.85663).

BOTTOM LINE
=============================================================================

True Leader-class defects (flag/step with a dead or dwarfed within-class
discriminator, priced large on the live champion):

  A. TacticLevel at 3p (+60.0000, at the CLAMP_BLIND rail): the step is the
     only big price for "a tactic is in play"; the real discriminator
     (TacticGain, how much strength it adds) is alive but 60x smaller. The
     sign is +60 at 3p and -36 at 4p -- the league is paying a rail-pinned
     constant to express something the smaller channel already says.

  B. AttackCostDoubled at 3p (+60.0000, at the CLAMP_BLIND rail): one leader
     can produce this; the holder-side protection value has no channel at all,
     so the whole fact is priced by a single 0/1 constant pinned to the rail.

  C. SpecialTechs at 4p (+49.3279): ~20 different special-tech effects all pay
     the same +12.33 per tech; no per-special-tech channel exists (cards.rs
     special-matcher returns None for most).

  D. Leader (all three: 2p +18.86, 4p -22.60): confirmed, already addressed on
     the card side by CardBoardLeader (itself near dead: -0.19/-0.03/-0.10).
     The board-side per-leader channel is missing entirely.

  E. WarImmune at 4p (+26.8831): real fact, correct sign gate, but one constant
     over all pacts and positions; the only related channel (PactBlocksAttack)
     is alive but small and prices a different fact.

  F. NoAggression (all three, max 4p -2.69): no discriminator channel exists
     anywhere in the bot -- the flat flag is the sole entry point. Same shape
     as Leader; smaller magnitude.

The pattern: the league pushes the largest possible coefficient onto a
0/1 or small-step coordinate when the within-class channel is missing or too
small to carry the fact, and at 3p two of those coordinates are pinned at the
CLAMP_BLIND rail (60.0) -- the same "pinned coordinate" failure mode the
Uprising fix (features.rs:885-892) already documented for a different key.

Rail note (weights.rs:2185-2192, 2210-2240): `clamp_bound(players)` is
CLAMP_T * P95_TOTAL_SPREAD / p95_candidate_spread, capped at CLAMP_CEILING
(120.0, the 2026-08-27 experiment). `TacticLevel` (line 2320) and
`AttackCostDoubled` (line 2329) both carry a p95 spread of 1.0 at 3p -- the
measured value for any 0/1 step -- which is exactly what lets a 0/1 coordinate
legitimately sit at 60.0 under the blind rail. The instrument sees "this step
flips by 1.0 between candidates" and cannot see "the 1.0 it scales is a flag
with no within-class channel." That is the measurement-side half of this
defect class: spread-based rails cannot distinguish a 1.0 swing on a
continuous residual from a 1.0 swing on a Leader-shaped flag.
