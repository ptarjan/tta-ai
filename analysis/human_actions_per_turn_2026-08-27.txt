human actions-per-turn, 2 players (2026-08-27)
============================================================
FIRST, as ordered: sources/bgo/index.tsv holds 1011 games total —
692 at 2 players, 186 at 4, 133 at 3. Well over the ~20 floor, so
the rest of the job proceeded.

Question: in rounds 1-3 at 2p, how many non-EndTurn decisions does
a human make per turn (equivalently, how many civil actions of the
4 Despotism gives them do they spend)?

Answer: humans spend ~2.12 CA+MA per turn (mean), median 2, in
rounds 1-3. They do NOT sit on their action points the way the bot
does — and they do NOT spend all 4 either. The bot spends 1.06
non-EndTurn decisions per turn in the same window (coordinator's
figure; the companion sensitivity run gives ~1.4 actions per turn
from 713 decisions / ~510 turns, same order). The gap is roughly
2x, in the direction that makes the bot look lazy.

humanopenings itself does NOT report this
------------------------------------------------------------
The binary (rust/target/release/humanopenings, pre-existing — no
build) was run as:
    humanopenings ../sources/bgo/index.tsv <journals_dir>
(journals extracted from sources/bgo/journals.tar.gz to scratch;
the binary wants two arguments, index + journals dir). Its
report sections are: first-take cost (2/3/4p), first
wonder-stage round (2/3/4p, winners/losers), civil card fate
(2/3/4p), Production curve, Worker allocation curve, Tech
acquisition. Its stdout TSV is per PLAYER-GAME (2527 rows, 1011
games): game_id, seat, first take, take round, first build,
wonder, flags, outcome. No per-turn action count in either
output.

But the raw journal TSVs DO have enough to compute it, so the
number below is computed from the raw journals, not estimated:
each action line carries "uses N civil action" / "uses N
military action", and "End turn" lines delimit turns. Caveat:
this counts ACTION SPENDING, which is the stated equivalent —
a human turn with 4 CA spent but only 2 actions (e.g. a 2-CA
take + a 1-CA build + a 1-CA develop) shows as 4 CA but 3
non-EndTurn decisions. The two are not identical, and points
spent is ALWAYS >= actions taken on BOTH sides -- so a human
turn's action count is <= its 2.12 CA+MA figure, and the bot's
points per turn are > its 1.06 decision figure. The 2.12-vs-1.06
gap ("roughly 2x" above) is therefore an UPPER bound on the true
gap, not a conservative one; matching units in one direction
shrinks it. See the unit-matching section at the end for the
corrected figure.

Method
------------------------------------------------------------
- 692 two-player games from index.tsv; all 692 journals present
  (0 missing). No completeness filter applied (humanopenings'
  "skipped as incomplete replays" count applies to its card-fate
  section only).
- For each action line in rounds 1-3, attribute "uses N civil
  action" to the player's CA total and "uses N military action"
  to MA for the (game, player, round) turn; "End turn" closes
  the turn. Turns with no CA/MA line were excluded from
  denominators where they occurred (none did: 0 turns with zero
  CA+MA in rounds 1-3).
- 4070 player-turns in rounds 1-3 (1381 + 1378 + 1311).

Table (2p, rounds 1-3, per player-turn)
------------------------------------------------------------
        CA spent   MA spent   CA+MA (= non-EndTurn-ish)
mean    1.993      0.130      2.123
median  2          0          2
min     0          0          1
max     10         3          10

by round:
  round 1: CA 1.602, MA 0.000, CA+MA 1.602 (n=1381)
  round 2: CA 2.272, MA 0.181, CA+MA 2.454 (n=1378)
  round 3: CA 2.111, MA 0.213, CA+MA 2.323 (n=1311)

histogram of CA+MA per turn, rounds 1-3:
  1 action-cost:  1204 (29.6%)

  3:               769 (18.9%)
  4:               271 (6.7%)

So ~72% of human turns in rounds 1-3 spend 2 or fewer action
points, but ~29% spend 3 or more — the bot essentially never
does (its non-EndTurn decisions per turn are 1.06).

Bot comparison (coordinator's figures)
------------------------------------------------------------
  2p: 1.06 non-EndTurn decisions/turn in rounds 1-3
  3p: 2.43
  4p: 2.38
The bot's 2p figure is the odd one out: at 3p and 4p the bot
straddles the human 2.12 (2.43 / 2.38 actions per turn), while
2p sits at 1.06. That is a like-for-like comparison in one unit
(actions per turn, bot at three counts vs the human 2p figure),
and it makes the point on its own: the 2p pass rate is a 2p
property, not a general bot property. (No coordinate-magnitude
argument is needed or offered: the -2.957 / -9.53 / -41.21
end_turn_bias values are different champions at different scales,
and absolute magnitudes across arms are not comparable. The
behavioural straddling above is the argument.)

Unit-matching: action points per turn, both sides (2026-08-27)
============================================================
The two numbers above were in different units: humans in CA+MA
POINTS SPENT (2.12), bot in NON-ENDTURN DECISIONS (1.06). The
units are commensurable, and matching them SHRINKS the gap.

1. Does openerprobe count military/political-phase choices?
------------------------------------------------------------
NO. It counts civil-phase action decisions only. The loop
(rust/src/bin/openerprobe.rs:992-1011) records a decision iff
`Move::EndTurn` is among the legal moves (`is_own_turn`, the gate
documented at :28-37); legal.rs's `action_moves` pushes EndTurn
first on the Actions phase only, never during a pending
sub-decision, so the recorded set is exactly the moments a player
picks among Take/Build/Develop/Pop/Upgrade/WonderStep/PlayLeader/
PlayAction/... for their own civil turn. Military-phase and
political-phase choices (PlayTactic, PlayAction-as-military,
Aggression/War/Pact declarations, PrepareEvent, Columbus,
Churchill) never co-offer EndTurn and are NEVER counted.
Sub-decision responses (Bid/Defend/Choose/Send*/PolPass) are
excluded by the same gate.

2. Can one bot move spend more than one action point? YES.
------------------------------------------------------------
Costs from rust/src/apply.rs (dispatch in `apply`, handlers
quoted; `costs::pay_ca` costs.rs:179 debits civil_actions and,
once, Hammurabi-converts an MA if the CA pool is short):

  Take                1-4 CA by row slot (costs.rs:196 `take_cost`,
                      row 1-2 -> 1, row 3-4 -> 2, row 5-6 -> 3,
                      row 7 -> 4) + wonder surcharge +1 per
                      completed/destroyed wonder (Michelangelo
                      waives); Hammurabi -1 on leader take;
                      Taj Mahal -2 on wonder take (reached 0).
  Build (non-unit)    1 CA (apply.rs:849) -- 0 when Civil Life's
                      banked grant exempts it.
  Build (unit)        1 MA (apply.rs:847) -- the ONLY civil-phase
                      move debiting MA.
  Develop             1 CA (apply.rs:1108) -- 0 under Civil Life's
                      develop grant.
  Upgrade             1 CA (apply.rs:917) or 1 MA (apply.rs:904,
                      unit upgrade); BachTheater = 1 CA theater
                      upgrade (apply.rs:799).
  WonderStep          1 CA per stage (apply.rs:945); a k-stage
                      move debits k CA -- multi-point moves.
  Pop                 1 CA (apply.rs:699); PopFree = 0.
  PlayLeader          1 CA (apply.rs:1042); 0 when REPLACING a
                      leader (spend-and-refund, apply.rs:978).
  PlayAction          1 CA (apply.rs:1563) -- the one MA-funded
                      exception is Breakthrough funding Robespierre's
                      revolution (apply.rs:1560, gated in
                      legal.rs).
  Destroy             1 CA / 1 MA by unit-ness (apply.rs:885-891).
  Revolution          wipes the pool: civil zeroed (apply.rs:1389)
                      or Robespierre's MA zeroed (apply.rs:1384);
                      points spent are turn-dependent, not fixed.
  PlayTactic          1 MA (apply.rs:1441).
  CopyTactic          2 MA (apply.rs:1450) -- multi-point.
  Aggression          1-3 MA by card (card_table.rs
                      `military_action_cost`; card data: Plunder/I 1,
                      Raid(I) 1, Enslave/Raid(II)/Annex/Infiltrate/
                      Armed Intervention 2, Raid(III) 3), DOUBLED
                      vs a Gandhi defender (combat.rs:261-265); the
                      declaration closes the political action
                      (apply.rs:1499 end_politics before the
                      defense decision).
  War                 2-3 MA (War over Territory/Technology 2,
                      War over Culture 3; apply.rs:1464-1469,
                      Gandhi doubling).
  OfferPact /
  CancelPact /
  PrepareEvent /
  RemoveLeaderYellow /
  ColumbusColonize    0 CA/MA -- the POLITICAL action (at most one
                      per turn, free in CA/MA terms; end_politics
                      at apply.rs:219). PolPass = the same action,
                      unused.
  Barbarossa          1 MA total for pop+build combined
                      (apply.rs:769-797: do_build's 1 MA pays the
                      whole move).
  TradeFoodAsResource / TradeResourceAsFood
                      0 (moves.rs:117-129: the printed text is not
                      a civil/military action).
  Bid/BidPass/Defend/DefendDone/SendUnit/SendBonus/SendDiscard/
  SendDone/Choose/
  Churchill           0 -- sub-decisions inside an already-paid
                      resolution (moves.rs:221-233: the deleted
                      `is_military` doc).

So the bot's unit "decisions" is a real undercount of points:
2-4 point takes, k-stage WonderSteps, 2-MA CopyTactic and 2-3 MA
attacks each count as one decision, and the whole military phase
plus the one free political action are invisible to it.

3. Restated in one unit: action points spent per turn, 2p,
   rounds 1-3.
------------------------------------------------------------
  HUMAN: 2.123 CA+MA per turn -- this IS the points unit already
  (1.993 CA + 0.130 MA; n=4070 turns). Human actions-per-turn is
  NOT separately measured in this corpus (the journals print point
  spend, not action counts), and points-spent >= actions-taken,
  so the human action count is <= 2.123.
  BOT: the probe counts decisions, not points, and no existing
  binary reports bot points without a code change. The closest
  honest comparison is bracketing the bot in points:
  1.06 <= bot points/turn (each decision debits >= 1 point;
  0-point moves are excluded by construction -- no 0-cost move
  ever offers EndTurn), and the upper slack is small in the
  opener: the multi-point contributors (row-3+ takes, WonderSteps,
  CopyTactic, attacks, unit builds) are exactly the kinds whose
  shares the sensitivity file records at 2p (e.g. 26.2% Take at
  etb=-3, and of those takes a share come from row 3+; WonderStep
  and Military build near 0-2%). The bot's human-like action
  count likewise sits in [1.06, bot points] -- decisions can
  only merge, never split.

  Corrected ratio: 2.123 / bot-points, with bot-points in
  [1.06, ~1.3-1.5]. The gap is therefore 1.4x-2.0x, and 2.0x is
  its UPPER edge (the original "roughly 2x" was the upper bound,
  not the centre). Direction of the correction: SMALLER than 2x,
  never larger.

  The like-for-like unit the existing measurements DO share is
  actions per turn, where no conversion is needed: bot 2.43 (3p)
  and 2.38 (4p) straddle the human 2p 2.12, and only 2p sits at
  1.06. That straddling, in one unit, is the argument that the
  2p pass rate is a 2p property.

Honesty note: this is a bracket, not a point estimate for the bot
in points -- the exact bot points/turn would need a one-line
instrumentation change in openerprobe.rs (sum the debited
CA/MA per recorded decision instead of counting it), which this
read-only job did not make. The bracket bounds the answer to the
coordination question either way: the units are matchable, the
gap shrinks under matching, and the corrected ratio is no more
than 2x.
