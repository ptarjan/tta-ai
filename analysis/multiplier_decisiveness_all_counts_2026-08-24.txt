Both perturbations are a COPY of the champion vector used as BOTH the freeze
argument to a fresh candidate_features call AND the vector dot is taken
against, because a multiplier can change the feature it multiplies. That is
why a key can show term_nonzero_n = 0 (the quantity is zero at the champion
weight) and still show flips under ABS (a different weight reaches a
different branch) -- build_fresh_credit at 4p and rival_take_share at 4p are
exactly that.

term_nz = term_nonzero_n, the count of decisions where at least one
candidate's score moved when the key was zeroed. maxflip = the larger of
flip_rate_zero and flip_rate_abs.

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
--------------------
A key is a prune candidate only if its term is zero in ALL THREE counts. A
3p-only scan is what produced the previous prune list, 36 of whose 38 entries
were wrong. Every "dead key" claim below is a three-count claim.

FINDING 1 -- THE SIGN, NOT THE MAGNITUDE
----------------------------------------
Keys where flip_rate_abs > flip_rate_zero: correcting only the SIGN, with the
magnitude held at the champion's own value, changes more decisions than
switching the key off entirely.

  3p  tech_board_credit    0.2786 abs vs 0.1382 zero   (w = -25.4575)
      territory_credit     0.0989 abs vs 0.0524 zero
      event_board_credit   0.0947 abs vs 0.0612 zero
      war_board_credit     0.0401 abs vs 0.0174 zero
  2p  card_board_leader    0.0849 abs vs 0.0664 zero
      build_fresh_credit   0.0266 abs vs 0.0001 zero
  4p  territory_credit     0.0449 abs vs 0.0164 zero

      bonus_card_credit    0.0269 abs vs 0.0265 zero
      rival_take_share     0.0059 abs vs 0.0000 zero  (w = -7.8582)

These coefficients scale a value function that ALREADY carries its own sign.
A negative scale does not let a bad card price negative -- the inner function
does that -- it inverts good and bad. dominance_repair in eval.rs already
accepts precisely this argument for CardBoardCredit's per-type offsets, added
after the leadersign investigation priced Hammurabi at -13.28 on a +0.885 raw
board benefit; the same argument was declined twenty lines away in weights.rs
for this family.

FINDING 2 -- TWO KEYS ARE 3p-ONLY, WITH LIVE WEIGHTS ELSEWHERE
--------------------------------------------------------------
tactic_board_credit and tactic_reach_credit have term_nonzero_n = 0 at BOTH
2p and 4p while carrying nonzero weights there (tactic_reach_credit is
-0.4116 at 2p and 0.1971 at 4p), yet both are live at 3p (13771 and 12861
decisions, 2.8% and 2.6% flip). Tactics are not a 3p-only mechanic. This is a
reachability defect to trace, NOT a prune candidate -- retiring them would
delete a channel that demonstrably works at one table size.

Same shape, one count each: restricted_resource_credit and rival_desire and
rival_take_share and free_action_credit all read zero at 4p only;
card_rate_credit reads zero at 2p and 3p and live at 4p; bonus_card_credit
and wonder_board_credit and defense_bonus read zero at 3p only.

FINDING 3 -- SCALE IS NOT COMPARABLE ACROSS COUNTS
--------------------------------------------------
science_rate_trailing is 20.6190 at 2p, 0.5413 at 3p, -0.7103 at 4p, and its
flip rate is under 0.25% in all three. tech_board_credit is -25.4575 at 3p
and -0.3168 at 2p for the SAME key. These are three independently climbed
vectors that never see each other; a magnitude in one says nothing about the
same key in another. Do not reason about a key's importance from its weight.

CAVEATS
-------
1. Every number is measured against ONE frozen champion per count. A key that
   never fires under this vector may fire under another. The prune claim is
   "unreachable under the shipped champion in 90326 decisions", which is the
   strongest statement this instrument can make, and it is not the same as
   "dead code".
2. Sample sizes differ by count because game length does; the decision counts
   are within 15% of each other, which is close enough to compare rates.
3. flip_rate_abs is identically 0.0 for every key with a positive champion
   weight, because abs() of a positive is a no-op. That is the harness's
   built-in sanity check -- it confirms the comparison is not firing on float
   noise -- and it means a positive key's row carries no sign evidence.
4. The 32-key set is derived at runtime from features.rs via include_str!, so
   no WeightKey variant name appears as a string literal anywhere (registry.rs
   forbids it). 54 of 160 keys have no f.set slot; the other 22 of those are
   written directly by eval::linear_features or reached through the
   .early()/.late() phase suffixes.

WHAT TO DO WITH THIS
--------------------
Retire gov_action_cost and card_board_bonus via RETIRED_KEYS in eval.rs --
never by deleting the variant, since parse_weights hard-errors on an unknown
name and would break every champion JSON including the weights baked into the
wasm advisor. Verify the retirement is a same-seed identity no-op rather than
duelling it.

Gate the family NonNegative in WeightKey::sign_intent. Note that the gate
RAISES a violator to 0, it does not flip it, so gating alone buys the "zero
it" column and not the "correct it" column; the validation is a re-climb
under the constraint, not a patch to the champion.
