STEP 3 -- interpretation.

The seven keys are passengers. Full champion beats seven-key-ablated
champion at 50.1% +/- 0.3 against a null of 50.0%, p = 0.61 -- a wash, not a
win. This matches what step 1 already suggested structurally: five of the
seven never cleared the scatter of the other ~174 keys in the first place,
and even the two that did (culture_rate_trail_frac, culture_rate_behind_near
at 2p) only reached the 70th percentile, not the top of the distribution.
Combined with the peer's finding that 7 of 2p's 69 accepted climb steps were
GROUP moves on all of Rivals at once, the picture is consistent: these keys
moved because they were dragged along by an accepted step that paid for
other Rivals keys, not because moving them paid for anything themselves.

Per the standing instruction: this is a wash, so no second (two-key) arena
was run -- that would be fishing on a null result, not confirming one.

This is the expected, unremarkable result for keys authored at 0.0 and
promoted without their own screen: most keys landed this way turn out to be
passengers (memory tta-most-keys-cannot-decide: 61 of 160 keys already known
never to flip a decision). Nothing here says the seven keys are wrong to
have or actively harmful -- only that, on the current 2p champion, they are
not doing any measurable work.
