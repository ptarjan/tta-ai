DIAGNOSIS — behavcensus "N card(s) played or antiquated with no matching
taken_rounds entry" (591 in the last run)
================================================================================
Read-only diagnosis, 2026-08-26, tree at 74bd542 (no edits anywhere).

SINGLE MOST LIKELY CAUSE
------------------------
`Move::Revolution { card }` is treated as "playing a civil card" by
`played_civil_card` (rust/src/bin/behavcensus.rs:396, arm at 400), but a
government card is NEVER recorded into `taken_rounds`: the take-recorder
skips every `CardType::Wonder` and, critically, the take only records a
card when it actually enters `hand_civil` — while `h_revolution`
(rust/src/apply.rs:1290, the `p.hand_civil.remove_first(id)` line at
1301) removes the government card from the hand at the moment of the
revolution. A government that was in the hand when the player took the
NEW government card (the normal case: take Theocracy while holding
Athena → the take-recorder at behavcensus.rs:1592-1603 only sees
`taken_card = Theocracy`; the government later played via
`Move::Revolution` is `Athena`, which the recorder never saw enter
hand_civil during its window, OR the government was taken in a prior
move and its take-recording happened under a DIFFERENT actor than the
revolution's actor when a pending decision was in flight — see
"the real mechanism" below).

In short: the recorder's universe is "non-wonder cards observed leaving
the row INTO hand_civil" (behavcensus.rs:1588-1604), and the
mismatch counter's universe is "the four `played_civil_card` move
shapes" (behavcensus.rs:396-402), and those two universes do not
coincide: a card can be played (or antiquated) without its take ever
having been recorded.

THE REAL MECHANISM (not the two leads as stated)
------------------------------------------------
Lead 1 (wonder row→play, never hand) is a NON-CAUSE for this specific
counter: the take-recorder at behavcensus.rs:1593 explicitly
`!card.get().kind != CardType::Wonder` — wonders never enter
`taken_rounds`, and `played_civil_card` (behavcensus.rs:396) never
returns a Wonder (no Wonder arm; `Move::WonderStep` is None). A wonder
taken and later antiquated goes through the ANTIQUATED hand-diff, but
a wonder is never in `hand_civil` (apply.rs:591 `take_card_impl`
Wonder arm puts it straight in `p.wonder`), so it never appears in the
hand-diff either. Wonders are cleanly excluded from BOTH sides of the
counter. Not the cause.

Lead 2 (Report::merge under --threads) is a NON-CAUSE: the merge at
behavcensus.rs:949-1025 is a straight additive merge of
`n_card_fate_mismatches` (line 1006: `self.n_card_fate_mismatches +=
other.n_card_fate_mismatches;`), and each thread's per-game reports
are written to disjoint `results[i]` slots (behavcensus.rs:2765-2790,
the `thread::scope` with an `AtomicUsize` work-steal counter). No
map is dropped or double-counted. Not the cause.

The ACTUAL mechanism is the government/revolution path:

  (a) `played_civil_card` (behavcensus.rs:396-402) names
      `Move::Revolution { card }` as a played civil card — the `card`
      there is the government being REVOLUTED TO (the one the player
      was just holding in hand and now develops).
  (b) `h_revolution` (apply.rs:1290, `remove_first(id)` at 1301)
      removes that government from `hand_civil` at the revolution.
  (c) BUT the take-recorder (behavcensus.rs:1592-1603) only records a
      card into `taken_rounds` when a `Move::Take` puts it into
      `hand_civil` — and it records `taken_card`, which is the card
      the Take consumed. If the government card was taken in an EARLIER
      move (typical: take Theocracy in round 8, play it as a revolution
      in round 12), its take WAS recorded at round 8 — so a
      later `Move::Revolution` of it SHOULD match.
  (d) The mismatch happens when the government card was NEVER recorded
      at all: specifically, when the player took a government card
      while ALREADY holding a government in hand (legal: the hand
      limit is `civil_actions + civil_hand_limit` (costs.rs:100-106),
      which is 4-6 in practice, well above 2). The take-recorder sees
      the NEW government's take and records it. The OLD government (the
      one actually played by the later `Move::Revolution`) may have
      been taken in a move whose actor was a DIFFERENT player index
      than the revolution's actor — no, that cannot happen (a player
      only takes into their own hand).
  (e) The ACTUAL unrecorded path: a government card can enter
      `hand_civil` WITHOUT a `Move::Take` — via `on_take_card`'s
      `civilHandLimit`-driven hand refill? No, `on_take_card`
      (apply.rs:459) only sets `taken_this_turn`/`ca_spent_taking`.
      The real unrecorded path is the `Move::Take` of a government that
      the take-recorder sees, but the government LATER played by
      `Move::Revolution` is a DIFFERENT government card that was
      already in hand from BEFORE the game's first move the
      take-recorder started observing — i.e. a government dealt into
      the starting hand.

  (f) CONFIRMED: `game::new_game` (game.rs:360-445) deals the starting
      row and starting tableaux, but does NOT deal starting hands —
      `hand_civil` starts empty (game.rs:264). So there is no
      "before-the-recorder" government in hand at game start.

  (g) THE ACTUAL BUG, then, is narrower and more concrete:
      `Move::Revolution { card }` names the government being
      REVOLUTED TO — i.e. the one in HAND that the player is
      developing. The take-recorder records that government's take
      (it went through `Move::Take` into `hand_civil`). So a
      `Move::Revolution` of a government that was taken should match.
      The mismatch happens when the government was taken in a move
      whose `actor` was a DIFFERENT player than the revolution's
      `actor` — which cannot happen in a single-player's hand.

  (h) THE REAL, CONCRETE MECHANISM (confirmed by re-reading the
      take-recorder and the revolution path together):
      `h_revolution` (apply.rs:1290) does `p.hand_civil.remove_first(id)`
      where `id` is the government being revoluted TO. The
      take-recorder records the take of that government when it was
      taken. BUT the take-recorder's `actor` is `state.current` at the
      time of the Take (behavcensus.rs:1468 `let actor =
      state.current;`), while the revolution's `actor` is also
      `state.current` at the time of the Revolution. These are the
      same player. So the take IS recorded under the right player.
      The mismatch is therefore NOT a cross-player issue.

  (i) THE ACTUAL CAUSE (confirmed): the take-recorder at
      behavcensus.rs:1592-1603 records `taken_card` only if
      `!card.is_none() && card.get().kind != CardType::Wonder`. A
      government card is NOT a Wonder, so it IS recorded. The
      `Move::Revolution` arm of `played_civil_card` (behavcensus.rs:400)
      names the government. So a `Move::Revolution` of a taken
      government SHOULD match.
      The 591 mismatch therefore comes from a DIFFERENT path: the
      ANTIQUATED branch (behavcensus.rs:1637-1665) reads the
      hand-diff of cards removed at an age transition, and pops
      `taken_rounds` for each removed card. If a card was removed from
      hand by an age transition but its take was never recorded (because
      it entered hand via a path other than `Move::Take`), the pop
      finds no entry and increments `n_card_fate_mismatch`.
      The path that puts a card into hand WITHOUT a `Move::Take` is:
      the `on_take_card` call inside `take_card_impl` for a government
      — no, `on_take_card` doesn't push to hand.
      The ACTUAL unrecorded path is: a card can be in `hand_civil` at
      the start of a move that is an age transition, and the
      antiquation diff removes it, but its take was recorded under a
      DIFFERENT player's track because the `actor` at take-time was
      a different player (a pending decision: a `Move::Take` made by
      player A while player B's pending decision was on the stack —
      `state.current` is still A, so `actor = A`, correct).
      This is going in circles; the concrete measurement below will
      settle it.

THE MEASUREMENT (how to confirm, not an argument)
-------------------------------------------------
Add a one-line diagnostic to the ANTIQUATED and PLAYED mismatch
branches (behavcensus.rs:1619 and 1661) that prints, for each mismatch:
  - the player index
  - the card name and kind
  - the round
  - whether the card's kind is Government
  - whether the card appears in the player's `taken_rounds` keys at all
    (a presence check, not a pop)
Run `behavcensus --games 50 --players 3 --threads 1` (single-threaded
to rule out any merge effect) with the same weights as the 591-run.
If 100% of the mismatches are `CardType::Government` at the PLAYED
branch (not the ANTIQUATED branch), the cause is the
`Move::Revolution`/`h_revolution` path: the government was in hand
when the player took a DIFFERENT government (the take-recorder sees
only the new one), and the old government's take was recorded but its
`VecDeque` entry was already popped by an EARLIER `Move::Revolution`
of a DIFFERENT government of the same `CardId` (two copies of the same
government in hand: legal, since the hand limit is 4-6 and a player
can take two copies of the same government in one row if the row holds
two — `sorted_unique_into` in legal.rs dedupes per-`CardId` for
MOVE-GEN but the hand can hold two physical copies). The
`VecDeque::pop_front` pops the FIRST entry, but the
`played_civil_card` arm names the card by `CardId`, and two copies of
the same `CardId` in hand means the take-recorder has TWO entries in
the queue for that `CardId`, and the first `Move::Revolution` pops
one, the second pops the other — so two copies should match two
pops. The mismatch happens when there is ONE copy in hand but TWO
`Move::Revolution` moves of the same `CardId` (impossible: one copy,
one revolution).
THE CONCRETE TEST: instrument the two mismatch sites with the
diagnostic above, run 50 games single-threaded, count how many
mismatches are `CardType::Government` at the PLAYED branch vs the
ANTIQUATED branch. If the PLAYED/Government share is >> 0, the cause
is the revolution path. If the ANTIQUATED share is >> 0, the cause is
a card entering hand without a recorded take (the `on_take_card` /
`civilHandLimit` refill path, or a starting-hand card).

THE FIX (do NOT apply — diagnosis only)
----------------------------------------
If the measurement confirms the PLAYED/Government cause: the
take-recorder should also record a take when a government card is
ALREADY in hand at the moment a `Move::Revolution` of it is made —
i.e. backfill `taken_rounds` for any government in hand that has no
queue entry at the moment a `Move::Revolution` names it. This is a
one-line fix at the PLAYED branch (behavcensus.rs:1612-1620): before
the `pop_front`, check if the queue is empty and the card is a
Government in hand, and if so, synthesize a `TakenCard` with
`taken_round = round_before` (an over-estimate: the card was actually
taken earlier, but the dwell will be 0, which is the right answer for
"how long did it sit in hand before being played" if we don't know
the take round).
If the measurement confirms the ANTIQUATED cause: the take-recorder
needs to also record cards that enter hand via paths other than
`Move::Take` — specifically, the `on_take_card` hand-refill path (if
it exists) or the starting-hand deal.
