  WonderProgress   features.rs:639, weights.rs:657   champ3p = 6.375

`progress` is the sum of the RESOURCE COSTS of stages already BUILT
(horizon.rs:629), in the same units as WonderRemaining (horizon.rs:630).
weights.rs:678-680 calls it "a RATE-shaped sunk-cost magnitude tuned to value
how much a wonder is worth finishing" - the structural complement of
WonderPromise, which fires at the TAKE and decays as stages are paid.
It is provably exactly 0.0 until the first stage is paid, pinned by
features.rs:1148 `wonder_in_progress_flips_to_one_the_instant_a_wonder_is_taken`.

WonderPotential is the identity-aware payoff channel (paid_fraction *
collect_fraction, cards.rs:2130-2147); also 0.0 at the take, growing as stages
are paid.
