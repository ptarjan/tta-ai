CA-waste reconcile: where "20.3% of civil actions wasted" came from,
and whether it conflicts with 1.06 non-EndTurn decisions/turn (2026-08-27)
============================================================
Read-only: grep of analysis/ + git log. No new measurement, no
estimate.

The two figures
---------------
(A) 1.06 non-EndTurn DECISIONS per turn, 2p, rounds 1-3, frozen
    champion (openerprobe: 360 non-EndTurn / 713 total own-turn
    decision points). Definition: how many times in a round-1-3
    civil phase the bot picked a non-EndTurn move, per turn.
    One decision debits 1-4 CA (takes), 1 MA (unit build), or 0
    (nothing 0-cost ever offers EndTurn).
(B) "20.3% of civil actions wasted", recorded as a standing
    headline defect in the project notes.

Where (B) came from
-------------------
The exact denominator was NEVER re-derived in this repo. The
earliest file that quotes it, analysis/opening_census_2026-08-24.txt
(Q1 section), says so explicitly:

  "the '20.3%' figure's exact denominator was not re-derived
   here, so the two percentages are of the same order but not
   proven to be the same unit"

and compares it against its own measured ">=1 unused CA in rounds
1-3" rate of 28.0% (84/300 2p player-games, bot, champion gen
108239), calling 28.0% "higher than the '20.3% wasted overall'
standing figure quoted in the task, i.e. the opener is WORSE than
the whole-game average". So (B) was already a second-hand "whole
game" number by 2026-08-24: whole game (not opener-only),
fraction-of-CAs (not fraction-of-player-games), 2p champion of the
era (gen ~108k, pre the 2026-08-25 climb restart).

The tool family that produced the underlying CA-waste numbers:
analysis/wasted_actions.py (deleted in 80314c7 "Delete dead
analysis/", 3 weeks old) + analysis/wasted_summary.py.
wasted_actions.py self-played the champion, recorded EVERY turn
that ended with civil actions unspent, and printed exactly:

  {n}/{turns} turns end with CA unspent ({share:.1%}),
  {ca_left} CA wasted = {ca_left/turns:.2f}/turn

i.e. two distinct rates: (i) turns-ending-unspent / total turns,
and (ii) total CA wasted / total turns. wasted_summary.py
aggregated the per-event dumps (ca_wasted_total,
ca_wasted_with_no_option, declined_a_move, ...). The 20.3% is one
of these two rates on a whole-game self-play run; which of the two,
and on how many games, is not recoverable from the repo (the .json
dumps and the script are both gone with 80314c7). What IS
recoverable: the rate family (CA wasted per turn, or turns ending
unspent) is NOT "player-games with >=1 unused", and the 28.0%
opener number from the same era is in the same order, which
brackets the 20.3% whole-game figure between the opener rate and
something milder — consistent with "whole game is better than the
opener" (later census: 4p 24.0% vs 2p 2.3% opening rates in
analysis/opener_census_postclimb_2026-08-26.txt).

Subsequent history of the defect:
- Root cause, analysis/ca_waste_cause_2026-08-24.txt (46b88e6):
  the 1-ply scoring asymmetry -- EndTurn is the only candidate
  whose apply arm runs economy::end_of_turn, so it was scored
  post-production against pre-production rivals. A timing bug in
  WeightedBot::choose, not a weight.
- Fixed in eval.rs (score EndTurn on the unmoved state, learned
  end_turn_bias instead); greedy.rs and quiescent.rs audited and
  fixed the same way (analysis/endturn_sibling_fix_2026-08-24.txt,
  1e145e6/02086c6/5ee71a3).
- Post-fix censuses: opening ">=1 unused CA in rounds 1-3" is now
  2.3% (2p) / 1.4% (3p) of player-games
  (analysis/opener_census_postclimb_2026-08-26.txt) and 3.3% /
  1.7% in the 2026-08-27 recheck
  (analysis/opener_recheck_2026-08-27.txt). The 20.3% figure has
  not been re-measured since the fix because its original
  denominator was never pinned down; both follow-up censuses
  explicitly say "NOT the same denominator as the original claim".

Do they conflict?
-----------------
No. They measure different things, at different times, with
different denominators:

  1.06 (A): DECISIONS per turn, opener-only (rounds 1-3),
            current frozen champion, openerprobe. Counts how
            often the bot chose to act vs pass. One decision can
            debit 1-4 CA, so points/turn >= 1.06.
  20.3% (B): CA WASTED per turn (or turns ending unspent), whole
            game, champion of ~gen 108k (2026-08-24 era),
            wasted_actions.py self-play. Counts CAs that reached
            EndTurn unspent, over all rounds.

Three separate gaps make the "70%+ unspent" reading invalid:
1. Denominator: (A) is per turn, (B) is a FRACTION of CAs, over
   the whole game. 1.06 decisions/turn against 4 CA is NOT a
   waste rate -- it is a decision rate, and multi-CA takes plus
   carry-over (unspent CAs roll into the next round and are
   spent there; the bot's r2-5 CA spend in
   analysis/opening_r2_5_actions_2026-08-26.txt runs NEGATIVE
   unused in rounds 2-5, i.e. it spends into carried CAs) mean
   points-per-turn comfortably exceeds 1.06.
2. Rounds: (A) covers rounds 1-3 only; (B) covers the whole
   game, where the bot spends more per turn (its later-round CA
   spend is not the opener's).
3. Era: (B) predates the EndTurn-scoring fix (46b88e6 et seq.).
   The defect it measured is gone: current opening waste is 2.3%
   of 2p player-games leaving any CA unused in rounds 1-3, i.e.
   the bot now spends essentially everything it holds. (B) is a
   historical defect figure, not a current-state one.

Plain statement: 1.06 says "the bot acts on ~half its decision
opportunities in rounds 1-3" (the pass-rate problem); 20.3% said
"the old bot ended whole-game turns holding unspent CAs in about
a fifth of the cases that mattered" (the EndTurn-scoring bug,
fixed). Neither number implies the other's denominator, and after
the fix the CA-waste side of the ledger is near zero while the
pass-rate side (1.06) is the live defect. They do not conflict.
