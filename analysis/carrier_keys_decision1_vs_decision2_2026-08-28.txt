Carrier keys: decision 1 vs decision 2 at 2p round 1
Date: 2026-08-28
Repo: /Users/pt/tta-ai (read-only code read, no build/run)

Scenario: 2-player game, round 1.
  Decision 1 (D1): the FIRST civil decision of the turn -- hand_civil EMPTY,
    ca = 1 (seat 1) or 2 (seat 2). All legal candidates are Takes (slots
    0..12) plus EndTurn.
  Decision 2 (D2): the SECOND own-turn decision of the same turn, reached
    only when ca >= 2 (seat 2): one take already played, so hand has 1
    card, ca = 0. Candidates: EndTurn (plus Take if the one taken card
    left a legal second take -- e.g. a different 1-cost slot still spare,
    which the take gate then empties once ca hits 0, so in practice D2's
    candidate set is EndTurn alone, or EndTurn plus takes when a 0-cost
    path such as a free leader exists -- the take gate costs CA, and at
    ca=0 only zero-cost candidates survive, of which a take never is).
    Either way D2 is the "refusal" decision: the bot chooses between
    EndTurn and (at most) a second take.

Setup facts (established in analysis/row_keys_r1_reachability_2026-08-28.txt
and analysis/science_keys_r1_reachability_2026-08-28.txt):
  - round = 1, phase Actions; the 13-slot row is dealt IN FULL from the
    20-card Age A deck before D1 (game.rs:426-434).
  - Both players start with Philosophy staffed: state_stats science = 1
    each; strength 0 each (no units); no governments, no pacts, no events,
    no tactics, no wonders.
  - A Take never advances the round, never changes production/strength,
    never changes the rival's board. It changes ONLY: the row (card
    removed, deck card slid in at the same or a later slot), hand_civil
    (+1 at D2), civil_actions (-1), ca_spent_taking (+cost), and the deck
    counts.

=====================================================================
1. row_urgency  (row::row_pressure, row.rs:312; consumed eval.rs:372-377)
=====================================================================
  D1: NON-ZERO (structurally reachable; typical magnitude 0..few).
    Six of the 13 freshly dealt cards (2p: slide = 2*sweep_count(2) = 6,
    row.rs:328, positional not round-gated) sit in the slide zone from the
    very first decision. Every visible, takeable card at index i < slide
    with card_potential > 0 contributes its card_potential (row.rs:360-361).
    The Age A row is full of developable cards with positive potential, so
    urgency is generically positive at D1.
    ACROSS D1 CANDIDATES: NOT constant. Each Take candidate removes a
    different row card (and slides a new deck card in), so the row the
    trial state sees differs per candidate; card_potential itself is
    w-dependent but evaluated on a DIFFERENT row per candidate. The term
    therefore does carry margin.
  D2: NON-ZERO, same structure on the post-take row (one slot slid).
  VERDICT: margin-active at BOTH D1 and D2; not zero at either.

=====================================================================
2. row_bargain_forgone  (row::row_pressure, row.rs:312; consumed
    eval.rs:373,380)
=====================================================================
  D1: REACHABLE, generically non-zero: cards at index i >= slide with
    positive cost-saving (costs::row_cost(i) - costs::row_cost(nxt) > 0,
    row.rs:383-384) and survive > 0 contribute saving*survive
    (row.rs:420). Whether any such card sits in slots 6..12 of a given
    opening row is a numeric fact about the deal; the code path is
    unconditionally reachable.
    ACROSS D1 CANDIDATES: not constant (row differs per candidate).
  D2: reachable, same.
  VERDICT: margin-active at both decisions in principle; zero only on
    deals where no slide-zone-far card has a positive saving.

=====================================================================
3. row_last_copy  (row::row_last_copy, row.rs:475; consumed
    eval.rs:385-387)
=====================================================================
  D1: REACHABLE, generically non-zero: no positional gate; for every
    visible, takeable, wanted card, gone = 1 - civil_outlook(name) > 0
    contributes (row.rs:507-509). On a fresh 20-card Age A deck many
    common cards have outlook 0 or 1, so gone > 0 is the norm.
    ACROSS D1 CANDIDATES: not constant -- a Take removes a copy from the
    row AND the deck, so the outlook (and hence gone) for the taken name
    changes in its own trial state relative to other candidates.
  D2: reachable, same, on the post-take row/deck.
  VERDICT: margin-active at both decisions; not zero at either (except
    the degenerate all-outlook>=1 deal).

4. science_rate_trailing  (NO f.set; consumed rivals.rs:977 inside
    feature_marginal, gated STANDING_KEYS, rivals.rs:976)
=====================================================================
  D1: STRUCTURALLY ZERO. trailing_fraction (rivals.rs:873) reads
    state_stats science: mine = 1, best = 1 (both seats, Philosophy x1);
    `mine >= best` -> 0.0. A Take does not change either player's
    production, so this holds for EVERY D1 candidate.
  D2: STILL STRUCTURALLY ZERO: same numbers, nothing legal in round 1
    changed production.
  VERDICT: ZERO at both D1 and D2 in a 2p round-1 game. Its 60.0 value is
    the CLAMP_BLIND rail (p95 spread unmeasured, weights.rs:2432/2499),
    not a fitted value; it cannot contribute to the round-1 margin at all,
    at either decision.

5. science_gap  (features.rs:1180)
=====================================================================
  D1: ZERO. needs.science = min printed science_cost over LEVELLED cards
    in hand (marginal_needs, features.rs:320-325); hand is EMPTY at D1 ->
    0. science_have = stock 0 (+pending gain, 0) -> gap 0. Constant
    across D1 candidates (no D1 candidate changes hand or science stock).
  D2: NON-ZERO IFF the taken card is levelled (Age A urban/production/unit/
    special tech are all takeable): needs = that card's cost (1-5), have =
    0 -> gap = cost > 0. If the taken card is not levelled (farm/mine/
    leader/action/government/wonder) the gap stays 0.
    ACROSS D2 CANDIDATES: constant (the hand is fixed by the first take;
    the remaining candidates do not change it).
  VERDICT: ZERO at D1 (constant); CONDITIONALLY NON-ZERO at D2 (then
    constant across D2 candidates). With the champion's negative weight
    (-1.199 preclimb, -5.815 postclimb), a developable card in hand makes
    the state score MORE negative relative to the no-card baseline -- a
    direct linear path into the refusal at D2.

  D2: ZERO. have (stock 0 + pending 0) can never exceed a need that is

  VERDICT: STRUCTURALLY ZERO at both D1 and D2 of a 2p round-1 game.

7. science_rate  (features.rs:688)
=====================================================================
  D1: NON-ZERO but CONSTANT: s.science = 1 for the decider at every D1

  D2: NON-ZERO but CONSTANT: still 1.0; the one take changed nothing the

  VERDICT: NON-ZERO at both, but margin-INERT at both: identical across
    all candidates of each decision, so it cancels out of the
    Take-vs-EndTurn margin.

10. card_board_credit  (NO f.set; weight-only, consumed inside
    cards::card_potential_core, cards.rs:2029-2036, as `base`)
=====================================================================
  This is not a linear feature: it gates board-aware card pricing inside
  card_potential, which is what row_urgency/row_bargain/row_last_copy
  themselves call to value each takeable row card, and what the take
  candidate's trial evaluate reaches through HandPotential/row terms. Its
  VALUE as a weight is non-zero in both champions (0.170/0.163), and the
  pricing it gates IS active on the round-1 row (the row is full of
  developable cards whose potentials are priced through this gate).
  D1: ACTIVE (not zero, not constant across candidates -- it scales the
    per-candidate row pricing).
  D2: ACTIVE on the post-take row, same.
  VERDICT: not zero at either decision; it is a multiplier on card
    valuation, so its effect on the margin is per-candidate and
    deal-dependent, not a single emitted feature value.

11. card_board_leader  (NO f.set; weight-only; cards.rs:600 maps Leader ->
    CardBoardLeader for board_credit_key)
=====================================================================
  D1: ACTIVE IFF a leader is in the 13-slot opening row: for that leader
    specifically, credit_board = card_board_credit + card_board_leader
    (cards.rs:2034-2036) scales its board-aware price. On deals with no
    leader in the row, this key multiplies nothing.
  D2: same, on the post-take row.
  VERDICT: deal-dependent at both decisions; structurally reachable, not
  guaranteed non-zero. The champion keeps card_board_credit and
  card_board_leader at equal magnitude and opposite sign (preclimb
  0.1698/-0.1502; postclimb exactly 0.1634/-0.1634), so a leader's
  credit_board collapses to ~0 -- the two largely cancel for leaders.

12. card_rate_credit  (NO f.set; weight-only; cards.rs:2029, 2312 --

  D1: ACTIVE: it scales the yield sum of every priced card (the
    non-board-aware fallback, cards.rs:2038-2040: sum_yields(scratch, w,

  D2: ACTIVE, same.
  VERDICT: not zero at either decision; multiplier on per-card yields,
    active across the whole row from the first decision.

13. ca_left  (features.rs:900: p.civil_actions, the pool REMAINING)
=====================================================================
  D1: NON-ZERO and CONSTANT across D1 candidates: ca = 1 or 2 for every
    candidate (the trial state is post-apply, so a Take candidate's trial
    has ca-1 and EndTurn's trial has ca -- see note below).
    NOTE: rank_moves evaluates each candidate in its POST-APPLY trial
    state (eval.rs:548-554), so across D1 candidates the feature takes
    the values ca-1 (every Take) and ca (EndTurn). That is exactly the
    signal the weight is supposed to read: postclimb's -1.049 makes the
    ca=0 trial (EndTurn from ca=1, and every Take from ca=2) score LOWER
    than the ca>0 trial. For seat 1 (ca=1): every Take lands at ca=0 and
    EndTurn at ca=1 -> the term alone pushes Take below EndTurn by
    1.049*1.0 = 1.049 in postclimb. For seat 2 (ca=2): Takes land at
    ca=1, EndTurn at ca=2 -> push 1.049 as well.
  D2: ca = 0 for EVERY D2 candidate (the take already spent it; EndTurn
    leaves 0, a hypothetical second 0-cost candidate also 0) -> value 0.0,
    CONSTANT across D2 candidates: no margin contribution at D2.
  VERDICT: D1 NON-ZERO and margin-ACTIVE (constant 1 apart between the
    Take group and EndTurn); D2 ZERO and constant.

=====================================================================
14. civil_action_gap  (features.rs:1201: max(hand_civil_count - ca_left, 0))
=====================================================================
  D1: hand_civil = 0, so gap = max(0 - ca, 0) = 0 for EVERY D1 candidate,
    whether measured pre-apply or post-apply (post-apply: a Take trial has
    hand 1, ca-1 -> max(1-(ca-1),0): for ca=1 that is max(1-0,0)=1, for
    ca=2 that is max(1-1,0)=0; EndTurn trial: hand 0, ca -> 0). So on the
    POST-APPLY trials (the ones actually scored):
      seat 1 (ca=1): Take trials read gap = 1, EndTurn reads 0 -> NON-ZERO,
        margin-active: the negative weight (-0.203/-0.879) punishes the
        Take trials, reinforcing the refusal.
      seat 2 (ca=2): Take trials read gap = 0, EndTurn 0 -> zero, constant.
  D2: hand 1, ca 0 -> gap = 1 for EVERY D2 candidate (post-apply of
    EndTurn leaves hand 1, ca 0) -> NON-ZERO but CONSTANT across D2
    candidates: no margin at D2 (it is a flat offset that cancels
    Take-vs-EndTurn within D2).
  VERDICT: seat-1 D1: margin-active (Take 1 vs EndTurn 0); seat-2 D1:
    zero; D2: non-zero (1.0) but constant across candidates.

=====================================================================
15. civil_action_surplus  (features.rs:1202: max(ca_left - hand_civil, 0))
=====================================================================
  Post-apply trials:
  D1:
      seat 1 (ca=1): Take trials: ca 0, hand 1 -> max(0-1,0)=0. EndTurn:
        ca 1, hand 0 -> 1. NON-ZERO, margin-active (positive weight
        +0.073/+0.550 REWARDS EndTurn relative to Take at seat 1).
      seat 2 (ca=2): Take trials: ca 1, hand 1 -> 0. EndTurn: ca 2, hand 0
        -> 2. NON-ZERO, margin-active (rewards EndTurn by 2 units of
        surplus at seat 2, even stronger).
  D2: every candidate: ca 0, hand 1 -> 0. CONSTANT (zero) at D2.
  VERDICT: D1 NON-ZERO and margin-active at BOTH seats (favors EndTurn);
    D2 zero.

  Keys that are structurally zero at D1 AND D2 in this scenario:
  science_rate_trailing, science_surplus, strength_deficit,
  rival_strength.
  Keys that are non-zero but constant (margin-inert) at both:
  science_rate, civil_actions.
  Keys that become margin-active only at D1 (via post-apply trial
  differences): ca_left, civil_action_gap (seat 1), civil_action_surplus.
  Keys margin-active at both D1 and D2: row_urgency, row_bargain_forgone,
  row_last_copy, card_board_credit, card_rate_credit, (card_board_leader
  if a leader is in the row).
  Key margin-active only at D2: science_gap (when the taken card is
  levelled).

  Reading for the round-1 refusal: at D1 the CA-family (ca_left,
  civil_action_gap at seat 1, civil_action_surplus at both seats) plus the
  row terms and the card-credit multipliers are the live carriers; the
  science family contributes nothing at D1 except science_rate's cancelling
  constant. At D2 the only science-family carrier is science_gap, and the
  CA-family goes flat (constant 1.0 gap / 0.0 surplus / 0.0 ca_left), so
  D2's margin is carried by the row terms, the card credits, and
  science_gap.
