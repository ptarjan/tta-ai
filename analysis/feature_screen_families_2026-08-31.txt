--- FAMILY A: CARD GRANULARITY (99 columns, prefix gran_ / ctrl_a_) --------

The claim under test: the evaluator carries one scalar per card TYPE. That is
literally what features.rs does. Every card in play is collapsed to a per-type
LEVEL (BestFarm..BestArena, BestUnit, TechLevels, GovLevel = the card's AGE) or
a per-type COUNT (NumTechs, SpecialTechs, Wonders, Leader as 0/1). So:

  * Monarchy and Theocracy are both GovLevel 1; Communism, Fundamentalism and
    Democracy are all GovLevel 3.
  * All twelve special techs are interchangeable: SpecialTechs += 1,
    TechLevels += age. Masonry reads identically to Warfare.
  * BestUnit is ONE max over Infantry/Cavalry/Artillery/Air, so Rockets,
    Tanks and Modern Infantry are the same number.
  * All 24 leaders are a single bit.
  * All 16 wonders are a count once completed.

Card identity reaches the score only through the eleven freeze-priced scalars
(HandPotential, WonderPotential, WonderPromise, ...), and even there as a
number, never as which card.

  (A1) government one-hot, 8 cols        (A6) in-progress wonder one-hot, 16
  (A2) government printed prices, 2      (A7) leader one-hot, 24
  (A3) special-tech one-hot, 12          (A8) obsolescence, structure,
  (A4) unit level BY TYPE, 4                  cost-to-benefit, 15
  (A5) completed wonder one-hot, 16      (A9) redundancy controls, 2

Printed data only (science_cost, resource_cost, peaceful_cost,
revolution_cost, stages, production), never costs::tech_cost, for the same
reason the hand screen gave: a discount-aware column would score for the wrong
reason.

--- FAMILY B: OPPONENT-RELATIVE / GAP-CONDITIONAL (52 cols, rel_ / ctrl_b_) -

phi's relative keys are of three kinds, and the gap in them is specific:

  * PLAIN SIGNED DIFFERENCES: StrengthRel, AttackTargetLead,
    AttackTargetWeakness, PactPartnerLead. Near no-ops in a linear eval.
  * ONE-SIDED HINGES WITH THE OTHER HALF MISSING: RivalCultureDeficit =
    max(0, their rate - mine) and RivalScienceDeficit exist. The matching
    LEADS -- max(0, mine - theirs) -- do not exist at all.
  * HINGES AGAINST A RULEBOOK THRESHOLD, not against the opponent:
    FoodGap/ScienceGap/WorkerGap/CivilActionGap and their Surplus mirrors
    hinge on marginal_needs, which is a rulebook quantity. Only
    CultureGap/CultureSurplus is opponent-relative, and it is on the culture
    STOCK, never the rate.

And NO gap key is scaled by the horizon: horizon::RATE_KEYS is
[CultureRate, ScienceRate, FoodRate, ResourceRate] and the rival rates are
deliberately excluded from it, so no "gap x how late it is" term exists.

  (B1) hinged halves, 12 cols            (B5) gap-conditional card value, 3
  (B2) sign x magnitude buckets, 15      (B6) tempo, hinged, 4
  (B3) gap x lateness / round, 10        (B7) redundancy controls, 2
  (B4) end-of-game projection, shares, 6

LEGALITY. Family B reads public rival state and only public rival state:
rating-track culture and science, the face-up tableau / government /
completed-wonder pile (and the rates effects::compute derives from them),
civil hand SIZE, and military hand SIZE. A rival's military hand CONTENTS are
face down and are never read. That is asserted, not promised:
feature_screen::tests::rival_military_hand_contents_are_not_read replaces
every card in the rival's military hand at unchanged hand size over a whole
self-play game and requires every one of the 185 columns to be unmoved.

No column in either family reads deck order, a rival's civil hand identities,
or the label.

6. WHAT THE "spanned" COLUMN SAYS THIS TIME
====================================================================

In the hand run, spanned-ness explained the zeros: five of eight positive
control probes recovered nothing because the other 173 columns rebuilt them.
That reading DOES NOT transfer to family B, and the numbers say so plainly.

  rel_x_culrate_gap_round   spanned 0.9524   gain +0.007580
  rel_bkt_culrate_m1        spanned 0.1875   gain +0.000883

The heavily spanned column gains 8.6x what the barely spanned one does. Linear
reconstructibility does not predict gain here, because the top of family B is
NOT a new fact -- phi already contains CultureRate and RivalCultureRate, so
their difference is 95% linearly recoverable. What is new is the SHAPE: a
product with the round counter, or a hinge, is not in the span of the linear
columns no matter how well the underlying quantity is.

Two internal checks confirm the screen is measuring the missing shape and not
a missing fact. Where phi ALREADY carries a hinge, the duplicate scores ~0:

  rel_culture_lead      +/- CultureSurplus       -0.000003   spanned 0.9999
  rel_culture_deficit   +/- CultureGap           -0.000002   spanned 0.9999

====================================================================
8. LIMITS -- READ BEFORE RANKING ANYTHING
====================================================================

1. It measures UNIQUE PREDICTIVE signal at one frozen weight vector, not
   decision value.

2. It predicts the FINAL MARGIN from a position, and rel_proj_final_culture_gap
   is by construction an estimate of that margin (current culture gap plus
   rate gap times horizon::rounds_left). Every input is current public state
   and there is no label leakage -- but a column shaped like the label should
   be expected to predict the label, and that is not the same claim as "a bot
   given it would choose better". This applies to ranks 1-13 as a group.

3. The rows come from the champion playing itself, at 2 players. Six of the
   sixteen wonders are never begun, so twelve granularity columns are
   unmeasurable here, and the family-A total is a lower bound on what a
   granularity family could score against a policy with wider coverage.

4. Family A's leader and wonder one-hots are sparse. The 24 leader columns
   are non-zero in 377 (Leonardo da Vinci) to 2916 (Joan of Arc) of the 3000
   games; the wonder one-hots range from 0 to 2842. Their block totals are
   the honest figure; individual sparse rows in the table are noisier than
   dense ones, which is what the "games" and "folds" columns are there to
   show.

5. phi depends on w. Every number is priced at gen 133131.

6. 2p only. There are no pacts at 2p, PactPartnerLead and AttackTargetLead are
   structurally zero, and the "best rival" max in family B is a max over one
   opponent. At 3p/4p every relative column would have to be re-measured.
