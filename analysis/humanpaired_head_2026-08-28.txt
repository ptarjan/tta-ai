humanpaired re-run at HEAD (post-f13707f), 2026-08-28
=====================================================

WHY THIS RUN EXISTS
-------------------
`humanpaired` is one of the four journal-driven binaries that scored candidate moves
through `rank_moves`, which applied `Move::EndTurn` before scoring it until `f13707f`.
Like `agreement` and `whydiverge`, it takes its positions from the human journal, so it
saw the RIGHT positions and put WRONG numbers on them. The error is bounded and a re-run
erases it. Every humanpaired figure taken before `f13707f` is superseded by this one.

SCOPE IS NOT THE SAME AS agreement / whydiverge
-----------------------------------------------
Those two ran on `head -200 analysis/guard_ids_882.txt` (200 games). This ran on whatever
`humanpaired` itself selects as held-out at tier >= Prince: 210 games, all 210 with a
journal processed, 67688 paired decisions, 101 skipped for human_move-not-in-legal_moves.
Do NOT place the three runs side by side as if they were the same sample.

READING IT
----------
Both sides are scored on IDENTICAL decisions, so the comparison is paired and the gap is
real: a weight vector fitted to imitate humans reproduces the human's move about 39% of
the time; the climbed champion does so about 24%.

Two things are worth noting and neither is an error to fix.

1. The champion's 24.1% here lands on top of the independent `agreement` run's 24.11%
   (different binary, different 200-game sample). Two instruments agreeing to two decimal
   places on separate samples is a sanity check that both re-runs are sound.

2. Age A INVERTS: the champion agrees with the human MORE than the human-fit weights do
   (58.5% vs 40.5%), and it is the only age where that happens. n=1306 is small and Age A
   is the most constrained part of the game -- few legal moves, so a high agreement rate
   is cheap -- but it is the one slice where the champion is not the worse imitator.

DO NOT FIT ON THESE NUMBERS. A higher agreement score is not a better bot. Fitting the
leaf eval or a gate on human agreement has lost three separate ways and is on the
do-not-do list; a divergence from a human is evidence to investigate, never an error to
minimise. In particular, the 39.1% vs 24.1% gap is NOT a reason to move the champion
toward human_weights.json -- HumanBot has never beaten the champion at playing the game.
