2p LEADER BOARD-PRICING SEED -- 2026-08-28, coordinator
(supersedes this file's first version, which claimed a three-arm
value-preserving migration; that framing was wrong and is corrected below)

WHY. `83d1c2e` made `CardBoardLeader` the ABSOLUTE multiplier on a leader's
board-swap diff. Before it, `cards.rs::card_potential_core` composed the scale
as `CardBoardCredit + CardBoardLeader`; after it, `credit_board =
board_credit_key(id).map_or(base, |k| w.get(k))` (`cards.rs:2044`) reads
`CardBoardLeader` ALONE for a Leader, and `CardBoardCredit` no longer reaches
leaders at all. The same commit gave the key `SignIntent::NonNegative`
(`weights.rs:1503`); default is 0.0 (`:1030`).

WHAT THE 2p HISTORY ACTUALLY SHOWS. The climb jsonl does not record per-key
mutations, but it does carry a `runaway` array, and `card_board_leader` appears
in it 42 times across gens 41342-42132 at values of -60.000 / -59.944 /
-59.043 -- i.e. pinned at its LOWER clamp bound. Under the old composite
semantics a large negative was how the climb cancelled `CardBoardCredit`, and
`dominance_repair` then snapped it to exactly `-card_board_credit` on the wall
(the gen-120300 champion was bit-exact equal-and-opposite at +/-0.1965856875).
The live gen-122950 file reads exactly 0.0 -- which is what the NEW NonNegative
gate does to a negative on the first load after the 04:59 rebuild.

So at 2p the key has been NEGATIVE for its whole recorded history and is now
exactly 0.0 by a sign snap. It has never held a learned positive value, and
`credit_board == 0.0` does not merely scale the channel down, it switches the
swap branch off (`cards.rs:2041`) and falls through to the static
`card_yields` path -- five of six leaders priced at exactly 0, ranking
destroyed. The decouple made this a free upward direction instead of a wall,
but 1826 gens produced no movement off zero.

WHAT WAS DONE. Arms stopped via the sentinel bracket; the 2p champion's
`card_board_leader` set to 0.357041 (its `card_board_credit`, the only
board-credit scale this champion has actually fitted) purely to break the zero
and give the climb a live coordinate to work from; arms restarted.
`card_board_credit` was NOT changed -- it still governs every card type with no
per-type key, through the `map_or(base, ..)` arm.

THIS IS A SEED, NOT A VALUE-PRESERVING MIGRATION. The migration designed for
this decouple, `cbl := max(0, cbc + cbl)`, is only value-preserving if applied
at the moment the code goes live. It was applied 13.5 hours late, after both
keys had drifted under the new semantics, so `card_board_credit`'s current
value no longer says anything about leaders. 0.357041 is a starting point, not
a reconstruction.

3p AND 4p WERE LEFT ALONE. Both already hold positive values (0.119401 and
0.030284), so their channel is ON and there is no zero-channel defect to fix.
They were migrated in the first pass and REVERTED in the second: folding a
drifted `card_board_credit` into an already-positive value would have nearly
tripled 3p on no evidence. Whether their positives are pre-rebuild offsets that
lost their `card_board_credit` partner, or post-rebuild learned values, cannot
be told from a snapshot and the logs cannot answer it either.

Pre-change copies of all three: `analysis/frozen/premigration_leaderboard_2026-08-28/`.

WHAT THE LEADER-DRIFT TEST DOES NOT SHOW. 2p `leader` fell from +14.0440
(frozen gen 121100) to +5.4946 (gen 122926). That is NOT the predicted hand-off
from the flat 0/1 leader flag to the board channel: the board channel read 0.0
for the whole window, so nothing took over. A frozen-vs-now snapshot 1826 gens
apart cannot separate a coupling from an unrelated drift, and no coupling story
survives when the other side of it is zero. The prediction is UNTESTED.

HOW TO JUDGE THE SEED. Compare the 2p champion against
`analysis/frozen/premigration_leaderboard_2026-08-28/rust_champion_2p.json`
after it has climbed. If `card_board_leader` is driven back toward 0 the seed
was wrong and the channel is genuinely unwanted at 2p; if it holds or grows,
the zero was a sign-snap artifact and not a preference.

ADDENDUM 2026-08-28 late: THE 3p/4p REVERT IS CONFIRMED CORRECT
================================================================================

The note above said a snapshot could not tell whether 3p's +0.119401 and 4p's
+0.030284 were stranded pre-rebuild offsets or post-rebuild learned values, and
that the climb logs could not answer it either (they record mutations BY GROUP
ONLY).  Two facts closed it.

1. #local-model repeated the runaway-array trace on the 3p and 4p climb logs.
   card_board_leader appears ZERO times in either.  The arrays are otherwise
   active (3174 non-empty records at 3p, 1603 at 4p), so the absence is real.
   Only 2p ever pinned this key to a clamp wall.

2. A frozen champion set from BEFORE the 04:59 rebuild answers it directly.
   analysis/frozen/opener_2026-08-28/ was taken Aug 27 19:10, i.e. under the OLD
   composite semantics:

     arm   pre-rebuild (Aug 27 19:10)      post-rebuild (Aug 28 18:32)
     2p    gen 119419  cbl -0.161409       gen 122950  cbl  0.0
     3p    gen  66647  cbl -0.014981       gen  69000  cbl +0.119401
     4p    gen  21726  cbl -0.115108       gen  22147  cbl +0.030284

   (analysis/frozen/clamp_ceiling_2026-08-27/, Aug 27 07:13, agrees: 2p
   -0.239828, 3p -0.053626, 4p -0.111971.)

ALL THREE ARMS WERE NEGATIVE BEFORE THE REBUILD.  The NonNegative gate therefore
snapped ALL THREE to 0.0 on first load -- the regression was three-arm, not
2p-only, and the earlier framing of 3p/4p as "channel already ON" was itself
wrong about the mechanism even though the revert it justified was right.

3p then climbed off zero to +0.119401 within 2353 gens and 4p to +0.030284
within 421 gens, unaided.  Those are post-rebuild learned values.  Overwriting
them with max(0, cbc + cbl) would have destroyed a live answer to the exact
question the 2p seed was written to ask.

WHAT THIS DOES TO THE SEED.  3p and 4p are now CONTROLS: they show what "the
channel is wanted" looks like when the climb starts from the same snapped zero.
It cuts both ways -- 2p sat at exactly 0.0 for 3531 gens without moving, while
the other two moved in a few hundred to a couple of thousand.  That is weak
evidence 2p does not want it.  The seed stays, the judging criterion is
unchanged, and the 2p result is not a foregone conclusion.

SEPARATELY, AND STILL OPEN.  2p's flat `leader` weight runs 25.9028 (g116808) ->
25.7481 (g119419) -> 14.0440 (g121100) -> 5.4946 (g122950).  Flat before the
rebuild, a 79% collapse after it, with the board channel reading 0.0 throughout.
So the collapse is real and post-rebuild but it is NOT the predicted hand-off --
nothing took over.  3p (0.319 -> 0.434) and 4p (-12.34 -> -10.45) show nothing
like it.  Do not cite this as confirmation of anything; it is a 2p-only open
question.

   Indistinguishable.  Exact 0.0 is a revolving door the climb enters and leaves

CONCLUSION: the sweep is a clean NEGATIVE.  card_board_leader remains the only
key the rebuild changed, and what marked it out was never the exactness of its
zero -- it was CROSS-ARM SIMULTANEITY, the same key crossing from negative on
all three arms in the window its NonNegative intent landed.  Three independent
climbs do not zero the same key at once by chance.

METHOD NOTE, reusable: "exactly 0.0" is NOT a fingerprint of a sign gate.  Run
the no-landing control window before attributing a batch of fresh zeros to a
code change.
