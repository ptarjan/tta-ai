VERDICT: NOT CITABLE as NonNegative.

NonNegative would assert "more relative strength is never worse" for
the early-game coefficient of a SIGNED difference. The claim fails,
and the failure is structural:

  (1) DECISIVE -- the feature is a signed difference and the gate
      constrains the WEIGHT. rel = strength - rival_str
      (features.rs:679) is signed and unclamped; sign_intent can
      only pin the coefficient, so a NonNegative gate asserts a
      monotone reading for a quantity that has none. This is the
      same reason the "lead/weakness measures are signed
      differences by construction" arm (weights.rs:1899-1903,
      MySeededPending/MyEventThreat/AttackTargetLead/
      AttackTargetWeakness/PactPartnerLead) keeps that whole
      family Free, and the same reason the StrengthRel family as a
      whole sits in no gate. A gate on a phase coefficient of a
      signed difference would freeze the sign of a quantity the
      rules do not make one-directional -- the bucket's own
      NonNegative citations (weights.rs:1563/1573) price
      magnitudes that are one-directional by rule, not signed
      differences.
  (2) No rule citation for the FOR side either. RULES_SPEC 5.3's
      "strongest/weakest" events key off MY OWN position, so being
      the strongest player can trigger a penalty aimed at the
      strongest. Hunted for it: the strongest-side effects in the
      base game are net POSITIVE (Foray, Uncertain Borders, Border
      Conflict, Crusades, Cold War, Refugees, International
      Agreement, Politics of Strength, Genghis Khan) -- so no
      counterexample in data/ or the spec. That kills only the FOR
      case: NonNegative is a monotonicity assertion, and "the
      rules never subtract for it" is not a citation.
  (3) CORROBORATION, not proof -- the measured coefficients: 2p
      -13.322 and 3p -20.557 (authored default -0.1), 4p +15.744
      (champions re-read by the coordinator at gen 118728 / 66331 /
      21649, agreeing with my reads). A NonNegative gate forbids
      negative weights, so the 2p/3p arms violate it. CAUTION per
      the coordinator (2026-08-27): this argument alone proves too
      much -- a Free key lands where it lands, and "the gate would
      forbid the measured value" would close every sign_intent
      question ever asked. It is load-bearing here only because
      limb (1) stands independently: the signed-difference
      structure is the proof, the 2p/3p numbers are corroboration
      that the structure bites in practice.


WHAT THE COORDINATE HOLDS
----------------------------------------------------------------
  features.rs:656-679
      rival_str = max over rivals of (base strength + offered-pact
      strength gain);  strength = my strength + my pending gains;
      rel = strength - rival_str
  features.rs:931-934
      Strength       = strength          (my absolute)
      StrengthRel    = rel               (SIGNED, NO CLAMP)
      StrengthDeficit= (-rel).max(0.0)   (the "behind" half, clamped)
      StrengthLead   = rel.clamp(0.0, STRENGTH_LEAD_CAP)
                     (the "ahead" half, clamped and capped)
  registry.rs:399  (WeightKey::StrengthRelEarly, "phase blend off
                   StrengthRel")
  weights.rs:1115  authored default -0.1 (string "strength_rel_early")
  weights.rs:1214  WeightKey::StrengthRel => WeightKey::StrengthRelEarly
                   -- the ONLY key with a phase pair post PHASECUT's
                   T1-A/C/D collapse (weights.rs:1205-1216 panics on
                   every other key)

So: `rel` is an unclamped signed difference; StrengthDeficit carries
the negative half (hinge, NonPositive at weights.rs:1618 "prices a
penalty the rules impose"); StrengthLead carries the positive half
(capped). StrengthRelEarly is a PHASE COEFFICIENT on the signed
value, not a new quantity.

THE PHASE BLEND (eval.rs:215-276)
----------------------------------------------------------------
eff(L) = (1-L)*strength_rel_early + L*(strength_rel + strength_rel_late)
GATED on horizon::combat_unreachable -- TRUE while round <
EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND (= FIRST_DRAW_ROUND + 1 = 3, horizon.rs:358-360)
OR no player holds a military card. So:

  * while combat is unreachable: the WHOLE coefficient IS
    strength_rel_early; strength_rel and strength_rel_late
    contribute nothing (L * ... is multiplied into the blend but
    the gate uses early_full/late_full exactly as written at
    eval.rs:268-271: total += early*early_full*v + late*late_full*v
    -- and EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND = 3, so in the literal opening
    lateness is small and early ~ 1).
  * once combat is reachable: strength_rel prices at FULL BASE
    exactly as pre-fix (eval.rs:273), plus the phase nudge on top.

Consequence: in the opening, strength_rel_early is the ONLY
coefficient on rel. If it is NonNegative, then in the opening, a
positive relative-strength difference (I am stronger than my
strongest rival) can NEVER be priced as a benefit -- and a negative
one is priced as a benefit (a NonNegative weight on a negative
value is a negative contribution, i.e. the evaluator REWARDS being
weaker in the opening). The gate does not just forbid "early
strength is bad"; it forces "early strength is good or neutral, and
early weakness is good or neutral."

That is the opposite of what the game's own structure says about
the opening, and the measured champions agree with the structure:
the authored default is NEGATIVE (-0.1), and the 2p/3p champions
price it large NEGATIVE.

THE RULES (the FOR side of the case -- and why it is thin)
----------------------------------------------------------------
RULES_SPEC 5.3 [RB p.16, CoL p.7]: "Statistic comparisons
('strongest/weakest' = strength rating [FAQ p.11]): ties broken in
favor of the current player, then proximity in clockwise order after
the current player... 2p: 'two strongest/weakest' reads as 'the
stronger/weaker'."

So the ranking is MY OWN position -- being the strongest player
does not just help me; it can put me in the target seat of a card
that says "the strongest player [does X]." The coordinator's
hypothesis: find one event or aggression where topping the strength
table is a liability, and that is the whole answer.

Hunt result (data/cards_military_actions.json + RULES_SPEC +
data/cards_civil.json, full text read 2026-08-27):

  strongest-side effects in the base military deck:
    Foray                strongest gains up to 3 food/resources
    Uncertain Borders    strongest takes a yellow token from the
                         weakest's bank
    Border Conflict      strongest gains 3 resources
    Crusades             strongest gains 4 culture
    Cold War             strongest gains 6 science
    Refugees             strongest gains 3 culture + 1 population
    International Agreement  strongest MAY spend up to 5 CAs
                         taking cards, GIVING UP the next Politics
                         Phase (RULES_SPEC item 14: "the strongest
                         player may use the option even in the last
                         round")
    Politics of Strength strongest draws 5 military cards (last
                         round: +5 culture instead)
  leader (data/cards_wonders_leaders.json):
    Genghis Khan         if among the two strongest, +3 culture at
                         turn end
  civil deck: NO strongest/weakest cards.

EVERY base-game effect that targets the strongest player is a GAIN
(or an optional gain). International Agreement is the only
strongest-side cost, and it is OPTIONAL ("may spend...") -- the
strongest player may simply not take it; a card you may decline is
not a penalty aimed at the strongest. Aggressions (RULES_SPEC 5.4-
5.5) key off the DEFENDER's strength (defender total >= attacker
strength => fail), not the attacker's rank; the target clauses are
per-card (Annex: "a rival with a colony"), none rank-based.

So the hunt comes up empty in the base game: there is NO event or
aggression in data/ or the spec where topping the strength table is
a liability. The strongest seat is, in the base game, a prize, not
a target. (Note for the record: expansion cards could change this
-- the data/ deck here is the base set -- but the shortlist's
citation standard is the spec + the deck the engine plays.)

What the FOR case can actually say:
  (a) the strongest seat is safe in the base game -- so "early
      strength is never worse" is not contradicted by any rules
      citation;
  (b) the engine ALREADY prices the strongest seat directly:
      events.rs:315 my_event_threat ranks TODAY's players
      (rank_players, events.rs:360-375) and credits/debits the
      seeded-event payload per seat, so a Barbarians I seeded that
      would hit me-as-strongest is priced NEGATIVE against me
      regardless of any sign gate on strength_rel_early.

Against that, the gate's assertion is the wrong shape: NonNegative
says "more is never WORSE," but the honest claim is "more is
sometimes BETTER AND sometimes costs nothing" -- the value of
rel > 0 in the opening depends on WHAT IS SEEDED, which my_event_
threat prices separately. A quantity whose sign of value depends on
another coordinate is not a quantity whose value is monotonically
non-decreasing. The bucket's own citations ("prices an
available/completed stock the rules never subtract for,"
weights.rs:1563; "counts row cards immediately usable; a bigger
count is never worse," weights.rs:1573) are claims about the
QUANTITY itself, not about a phase coefficient on a signed
difference.

THE EARLY-BLEND QUESTION (does the phase change the test?)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Yes, decisively -- the blend is the whole story.

  * In the opening (combat unreachable), strength_rel_early is the
    ONLY coefficient on rel (eval.rs:268-271, gate above). The
    gate would apply to the entire opening's relative-strength
    pricing.
  * Is there an age where leading on strength is priced badly? In
    the base game, NO -- every strongest-side effect is a gain, so
    no age subtracts for leading. But that is the FOR side's best
    case, and it is not the gate's shape: the gate asserts
    monotonicity of the VALUE, while the base game gives
    "gains if you lead, nothing if you don't" -- a step function
    with a flat bottom, which is non-decreasing (fine for
    NonNegative on the quantity) but the coefficient is a MULTI-
    PLIER on a signed value, and the multiplier's own sign is a
    free choice the gate would freeze.
  * The measured multipliers: 2p -13.32, 3p -20.56 (authored
    -0.1). The 2p/3p champions found that in the opening, a
    positive relative-strength lead is WORTH NEGATIVE -- i.e. being
    the strongest in a no-combat opening is priced as a small
    liability. The plausible mechanics, all in the code:
      - my_event_threat: being the strongest seat while a weakest-
        side event is seeded is fine, but while a strongest-side
        event is seeded the gain is mine to lose -- and the
        coordinate's negative price is the climb's way of keeping
        the opening from over-investing in strength when combat
        cannot happen yet (the EARLYMIL structural fix,
        eval.rs:215-254, was made precisely because the base
        strength term dominated the opening and bought military
        units before mines).
      - StrengthLead (rel.clamp(0, CAP)) already carries the
        "I am ahead" half as a separate, clamped, capped
        coordinate; StrengthRelEarly is the phase coefficient, not
        the lead.

DOES StrengthDeficit CARRY THE "BEHIND" HALF?
----------------------------------------------------------------
Yes, for the hinge: StrengthDeficit = (-rel).max(0.0)
(features.rs:933) is NonPositive at weights.rs:1618 ("prices a
penalty the rules impose") -- so the "behind" half has its own
gate, and the "ahead" half has StrengthLead. But StrengthRelEarly
is NOT "only ever the surplus half": it multiplies the WHOLE signed
rel, both halves, in the opening. A NonNegative gate on it would
constrain the SURPLUS half (fine, if it were a stock) and, worse,
would forbid the deficit half from being priced positive in the
opening -- which is exactly what a phase coefficient exists to do:
say how much a unit of relative strength is worth RIGHT NOW, and
the opening's answer, per the 2p/3p champions, is "not much, maybe
a little negative."

A NonNegative gate forbids negative weights (eval.rs:895-902,
non_negative_gates; enforced at load by dominance_repair and at
mutation time by climb.rs's under-mutation guard, eval.rs:860-888).
The evidence against it is the 2p and 3p arms: they price the key
NEGATIVE (-13.32, -20.56), and a NonNegative gate forbids that.
The gate would have rejected the 2p and 3p champions' own measured
finding. (The 4p +15.74 is legal under NonNegative -- it is not a
contradiction of the gate, only of the FOR case's "more is never
worse" intuition, which is not a gate's job.) And the sign FLIP
itself -- from the negative authored default through the 2p/3p
negative regime to 4p positive -- is the climb discovering that
the opening's answer depends on the number of players: with four
rivals the strongest seat is worth more in the opening, with two
rivals less. A gate freezes one answer for all arms.

Also note the collinearity warning from the auction_bid erratum
applies with extra force here: StrengthRel, StrengthDeficit,
StrengthLead and the two phase coefficients are five terms on the
same underlying difference rel (and its two hinges) -- exactly the
shape where one term's sign can be an artifact of the others
splitting the weight. The 4p +15.74 vs 2p -13.32 spread is the
climb re-balancing a family of collinear terms across player
counts, not a clean reading of "early strength is good at 4p."
But none of that rescues the gate: a gate must hold in EVERY arm,
and the 2p/3p arms already violate it.

BOTTOM LINE
----------------------------------------------------------------
DO NOT give StrengthRelEarly a NonNegative sign_intent.

  1. DECISIVE -- the structural limb: rel is a signed, unclamped
     difference (features.rs:679) and sign_intent constrains the
     WEIGHT, so a signed feature has no monotone reading for the
     gate to pin. The signed-difference family is already Free on
     exactly this ground (weights.rs:1899-1903); NonNegative's own
     citations (weights.rs:1563/1573) price one-directional
     magnitudes, not signed differences.
  2. No rules citation for the FOR side: the base game's
     strongest-side effects are all gains (International
     Agreement's cost is optional), so "the rules never subtract
     for leading" is not a monotonicity citation.
  3. CORROBORATION (per the coordinator: corroboration, not
     proof) -- the gate would also forbid the 2p (-13.322) and 3p
     (-20.557) champions' measured negative opening coefficient,
     the EARLYMIL finding that the opening should not over-invest
     in strength while combat is unreachable. The "gate would
     forbid the measured value" argument alone proves too much
     (a Free key lands where it lands); it bites here only
     because limb 1 stands independently.
  4. StrengthDeficit (the behind half) and StrengthLead (the ahead
     half) already carry the hinge structure with their own
     (correct) gates; StrengthRelEarly stays Free, as the
     structural fix (eval.rs:215-254) and the 2026-08-13 EARLYMIL
     work intended.

The authored default -0.1 is the right shape: small, negative
(early lead is not worth much when nothing can be fought), Free
(each player count may price it differently -- and the 4p arm has
already done exactly that).

This closes the sign_intent shortlist: no further keys remain.
