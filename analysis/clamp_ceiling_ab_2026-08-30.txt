CLAMP_CEILING 60 -> 120: the A/B verdict
========================================
2026-08-30. Decision: **KEEP 120. No revert.**

BACKGROUND
==========
On 2026-08-27 `CLAMP_CEILING` was split from `CLAMP_BLIND` and raised 60 -> 120
(`06eb563`/`f942741`) as a deliberate experiment. The A side was frozen at
`analysis/frozen/clamp_ceiling_2026-08-27/` (2p gen 116808, 3p gen 65450,
4p gen 21445). The rule set in advance: if the climbed champions do not beat
the frozen A side, revert to 60.0 -- do not split the difference.

PRECONDITION: WAS THE CONSTRAINT BINDING?
=========================================
The naive test ("has any weight gone above 60?") was run on 2026-08-27 at 08:20
and answered NO at all three counts while the experiment was in fact live. It
would have produced the wrong verdict, because a freshly raised wall takes time
to be reached and most keys never approach it.

The correct test is per-key and starts from the five keys that sat at EXACTLY
60.0000 the morning the ceiling was raised. Four of them had a measured bound
(CLAMP_T=1.0, bound = P95_TOTAL_SPREAD[i]/spread, capped at CLAMP_CEILING)
that now exceeds 60, so only those four were genuinely freed:

  key                        count  measured bound   value on 2026-08-30
  strength                    3p     96.76            51.32
  attack_cost_doubled         3p    120.00            81.77
  workers_late                4p    120.00            32.92
  special_techs               4p    120.00            38.73

  best_theater                4p     -- EXCLUDED: zero spread row, so it falls
                                     to CLAMP_BLIND=60 and can never rise.

**All four moved off exactly 60.0000, and one went through the old wall.**
`attack_cost_doubled@3p` at 81.77 is a value that is unreachable at
CLAMP_CEILING=60, so the raise was load-bearing and not merely permissive.
The other three fell AWAY from the wall, which is the alive-key signature from
`tta-ceiling-move-is-a-reachability-test`: a key pinned by a wall it does not
care about follows the wall up, a key that was genuinely constrained comes off
it. Zero of the four followed the wall. The precondition is met and the A/B
measures something real.

RESULT
======
`kindmatch`, LIVE champion against the frozen A champion of the same count,
240 games each, run one at a time. Live champions were copied to /tmp first and
their gen recorded, because the climb rewrites `experiments/rust_champion_Np.json`
every few minutes.

| players | live win% | null   | +/-  | games | live gen | margin over null |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2p | 59.58% | 50.00% | 6.09 | 240 | 128637 | +9.58  |
| 3p | 48.96% | 33.33% | 6.37 | 240 |  72936 | +15.63 |
| 4p | 34.79% | 25.00% | 6.88 | 240 |  23136 | +9.79  |

Every interval clears its null:
  2p [53.49, 65.67] excludes 50.00
  3p [42.59, 55.33] excludes 33.33
  4p [27.91, 41.67] excludes 25.00

The nulls are the ones `kindmatch` prints itself, not assumed ones.

VERDICT
=======
The climbed champions beat the frozen A side at all three counts, so the
pre-registered revert condition is not met. **CLAMP_CEILING stays at 120.**

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
=======================
This duel does not isolate the ceiling. The live side has also climbed three
more days than the frozen side (2p 116808 -> 128637, 3p 65450 -> 72936,
4p 21445 -> 23136), so "the raise helped" and "more generations helped" are
confounded and this design cannot separate them. The honest statement is the
one the decision rule asked for: the post-raise champions are not worse, so
there is no case for reverting.

The one piece of corroboration beyond that is the precondition: the climb did
use the new room (`attack_cost_doubled@3p` = 81.77 is unreachable at 60), and
3p -- the count carrying that key -- has the largest margin over its null
(+15.63). That is suggestive, not decisive; a single count is one observation.

REPRODUCTION
============
  cd rust && cargo build --profile difftest --bin kindmatch
  cp experiments/rust_champion_2p.json /tmp/clampab_live_2p.json   # record gen
  ./target/difftest/kindmatch --players 2 --games 240 \
      --weights /tmp/clampab_live_2p.json \
      --weights analysis/frozen/clamp_ceiling_2026-08-27/rust_champion_2p.json
and the same for 3p and 4p.
