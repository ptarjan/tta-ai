sign-gated clamp); "raw_json" is the same key read straight out of the
champion JSON file, unclamped. They differ exactly where dominance_repair
clamped a negative-trained weight to 0.0.

BLACKOUT = every card of the type prices to EXACTLY 0.0 at that size.
FLATLINE = every card of the type prices to the exact SAME NONZERO value
  at that size (n>1 only is meaningful; a flatline with n=1 is a
  degenerate case, called out below where it occurs).
