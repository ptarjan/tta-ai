CEILING PLAYTEST -- 3-PLAYER -- 2026-08-24

QUESTION
Champion /private/tmp/opencensus/champ3p.json has five weights pinned at
exactly 60.000 -- blue_free, culture_rate, food_stock, resource_stock,
workers_late -- which is CLAMP_BLIND in rust/src/bin/climb.rs
(effective_bound = min(spread-derived, 60.0)). Is that ceiling itself the
defect: would the champion play better if those five keys could go higher
or lower than 60? kindmatch and behavcensus load via
tta::bots::weighted::eval::load_weights (parse_weights + dominance_repair,
NO clamp), so a value above 60 is played exactly as written -- a
climb --gens 0 round-trip would clamp it and lie about this, so it was not
used.

METHOD
Binary freshness check (not rebuilt):
  target/release/kindmatch mtime 1787622407
  src/bin/kindmatch.rs mtime  1786824906
  binary is newer -> proceeded without rebuilding.

All duels: 240 games, 3 players, seed 1001, 5 threads, from
/Users/pt/tta-ai/rust, run in both seat arrangements (challenger as A,
one rotating seat vs two champion seats; then champion as A, mirrored).
The tool's own printed null for 3p is 33.33% every time -- quoted verbatim
below, never assumed.

DOMINANCE_REPAIR REASONING (rust/src/bots/weighted/eval.rs)
DOMINATES = [(ResourceStock, BlueFree), (WonderPotential, WonderPromise)].
dominance_repair enforces hi >= lo: if BlueFree > ResourceStock, ResourceStock
is silently raised to match BlueFree. culture_rate, food_stock and
workers_late appear in no DOMINATES pair and no sign-gate table, so they are
never touched by repair in either direction.

- Step A variants (all five keys scaled by the same factor together):
  blue_free and resource_stock move in lockstep and stay equal to each
  other at every step, so b > a never holds -- dominance_repair is a
  provable NO-OP for both the UP and DOWN full-vector variants.
- Step B per-key variants (one key moved, the other four left at 60):
  - resource_stock alone -> 30, blue_free left at 60: a=30 (hi), b=60 (lo),
    b > a violates the rule, repair sets ResourceStock back to 60. This
    variant is INEXPRESSIBLE: kindmatch would silently play resource_stock
    = 60, not 30, so it would not test what it claims to. SKIPPED, not
    played, reported here as the finding instead.
  - blue_free alone -> 30, resource_stock left at 60: a=60 (hi), b=30 (lo),
    no violation. Expressible, played.
  - culture_rate, food_stock, workers_late alone -> 30: not in any gate
    table, always expressible, played.
  - Per-key UP variants (resource_stock or blue_free alone at 120) were
    never built: Step A's UP direction lost decisively in both
    arrangements, which per the test plan skips the entire UP per-key
    sweep.

VERDICT
The ceiling is not worth raising: the full-vector UP variant (all five
keys doubled to 120) lost decisively and unambiguously in both seat
arrangements, and per the test's own stopping rule that closed off any
further UP investigation. Lowering is not supported either, in the
uniform sense the test set out to check: the full-vector DOWN variant's
loss was comparatively mild and one of its two arrangements was not even
statistically distinguishable from the null, and the per-key breakdown
shows that "loss" is not spread evenly across the five pinned keys --
blue_free alone accounts for a far larger, and far more decisive, loss
than all five moved together, culture_rate and food_stock contribute
moderate losses, and workers_late contributes nothing measurable at all
(resource_stock could not be tested in isolation, see caveats). Nothing
in either direction, full-vector or per-key, won a single duel. CLAMP_BLIND
at 60.0 does not look like the defect here: every direction this test
could actually measure was a loss or a wash, never an improvement, which
is a full and flat close of the "raise or lower the ceiling" question for
this champion under this method.

CAVEATS
- Single fixed seed (1001) per duel throughout; no seed-repeat variance
  estimate exists beyond the tool's own printed binomial CI, so a
  seed-dependent artifact cannot be ruled out for any individual number
  above, though the direction is consistent across the many duels run.
- resource_stock could not be tested in isolation at 30 in the DOWN
  per-key sweep: dominance_repair silently reverts it to 60 whenever
  blue_free stays at 60, making that specific variant inexpressible by
  construction, not by choice. Its per-key DOWN effect is simply unknown.
- No per-key UP sweep was run at all (skipped per plan after Step A's UP
  loss), so nothing here rules out blue_free, culture_rate, food_stock,
  resource_stock or workers_late individually benefiting from a value
  above 60 -- only that moving all five up together at once loses badly.
- All duels used --players 3 only, per the assignment; nothing here
  speaks to 2p or 4p behavior of the same five keys.
- Games-per-duel was 240, matching the assignment; this is enough to
  resolve the large blue_free effect cleanly but leaves the food_stock
  mirror and the DOWN full-vector mirror sitting right at the edge of
  significance (p = 0.0504, p = 0.2737) rather than cleanly inside or
  outside it.
