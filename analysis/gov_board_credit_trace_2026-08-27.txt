
gov_board_credit: dead-key trace, the card_rate_credit way (2026-08-27)
=======================================================================

VERDICT: NO. gov_board_credit is LIVE, not the card_rate_credit shape. Its
credit is applied (cards.rs:1958-1962), and nothing before it returns for a
Government card -- the only two branches that return before the gov branch
(unit_tech_credit at cards.rs:1952-1955, tech_board_credit at
cards.rs:1956-1958) are both gated on card types Government never is. The
"earlier gated branch" the coordinator asked about (the board-aware swap-diff
return at cards.rs:2049-2050) runs AFTER the gov credit site, and a
Government card never reaches it anyway: the gov branch returns first, and
`board_yields` (board_yields.rs:856-858) returns None for every non-swap
type regardless.

WHAT THE CREDIT MULTIPLIES
The credit multiplies `gov_value` (cards.rs:1365-1388), a board-aware price:
sum over the government's gain triples of amt * priced_marginal(feature),
plus the cheapest placement route (min over routes of sum over cost triples),
each marginal priced through the live feature weights. It is NOT a flat
printed-value multiplier: `gov_value` returns 0.0 when
`board_yields::government_plans` (board_yields.rs:683) finds no placement
route.

WHO PRODUCES THOSE QUANTITIES
`board_yields::government_plans` reads the government card's effects
(its civil/military action counts and any rate effects) against the
player's current board: it emits the gain triples the government would
produce and the legal placement routes (which slot it would replace, at
what cost). So the quantities are produced by the CARD's data plus the
BOARD state, exactly like tech_value/action_value.

WHICH CARD IDS PRINT THEM -- AND WHY IT DOES NOT MATTER
`card_rate_credit`'s death was structural: `sum_yields` multiplies ONLY
`YieldKind::Rate` triples by the credit (cards.rs:543, and the parallel
sum at cards.rs:1166), and only 10 cards in the whole deck print Rate
fields (8 worker builds -- Lab/Temple/Library/Theater families -- and 2
leaders, per the 2026-08-24 census), which all take the board-aware early
return before `card_yields` is ever reached, so the credit multiplies
nothing anywhere.
gov_board_credit has no such reach problem: it multiplies `gov_value`, and
ALL 33 government cards (data/cards_civil.json, type "government") pass
through the same dispatch (cards.rs:1956-1962). It is irrelevant that only
3 of the 33 print a rate field (Theocracy culture=1, Fundamentalism
science=-2, Democracy culture=3): the credit scales the WHOLE `gov_value`,
whose gain triples include action counts and route costs, not rate fields.
A government card with no rate effect still has `gov_value` > 0 on a board
with a placement route, and the credit multiplies that.

THE EARLIER GATED BRANCH, CHECKED EXPLICITLY
For a Government card entering `card_potential_core` (cards.rs:1919):
  - cards.rs:1952-1955 unit_tech_credit: gated on `kind.is_unit()` --
    Government is not a unit (cards.rs:129-131). Never fires.
  - cards.rs:1956-1958 tech_board_credit: gated on
    `board_yields::is_levelled_type(kind)` (board_yields.rs:253-255:
    worker types + SpecialTech) AND `tb != 0.0`. Government takes no
    workers (cards.rs:141-143) and is not SpecialTech. Never fires.
  - cards.rs:1959-1963 gov_board_credit: fires whenever gb != 0.0.
So when `gov_board_credit != 0.0` the function RETURNS the board-aware
price at cards.rs:1961 -- before the `credit_board` gate (cards.rs:2034),
before the swap-diff return (cards.rs:2049-2050), before `card_yields`
(cards.rs:2056-2058). The "interception" documented at cards.rs:2088-2094
is real and it is the LIVE path, not a shadow.
The reverse direction matters for the champion: when `gov_board_credit ==
0.0` (the 2p and 4p champions), a Government card falls through to the
generic `credit_board` path. Government is a swap type (board_yields.rs:
240-242) and `board_credit_key` returns None for it (cards.rs:597-640
bucket; card_board_government retired 2026-08-13), so it is priced by
`card_board_credit` * swap-diff (cards.rs:2049-2050). That is the fallback,
and it is a different credit -- consistent with the 2p/4p champions
carrying card_board_credit != 0.

WHY THE 3p VALUE IS NOT A LATENT LANDMINE LIKE -5.94
The -5.94 shape was: a weight the climb could mutate, on a key that can
never fire, so a fitted nonzero value is pure noise that a future
structural change could suddenly activate. gov_board_credit at 3p (0.5677
per the coordinator; the frozen 2026-08-27 3p champion carries 0.7889)
sits on a key that DOES fire: every government-card decision the 3p
champion reaches with a placement route available is priced through it.
Its magnitude is a fitted price, not a latent landmine. (Corroboration,
not proof: the 2026-08-24 credit-sensitivity duel,
analysis/credit_sensitivity_2026-08-24.txt:69-72, found gov_board_credit
LIVE at 10x/1.0 on both counts -- win shares 6.39 / -0.213 -- while
card_rate_credit was SILENCED at 2p.)

ONE-SENTENCE ANSWER
gov_board_credit is live at every count where it is nonzero: it is applied
at cards.rs:1958-1962, no earlier branch can return for a Government card
(unit/levelled gates both exclude the type), the board-aware swap-diff
return it could theoretically shadow (cards.rs:2049-2050) is unreachable
for governments whether or not the credit fires, and it multiplies a
board-aware price all 33 government cards flow through -- it does not have
the card_rate_credit problem.
