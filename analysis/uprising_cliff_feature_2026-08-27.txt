Uprising Cliff — does the leaf eval see it?
2026-08-27
================================================================

CONTEXT
The rules make an uprising a STEP FUNCTION, not a slope. RULES_SPEC 6.3 /
6.6 step 2 (docs/RULES_SPEC.md:181, :189):

    "Uprising check (end of turn, after discarding excess military cards):
     if discontent workers > unused workers, skip the entire Production
     Phase (score/corruption/production/consumption all skipped)."

  - "discontent workers" = happy_required(yellow_bank) − happiness rating,
    min 0  (RULES_SPEC 6.3; economy::happy_required).
  - "unused workers" = p.workers_free (state.rs:497) — the unassigned
    tokens; per RULES_SPEC 6.3 they "do not reduce discontent workers; they
    only prevent the uprising."

So the cliff is the comparison  discontent > workers_free, and when it trips
the ENTIRE production phase (science + culture scoring, corruption, food
production, consumption, resource production) is forfeited — a 0/1 gate
with no slope below it.

Question: is that cliff represented in the weighted leaf eval, and if so by
which coordinate?

----------------------------------------------------------------

Notes on the near-misses:
  - Workers/ProdWorkers/UrbanWorkers/UnitWorkers (3-6) are counts of workers
    PLACED ON CARDS (sweep_tableau, features.rs:189-192; `workers` =
    total placed, `prod_workers` = farm+mine, etc.). They do NOT count
    workers_free — a worker on a farm is ProdWorkers, not FreeWorkers.
    They touch "free workers" only in the sense "the complement," and
    carry no happiness/discontent term. Listed for completeness; they are
    not part of the discontent/free-worker surface.
  - RivalFreeCa / RivalFreeWorkers are RIVAL-board facts (rivals.rs:112,
    :123 — `q.workers_free` max/mean over non-resigned rivals). They touch a
    rival's free workers, not the player's own discontent-vs-free comparison
    that the uprising check uses. Listed because they grep-hit "free
    worker"; they are not on the own-board uprising surface.
  - There is NO WeightKey::Unhappy / "Unhappy*" coordinate. The grep
    candidates "Unhappy*" do not exist in this crate; the shortfall side of
    happiness is `Discontent` (hinged) and `HappyMargin` (capped), and the
    surplus side is `HappySurplus`.

So the own-board discontent/happiness/free-worker surface is exactly:
  FreeWorkers, FreeWorkerWall, HappyMargin, Discontent,
  HappyMarginAfterNextPop, Uprising, HappySurplus.
  (plus the Workers-family card counts and the Rival* facts, which are not
   on the uprising surface.)

Q2. For each: LINEAR or HINGED?
----------------------------------------------------------------
A "hinge" = max(0, a-b) or a clamp/step. "Linear" = passes the quantity
through (optionally + a pending-gain offset, or × a scale) with no max/clamp.

  1. FreeWorkers          :840   LINEAR.
       f64::from(p.workers_free) + g(FreeWorkers). Raw count + pending gain.
       No max, no clamp. Monotone in workers_free.

  2. FreeWorkerWall       :861   HINGED (step / indicator).
       `if p.workers_free == 0 { hand_value } else { 0.0 }`. A 0/1 gate on
       workers_free == 0, scaled by hand_value (features.rs:610), not a
       slope. Hinged at exactly zero.

  3. Workers              :862   LINEAR (raw placed-worker count).
  4. ProdWorkers          :863   LINEAR.
  5. UrbanWorkers         :864   LINEAR.
  6. UnitWorkers          :865   LINEAR.
     (3-6 are plain i32->f64 of card-placement counts; not on the
      discontent surface, but linear.)

  7. HappyMargin          :868   HINGED (one-sided clamp).
       margin.min(3.0). margin = happy − happy_required + g(Happy)
       (features.rs:573) is LINEAR in the rating, but the EMIT clamps the
       upper side at 3.0. So: linear below 3, flat above. The lower side is
       NOT clamped here — it runs to −inf, which is exactly why Discontent
       exists as the hinged mirror.

  8. Discontent           :869   HINGED.
       discontent = (-margin).max(0.0) (features.rs:574) = max(0,
       happy_required − happy − g(Happy)). A max(0, ·) hinge: 0 when the
       player is content, positive shortfall when not. Linear in the
       shortfall, zero on the surplus side.

  9. HappyMarginAfterNextPop :870   HINGED.
       discontent_next_pop = (-margin_next).max(0.0)
       (features.rs:591), the same max(0,·) hinge evaluated one population
       increase forward (happy_req_next off next_yellow_bank, :589).

 10. Uprising             :893   HINGED (step / indicator), and the ONLY
       coordinate that hinges on the COMPARISON, not on a single quantity.
       `if discontent > f64::from(p.workers_free)
          { uprising_production_at_stake } else { 0.0 }`
       The predicate `discontent > workers_free` IS the cliff. When it trips
       the emit is scaled by the production actually at stake
       (s.science + s.culture + s.food + s.resources, :891) — a UNITS FIX
       from a bare 0/1 indicator (see the doc comment at features.rs:872-
       890 and the test `uprising_scales_with_the_production_actually_at_
       stake_not_a_flat_0_1`, :1361). 0 below the cliff, production-at-
       stake above it. No slope anywhere.

 11. RivalFreeCa          :1112  LINEAR (rival max, f64::from).
 12. RivalFreeWorkers     :1160  LINEAR (rival max, rivals.rs:123).
 13. HappySurplus         :1197  HINGED (one-sided).
       margin.max(0.0) (features.rs:1197) = max(0, happy − happy_required
       + g(Happy)). The surplus mirror of Discontent: 0 when short, linear
       in the surplus when content.

Summary table (own-board surface):
  FreeWorkers            LINEAR
  FreeWorkerWall         HINGED  (step at workers_free == 0, × hand_value)
  HappyMargin            HINGED  (upper clamp .min(3.0); linear below)
  Discontent             HINGED  (max(0, happy_req − happy − gain))
  HappyMarginAfterNextPop HINGED (max(0,·) one pop forward)
  Uprising               HINGED  (step: discontent > workers_free ? prod : 0)
  HappySurplus           HINGED  (max(0, margin))

  (Workers/Prod/Urban/Unit: LINEAR card counts; RivalFreeCa/RivalFreeWorkers:
   LINEAR rival facts — not on the uprising surface.)

Q3. Is there a coordinate that fires specifically on discontent > free_workers
    — i.e. the cliff itself?
----------------------------------------------------------------
YES. It is `Uprising` (features.rs:893).

    f.set(WeightKey::Uprising,
          if discontent > f64::from(p.workers_free)
            { uprising_production_at_stake } else { 0.0 });

  The predicate `discontent > p.workers_free` is EXACTLY the rules'
  uprising condition (RULES_SPEC 6.3: "if discontent workers > unused
  workers, skip the entire Production Phase"). When it is true, the
  coordinate is non-zero (scaled by the production the rules actually
  forfeit: science + culture + food + resource rates, features.rs:891);
  when it is false, it is exactly 0.0. This is a step function with the
  hinge placed precisely on the cliff — not a slope, not a smoothed proxy.

  It is classified NonPositive in sign_intent (weights.rs,
  `Discontent | Uprising | StrengthDeficit | FamineDeficit =>
  NonPositive("prices a penalty the rules impose")`), i.e. the eval treats
  a positive Uprising as a pure cost, matching the rulebook (a tripped
  uprising forfeits production, never gains it).

  The companion coordinates that feed the SAME comparison but do NOT hinge
  on it:
    - Discontent (features.rs:869) — the LHS of the comparison, hinged at
      0. It is large when the player is unhappy, independent of how many
      free workers there are.
    - FreeWorkers (features.rs:840) — the RHS of the comparison, LINEAR.
      It is large when the player has many idle workers, independent of
      discontent.
  Neither Discontent nor FreeWorkers alone knows about the cliff: you can
  have big Discontent and big FreeWorkers (no uprising, because
  free > discontent) or vice versa. Only Uprising's PREDICATE combines the
  two into the inequality. So the cliff is visible to the leaf eval —
  exclusively through Uprising's `discontent > workers_free` gate.

  Plain statement for the coordinator: the cliff is NOT invisible to the
  leaf eval. It is represented by a single dedicated coordinate,
  `Uprising`, whose emit is a step on exactly `discontent > workers_free`
  (features.rs:893), scaled by the production at stake. Below the cliff the
  coordinate is 0.0; above it, production-at-stake. No other coordinate
  hinges on that comparison.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Caveats
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - `discontent` and `p.workers_free` inside the Uprising predicate are the
    CURRENT board values (features.rs:574, :840's source p.workers_free).
    The pending-gain `g(GainFeature::FreeWorkers)` is added to the EMITTED
    FreeWorkers coordinate (features.rs:840) but NOT to the workers_free
    term inside the Uprising predicate — the predicate uses the raw
    `p.workers_free` field. So Uprising is a same-turn cliff test, not a
    projected one. (HappyMarginAfterNextPop, by contrast, is the
    projection — but it projects the next POPULATION increase's happiness,
    not the free-worker count.)
  - The test `uprising_scales_with_the_production_actually_at_stake_not_a_
    flat_0_1` (features.rs:1361) pins: same discontent/workers_free
    overshoot + bigger production base ⇒ bigger Uprising; and
    `discontent == workers_free` (not strictly greater) ⇒ exactly 0.0
    (features.rs:1385-1389), matching the rules' strict `>`.
