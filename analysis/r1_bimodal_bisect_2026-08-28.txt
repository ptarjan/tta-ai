Bisect: Round-1 Take-vs-EndTurn Margin Bimodal Distribution
================================================================

Date: 2026-08-28

PROBLEM
-------
Between preclimb (2026-08-25) and postclimb (2026-08-26), the distribution of 
Take-vs-EndTurn score margins in round 1 became highly bimodal, with ~0% of 
positions having |margin| < 1.0 (compared to ~10.3% in PRE).

This means roughly 40% of positions cluster near -11 (strongly prefer EndTurn),
and the rest cluster near +41 (strongly prefer Take), with almost no middle ground.

SUCCESS CRITERION
-----------------
A variant is "smooth" when |margin| < 1.0 rises ABOVE 5.0%.
(This indicates the bimodality is broken, returning to a smooth distribution.)

MEASUREMENT METHOD
------------------
Binary search bisection of weight diffs:
1. Computed C = set of keys with differing values (excluding end_turn_bias)
   |C| = 156 keys
2. Also noted key-set changes:
   - Removed: card_board_bonus
   - Added: food_stock_needed, free_workers_needed, resource_stock_needed, science_needed
3. Iteratively reverted subsets of C to PRE values, testing smoothness
4. Stopped when reaching a set of ≤5 keys or after 14 runs (whichever came first)
   → Used 13 runs, reached a 4-key minimal smooth subset
