What the trajectory shows
-------------------------
2p is where the opener broke. CA debited/turn collapsed 2.532 -> 1.260 between preclimb
(08-25, gen 108828) and postclimb (08-26, gen 113181) -- a 50% drop in one day of climbing
-- and then sat at 1.136 (clamp, gen 116808) and 1.320 (opener, gen 119419). The unspent
CA went the other way: 0.635 -> 1.907 -> 2.031 -> 1.847, and the share of turns ending with
>=1 unspent CA went 27.3% -> 70.6% -> 88.4% -> 76.4%. The climb is actively unlearning how
to use civil actions at 2p. It is NOT monotonic: the last step (clamp -> opener) recovered
a little (+0.184 CA/turn), but the level is nowhere near the preclimb one.

3p barely moved: 2.908 -> 2.665 -> 2.659 -> 2.656, essentially flat after the first step.
The 3p champions are tight spenders in every snapshot: ~4.0 CA/turn in rounds 2 and 3 (the
full 4-grant), almost nothing left unspent (left-unspent r2/r3 ~0.000/0.02-0.03, ~34% of
turns ending >=1, dominated by round 1's fixed 2.000 leftover). If anything the 3p opener
got marginally TIGHTER over time, not looser.

4p is non-monotonic and noisy: 3.177 -> 2.398 -> 2.868 -> 2.047. It fell hard at the
postclimb step, recovered at clamp, then fell the hardest at the final step. Left unspent
follows (0.323 -> 1.102 -> 0.632 -> 1.453). Same direction as 2p on net (the final
snapshot is the weakest CA spender of the four at 4p) but with no clean monotone shape.
