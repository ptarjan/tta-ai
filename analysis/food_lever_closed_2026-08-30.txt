The food lever is CLOSED: the FoodRate NonNegative floor will not be landed
===========================================================================
2026-08-30. Decision: **do not land the `FoodRate => NonNegative` sign_intent.**

This closes a chain queued since 2026-08-27 (`analysis/opener_recheck_2026-08-27.txt`,
`c47e3df`) and gated in turn on the clamp-ceiling A/B, on a collinearity check, and
finally on a forced-food arena. The precondition checks all PASSED. The arena did not,
and it is the one that decides.


1. THE ARENA RESULT
===================
`analysis/forced_food_arena_2026-08-29.txt` (`099371c`), 2p, 2500 games, champion
gen 128724, both seats playing the SAME weights; the only difference is that the
forced seat takes a legal food build/upgrade at every r3-r9 own-turn decision.

  forced side wins 233/2500 = 9.3%, 95% CI (Wilson) [8.2%, 10.5%]
  null 50% (symmetric seats, identical weights)
  mean final culture: champion 191.653, forced 96.708   (forced - champ -94.946)
  end-of-r12 food:    champion   1.821, forced  5.946   (+4.125)
  end-of-r12 pop:     champion  15.103, forced 11.979   (-3.124)

The interval lies entirely below the null. This is not a wash; forcing food loses
nine games in ten.

**The (c) check passes, which is what makes the result usable.** The pre-registered
worry was that the forcing might not actually move food, in which case a win rate
would measure nothing. It moved food from 1.821 to 5.946 -- 3.3x, +4.125 absolute.
It undershot the 8.00 the human curve suggested, because the forcing only fires where
a food build is LEGAL, so it is a partial forcing. But it is a large real shift in
the intended direction, and the loss is attached to it.

The mechanism is stated by the same table: the food is bought with civil actions, and
the civil actions come out of population. -3.1 population at r12 against +4.1 food.
That is the ledger `analysis/food_supply_upstream_2026-08-29.txt` already priced --
the forced builds displace Urban 48.1% / Military 19.5% / Mine 18.4% -- and it agrees
with `tta-farm-loses-on-strength-not-food`, which found food_rate to be 2.5% of a
+7.97 farm-vs-mine margin, arrived at by a completely different method.


2. THE FLOOR'S MOTIVATION HAS ALSO EVAPORATED ON ITS OWN
========================================================
Independently of the arena, the weight the floor was meant to constrain is no longer
where the proposal found it. Live champions, read 2026-08-30:

  count  gen      food_rate   resource_rate  food_stock  famine_deficit  free_workers
  2p     128753   -0.0567     +0.1100        +0.0642     -0.0266         +0.5748
  3p      73000   -0.1350     +6.4463        +38.8754    -0.1011         +6.2572
  4p      23169   +0.1726     +0.3351        +0.1776     -0.0211         -0.5692

The proposal was written against 2p `food_rate` = **-1.0516** and argued that the
climb prices food as an active liability, which the rules forbid. Today all three
arms sit within +/-0.18 of zero, and 4p is positive. The bot does not price food as
bad. It prices food as **nothing**.

This is not a global rescaling artefact -- that was checked, because a dot-product
eval is invariant to scaling the whole vector and comparing raw magnitudes across
generations would be a trap. Against the frozen 2p A-side champion (gen 116808) the
whole 174-key vector shrank only to 0.643 of its norm (106.31 -> 68.35), while:

  key            frozen      live      live rescaled to the frozen norm
  food_rate      -1.0516    -0.0567    -0.0883     <- 12x toward zero
  food_stock     +0.7428    +0.0642    +0.0998     <- 7x toward zero
  free_workers   +6.3142    +0.5748    +0.8941     <- 7x toward zero
  strength      +15.2298    +9.1623   +14.2507     <- essentially unmoved
  end_turn_bias  -2.3476    -3.3349    -5.1869     <- grew

So the food family specifically decayed while `strength` held its share of the
vector. Scale-corrected, `food_rate@2p` is -0.088 against a vector norm of 68: about
0.13% of the champion. A `NonNegative` gate would move it from -0.088 to 0.0.


3. WHY THAT SETTLES IT
======================
Three arguments, any one of which is sufficient, and they are independent:

(a) **The chain it was meant to open is refuted.** The floor's value was never the
    0.09; it was that food would convert into population and then into culture. The
    arena bought +4.1 food directly and got -94.9 culture. Whatever is wrong with
    this bot, more food is not the missing piece.

(b) **The intervention is unmeasurable.** An 0.09 move in a 68-norm vector cannot
    flip a decision. `analysis/farm_vs_mine_decomposition_2026-08-28.txt` (`33b3d76`)
    already computed that food would have to reach roughly +0.494 to make the
    Farm/Mine pair competitive; a floor at 0.0 cannot get there by construction.
    Corrected Age-A arithmetic (`2d4e83e` + `7d3b350`) puts the 2p gap at -0.761 with
    both sides positive -- a RANKING failure, not an absolute-value failure, which a
    floor at zero does not address either.

(c) **A sign_intent is irreversible and this one is unfalsifiable.** Per
    `tta-sign-gate-snaps-to-zero` the gate is the one decision the climb cannot undo:
    it pins the key at 0.0 forever. Landing an irreversible constraint whose effect
    is below the noise floor means the A/B it was promised can never resolve, and the
    repo carries a permanent restriction it can never re-test. That is a bad trade
    even if the constraint is philosophically correct.

The rules argument itself is NOT withdrawn. More food production is still never bad
by the rules -- corruption is levied on the blue bank (Sec 6.2), food is the
shortfall buffer (Sec 6.6 3b), a consumption shortfall costs 4 culture per missing
food (Sec 6.6 3d), and no negative `food`/`foodProduction` field exists anywhere in
base-2015 card data (`89141ca`, audited `8d1ad71`). A negative `food_rate` weight is
still a fit the rules do not license. It is simply too small to be worth an
irreversible gate, and the behaviour it was blamed for is not caused by it.

Both preconditions that were cleared, for the record, so nobody re-runs them:
  - collinearity: `analysis/food_family_collinearity_2026-08-28.txt` (`5277728`).
    Eight food readers enumerated; `FamineDeficit` is a strict function of
    FoodStock+FoodRate and silent outside the famine zone; the only wide sink is
    `FoodStock`, large positive on both converged arms. No collinear neighbour was
    expressing "food is bad", so the floor WOULD have been a real intervention rather
    than a relabelling. That check stands; it just no longer matters.
  - the clamp-ceiling A/B it was blocked behind closed on 2026-08-30
    (`analysis/clamp_ceiling_ab_2026-08-30.txt`, `3c0ee2a` + `7af9635`).


4. WHAT SURVIVES, AND WHAT DOES NOT
===================================
UNCHANGED and still true -- these are measurements, and the arena does not touch them:
  - The bot's food production runs backwards from round 2 while the human curve
    climbs 2.00 -> 4.95 (`de5f526`).
  - Food is the binding reason at >= 39.7% of pop-illegal decision points, 44.5%
    late (`b4e1296`).
  - The Farm is legal at 1682 opener + 1405 mid-game decisions and chosen zero times.
  - `bestMine` is 1.00 in all 21 sampled rounds; the bot never upgrades mine tech.

REFUTED as a LEVER:
  - "Buy more food and the population/culture follows." Directly tested, directly
    lost. Food bought with civil actions is a net -94.9 culture at 2p.

The correct reading of the two survivors together is that the food shortage is a
SYMPTOM of the civil-action and population economy, not an input to it. The bot is
food-poor because it is small ([[tta-bot-is-small-not-militarised]]) and runs at zero
worker slack, not small because it is food-poor. Every remaining food finding should
be filed under the economy, not under food.

Per the dispatch, no milder forcing rule was sought after the loss, and none should
be: the point of pre-registering "a loss is the valuable result" is that you do not
go shopping for a variant that wins.

DO NOT re-open the food lever without a mechanism that gets food WITHOUT spending a
civil action on it. That is the only door the arena leaves open, and nobody has
proposed one.
