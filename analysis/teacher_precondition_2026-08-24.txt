READINGS

1. The student is not broken. It beats random 96% and ties greedy. It is
   simply far below the champion: 0 wins in 240.

2. Narrowly, the precondition PASSES. On the common opponent the teacher
   (15.21%) is stronger than the student (0.00%). Imitating it would move
   the policy head forward.

3. The loop is still NO-GO, for a reason that outranks the precondition.
   Ordering by strength: champion-greedy > beam-alone (39.5%) >
   shortlist+beam (15.21%) > policy alone (0%). Every layer added on top
   of the champion vector makes play WORSE. Search with the champion as
   leaf eval loses to the same vector used greedily, and the human
   shortlist loses more on top of that. A better policy head only feeds a
   pipeline that is already behind plain 1-ply greedy.

4. This contradicts "the shortlist is not the bottleneck / top-8 already
   95%". Top-8 recall on human moves is high, but the shortlist still
   costs 24 points of win rate against the champion, because the moves it
   drops are the ones the champion vector wanted, not the ones humans
   wanted. Recall against humans is the wrong yardstick for a shortlist
   feeding a champion-scored beam.

5. The leaf evaluation is the whole job, again and more sharply: an eval
   that gets WORSE under deeper search is an eval calibrated only to the
   depth it was fitted at.
