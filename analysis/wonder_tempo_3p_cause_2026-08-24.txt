Why the 3p champion starts wonders late: it buys cards instead
==============================================================
Measured 2026-08-24. Prerequisite reading: analysis/wonder_tempo_2026-08-24.txt,
which established that 3p first-wonder-stage round 10+ wins 24.6% against a
33.3% null (p=0.0171) while the 2p late median is NOT a defect.

Spend is the real post-move CA delta, so discounted and free moves correctly
contribute 0. The LATE wonder-step column is 0.0% BY CONSTRUCTION - the group
is defined by having no stage before round 10 and the table stops at 9. The
informative question is where that 9-15% of the budget goes instead, and the
answer is `take`: LATE's take share is higher in 5 of the 7 rounds, by +10.7pp
(r4), +6.4pp (r5), +9.7pp (r8) and +8.4pp (r9).

Findings
--------
1. LATE IS NOT RESOURCE-CONSTRAINED. Resource production, food production,
   science and buildings are all within noise of EARLY at the end of round 6.
   The bot is not failing to afford a wonder stage; it is declining to buy one.
   That kills the obvious economic explanation before it is proposed.

2. LATE IS NOT BEHIND AT ROUND 6 - it is marginally AHEAD on culture (7.57 vs
   6.43). It loses later, from an equal or better position. So the cost of the
   plan is deferred, which is exactly why a leaf evaluation that scores the
   board at round 6 cannot see it.

3. THE REALLOCATED BUDGET GOES TO `take`, NOT TO develop/build/pop, and it
   accumulates: LATE ends round 6 holding median 2 cards against EARLY's 1
   (p75 3 vs 2, max 5 vs 4). The champion at 3p prices "take another card"
   above "advance the wonder already paid into".

Caveat, stated plainly
----------------------
This is a conditional split WITHIN self-play, so it is correlational. Points
2 and 3 are partly two views of one fact: a group that spent no CAs on wonder
stages will of course hold more of everything else. Point 1 is the one that
does not have that circularity, and it is the load-bearing one - affordability
is ruled out.

This does NOT license a weight change on its own. See the recorded error in
[[tta-selfplay-winrate-vs-advice]]: a mechanism that explains the observation
is seductive precisely because it explains it. The settling test is a COMMON
OPPONENT duel plus absolute score, not a replayed position and not a mirror
match - raise the wonder-step valuation at 3p, then duel the new vector and
the frozen champ3p against the same fixed reference and compare margins.
