COORDINATOR CORRECTIONS APPLIED (binary at ade726b; spec af57966 untouched)
  1. S_d is now the spread over candidates of the per-move DELTA the probe
     causes:  d_m = dot(pw, phi_p(m)) - dot(champion, phi_c(m))
              = dot(champion, phi_p(m) - phi_c(m))
     (pw and champion differ only in coordinate k, and k has no linear
     feature coordinate, so both forms are identical: the delta is purely
     the pricer re-pricing effect, baseline removed.)
  2. The flip rates are CREDIT-CLASS, not per-key: both flip vectors
     perturb all 20 credit keys at once, so the flip rate is ONE number
     per count ("flip rate for the credit half as a whole"), not a column
     on 20 rows. The per-key `touched` column is per-key and stands.

WHAT THE DELTA FORM FOUND
  The linearity test (S_d(2.0) ~= 2*S_d(1.0), within 1% relative, at each
  firing decision; a key is GATED when it fails at MORE than half its
  firing decisions) now FAILS for MOST keys, every count -- and the
  readings are REAL per-key quantities, not the total spread:
    2p: 17 of 18 firing keys gated (2 keys never fire)
    3p: 16 of 19 firing keys gated (1 never fires)
    4p: 16 of 19 firing keys gated (1 never fires)
  So per the spec's escape hatch, the table is NOT forced: the 20 rows
  stay at CLAMP_BLIND, and the RUST TABLE emits 0.000000 for every gated
  key. Only a handful of (key, count) pairs pass linearity and carry a
  defensible slope; none is a consistent champion across counts (a key
  that passes at one count is usually gated at another).

  The gated readings are NOT the flat-at-T artifact of the first run.
  S_d(1.0) and S_d(2.0) are distinct per-key numbers (e.g. 2p:
  card_board_credit 333.97 / 775.72; unit_tech_credit 151.69 / 309.96),
  and the ratios S_d(2.0)/S_d(1.0) scatter around 2.0 with a wide spread
  (roughly 0.24 to 1.76), i.e. the pricer output is NOT linear in c
  between the two probes for most keys. This is the genuine finding about
  the credit pricers the first run could not test: the per-key |c|-linearity
  the bound relies on does not hold, so no per-key normalized slope is
  defensible at the two probes the spec pins (c = 1.0, c = 2.0).

  A few (key, count) pairs DO pass linearity and carry a defensible
  slope in the RUST TABLE (all others 0.000000):
    2p: bonus_card_credit 0.391117
    3p: aggression_board_credit 7.967209, pact_board_credit 1.955373
    4p: unit_strength_credit 503.905097, territory_credit 63.607113,
        gov_board_credit 175.810309, pact_board_credit 26.711877,
        unit_tech_credit 796.642824, restricted_resource_credit 3.456802,
        free_action_credit 0.027570
  The RUST TABLE as emitted is therefore almost all zeros and is NOT
  suitable for splicing into p95_candidate_spread as-is: a row with two
  zeros and one number would clamp two of the three player counts to
  CLAMP_BLIND on the strength of a single-count reading -- exactly the
  mixing the spec forbids. Treat the table as evidence, not as a paste.
