  engine=[135,203] index=[127,211] (sum 338 both sides, exactly zero-sum)
  Journal: Purple declares War over Culture on Orange (III/17), resolves
  next turn: "Purple wins War over Culture Attacker's strength: 43;
  Defender's strength: 12." Spoils rule: victor takes 5 + advantage
  culture. 5 + (43-12) = 36, and the journal's own running culture
  totals for both players confirm exactly a 36-point transfer actually
  happened (Purple's post-war checkpoint arithmetic: 80 + 36(war) +
  3(event) + 14(end-turn) = 133, matches the printed "(now 133)";
  symmetric check on Orange nets the same 36-point loss once you count
  the intervening Wealthy-Territory event (+3) and Fast Food Chains
  wonder completion (+18) between Orange's two checkpoints).

  Added a temporary SCOREDIV_WAR_DEBUG env-gated eprintln in
  combat::resolve_war_outcome (reverted before finishing -- see "final
  state" below) and reran the isolated game. Engine's own numbers at
  resolution: attacker_strength=43 (MATCHES journal exactly), but
  defender_strength=20 (journal says 12 -- off by exactly +8, which is
  exactly the game's whole score delta). Defender (Orange) had leader
  Napoleon Bonaparte (elected turn 14, never replaced) and NO current
  tactic (never played/copied one all game) but 4 distinct military
  unit types on the board with workers (Warriors/Cannon/Cavalrymen/Air
  Forces). Special::StrengthPerUnitType (Napoleon, +2 per distinct
  type) computes +8 there unconditionally -- it does not check
  `p.tactic.is_none()` the way `army_strength()` does.

  I had built a hypothesis (raw unit strength should always count per
  the rulebook's "a military unit always contributes its strength, even
  if it is in an army" note, cge_code_of_laws.pdf, and 1+3+3+5=12
  exactly matches the journal's defender strength; Napoleon's
  per-type bonus should require a tactic/formed army the same way
  army_strength does) but had NOT yet turned it into a tested fix when
  the steer arrived. I did NOT land this hypothesis as a fix: 7523597
  is now confirmed EXACT under the sibling's landed commit (see below),
  and I have not independently verified my hypothesis is the same
  mechanism as theirs, only that both explanations predict this one
  game's outcome. I am not re-opening Special::StrengthPerUnitType --
  it is on the SETTLED list, and the settled claim (that the +2-per-
  distinct-TYPE-not-per-worker formula is correct) is not something
  this trace contradicts; I was chasing a DIFFERENT, narrower question
  (does it need a tactic) that this game alone cannot cleanly decide
  once the antiquation-timing explanation is on the table too.

  military-specific hypotheses I could test against the 26-game ID list
  (war occurred; Impact-of-Strength event fired) shows any enrichment
  at all (1.14x and ~1.05x respectively -- noise). Given the explicit
  instruction not to manufacture a second finding out of what's left,
  and given the task's own repeated warning that 26-36 failures spread
  over many overlapping cards is weak evidence per card, my read is:
  cardblame's per-CARD ranking is picking up that players who reach
  Age III/IV and build tactics/leaders are the same players whose games
  are long, card-dense, and have many independent opportunities for the
  OTHER, unrelated single-action bugs visible in the delta-shape list
  above (TakeCard/BuildBuilding/UpgradeUnit/EndTurn drift) to fire --
  not a shared military-specific root cause. I did not find a third
  hypothesis that both (a) is testable cheaply against this ID list and
  (b) is specific to the flagged cards, so I am not chasing further.

CONCLUSION

  overwhelmingly a symptom of the war-resolution antiquation-timing bug
  the sibling worker already found and landed (55bbca5). After merging
  that fix, neither of the two clean military-specific hypotheses I
  could test (war occurred anywhere in the game; an Impact-of-Strength
  final event fired) shows any residual enrichment on the remaining 26
  mismatches -- this is the clean, publishable negative the coordinator
  asked me to report if I found it. I made no code changes of my own;
  I am not re-opening Special::StrengthPerUnitType/Napoleon (settled),
  and I did not chase the residual per-card cardblame signal further
  because I could not turn it into a testable, non-overlapping second
  hypothesis within budget.
