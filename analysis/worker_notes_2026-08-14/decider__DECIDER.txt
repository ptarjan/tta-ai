TRACE: game 7523376 (the KNOWN RELATED FACT game)
====================================================
Isolated per the method (`/tmp/one.tsv` = header + this game's index.tsv
row). REPLAY_DEBUG_ALL=1 trace + two temporary eprintln probes (added,
used, then `git checkout -- rust/src/replay_common.rs`'d back out --
NOT part of the final diff) pinned the mechanism precisely:

The stop is at line 265 ("Orange discards 1 card", round 14), but the
`Replayer::check_discard_phase_oracle`/`prescan_military_hand_ledger` cross-
check (already in the codebase, not added by this pass) independently flags
the FIRST hand-size divergence for PURPLE two rounds earlier:

  "7523376 line 203 (round 11 age II, Purple): journal's cross-validated
  excess 0, this binary computes 1 (hand_military_len 4 limit 3)"
  classified `SimulatorBug` (ledger agrees with journal; the FORWARD
  SIMULATOR's own hand_military is wrong), last ledger event PrepareEvent.

Walking the ledger-vs-simulator comparison at EVERY checkpoint (not just the
flagged one -- added via a temporary `TMPDEBUG3` print in
`check_discard_phase_oracle`, reverted after use) for Purple across the
whole game shows simulator and ledger agree exactly through round 8
(both 4), then FIRST disagree at round 10 (simulator=3, ledger=2) -- one
round earlier than where it crosses the visible discard-limit threshold and
gets reported. Between the round-8 and round-10 checkpoints the journal
shows (lines 165-167, game `7523376.tsv`):

  165: "Purple ... Discard Phase 2 military cards must be discarded"
  166: "Purple ... End turn ..." (no draw clause this round)
  167: "Purple ... discards 3 cards"

BGO's own two independent renderings of "how many to discard" DISAGREE with
each other here: the modal announcement says 2, the actual resolution line
says 3. `prescan_discard_phase_oracle` (already in the codebase) correctly
refuses to trust this entry when the two disagree (`out.insert` only fires
when `announced == actual`, `replay_common.rs` ~line 4344), so round 9's
checkpoint is silently dropped from the oracle rather than either number
being blindly believed -- exactly the right call given the ambiguity.

But `prescan_military_hand_ledger` (the OTHER, always-on bookkeeping used
for cross-validation) unconditionally trusts the "discards 3 cards" line
(-3) with no gate at all, while the LIVE SIMULATOR's own discard resolution
is driven purely by its own live `hand_len - limit` computation at that
moment (computed 2, not 3) via `interact::discard_excess_military` /
`Pending::Choice(DiscardMilitary)` -- NOT by reading the journal's count.
So the simulator discards 2 real cards where the true game discarded 3,
landing 1 card over -- a silent, single-card "phantom" that sits under the
visible excess threshold for two more rounds before crossing it at line 203,
and it is what game `7523376`'s eventual stop at line 265 traces back to.

I could not determine from the text alone why BGO's own two renderings
disagree by exactly 1 here (no candidate mechanism -- Robespierre's
election, the Republic revolution, or Patriotism's one-time "+1 military
action" immediately before it -- has a documented TTA rule that forces an
extra, hand-limit-independent military discard; `Patriotism`'s own
`CardEffects.military_actions: 1` looks like a one-time immediate grant,
not a permanent stat contribution, so it should not even move
`state_stats().military_actions`, the field `check_discard_phase_oracle`'s
own `limit` formula reads). This looks like an as-yet-undocumented THIRD
"BGO discard-line-timing quirk" (the module doc at `replay_common.rs`
~4234 already documents one: discard-before-End-turn ordering instability
on game `7523347`) rather than a bug in this binary's own bookkeeping.


WHY NO FIX WAS LANDED
========================
The two changes that would naturally address "simulator's live discard
count should match the journal's real discard count" are BOTH explicitly
closed off by this task's own forbidden list, and for exactly this shape:

1. Force `apply_one`'s `ActionClass::Discard` arm to make the simulator
   discard the journal's own count instead of its live-computed count.
   FORBIDDEN -- already measured 456 regressions to 1 win by a prior pass.
   This is precisely the fix this bug wants: the failure mode (simulator's
   live excess computation disagreeing with the journal's true discard
   count) is generic across the whole "discards" subgroup, and forcing the
   journal's count is the obvious generalization -- which is exactly why it
   was already tried, and exactly why it was this catastrophic. Reasonable
   inference: for most of the OTHER 87 games in this subgroup, the
   simulator's live count is actually CORRECT and it's the eventual
   consuming code path (or a different, unrelated card-identity mismatch)
   that trips later -- forcing the count blind breaks all of those.

2. Change which cards `interact::discard_options`' auto-resolution evicts,
   or its ordering, so a genuinely-uncertain 1-card gap gets absorbed
   differently. FORBIDDEN outright by this task -- "load-bearing for bot
   gameplay".

Both are closed for good reason (the corpus-wide regression count on #1 is
concrete, measured evidence, not a hypothesis), and I could not find a
third, narrower intervention that fixes 7523376's specific 2-vs-3 BGO
mid-turn discard-count disagreement without touching either of these two
surfaces. No fix was landed rather than risk reproducing either measured
regression.
