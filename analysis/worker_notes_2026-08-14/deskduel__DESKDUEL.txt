CLAMP-PINNING CHECK
CLAMP = 60.0 (magnitude), only "culture" is exempt (frozen at 1.0, not
mutated). A coordinate sitting at exactly +/-60.0 is "pinned" -- it hit the
wall and mutation can't move it further; the RUNAWAY log line is climb's own
alarm for this.

  desk_2p (gen 57406): 1 pinned  -- tech_redundancy_discount = 60
  desk_3p (gen 26270): 1 pinned  -- attack_target_weakness = 60
                        (matches the RUNAWAY log line reported for this arm)
  desk_4p (gen 12129): 2 pinned  -- best_theater = 60, rival_free_ca = 60
                        (also 2 more coords near the wall: workers = 57.5,
                        workers_late = 58.7)
  desk TOTAL: 4 pinned coordinates across the three vectors

  mini_2p (gen 44852): 0 pinned
  mini_3p (gen 15861): 1 pinned  -- workers_late = 60
                        (also culture_rate = 59.6, workers = 59.0 near wall)
  mini_4p (gen 9650):  2 pinned  -- tactic_level = -60, unit_workers = -60
                        NOTE: this vector's `workers` = 101.14, which
                        EXCEEDS the clamp entirely -- almost certainly a
                        legacy value from before CLAMP=60 governed this
                        weight (clamp() only fires on values written by
                        mutation, not on values loaded from an older file),
                        not something the current climb could produce.
  mini TOTAL: 3 pinned coordinates across the three vectors

Verdict on pinning: the desktop arm is not uniquely pathological here --
4 pinned coordinates vs. the mini's 3, roughly the same rate. Pinning shows
up on both sides at every player count. It is a real, pre-existing
shortcoming of the mutation/clamp scheme in general (both climbs hit it),
not something specific to the desktop run that should disqualify its
champions outright. The 3p pin on attack_target_weakness is worth watching
(it's the coordinate that triggered the RUNAWAY log), but the 3p vector
still won its duel against a mini 3p champion that has its own pin, so
pinning alone isn't deciding this outcome.

  2p: INSTALL-CANDIDATE -- large, unambiguous duel win (74% vs 50% null).
      Test on the mini's current (newer) engine before promoting, since the

  3p: TEST FURTHER -- duel win is real but thin (42.1% vs 33.3% null,

Bottom line: the duel evidence (not vs_anchor) says the desktop's climb IS
currently producing better weight vectors at 2p and 4p, and probably at 3p
too but with less margin -- for ALL THREE, "better" means "test on the
mini's real engine before installing," because the duel was necessarily run
under yesterday's rules.
