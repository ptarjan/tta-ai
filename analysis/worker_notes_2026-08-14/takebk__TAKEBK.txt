STEP 3 -- TRACE: game 7522905, Urban Growth Budget rejection (FIXED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Journal (rounds trimmed to the relevant turn, /private/tmp/bgo-journals/journals/7522905.tsv):
  16  Grey takes Rich Land in hand Grey uses 1 civil action
  17  Grey takes Frugality in hand Grey uses 2 civil action
  18  Grey takes Frugality in hand Grey uses 1 civil action
  19  Grey puts Frugality back in the row Grey gets 2 civil action
  20  Grey takes Urban Growth in hand Grey uses 2 civil action   <-- rejected

This game's own `CA_TOTAL_UNDERCOUNT` self-check (already instrumented,
`replay_common.rs` ~line 6981, doc: "civil action total" handoff) already
flagged it independently of the bucket investigation:
  CA_TOTAL_UNDERCOUNT game=7522905 actor=3 lineno=20 age_civil=A round=1 \
      take_spend_this_turn=5 ca_total=4 deficit=1

Root cause: Frugality (A) has TWO physical copies in the Age A deck
(`count: [2, 2, 2]` in card_table.rs; `costs::can_take_gated`'s own doc:
action cards are exempt from the one-per-name rule specifically because
several copies of the same name can be in the row/hand at once). Grey took
BOTH physical copies (line 17, cost 2 CA; line 18, cost 1 CA -- two
different row slots, hence two different costs for the "same-named" card),
then put ONE of them back (line 19, refund 2 CA).

`prescan_putback_skips` (replay_common.rs ~line 5531) pairs a PutBack with
an open TakeCard of the SAME card+actor using a per-card LIFO stack, popping
the MOST RECENTLY pushed entry (`Vec::rposition`, "last matching actor").
Its own doc comment stated the (until now unverified) premise this depends
on outright: "every row/hand card is a unique instance in the table (the
same name never denotes two different physical cards within one game)".
That premise is FALSE for a multi-copy action card. Popping "most recent"
matched the putback to line 18 (the 1-CA take) instead of line 17 (the 2-CA
take the refund of 2 actually names), silently canceling the WRONG pair:
line 18+19 got skipped (net contribution 0, correctly), but line 17's 2-CA
take was left standing as Grey's "real, kept" Frugality -- when the refund
amount is direct, unambiguous journal evidence that line 17 is the one
being undone, not line 18.

Running civil-action total with the WRONG pairing (old behaviour):
  16 (1) + 17 (2, wrongly kept) + 20 (2) = 5   vs.  ca_total = 4   => deficit 1,
  manufacturing the Budget rejection on line 20's Urban Growth take.

With the CORRECT pairing (17+19 skipped, 18 kept):
  16 (1) + 18 (1, correctly kept) + 20 (2) = 4   vs.  ca_total = 4   => exactly affordable.

FIX: `prescan_putback_skips` now records each open take's own parsed cost
(`civil_and_military_uses(line.text).0`) alongside `(lineno, actor)`, and a
PutBack first tries to match an open entry whose recorded cost equals the
putback's own refund amount (`trailing_gets_civil_action`), falling back to
"most recent" (the original, single-copy-safe behaviour) only when no cost
match exists or the card has just one open take (the overwhelmingly common
case -- confirmed unchanged by a dedicated test, see below).

Verified in isolation (REPLAY_DEBUG=1, one-row index for 7522905 only):
  - Before the fix: CA_TOTAL_UNDERCOUNT fires at line 20; replay stops at
    round 1 with IllegalMove: Take (Budget).
  - After the fix: no CA_TOTAL_UNDERCOUNT anywhere in this game; the replay

    "StuckPending: PlunderSplit choice open for player # but no

    (see final counts below) -- but the fix itself is real, narrowly scoped,
    and independently confirmed by the corpus's own CA-total oracle, not a
    manufactured completion.

INVESTIGATION METHOD NOTE (how the split was actually done)
-------------------------------------------------------------
`costs::take_rejection`'s own `IllegalMove: Take` diagnostic
(`REPLAY_DEBUG=1`, "DEBUG TAKE REJECT: ...") only fires when the §2.5
gates it checks (cost/hand_full/leader-age/duplicate) are the reason. For
the 11 pending-blocked games it printed NOTHING at the failing line (it
returned None -- "legal by its own rules") even though the overall move
was still rejected as IllegalMove: Take, because the TRUE blocker
(`r.state.pending`/`queue` nonempty) is invisible to it. This was diagnosed
by adding a temporary, since-reverted debug print immediately before the
`take_rejection` call showing `legal::legal_moves(&r.state).as_slice()
.contains(&Move::Take{slot})` (false) alongside `r.state.pending.top()`
(Some(Choice(Annex{..})) / Some(Choice(WarTech{..})) / Some(Choice{kind:
DiscardMilitary,..})) for every game in the bucket. No dump/debug
instrumentation was left in the tree beyond what was already there before
this pass (the temporary prints were reverted).

Also confirmed as a red herring during investigation: running a SINGLE
isolated game (one-row index.tsv) reproduces the SAME stop line/mismatch
as the full 1,011-game corpus run for every game checked -- the earlier
appearance of "10 of 15 games don't reproduce their DUMP line when run
alone" was an artifact of an early ad-hoc debug print (missing the
game-id field, silently misattributing lines across a batched multi-game
run), not a real per-game nondeterminism. Fixed once the print correctly
attributed lines to games; do not re-open "isolation doesn't reproduce
the bucket" as a real finding.
