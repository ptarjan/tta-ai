STEP 1: what the sources say, verbatim
============================================================

/Users/pt/tta-ai/sources/ does NOT contain BGA's actual game-logic source
(no throughtheages.game.php or equivalent) -- only
`bga_throughtheages_material.inc.php`, which is card TEXT/metadata, not
targeting logic. So "BGA's own implementation" is not directly inspectable
this pass; the rules oracle (RULES_SPEC.md) and the two official errata PDFs
are the highest-authority sources actually available, and they are decisive:

- FAQ v1.5, p.11 (`sources/faq_v15.pdf`), verbatim: "Weakest/Strongest
  Civilization(s): When this phrase occurs, it is referring only to
  Military Strength, not to Culture or Population or anything else."

- FAQ v1.5, p.11, same page, the paragraph just above it (defining Military
  Strength itself): "Ties during Wars and Aggressions: If the Military
  Strengths of two warring Civilizations are tied at the moment of
  execution of a War, then there is no effect from that War. The same goes
  for Aggressions. (Note that Military Strength includes your Strength
  that arises from all sources, including from Wonders and Leaders and
  Territories.)" -- i.e. Military Strength (== "weakest/strongest") is
  explicitly an ALL-SOURCES quantity, the same one used for combat.

- Code of Laws, p.12 (`sources/cge_code_of_laws.pdf`, "GENGHIS KHAN" leader
  errata), verbatim: "When forming armies, each infantry unit can be either
  cavalry or infantry (but not both) to maximize the civilization's total
  strength rating. The infantry units are still considered infantry for
  other cards, such as the Great Wall, regardless of their role in forming
  armies." -- confirms tactic-formed armies count toward "strength rating"
  and that the substitution is chosen to MAXIMIZE it, exactly matching
  RULES_SPEC 10.3's "grouping chosen to maximize total tactical strength
  (no choice, always maximal)" and this engine's `army_strength`.

- RULES_SPEC.md 5.3 (already quoted by build2, re-confirmed): "Statistic
  comparisons (\"strongest/weakest\" = strength rating [FAQ p.11])".
- RULES_SPEC.md 10.3 (already quoted by build2, re-confirmed): "Each army
  adds the card's tactical strength to your strength rating, on top of the
  units' own strength."

CONCLUSION FROM SOURCES: "weakest civilization" for scripted events IS the
live, tactic-inclusive Military Strength -- the SAME quantity used for
War/Aggression combat resolution, not a separate/simpler track. This is
exactly what `events::apply_single_target(..., RankStat::Strength, ...)` ->
`effects::state_stats(...).strength` (which adds `army_strength(p)`, the
tactic-formed-army bonus) already computes. build2's open question
("does BGA use the same live combat strength, or a separate simpler
track?") is answered: SAME quantity, by direct rules citation. This alone
would already close the avenue as a naming/definition question -- the corpus
check below is the independent, quantitative confirmation the task asked
for.

STEP 3: corpus cross-check -- the decisive, quantitative evidence
============================================================

Method: for every game in the 1011-game corpus, ran `REPLAY_DEBUG_ALL=1
./target/difftest/replay <id>` (using the temporary `TMPDEBUG_WEAKEST`
probe from STEP 2), and for every "Current event:" block in the journal's
own raw text whose FULL description is a SINGLE sentence containing
"weakest civilization" (singular -- e.g. "Reign of Terror": "The weakest
civilization loses 1 population."), extracted:
  (a) the journal's own named victim (BGO's raw text always follows the
      description with "<Color> <loses/gains/...>", copied verbatim into
      this engine's own `raw_text` debug field -- ground truth, no separate
      journal parse needed), and
  (b) this engine's own live pick, read off the nearest following
      `apply_single_target: ... stat=Strength best=false ...` trace line
      that the SAME resolution emits.

IMPORTANT METHODOLOGY NOTE, so the next worker doesn't repeat the mistake:
an earlier version of this script also tried to handle COMBINED
strongest+weakest cards (e.g. "Refugees": "The strongest scores 3 culture
and gains 1 population. The weakest civilization loses 3 culture and 1
population.") by pairing description-sentence order with named-outcome-
clause order positionally. This is UNSOUND -- verified by hand against
"Border Conflict" ("The weakest civilization must destroy...The strongest
civilization gains 3 resources.; Purple produces 3 resources; Orange must
destroy..."): the weakest-effect sentence comes FIRST in the description
but its named outcome comes SECOND in the clause list, the opposite of
Refugees' own ordering. There is no reliable positional rule connecting
description-sentence order to named-outcome-clause order for combined
cards from the text alone. The corpus check below therefore ONLY uses
single-sentence, single-effect "weakest civilization" cards, where the sole
outcome clause is unambiguous. This drops from 715 raw "weakest
civilization" text occurrences (including combined and plural
"civilizations" cards) to 324 cleanly-attributable single-target events --
smaller N, but every one of the 324 is a sound, unambiguous ground-truth
comparison, which is what the task asked for ("agreement counts... not just
an anecdote").

    320 / 324 agree with the journal's named victim = 98.8%

The current (tactic-inclusive) definition wins by a wide, decisive margin --
