STEP 3: why this was NOT shipped as a fix -- the "weakest" mismatch itself is
unresolved, not confirmed wrong
============================================================

Chased whether the engine's strength-rating ARITHMETIC is buggy (which would
have been the fix) and could not confirm it is:

- RULES_SPEC.md 5.3: "Statistic comparisons (\"strongest/weakest\" = strength
  rating [FAQ p.11])".
- RULES_SPEC.md 10.3: "Each army adds the card's tactical strength to your
  strength rating, ON TOP OF the units' own strength" -- i.e. tactic/army
  bonuses ARE part of "strength rating" by the rules text itself, not a
  combat-only side calculation excluded from stat comparisons.
- Orange's +4-over-raw-units bonus traces to a REAL, correctly-firing
  synergy: Orange elected Genghis Khan at round 8 ("Special::
  InfantryCountsAsCavalryForTactics", RULES_SPEC 10.6 confirms this is the
  actual printed rule) immediately after setting up a Heavy Cavalry tactic
  (round 8), letting Orange's 3 plain Warriors (Infantry) count as Cavalry
  and form actual armies for tactical-strength purposes -- exactly the
  well-known Genghis-Khan-plus-cavalry-tactic combo real TTA players use.
- Purple's +3-over-raw-units bonus ALSO traces to a real, correctly-firing
  ability: Purple's leader Joan of Arc carries `Special::
  StrengthPerTempleOrGovernmentHappy(1)` (confirmed against
  `src/card_table.rs`), and Purple has a Religion temple + Theocracy
  government in play, both potential happy-face sources.
- `apply_single_target`'s own ranking logic, given those computed VALUES, is
  arithmetically self-consistent (ascending sort, first = weakest, matches
  the printed `values`/`ranked` debug pair exactly) -- the bug, if it exists,
  is not in the ranking/tie-break code, only (possibly) in one of the two
  underlying VALUES.

In other words: both Orange's and Purple's individual strength contributions
trace to correctly-implemented, real special-card abilities, and the "weakest
= strength rating, INCLUDING tactic bonuses" reading is directly supported by
RULES_SPEC's own quoted text. I could not find, inside this pass's budget, a
concrete, provably-wrong rule being applied -- which is the bar this task sets
before touching engine code ("fix the engine... find what we have wrong").
Shipping a change to `apply_single_target`/`state_stats`/`army_strength`
without being sure which of the two values (if either) is actually wrong
would risk exactly the "manufactured win" this task explicitly forbids. This
is reported as an unresolved, partially-traced lead, not a confirmed bug.

One live possibility not yet checked (handoff item below): BGA's own actual
"weakest civilization" comparison for scripted events MAY use a simpler,
non-combat "strength rating" track (a running total independent of the
CURRENT tactic's army-formation state) rather than the live combat-strength
recomputation `state_stats`/`army_strength` were built for -- i.e. two
DIFFERENT numbers that happen to share the name "strength rating" in
RULES_SPEC's prose. `sources/` (BGA's own implementation) was not checked for
this distinction this pass; that is the concrete next step.
