ROOT CAUSE (one sentence)
--------------------------
Winston Churchill's once-per-turn choice ("score 3 culture" OR "you have 3
science and 3 resources for developing military unit technologies and
building/upgrading units", sources/bga_throughtheages_material.inc.php #186)
was NEVER modeled for the military branch: BGO does not print a distinct
journal line announcing that choice (unlike the culture branch's "End turn
Winston Churchill scores 3 culture." suffix), so nothing in the replayer
ever applied Move::Churchill{Military}, leaving p.mil_discount/
p.mil_sci_discount permanently at 0 for every Churchill-led player who
picked the military option -- and every later Build/Upgrade/Develop line
that tried to spend from that (correctly nonzero, per BGO) pool was
rejected as an IllegalMove.


HOW IT WAS FOUND
-----------------
1. Dumped both buckets' raw failure text via REPLAY_DUMP_BUCKET and grouped
   by card name -- no single card dominated either bucket (spread across
   ~40 different cards each), ruling out a per-card cost bug.
2. Re-ran both buckets' 168 games together with REPLAY_DEBUG=1, extracted
   the LAST "DEBUG try_apply fail" + detail block per game (the existing
   `costs::build_cost_for`/`tech_cost_net` diagnostic already printed there).
   Every single Build/Develop failure showed a real, non-None printed cost
   -- so these are NOT "unbuildable card" or parser-gap failures, they are
   genuine affordability failures against costs the engine itself agrees are
   correct.
3. Tabulated the `leader=` field across all 168 failures:
     Develop bucket (65 total): 34 have leader=Winston Churchill, and 26 of
       those 34 are develops of a military-UNIT tech (Riflemen, Cavalrymen,
       Cannon, Air Forces, Tanks, Modern Infantry, Rockets, Military Theory)
       -- exactly the CardType set costs::tech_cost_net applies
       p.mil_sci_discount to.
     Build bucket (103 total): 28 have leader=Winston Churchill, and 24 of
       those 28 are builds of a military unit -- exactly costs::
       build_cost_net's is_unit() gate for p.mil_discount.
4. Traced one concrete example end to end (game 7523818, round 16, Orange
   builds Cannon then Air Forces): journal shows "Orange plays Wave of
   Nationalism Orange gets 6 military resource" then "... Military Build-Up
   ... gets 8 military resource" then two builds paying "loses N military
   resource" -- so p.mil_discount really is a real, spendable per-turn pool
   in the true game, our engine already implements h_play_action's own
   ResourcesForMilitaryUnitsPerStrongerCivilization grant correctly for
   THOSE two cards, and the Build arm's own cost pre-check
   (total_paid_for_build == build_cost_for) already passes by construction
   -- so the bug had to be downstream, in real affordability, which is
   exactly where Churchill's missing synthesis lives.
5. `lost_military_resource`'s own doc comment (pre-existing, in
   replay_common.rs) had already flagged the tell: "even on lines with NO
   preceding Patriotism-style grant visible in the journal at all (a
   currently-unexplained baseline case)". That unexplained case IS
   Churchill's silent choice -- confirmed by grepping every Churchill-
   related phrase in the whole corpus (`grep -rh Churchill
   bgo-journals/journals/*.tsv`): the ONLY two phrasings that ever appear
   are "elects Winston Churchill" and "scores 3 culture."; the military
   option is never announced as its own line, corpus-wide, 0 exceptions.


THE FIX
-------
File: rust/src/replay_common.rs (single file, +171/-8 lines, one commit
"Synthesize Winston Churchill's silent military choice for
Build/Upgrade/Develop" on top of ed3ccc9 in the clone).

Added `lost_military_science(text)`, the DevelopTechnology-side twin of the
pre-existing `lost_military_resource(text)` (reads the "loses N military
science" clause, e.g. "Orange discovers Air Forces Orange loses 3 military
science; Orange loses 9 science").

Added `maybe_synthesize_churchill_military(r, actor, raw_text)`: if the
line shows evidence of a military-resource or military-science spend that
the player's CURRENT pool cannot cover, and the player's leader is Winston
Churchill with the choice not yet spent this turn, applies
`Move::Churchill{choice: Military}` (the existing, already-legal move --
legal.rs:611-614 already offered it, it was simply never called) before the
Build/Upgrade/Develop line is applied -- exactly mirroring how the
pre-existing `apply_churchill_end_turn_choice` synthesizes the Culture
choice off the "End turn ... scores 3 culture." line's glued-on prefix.

Gated on the pool being SHORT of what the line needs, not merely on the
clause's presence: a player already topped up by Wave of Nationalism/
Military Build-Up/Patriotism this same turn needs no synthesis, and
synthesizing anyway would wrongly mark `churchill_used`, breaking this
turn's own end-of-turn Culture-choice line if that is what the human
actually picked (the two choices are mutually exclusive by the rules, and
`try_apply` legality-checks the synthesized move exactly like every other
synthesized move this file inserts -- a wrong guess fails loud, not
silently).

Called at the three sites that can hit `p.mil_discount`/`p.mil_sci_discount`:
  - ActionClass::BuildBuilding | ActionClass::BuildUnit (before the
    existing want/got cost pre-check)
  - ActionClass::UpgradeUnit | ActionClass::UpgradeProduction (before
    convert_trade_food_shortfall)
  - ActionClass::DevelopTechnology (before try_apply -- this arm previously
    had NO cost check of any kind)

Also updated the stale doc comment on `lost_military_resource` that called
this shape "a currently-unexplained baseline case" -- it is now explained
and cross-referenced.

PICKUP PLAN -- biggest remaining sub-cause
--------------------------------------------
After this fix, 10 of the remaining 82 Build failures still show a "loses N
military resource" clause. 6-7 of those have "Wave of Nationalism" or
"Military Build-Up" (both `Special::ResourcesForMilitaryUnitsPerStronger
Civilization`) visible nearby in their own journal -- e.g. games 7523818,
7521837, 7522242, 7521775, 7523383, 7523438, 7521943.

Traced game 7523818 (Napoleon Bonaparte leader, round 16) all the way down:
`apply.rs`'s `h_play_action` handler for
`ResourcesForMilitaryUnitsPerStrongerCivilization` computes `n` = "number of
OTHER active players with `effects::state_stats(...).strength` strictly
greater than mine" and multiplies by the per-player-count table entry. In
this game the human's own Wave of Nationalism/Military Build-Up plays
clearly returned a nonzero grant (BGO printed "gets 6"/"gets 8"), but a
targeted debug print at that exact call site showed this binary computing
`mine=16, n=0` -- i.e. this binary believes its OWN reconstructed strength
(16) already exceeds the opponent's, when the true game required at least
one stronger civilization. This is a SEPARATE root cause from Churchill's
(it fires through `h_play_action`'s own explicit `PlayActionCard` line, not
a silently-missing move) -- most likely either the ACTING player's own
`state_stats.strength` is over-counted (Napoleon's `StrengthPerUnitType`
double-applying, or a build the true player hadn't actually made yet still
counted) or the OPPONENT's `state_stats.strength` is under-counted in this
binary's reconstruction. `effects::army_strength` requires `p.tactic` to be
set; worth checking whether the opponent's tactic assignment has drifted by
this point in a way that zeroes their army-strength contribution.

Start there: add the same one-line debug (`mine`/`n`/`per`/opponent
strengths) at apply.rs's `h_play_action`
ResourcesForMilitaryUnitsPerStrongerCivilization block, re-run just the 6-7
affected games with REPLAY_DEBUG_ALL=1, and compare both players'
`state_stats.strength` breakdown against what the journal's surrounding
lines imply (unit counts, tactics played, leader).

Remaining 36 Develop failures (down from 65, 0 of them show a "military
science" clause any more -- the Churchill sub-cause is fully cleared from
Develop) are a long tail spread across many non-unit techs (Computers x6,
Satellites x3, Engineering x3, Democracy x3, Civil Service x3, ...) with no
single dominant leader or card -- likely several independent, smaller
science-cost/discount-tracking bugs. Needs individual per-game triage, not
one shared cause; a good next split point if picked up by a fresh worker.
