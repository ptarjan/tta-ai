STEP 1: confirmed the report's CODE claims, corrected its RULES claim
------------------------------------------------------------------------
`apply_gains_block`'s `blueTokens` key (events.rs, both the `apply_gains`
copy at line ~127 and `apply_gains_block`'s own copy) does exactly what
noworker2 said: `p.blue_total = (p.blue_total as i32 + sign * n).max(0)`,
touching ONLY the abstract `blue_total` counter -- never `workers_free`,
never `yellow_bank`. This part of the report is accurate.

Where the report goes wrong: it assumes "blue token" and "population
worker" are the same pool, citing FAQ p.15's "Lose 1 population: unused
worker -> yellow bank; if none, a worker off a card" as the applicable rule,
and treating Barbarians' `QueueItem::LosePop` as the model Crime Wave should
follow. They are NOT the same pool. In this engine (state.rs:492-500) and in
the real game:
  - Population/workers are YELLOW tokens: `p.yellow_bank` (unborn
    population) / `p.workers_free` (placed, unused workers). Losing
    population (Barbarians, `Special::DecreasePopulation`) correctly costs a
    YELLOW token via `QueueItem::LosePop` -- confirmed correct, not touched
    this pass.
  - Food/resource STORAGE CAPACITY is tracked by BLUE tokens: `p.blue_total`
    (bank + cards combined, `economy::blue_used`/`blue_available` derive
    occupancy from held food/resources via denomination math, §6.4). CoL
    p.10, quoted directly from `sources/cge_code_of_laws.pdf` (via
    pdftotext -layout): "Your food and resources are represented by blue
    tokens on the mine and farm technologies you have in play. Each blue
    token represents the quantity printed at the bottom of the card."
  - Card text for Crime Wave (`sources/bga_throughtheages_material.inc.php`
    line 2175): "The 2 weakest civilizations lose 3 culture and return 1
    blue token to the box." This is unambiguously the BLUE pool, not
    population.

STEP 2: what the rulebook actually requires for losing a blue token
------------------------------------------------------------------------
CoL p.10 (same page, quoted verbatim, pdftotext -layout of
sources/cge_code_of_laws.pdf around "If you lose blue tokens"):
  "If you lose blue tokens, you return blue tokens from your blue bank to
  the box. If there are not enough in your blue bank, you also return
  tokens from mines and farms of your choice."
This matches RULES_SPEC.md line 185 (§6.5) exactly. So losing a blue token
is: (a) take from the BANK first (`blue_available`), (b) if the bank is
short, ALSO strip a token off a farm/mine card of the OWNER'S CHOICE --
which, since a token on a farm/mine card literally represents held
food/resources (CoL p.10 again), would reduce actual held food or resources,
not just the abstract total. This is a genuinely different mechanism from
"lose 1 population" (FAQ p.15) -- confirmed by CoL p.17's OWN text drawing
the yellow/blue distinction in adjacent paragraphs ("If you lose yellow
tokens, return tokens from your yellow bank to the box... If you lose blue
tokens, you return blue tokens from your blue bank...").

STEP 3: is the (b) branch ("strip from a card") ever reachable?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Census of `card_table.rs`: exactly TWO base-game cards ever print a nonzero
`blue_tokens` key inside an `EventBlock` reached through `apply_gains_block`

Instrumented `apply_gains_block`'s blue_tokens branch with a temporary

CONCLUSION: the current code -- decrementing `blue_total` directly,

PART 2: WIDENED SCOPE -- corruption/blue-token-capacity off-by-one
============================================================
Mid-pass, the coordinator reported convergent evidence from a sibling
worker: games 7522024, 7521657, 7521791 each show `economy::end_of_turn`'s
corruption calc computing `blue_available == 10` (bands to a 2-resource
charge) where the real BGO journal shows ZERO corruption at that exact
checkpoint (needs `blue_available >= 11`) -- an off-by-one in the direction
of "we think 1 more token is occupied/1 fewer available than reality".
Asked to widen from Crime Wave to every blue-token mutation site and check
the `blue_total == occupied + available` invariant.

METHOD: enumerated every non-test mutation site of `p.blue_total` in the
engine:
  - apply.rs `on_enter_play`/`on_leave_play` (lines 347/405): the reversible
    card-symbol +yellow/+blue mechanic, tech/leader/wonder enter-leave-play.
  - game.rs `on_leave_play` (line 674): the antiquation-specific duplicate
    of the same mechanic, for antiquated leaders.
  - interact.rs `gain_colony`/`lose_colony`/`Annex` handling (lines
    483/1614/1658): the SAME mechanic for territory-card colonization
    (§11.5, RULES_SPEC line 287: "Apply its PERMANENT effects... FIRST").
  - events.rs `apply_gains`/`apply_gains_block`'s two `blueTokens` branches
    (Part 1, above): the card-TEXT one-shot loss mechanic.
None of these are missing an inverse, none double-apply (`Annex` correctly
calls `lose_colony` on the victim BEFORE crediting the new owner), and the
wonder-antiquation branch (game.rs ~638) correctly does NOT touch
`blue_total` at all, relying on `blue_used`'s own re-derivation from
`p.wonder_steps` once the wonder card is discarded -- confirmed by its own
doc comment and cross-checked against `economy::blue_used`'s actual formula.

LIVE TRACE (game 7522024, `REPLAY_DEBUG=1 REPLAY_DEBUG_ALL=1 ./target/
difftest/replay /tmp/one.tsv journals 7522024`): confirmed the discrepancy
is REAL. At round 10's end-of-turn for Orange (idx=0):
  `blue_total=15 blue_used=5 corr=2` (our engine)
vs. the journal's own round-10 "End turn" line (line 237 of
7522024.tsv): "...3 food - consumption: 1 (now 7); 3 resources (now 5)" --
NO "CORRUPTION!" marker, i.e. the real game charged 0.

Traced `blue_total`'s own history backward: it was 16 (full bank) through
round 9's own end-of-turn (`blue_total=16 blue_used=3 corr=0`, matching the
journal's own no-corruption line for round 9), then dropped to 15 exactly
when Orange colonized "Vast Territory (I)" in round 8 (`gain_colony`
crediting `perm.blue_tokens = -1`, its printed permanent effect being "+3
yellow tokens & -1 blue token" -- confirmed in both card_table.rs and
`sources/bga_throughtheages_material.inc.php` line 958). This -1 is applied
by exactly the same, already-verified-correct `gain_colony` mechanic Part 1
above traces for Columbus Colonize. Walked every other Orange action
between the two checkpoints (Printing Press build, Constitutional Monarchy
develop, two Irrigation upgrades, Religion build, Good Harvest's "produce 4
food immediately, ignore corruption/consumption" event, Warriors build) --
each is individually correct against `economy::gain_food`/
`worker_capped_production`/`tokens_for`'s own formulas and CoL p.11's "gain
nearest lower achievable value" rule. `blue_used` never exceeds `blue_total`
anywhere in this trace -- there is NO internal invariant violation, the
model is fully self-consistent by its OWN rules. It simply disagrees with
BGA's ground truth by exactly one blue token's worth of capacity at this
one checkpoint.
