WARTECH -- War over Technology split-spoils pending
=====================================================

VERDICT: REPLAYER bug, not an engine/model bug. The engine already models
the split correctly. The rule genuinely permits splitting spoils between a
stolen tech card and science. FIXED (committed in the clone). Zero
regressions. 24 games flip from stuck to completed-and-score-matching,
including both named games (7522455, 7522967).

THE CHEAP CHECK (card_table.rs vs BGA source): NOT the answer this time.
War over Technology's baked constants (id 179 in
sources/bga_throughtheages_material.inc.php) are correct:
  'winner' => "Produces same amount; Special (blue) technologies can be
  stolen instead of science equal to their cost"
Our engine's card_table.rs / interact.rs already implement exactly this.
No constant was wrong.

THE RULE (established before touching code):
- docs/RULES_SPEC.md line 143: "War over Technology (II, 2 MA) | science
  points equal to advantage; may take known blue special techs instead at
  their science cost (not ones you have in hand/play; higher-level steals
  replace yours); capped by loser's science + eligible special-tech value"
- interact.rs's own doc comment (war_tech_options / war_tech_spoils) cites
  Code of Laws p.3 ("victor takes the card from the defeated civilization's
  PLAY AREA... cannot steal a special technology that is the same as one
  he or she already has in play or in hand") and FAQ p.8 ("some or all" --
  mixing steals and science IS explicitly legal, not a BGO invention).
So: NOT undecidable. The split is a real, printed rule. The victor may take
one or more stealable blue specials (paid out of the strength advantage at
each card's PRINTED cost) and convert whatever advantage remains into
science, in any order, repeatedly.

THE ENGINE SIDE WAS ALREADY RIGHT:
- interact.rs: war_tech_spoils -> offer_war_tech pushes a
  Pending::Choice(WarTech { victim, budget }) whenever the loser holds a
  stealable special tech; picking a Card re-offers with budget reduced by
  that card's cost (the re-offer loop IS the mixing mechanism); picking
  Science calls take_war_science and stops. combat.rs already had a test
  proving this: apply_war_spoils_technology_the_victor_may_mix_cards_and_
  science.
- The bug was entirely on the REPLAY side: replay_common.rs's
  resolve_intervening had ChoiceKind::WarTech grouped into a no-op
  fallthrough arm, with its own comment: "`Annex`/`WarTech` have not been
  needed by any corpus game sampled so far." That was true when written;
  it is no longer true -- 85 corpus games win a War over Technology, 36 of
  them include a real steal.

JOURNAL GRAMMAR (read directly, both named games):
7522455.tsv line 198 (WinWar confirmation, auto bookkeeping, no
  continuation):
  "Orange wins War over Technology Attacker's strength: 27; Defender's
  strength: 14; Orange takes spoils of war"
7522455.tsv line 201 (separate line, the steal pick):
  "Orange takes spoils of war Orange steals Navigation from Purple"

  "Orange takes spoils of war Orange gets 6 science; Purple loses 6 science"

7522967.tsv line 418 (confirmation), 420 (steal Masonry from Green), 421
  (gets 4 science) -- identical shape, different game.

So BGO logs ONE "takes spoils of war" line PER PICK, in the exact order
the picks were made -- which lines up 1:1 with offer_war_tech's re-offer
loop. Confirmed via grep: this "<Color> steals <Card> from <Color>" and
"<Color> gets N science; <Color> loses N science" shape appears with no
other variant anywhere in the corpus (checked all occurrences).

CORPUS-WIDE COUNT:
- 85 journal files contain a "wins War over Technology" line at all.
- 36 of those include at least one "steals" resolution line (i.e.
  genuinely mixed or all-steal spoils).
- 82 of the 85 have >=2 "takes spoils of war" occurrences per file (i.e. a
  real resolution beyond the bare WinWar confirmation) -- meaning the vast
  majority of War-over-Technology wins open a real Pending::Choice(WarTech)
  that the old replayer could never resolve.
These are all candidates for the bug (any corpus game reaching a real,
multi-option WarTech choice was structurally unable to complete before this
fix, regardless of whether the human ultimately picked all-science,
all-steal, or a mix).

THE FIX (rust/src/replay_common.rs):
1. New enum WarSpoil { Steal(CardId), Science }.
2. New Replayer field war_tech_spoils: HashMap<u8, VecDeque<WarSpoil>>, set
   post-construction by replay_game (same pattern as produces_grants /
   discard_phase_oracle -- avoids threading a new parameter through the
   ~85 positional Replayer::new test call sites).
3. parse_war_tech_steal_line / parse_war_tech_science_line: pure text
   parsers for the two journal shapes above (the confirmation-line
   "...takes spoils of war" with no continuation correctly fails to match
   either).
4. prescan_war_tech_spoils: one global pass building the per-actor FIFO in
   journal order.
5. resolve_intervening gained a real ChoiceKind::WarTech arm (pulled out of
   the no-op fallthrough, doc comment on that fallthrough updated to drop
   the stale "WarTech has not been needed" claim -- Annex is still
   genuinely unneeded and stays there): pops the front WarSpoil, resolves a
   Steal to whichever ChoiceOption::Card matches (StuckPending/ParserGap if
   it doesn't -- same failure style as every other FIFO-backed choice in
   this file), resolves Science to interact::WAR_TECH_SCIENCE_IDX, and lets
   the loop's own `continue` naturally drain a multi-pick split within one
   resolve_intervening call (each apply_move may reopen a fresh
   Pending::Choice(WarTech) via offer_war_tech's re-offer, which the same
   arm picks up again next iteration).
