ANSWER TO Q2: science drift is DOMINATED BY GROWTH, the opposite of
RESOURCES. RESDRIFT found resources' offset is a one-time missed event
that then stays perfectly flat forever once introduced (confirmed on 3
traced games, 0 counterexamples). Science is the reverse: 70% of
multi-checkpoint offset sequences keep growing in magnitude every time
that actor's turn comes up again, most never plateauing before the game
ends. This is a WRONG PER-TURN RATE / recurring mis-accounting, not a
single missed/extra event -- the single biggest structural difference
between the two defect populations found in this pass.

Growth-rate shape (of the 76 growing sequences, all consecutive-checkpoint
increments in |delta|, i.e. how much extra/missing science accrues each

leader effect that is only sometimes active) than with a flatly
mis-configured constant production-rate field (which would add the exact
same amount essentially every round).

CAUSE 1 (DOMINANT, CONFIRMED WITH A CLEAN NUMERATOR): the Scientific
Cooperation pact's PARTNER CHARGE is not implemented -- this is the exact
gap SCIDRIFT's own writeup already named on game 7523162
("science_pact_partners_can_pay does not exist in this tree"), and this
pass shows it is not a one-off, it is THE dominant mechanism behind the
whole GROWING-OVER population.

  The pact's own journal text states the rule outright when proposed, e.g.
  (7522640, line 383): "Green proposes Scientific Cooperation to Grey
  Green is A; Grey is B; Every time one of the civilizations plays a
  technology, it pays 2 science less and the other civilization pays 1
  science." Every subsequent "X discovers Y ... Z loses 1 science" line
  (Z = the OTHER pact partner, not the developer) is one of these charges.
  If the engine skips it, the OTHER partner's own science is over-counted
  by exactly 1 every time -- and because EITHER partner can trigger a
  charge to the other, BOTH partners typically end up with their own
  independent growing offset in the same game (matches 7522640's own two
  parallel growing sequences, Grey and Green, found in Step 2).

  MEASURED: of the 38 games classified GROWING-OVER (the first-diverging
  actor's own offset sequence, abs(delta) monotonically increasing, ends
  higher than it started), 38/38 (100%) had a Scientific Cooperation pact
  PROPOSED somewhere in the journal before that game's first divergence.
  Base rate check (the obvious next question -- is this just a common
  card everyone plays?): a random sample of 60 NON-diverging games (drawn
  from the 936 games outside the 74-game diverging population) found the
  phrase "Scientific Cooperation" in only 1/60 (1.7%). Also checked the
  OTHER science-diverging subgroups for comparison: FLAT-OVER games have
  the pact in only 3/13 (23%) of their whole journals, UNDER games in
  7/18 (39%). The 100% figure for GROWING-OVER against a 1.7% general
  base rate is not a coincidental correlation.
  CAVEAT: did not have budget to single-step through economy's own
  DevelopTechnology cost path to find the specific missing charge call
  (that was already SCIDRIFT's own "next lead," still open) -- this pass
  confirms the SIZE and SHAPE of the population this cause explains, not
  a line-of-code fix.

CAUSE 2 (SMALLER, CONFIRMED ON ONE CLEAN REPRO, LIKELY MORE): the WarTech

READING: OVER and UNDER are NOT the same shape, and this pass's evidence
points at (at least) two structurally different bugs, not one bug with
two signs:
  - OVER is dominated (68%) by a PERSISTENT, RECURRING, GROWING offset --
    explained for essentially all of it by the Scientific-Cooperation
    partner-charge gap (Cause 1, Step 3): a real charge that should apply
    repeatedly across the game is skipped every time it should fire.
  - UNDER is dominated (61% = 50% self-correcting-single + a chunk of the
    22% flat) by a TRANSIENT, SELF-CORRECTING blip: the engine reads a
    momentarily-wrong value at one specific checkpoint and is back in sync
    one round later, with no compounding. This looks like a TIMING
    artifact (something not fully applied yet at the instant the
    end-of-turn snapshot is taken, but applied correctly by the time the
    NEXT snapshot happens) rather than a wrong formula -- consistent with
    the general "discard-blocked-turn" timing hazard class of bug this
    project's oracle machinery was built to guard against for OTHER stats
    (culture), but evidently not fully closed for science's own
    checkpoint. CANNOT TELL from this data exactly which timing gap it is
    -- would need single-stepping state right at one of these blips (e.g.
    7522092 line 213) to confirm.
  - The WinWar/WarTech investigation (Step 3, Cause 2) is a partial
    exception to this UNDER=self-correcting story: only 1 of the 3
    UNDER-group games whose LAST action before the checkpoint was WinWar
    is an actual War over TECHNOLOGY (7522092, itself in the
    self-correcting bucket); the other 2 (7521829, 7522717) are Wars over
    TERRITORY -- ActionClass::WinWar evidently covers both war-types, so
    "last_action_class=WinWar" is not by itself evidence of the WarTech
    spoils bug. 7522717 is notably FLAT (not self-correcting) at -1 for 8
    straight checkpoints despite being a Territory war, so it is NOT
    explained by Cause 2 either -- another open, unexplained flat-UNDER
    case.
  - A genuine complication to the clean OVER/UNDER story: 6/131
    (game,actor) sequences corpus-wide cross sign at least once (Step 2),
    5 of them negative-then-positive -- so a small number of games counted
    here as "UNDER" (because their FIRST checkpoint happened to be
    negative) are, a few checkpoints later, indistinguishable from the
    GROWING-OVER population (7523162 is the clearest example, already
    flagged in Step 2). The 56/18 split is a reasonable first cut but the
    boundary is softer than the raw count implies.

CONCLUSION: science drift is NOT one bug. The two named, sized causes
found this pass (Scientific Cooperation partner-charge, WarTech spoils
transfer size) together explain roughly 39/74 (53%) of the population with
real confidence (38 growing-OVER + 1 anomaly), both DIFFERENT in shape and
size from resources' own dominant cause (corruption-deduction skip). The
remaining ~35 games (13 flat-OVER, ~18 UNDER largely self-correcting, plus
edge cases) are a genuine unresolved long tail -- per this project's own
standing rule, do not spend more time hunting a single shared cause for
them; the self-correcting UNDER blips in particular look like a
qualitatively different (timing-related) bug class from the growing-OVER
pact-charge bug and should be scoped as their own separate follow-up, not
folded into the same fix.
