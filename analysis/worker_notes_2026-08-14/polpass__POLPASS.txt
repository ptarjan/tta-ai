2. ROOT CAUSE #1 (54 games): over-eager auto_skip_politics
============================================================

`game::start_turn` used to call `auto_skip_politics(state)` (game.rs, right after opening
`Phase::Politics` for the new turn). That function read `legal::legal_moves(state)`, and if it
had exactly ONE option, set `politics_done = true` and jumped straight to `Phase::Actions`
*without ever consuming a real `Move::PolPass`*. Outside Age IV this basically never fires
(PolPass + Resign = 2 options, per `legal::politics_moves`'s own §5.11 comment: "not in Age IV").
In Age IV, where Resign is not offered, an empty military hand (or nothing usable in it) means
legal_moves().len() == 1 and this DOES fire.

RULES_SPEC.md §5.0 (not in the settled/do-not-relitigate list): "In the Politics Phase you may
perform AT MOST ONE political action (or skip)." Skipping is the player's own move, not a
condition under which the phase can silently fail to exist. BGO's own journals prove this
empirically: 54 different real human games logged an explicit "<Color> passes Political Phase"
at EXACTLY the turn `auto_skip_politics` had already closed -- the client always presents the
choice and logs the click, even when Pass is the human's only sane option.

FIX: removed `auto_skip_politics` entirely (both its direct call and the deferred
`QueueItem::AutoSkipPolitics` path used after a War-over-Technology spoils decision). The
Politics Phase now always opens (`state.phase = Phase::Politics; peek_top_event(state, idx);`)
and waits for whatever move the caller submits. For self-play/bots this changes NOTHING about
game state -- the bot's only legal move in that situation is still `PolPass`, it just now has to
submit it explicitly instead of having the engine submit it on its behalf. Removed the now-dead
`QueueItem::AutoSkipPolitics` variant (state.rs) and its match arm (interact.rs) since nothing
constructs it anymore.

Files touched: rust/src/game.rs, rust/src/state.rs, rust/src/interact.rs (doc-comment + match-arm
cleanup only in the latter two).

============================================================
3. ROOT CAUSE #2 (9 games): International Agreement's skip_next_politics
============================================================

International Agreement (CoL p.12): "The strongest civilization may immediately take civil
cards up to N civil actions BY GIVING UP its next chance to take a political action." The
engine's `events.rs` handler for `optional_take_cards_with_civil_actions` used to set
`state.players[idx].skip_next_politics = true` unconditionally the instant the event revealed --
before the player had made any choice at all. `game::start_turn` reads `skip_next_politics` and,
if set, skips straight to `Phase::Actions` on that player's own next turn (this is a SEPARATE
mechanism from auto_skip_politics -- fires even when several other real political options
exist).

The bug: a player who fully DECLINED the offer (picked `Word(Stop)` in the `TakeRow` choice
without ever taking a single card) still had their next Politics Phase forfeited. The rule text
ties the forfeit to USING the privilege ("by giving up..." describes the cost of taking cards
this way, not a tax on merely being offered the choice). Confirmed against real journals: 9
games show "<Color> declines international agreement" immediately followed, next turn, by that
same player's own perfectly legal "<Color> passes Political Phase" -- illegal against this
engine because skip_next_politics had already forced them into Phase::Actions.

FIX: moved the `skip_next_politics = true` write out of `events.rs` (reveal time) and into
`interact.rs`'s `ChoiceKind::TakeRow` handler, in the `ChoiceOption::Slot` arm -- i.e. it is now
set the first (and only) time the player actually takes a card. Declining via `Stop` without
ever taking anything leaves it false, as it always should have.

Files touched: rust/src/events.rs, rust/src/interact.rs.

Note on discovery process: my first pass mis-attributed one game (7522582) to this bug because
its journal file happens to also contain a later, unrelated "declines international agreement"
line -- the string search was game-wide, not causally scoped to the actual first failure. Its
REAL first failure (line 316, "Purple passes" with no "Political Phase" suffix) turned out to be
an unrelated auction-echo issue -- see the tail below. Caught this by re-running the bucket after
the fix and finding 7522582 still present; do not trust a whole-file grep as proof of causation,
always re-verify against the post-fix bucket.

6. HONEST REMAINING TAIL (3 games, NOT fixed, do not claim otherwise)
============================================================

    DUMP 7523633 line 235: Orange passes Political Phase
    DUMP 7522941 line 168: Purple passes Political Phase
    DUMP 7522582 line 316: Purple passes

(a) 7523633 and 7522941 (2 games): the engine trace shows `phase=Politics
    pending_top=Some(Choice(Choice{ kind: WarTech{ victim, budget }, ... }))` at the moment of
    failure -- a War-over-Technology spoils choice (§5.7/§5.8) is still open when the journal's
    own next line is the player's PolPass. In 7522941 the journal shows exactly two "takes
    spoils of war" lines (science, then a stolen tech) before the pass, so the human clearly
    considered the spoils resolved; our reconstruction still has an unclosed WarTech pending
    with no observed "science"/further-steal line to drive it shut, and legal.rs correctly
    refuses PolPass while any pending choice sits open. This looks like a real WarTech
    resolution/closing gap (possibly in how `interact.rs`'s WarTech offer decides it has nothing
    left to offer, or a budget-tracking drift), not something to force through the replayer. Not
    investigated further -- flagging honestly rather than guessing at a fix under budget
    pressure.

(b) 7522582 (1 game): the offending line is "Purple passes" with NO "Political Phase" suffix,
    logged the turn after an auction ("Vast Territory is abandoned. Orange is out of bid (no
    unit)" then "Purple passes" on the very next line). `replay_common.rs`'s `ActionClass::Pass`
    handler already special-cases this shape -- it tries `Move::BidPass` when
    `state.pending.top()` is an `Auction`, else falls back to `Move::PolPass` -- but by this
    journal line the auction has already resolved (pending is empty), so it falls through to
    PolPass and fails because (as in the fixed cases) the state is correctly already past
    Politics for other reasons. This reads as a BGO client echo/confirmation line for an already
    -settled auction decline, misclassified by the replayer's own text-based `ActionClass::Pass`
    parsing rather than an engine rule bug -- flagged, not touched, since fixing it would mean
    editing replay-side move classification under uncertainty about what the line actually means,
    and it is exactly the kind of "make the replayer tolerate an ambiguous line" the brief warns
    against without being sure of the underlying cause first.
