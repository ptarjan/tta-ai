WONDER worker -- IllegalMove: WonderStep bucket (~14 games)
Verdict: CLEAN NEGATIVE. No code change made. This bucket is not a wonder
rules bug; every game in it is blocked by an OTHER pre-existing, already
partially-documented bug outside the wonder mechanism.

METHOD

1. Cheap check first (card_table.rs vs BGA material).
   Pulled every wonder that appears in the bucket and diffed its baked
   stage-cost array and any relevant special-tech constants against
   sources/bga_throughtheages_material.inc.php:
     - Kremlin: stages [4,4,4], culture 2, happy -1, CA 1, MA 1 -- matches.
     - Internet: stages [2,3,4,3,2] -- matches BGA's 'resscost' array
       exactly.
     - First Space Flight / Great Wall / Transcontinental Railroad /
       Hanging Gardens / St. Peter's Basilica / Fast Food Chains: stage
       arrays all match BGA's own resscost arrays (spot-checked each).
     - Masonry / Architecture / Engineering (the three "Construction"
       techs that let you build multiple wonder stages in one civil
       action): card_table.rs's wonder_stages_per_action is 2 / 3 / 4
       respectively, matching BGA's card text verbatim ("You may build up
       to 2/3/4 stages of wonder in 1 civil action"). No cost discount is
       printed on any of these three cards for wonder stages (only for
       urban buildings), and costs.rs's wonder_stage_cost applies none --
       correct, RULES_SPEC.md 3.8 confirms no such discount exists.
   Constants are clean across the board. This ruled out the "baked
   constant is wrong" hypothesis (the one that has been the answer twice
   this week elsewhere) for this bucket.

2. Split the bucket before assigning a cause.
   REPLAY_DUMP_BUCKET=WonderStep ./target/difftest/replaystats ... found
   exactly 14 DUMP lines (matches the bucket's own reported count):

     First Space Flight   4 games  (7522652, 7522047, 7522762, 7521689)
     Fast Food Chains      3 games  (7522729, 7522350, 7522795)
     Internet               2 games  (7522236, 7522348)

     Transcontinental RR    1 game   (7522936)
     Hanging Gardens        1 game   (7521783)
     St. Peter's Basilica   1 game   (7522455)
     Kremlin                1 game   (7523537)

   8 different wonders across 14 games is itself a strong signal against
   a single baked-constant bug (which would cluster on one wonder) --
   confirmed by tracing every game with REPLAY_DEBUG=1 (the existing
   try_apply-fail diagnostic already in replay_common.rs prints
   wonder_stage_cost/wonder_steps/resources at the failure point).

   Result: the 14 split into exactly TWO causes, neither of them a wonder
   rules bug:

   A) 13/14 games -- a plain resource shortfall at the moment of the
      WonderStep, NOT a wonder-cost-model bug:

        game      wonder                 k  cost  our resources  short by
        7522652   First Space Flight     4  16    11              5
        7521689   First Space Flight     4  16    11              5
        7522047   First Space Flight     2  13    12              1
        7522762   First Space Flight     1   1     0              1
        7523583   Great Wall             2   4     3              1
        7522236   Internet               2   5     4              1
        7522348   Internet               2   5     0              5
        7522936   Transcontinental RR    1   3     2              1
        7521783   Hanging Gardens        1   2     1              1
        7522729   Fast Food Chains       1   4     3              1
        7522350   Fast Food Chains       1   4     2              2
        7522795   Fast Food Chains       1   4     3              1
        7523537   Kremlin                1   4     3              1

      In every one of these, legal::legal_moves correctly offers
      WonderStep for k values the player CAN afford, and correctly omits
      the k the human actually played only because our reconstructed
      p.resources is a few points below the true value. The wonder cost
      model itself (costs::wonder_stage_cost, legal.rs's stages_left/
      wonder_stages gating) is exactly right -- see costs.rs's own
      wonder_stage_cost_sums_the_next_k_printed_stages /
      wonder_stage_cost_reads_from_the_current_progress tests, both still
      passing.

      Traced one of these (7523537, Kremlin) end-to-end with
      REPLAY_DEBUG=1. Long before the wonder build, the SAME game already
      shows repeated "DEBUG end-turn science drift" and "DEBUG end-turn
      resources drift" lines every couple of rounds from round 10 onward
      (e.g. "journal says (now 12), this binary computes 8 (delta -4)" at
      round 12; "(now 13), computes 12 (delta -1)" at round 13) -- i.e.
      Orange's production/spending reconstruction has been silently
      drifting low all game, completely independent of wonders. By round
      14 the accumulated drift is exactly enough to make the true 3rd
      Kremlin stage (cost 4) unaffordable in our reconstruction (we show
      3 resources instead of 4).

      This is the SAME cross-bucket mechanism docs/REPLAY.md already
      documents as open and NOT wonder-specific: its "Pop bucket" section
      describes a multi-mechanism food/resource/science production drift
      (yellow_bank tier drift, `events::extra_production` computing one
      higher than end-of-turn production, etc.), explicitly still open
      after several passes, and explicitly warns "worth stating... a
      large share of the remaining ... games at once" and "do not
      re-chase this from the Pop bucket" for one of its sub-causes. The
      IllegalMove: Build (32 games) and IllegalMove: Upgrade (15 games)
      buckets show the identical "short by a small amount" affordability
      shape and almost certainly share this same root cause -- flagging
      here since fixing that shared root cause would likely clear WonderStep,
      Build, and Upgrade buckets together (this is exactly the situation
      the "broadcast cross-bucket causes" playbook describes: a shared
      cause behind several buckets' symptoms, not 13 separate wonder
      bugs). I did not attempt to fix it: it's explicitly out of scope
      for this bucket, already flagged in docs/REPLAY.md as a deep,
      still-open, multi-pass investigation, and not a wonder rule at all.

   B) 1/14 games -- a stuck pending Choice, not a resource problem at all:

        7522455, St. Peter's Basilica. REPLAY_DEBUG=1 shows the failure
        point has phase=Politics with pending_top=Choice(WarTech{
        victim:1, budget:13}) still open, so legal_moves is just
        [Choose{0}, Choose{1}] -- the WonderStep move is never even
        offered because an EARLIER decision was never resolved.

        The journal explains why: at line 198 "Orange wins War over
        Technology ... Orange takes spoils of war" is followed by TWO
        separate resolution lines (204/205 by original numbering):
          "Orange takes spoils of war Orange steals Navigation from
           Purple"
          "Orange takes spoils of war Orange gets 6 science; Purple
           loses 6 science"
        i.e. the human split a single War over Technology's spoils
        budget between stealing ONE blue tech card AND converting the
        remainder to science, as two separate takes. Our engine's
        Pending::Choice(WarTech) modeling (the `Choice { player, kind:
        WarTech{...}, options: OptionList{...} }` seen in the debug dump)
        appears to only support taking ONE option, not a split
        card-then-remainder-science sequence, so the choice is never
        resolved and everything after it (including the wonder build 6
        journal lines later) is blocked.
        This is a War/combat spoils-of-war Choice bug, not a wonder bug
        -- it just happens to manifest here because the blocked pending
        choice sits between the war and this player's next wonder build.
        Left for whoever owns the War/Combat or Choice-resolution bucket.

3. No fix landed for this bucket. Both root causes live outside the
   wonder mechanism (a general resource/science production drift, and a
   WarTech multi-take Choice gap), and RULES_SPEC.md/BGA data confirm the
   wonder cost model and multi-stage-per-CA gating (Masonry/Architecture/
   Engineering) are already correct.
