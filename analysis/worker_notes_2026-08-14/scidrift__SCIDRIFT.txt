STEP 0: DOES THE ORACLE EVEN EXIST -- YES
===========================================================================
Found the existing culture-oracle machinery first (`replay_common.rs`'s
`CultureOracleDivergence`, `record_culture_check`, `flush_pending_
culture_check`, keyed off `trailing_now_culture` parsing an `"End turn ...
N culture (now M) ..."` clause). Grepped the raw corpus and confirmed BGO
prints SCIENCE and RESOURCES on the exact same "End turn ... scores:" line,
same running-total grammar, right next to culture:

  End turn Grey scores:; ; 2 culture (now 26); 3 science (now 6); 0 food -
  consumption: 2 (now 0); 6 resources (now 16)

A `trailing_now_science` helper (and its `trailing_now_resources` twin)
ALREADY existed in `replay_common.rs` -- but only as `REPLAY_DEBUG`-gated
eyeball tooling for a different investigation (chasing `IllegalMove:
Develop`/`Build` science/resource-shortfall buckets one spend at a time),
explicitly NOT the structural, snapshot-based, discard-aware checkpoint
culture has. That distinction matters: the naive `REPLAY_DEBUG` science
check reads the LIVE `state.players[actor].science` the instant an "End
turn" line is dispatched, with no defer-on-open-discard logic, and it fires
"drift" on nearly every single End Turn in some games (confirmed on
7523162 itself) -- almost all of it false positives from exactly the
discard-blocked-turn timing hazard `CultureOracleDivergence`'s own doc
already named and solved for culture. Building a naive reuse of that
existing helper would have produced a flood of noise, not a real oracle.
So: buildable, and worth building properly (mirroring culture's full
snapshot+defer shape), not as a shortcut.

OVERLAP (the coordinator's specific ask -- same games/same sign, or
disjoint?):
  science-diverging games: 70. resource-diverging games: 312.
  Games in BOTH sets: 35 (50% of science's 70; only 11% of resources' 312).
  Science-only: 35. Resource-only: 277.
  Of the 35 overlapping games:
    - same sign (both over- or both under-counting): 27/35 (77%)
    - same ACTOR at the first divergence checkpoint: 11/35 (31%)
    - which fires first in journal order: science first in 19, resources
      first in 15, tied (same line) in 1
  READING: this is neither "one bug" nor "two fully disjoint bugs." Most

  counters simultaneously" as the shared mechanism -- more likely a shared
  KIND of bug (e.g. a production-recompute or corruption interaction) that
  independently mis-fires on each resource type on its own schedule.

Manual reconstruction of Grey's science through round 9, using ONLY
journal-stated amounts and the baseline engine's own (unimplemented-
partner-charge) behaviour:
  round 8 End Turn (line 264): baseline agreed exactly at 8 (no earlier
    divergence flagged for this game).
  line 268 (round 9): Orange develops Riflemen under the just-accepted
    Scientific Cooperation pact -- journal charges Grey 1 science as the
    partner. The baseline engine does NOT implement this charge at all
    (PACTS's own confirmed gap), so the ENGINE's own science stays at 8
    here (journal's true value would be 7, but that is not what the
    engine computes).
  line 292 (round 9): Grey develops (peacefully) Constitutional Monarchy
    -- journal states "4 science points spent; Grey loses 4 science."
    The engine's own DevelopTechnology cost path applies this deduction
    (validated: the replay proceeds past this line without a Mismatch, so
    the engine's own computed cost agrees with the journal's stated 4).
    Engine's own running total: 8 - 4 = 4.
  round 9 production (folded into the line-296 "End turn" clause): journal
    labels this turn's science production rate "3" (`trailing_now_
    science`'s own doc: the leading number in "N science (now M)" is a
    RATE label, not necessarily the exact delta applied). Expected engine
    total after production, given the engine's own pre-production baseline
    of 4: 4 + (whatever the engine's own production step actually adds).

The oracle's own snapshot says the ENGINE's post-production total is 5,
not the naively-expected 7 (4 + 3). That means the engine's OWN production
step for Grey, round 9, added only 1 science, not 3 -- a 2-point
PRODUCTION shortfall, not a mystery post-hoc subtraction happening after
the checkpoint. This is a materially different (and more specific) claim
than PACTS's "silent drop between line 296 and 301": the anomaly is
INSIDE round 9's own end-of-turn production step, coincident with (but not
yet proven caused by) Grey's own government change taking effect that
same turn (Constitutional Monarchy, elected via `ElectLeader`-adjacent
`ChangeGovernment` at line 292, in the same turn whose production this
oracle flags).
