the 131 measures a DIFFERENT, coarser-grained symptom (checkpoint-to-
checkpoint drift) than the 50's (mid-turn drift). CONCLUSION FOR THE NEXT

either a same-turn ledger check or per-action instrumentation, not another
look at this 131 list.

divergence of ANY player, not the specific player who later fails the
Build, so several of these 11 pairings may be coincidental (the oracle's
"resources" tag firing on a bystander whose drift has nothing to do with
the actor who runs out of resources rounds later).

3. HAND TRACE -- two games, same underlying mechanism, one important caveat
===========================================================================

--- 7521791 (delta -2, round 8, line 197, actor Green) ---
Failing Build: line 227 (round 9) -- Green tries to Build Religion (cost 3),
reconstruction has `resources=1` (`DEBUG try_apply fail` dump). Journal-
implied resources at that point = 1 (our value) + 2 (the round-8 drift, if
it persisted uncorrected) = 3 -- EXACTLY the build cost. This is the one
game where the -2 drift cleanly, arithmetically explains the later Build
failure.

Traced the -2 to its source with `REPLAY_DEBUG_ALL=1 ./target/difftest/
replay ... 7521791`:
  - `economy.rs:676` (`end_of_turn`, step 3b corruption): at Green's round-8
    End Turn, `corr = corruption(blue_available(p))`. Reconstruction:
    `blue_total=16, blue_used=6 -> blue_available=10 -> corr=2`
    (`economy.rs:106-116`, band 6-10 -> corruption 2). The journal's own
    round-8 End Turn line for Green has NO "CORRUPTION!" clause at all (cf.
    Purple's own round-8 line one turn earlier: "CORRUPTION! Purple loses 2
    resources", printed explicitly when it applies) -- the human's real
    corruption was 0 this turn, i.e. their true `blue_available` was >= 11.
  - `blue_used(p)` (`economy.rs:430`) sums blue tokens covering CURRENT
    stock: `food_tokens(p.food) + mine_tokens(p.resources) + wonder_steps`.
    At this checkpoint reconstruction has `p.food=3`; hand-traced backward
    (`REPLAY_DEBUG_ALL`, journal line 166 "Green increases population Green
    spends 4 food") and found the true food entering the corruption step
    should be 2 LOWER than what we hold, tracing to a single IncreasePopulation
    action at round 7 (journal line 166): the reconstruction pays only 2
    food for that action (`economy.rs::pop_food_cost`, `pop_cost_base(13)=3`
    minus Moses's `PopIncreaseFoodDiscount(1)` = 2), but the journal states
    the human paid 4.
  - VERIFIED our formula is rules-correct on its own terms: `pop_cost_base`
    (`economy.rs:47-62`) matches `docs/RULES_SPEC.md` section 6.1's table
    exactly (18-17:2, 16-13:3, 12-9:4, 8-5:5, 4-1:7); Moses's
    `PopIncreaseFoodDiscount(1)` (`card_table.rs:1622`) matches the VASSAL
    module's own card text ("increase their population for 1 Food less",
    `sources/vassal_NewTTA_2.49_buildFile.xml`). Hand-counted every one of
    Green's PRIOR "increases population" actions (round 3, round 4 x2,
    round 5 x2 -- 5 total) against a from-scratch yellow-bank simulation
    (start 18, -1 token/action, Moses discount -1 food) and every one of
    those five costs matches the journal EXACTLY (1,1,2,2,2). The sixth
    action (round 7, line 166) is where it breaks: our formula, fed our own
    `p.yellow_bank=13`, computes cost 2; the journal says 4, which under the
    SAME formula implies the human's true yellow_bank was in the 8-5 band
    (base 5, minus Moses's 1 = 4) -- i.e. TRUE population was already several
    tokens ahead of what this binary's own population/yellow_bank tracking
    believes, despite the first five actions agreeing exactly. Ruled out:
    wrong band table (matches RULES_SPEC verbatim), wrong Moses value
    (matches source data verbatim), a missed 6th "increases population" line
    for Green between rounds 5 and 7 (grepped the full journal range,
    lines 108-166, for every "population"/"immigrant" mention -- none).
  - CONCLUSION: this is NOT a resources bug, a corruption-formula bug, or a
    pop-cost-formula bug -- all three are independently confirmed
    rules-correct. It is a POPULATION (`p.yellow_bank`) tracking divergence
    that already existed by round 7, invisible because nothing
    cross-validates population against the journal (see `drift__DRIFT.txt`'s
    own exclusion list: population was excluded from that pass's oracle set
    as "not independently comparable" -- true for a bare population COUNT,
    but every "increases population" line's stated food cost, and every
    End-Turn line's stated consumption, is ITSELF an independent, exact
    read of the true yellow-bank band, unexploited by any existing check).
    The 2-resource corruption overcharge two rounds later is a downstream
    symptom of that earlier, currently-invisible drift, not a local bug to
    patch at the corruption site.

--- 7522890 (delta +2 as globally recorded, but see below) ---
Globally-recorded first "resources" divergence: actor Grey (seat 3), round 6,
"CORRUPTION! Grey loses 2 resources" in the journal but reconstruction pays
NO corruption that turn (opposite-direction bug: reconstruction thinks
`blue_available>=11` when the journal says otherwise) -- again a corruption-
band boundary miss, not investigated further since Grey is not even the
player who fails to Build. The player who actually fails ("IllegalMove:
Build { card: Knights }", actor Green/seat 2) has its OWN, LATER, separately-
guarded `end-turn resources drift` print (`REPLAY_DEBUG=1`): "actor=2 journal
says (now 8), this binary computes 7 (delta -1)" -- the shortfall-explaining
direction, occurring well before the failing Build. IMPORTANT METHODOLOGICAL
CAVEAT for whoever resumes this: `maybe_record_drift`'s "first-wins, never
overwritten" convention (`replay_common.rs:2449`) records the first
divergence of ANY actor in the game, not the first divergence of the SPECIFIC
actor who later fails the Build. In a 3-4 player game, a bystander's drift
(here, Grey's) can win the race and get tagged into the corpus-wide 131/50
tables, burying the failing actor's own, more relevant drift (here, Green's)
one line down. This means the already-weak 11/50 overlap in section 1 is
itself an OVERESTIMATE of how many of the 50 have a truly relevant resources
divergence recorded against them, and an UNDERESTIMATE of how many of the
other 39 might have one recorded against the RIGHT actor if the oracle were
per-actor instead of per-game. Left unquantified this pass (would need
rerunning the oracle keyed by (game, actor) instead of by game alone).
