============================================================
STEP 1: the rule, quoted from the primary source
============================================================

sources/cge_code_of_laws.pdf, p.6, "Production Phase" / "Resource
Production" (2015 base game, End-of-Turn Sequence, printed on the player
board):

  "Resource Production
   - For each worker on your mine [pick] technology cards, move one blue
     token from the blue bank to that card.
     - If there are not enough tokens, move all that you can to the mine
       technology cards, beginning with those of the highest level."

"Food Production" (same page, step c, immediately above) is the identical
rule for farm cards: "For each worker on your farm [pick] technology cards,
move one blue token from the blue bank to that card... beginning with those
of the highest level."

This is WORKER-COUNT-based: the number of NEW tokens the bank supplies this
step equals the number of producing workers (capped by whatever the bank
actually has, prioritizing the highest-level cards when short). It is a
DIFFERENT rule from CoL p.11's general "gain N food/resources" (RULES_SPEC
6.4, "Blue-token economics"): "move tokens from bank to cards, total exactly
the amount (if the bank lacks tokens, gain the nearest lower achievable
value)". That p.11 rule IS value/denomination-based (minimal-token "making
change"), and is what card effects, tactics, colonies, and leader/wonder
flat bonuses use when they say "gain N food/resources" outside the
end-of-turn Production Phase.

The two rules coincide only when every producing card is denomination 1
(one un-upgraded token = one unit of value either way). They diverge the
moment a card is upgraded past level 1 with several workers on it, or when
producing cards of different levels compete for a short bank -- see STEP 2.

STEP 2: the mechanism as coded, and where it was wrong
============================================================

rust/src/economy.rs, `end_of_turn` steps 3c/3e (before this patch) called:

    gain_food(p, s.food as u16);       // 3c
    gain_resources(p, s.resources as u16);  // 3e

`gain_food`/`gain_resources` are the CORRECT implementation of CoL p.11's
general rule: they search for the largest amount reachable via `tokens_for`
(a greedy MINIMAL-representation over the player's owned farm/mine
denominations), capped by `blue_available`. Using this SAME function for the
Production Phase step conflates "how much value can this scalar total grow
to, given the bank" with "how many workers can this turn actually place a
token", which are different questions whenever a producing card's level > 1.

Confirmed by direct comparison (both models computed side-by-side against
the real corpus, via a temporary instrumented build -- not the shipped
patch): 9 of 1011 games hit a real numeric DIVERGENCE between the two models
during the Production Phase (`ECONCAP_PROBE` instrumentation, since removed;
not in the patch). Two were hand-traced in full:

--- Game 7521689 (Bill Gates), the game wstep found: NO-OP for this fix ---

Orange's mine tableau never leaves level 1 (only Bronze, denomination 1, is
ever staffed) for the entire game. `mine_denoms=[1]` throughout every
end_of_turn dump (REPLAY_DEBUG_ALL=1 REPLAY_DEBUG_TECHS=1). At
denomination 1, the value-based and worker-count-based models are
IDENTICAL by construction (one token == one unit of value either way), so
this patch changes NOTHING for this player's mine production. All of this
player's non-trivial resource income comes from Bill Gates'
`ResourcesPerLabEqualToLevel` special (labs, not mine technology cards --
CoL p.6's literal text is "workers on your mine technology cards", so this
bonus is correctly left on the general CoL-p.11 "gain" path, unchanged by
this patch, since a lab is not a mine). The residual gap wstep found (full
uncap still 2 short) is therefore a DIFFERENT, still-unidentified bug --
this patch does not touch it and does not claim to. Re-ran the replay after
the fix landed: game 7521689's failure point and legal-move list at line 358
(round 18, WonderStep on First Space Flight) are BYTE-IDENTICAL to before
the fix (diffed the two REPLAY_DEBUG_ALL traces).

============================================================
STEP 3: the fix
============================================================

File: rust/src/economy.rs only.

1. `production_denom(kind, prod) -> i16`: extracted from `Denoms::of`'s
   inline match (a farm's denomination is its printed `food`, a mine's is
   its `resources`, 0 otherwise) so `Denoms::of` and the new function below
   share one exhaustive match over `CardType` -- clippy denies `_ =>`
   wildcards, and a second copy of this match would be a second place to
   forget a new card type.
2. `worker_capped_production(techs, kind, free) -> u16`: the actual CoL p.6
   model. Builds (denomination, summed worker count) pairs for every
   Farm/Mine-kind card WITH workers, sorted by denomination descending
   ("beginning with those of the highest level"), then greedily hands out
   at most `free` tokens, one per worker, to the highest-level card(s)
   first. Returns the resulting value. A worker with no free token gets
   none; a card with more workers than remaining free tokens gets partial
   coverage; a level's workers pool if (hypothetically) two cards printed
   the same level, though the base game never has two simultaneous
   farm/mine cards at one level.
3. `end_of_turn` steps 3c/3e: each now computes `full` (worker_capped
   production with `free=u16::MAX`, i.e. the farm/mine cards' own uncapped
   worker production) and `got` (the same call with the REAL
   `blue_available(p)`), adds `got` directly to `p.food`/`p.resources`, then
   routes whatever `s.food`/`s.resources` carries BEYOND `full` (leader/
   wonder/government/special flat bonuses -- Bill Gates'
   `ResourcesPerLabEqualToLevel`, `DoubleBestMine`, etc. -- none of which are
   literally "a worker on a farm/mine technology card") through the
   UNCHANGED `gain_food`/`gain_resources` (CoL p.11's general rule),
   spending whatever tokens the farm/mine-card production above didn't use.
   `gain_food`/`gain_resources` themselves are UNTOUCHED and still used
   as-is by every other caller (apply.rs, events.rs, interact.rs -- card
   effects, tactics, colonies, government/leader/wonder flat gains), which
   is correct: CoL p.11's "gain N food/resources" really is value-based.

Unit tests added (economy.rs's existing `#[cfg(test)] mod tests`):
  - worker_capped_production_is_bounded_by_worker_count_not_by_free_tokens
  - worker_capped_production_services_the_highest_level_card_first_when_short
  - end_of_turn_resource_production_caps_by_worker_count_not_the_minimal_token_representation
    (the exact 7521775 scenario above: resources 29 -> 25, not the
    old model's 26)

Confirmed RED: reverted both `end_of_turn` call sites to plain
`gain_food(p, s.food as u16)` / `gain_resources(p, s.resources as u16)`
(saved the fixed file first, restored after) and reran
`cargo test --profile fasttest --lib economy::` -- the integration test
failed exactly as expected (left=26 old-model actual, right=25 expected);
the two direct `worker_capped_production` tests stayed green since they
exercise the function directly, not the (reverted) call sites -- correctly
showing the FUNCTION was never broken, only its wiring. Restored the fix,
reran -- all 3 new tests green, 60/60 in the `economy::` module.

============================================================
RULED OUT
============================================================

- Removing the cap entirely ("uncap"): explicitly ruled out per the task
  brief and confirmed independently -- blue tokens are a finite, tracked
  pool (16-17 total per player in the traced games) that legitimately bounds
  production; CoL p.6's own text says "if not enough tokens, move all you
  can", i.e. a hard cap is correct, just the WRONG kind of cap was coded.
- Extending the worker-count rule to leader/wonder/government/special flat
  bonuses (Bill Gates' lab bonus, DoubleBestMine, etc.): NOT done. CoL p.6's
  text is specifically "a worker on your mine/farm technology card"; a lab
  is not a mine card, and DoubleBestMine's bonus is not tied to any
  particular worker at all (it is a flat re-add of the best mine's own
  printed value, unscaled by workers). Both stay on the pre-existing CoL
  p.11 "gain" path, unchanged by this patch. This IS the open question
  wstep flagged ("if it even applies to a leader-ability bonus... itself
  unverified") -- still unverified, deliberately left alone rather than
  guessed at, since BGA's own PHP game-logic source is not present in
  sources/ (only card TEXT, in bga_throughtheages_material.inc.php) to
  cross-check against, and getting this wrong risks a corpus-wide
  regression in a hot path every game runs through every turn.
- Rewriting `blue_used`/`blue_available`'s underlying "minimal-token
  representation" model (economy.rs's own long-standing documented
  simplification: token counts are DERIVED from the scalar food/resources
  total via `tokens_for`, not tracked as physical per-card placements): NOT
  done, and NOT this bug. That simplification is a foundational,
  pre-existing architecture choice (state.rs has no per-card token-slot
  field) used consistently for corruption, `pay_resources`, and both the old
  and new production models alike; redesigning it into genuine per-card
  token placement is a much larger project than this single-rule fix and
  was never in scope here.
