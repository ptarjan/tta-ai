=== CAUSAL DRILL-DOWN: Bill Gates / ResourcesPerLabEqualToLevel ===

I read journal context around the checkpoint for 3 of the 25 round16/17-delta=-2
repro games: 7521657 (Purple, TakeCard-adjacent, round16), 7523101 (Purple,
DevelopTechnology-adjacent, round16), 7523624 (Orange, WinWar-adjacent, round17).

All three had the leader "Bill Gates" elected by the diverging actor before the
checkpoint (7521657 round15, 7523101 round14, 7523624 earlier). Internal journal
arithmetic (previous "now" + this round's stated production - stated spends) is
self-consistent in all three -- the BGO-reported total is not itself weird, the
engine is just short by 2.

Bill Gates' rules text (confirmed from rust/src/effects.rs and card_table.rs, NOT
guessed): while in play, "every lab produces resources equal to its level" --
implemented as Special::ResourcesPerLabEqualToLevel in
rust/src/effects.rs (apply_special, ~line 1036):

    ResourcesPerLabEqualToLevel => {
        for (id, slot) in p.techs.iter() {
            if id.kind() == CardType::Lab {
                stats.resources += id.level() as i32 * slot.workers as i32;
            }
        }
    }

Given this pattern held for all 3 read-in-detail repros, I tested it quantitatively
against the full 124-game Group C set: for every diverging (game, actor, checkpoint
lineno) triple, I scanned the journal up to that line for "<actor> elects Bill
Gates" / "Bill Gates dies" to determine whether Bill Gates was the diverging
player's OWN leader and still alive at the checkpoint (script embedded in the
tool-call history; logic: track elected=True on "<actor> elects Bill Gates", reset
to False on "Bill Gates dies" seen after election).

Results (all counts are exact id-set counts, not means):
  - Group C overall: 38/124 (30.6%) have Bill Gates in play at the checkpoint.
  - Of the 41 delta=-2 games: 31/41 (75.6%) have Bill Gates in play.
  - Of the 38 Bill-Gates-in-play games: 31/38 (81.6%) have delta EXACTLY -2.
    (Other 7: -12 x1, -4 x2, -1 x1, +2 x2, +3 x1 -- so Bill-Gates-in-play is not a
    100% predictor of exactly -2, but -2 is overwhelmingly the modal outcome when
    he's in play.)
  - Of the 45 round-16/17 games: 29/45 (64.4%) have Bill Gates in play.
  - The intersection round-16/17 AND delta=-2 AND Bill-Gates-in-play = 23/25 (92%)
    -- i.e. the round-16/17 delta=-2 cell is almost entirely the Bill Gates
    population; it is not a separate "era transition" effect. The round clustering
    is very plausibly just WHEN Bill Gates tends to be elected/producing in these
    Emperor-level 4-player games (age III, mid-late game), not a round-boundary
    recompute bug. I did not find an age-transition (III->IV) at the checkpoint in
    2 of 3 hand-read repros, which argues against the "era-transition recompute"
    theory from the prior pass.

Baseline check (to rule out "Bill Gates is just a popular leader" confound): across
ALL 1011 games (not just Group C) 461/1011 (45.6%) mention "Bill Gates" at all;
within Group C, 78/124 (62.9%) mention him anywhere in the journal. Both of these
are much looser tests (mention anywhere vs. "elected by the diverging actor and
alive at the exact checkpoint line") -- the tight test (38/124, 30.6%) is already
lower than the loose Group-C rate, which is expected since most Bill-Gates games do
NOT diverge at all; the point of the tight test is the 81.6% hit-rate for
delta=-2 conditional on him being in play, which is far above any of these base
rates and is the real signal.

WHAT THIS IS: 31/124 (25%) of Group C consists of games where the diverging
player has Bill Gates as leader and the divergence is exactly delta=-2. This is my
LARGEST identified sub-cluster and by a comfortable margin (next largest
attributable grouping I found was the ambiguous +1/+2 tail, not a mechanism).

WHAT I DID NOT ESTABLISH (mechanism-level): I did NOT pin down the exact code path
that causes ResourcesPerLabEqualToLevel to under-count by exactly 2. Candidate
sub-hypotheses I did not get to test against the corpus:
  - a timing bug where a lab's workers/level used by apply_special is read before
    a same-turn lab build/upgrade/worker-assignment lands (so it uses last turn's
    lab state) -- consistent with "always short by the SAME small amount" if the
    typical miss is one level x one or two workers, but I did not confirm a lab
    build/upgrade actually happened on the diverging turn in my 3 read repros (in
    7521657 and 7523101 no explicit lab-affecting action appears in that turn's
    logged lines, which argues AGAINST a same-turn-edit race and FOR something
    structural, e.g. Bill Gates's bonus being computed against a stale/partial view
    of p.techs, or one specific lab card being silently excluded from the
    CardType::Lab match).
  - whether the -2 is literally "one level-2 lab, 1 worker, double-counted-out" or
    "one level-1 lab with 2 workers" or "two level-1 single-worker labs, one
    dropped" -- I did not cross-reference the repro games' actual lab tableau
    (level + worker count per lab) against the -2 magnitude to distinguish these.
  - I did not check whether the 7 Bill-Gates-in-play games with delta != -2 (the
    -12/-4/-4/-1/+2/+2/+3 outliers) share a distinguishing feature (e.g. multiple
    labs, a second leader-adjacent card) that would explain the different
    magnitude/sign -- if so it may be the SAME bug scaling with lab count/level
    rather than a second bug.
  - I have NOT looked at effects.rs's call sites closely enough to say whether
    apply_special(ResourcesPerLabEqualToLevel) is even reachable at the right point
    in the per-turn recompute vs. some other snapshot; I only confirmed the
    match-arm's arithmetic reads current p.techs, which looks correct in isolation.
  - This is circumstantial/statistical, not a reproduced-in-debugger root cause. A
    fix-oriented follow-up worker should NOT skip straight to "this line is the
    bug" -- confirm by instrumenting one repro (e.g. 7521657) and comparing engine's
    computed Bill Gates contribution against what the lab tableau implies by hand.

=== WHAT I DID NOT ESTABLISH (population-level) ===

  - I did NOT run the same leader/card enrichment test (elected-by-actor-and-alive-
    at-checkpoint) for any leader OTHER than Bill Gates against the +1/+2 remainder.
    It is very possible another leader with a per-turn special (Sid Meier
    CulturePerLabEqualToLevel's resource-analog, Leonardo/Newton/Einstein
    SciencePerBestLabOrLibraryLevel, DoubleBestMine, etc.) explains a chunk of the
    +1/+2 tail the same way Bill Gates explains the -2 cluster. This is the most
    promising next step and I ran out of budget before trying it.
  - I did not check whether last_action_class enrichment (TakeCard is 42/124 = 34%
    of ALL of Group C, and also the modal class inside the round16/17 delta=-2
    cluster at 10/25 = 40%) is causal or just reflects TakeCard being the most
    common last-logged action before an End Turn generally. I believe it is the
    latter (not causal) but did not rule it out rigorously.
  - I did not check 2-player vs 3-player vs 4-player game mix for enrichment.
  - I did not check whether the -4 and -12 outliers among the Bill-Gates-in-play
    set are actually 2x and 6x repeats of the same -2-per-lab bug (i.e. players
    with 2 or 6 labs), which would mean the "cause" count is really the SAME single
    bug and the true blast radius could be larger than 31 if I mis-scored some
    non--2 Bill-Gates games as a different bug rather than the same bug with a
    bigger lab tableau. Worth 10 minutes of follow-up: pull the lab tableau
    (level, worker count) for those 7 outliers.
