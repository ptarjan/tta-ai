THE FIX ATTEMPTED, AND WHY IT IS UNSOUND (measured, not guessed)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Implemented `Replayer::force_additional_discards(actor, n)` (mirroring
`repay_military_hand_deficit`'s own protected-victim search: never a card
`DiscardSolver::needed_after` proves is played by name later, non-Event/

arm whenever `parse_discard_count_line(raw_text)`'s own observed count
exceeds how many cards `resolve_discard` actually removed. Unit-tested
compile-clean, `cargo clippy` clean.

Measured on the full 1011-game corpus (`SCOREDIV_DUMP_COMPLETED=1
./target/difftest/replaystats /Users/pt/tta-ai/sources/bgo/index.tsv
/private/tmp/bgo-journals/journals`):

    comm -13 (wins): 1 game.

Root cause of the regression, hand-traced on the narrowed variant's own
counter-example (game `7523818`, round 8, Orange): this binary's own
`hand_military` was EXACTLY at the limit (3 cards, limit 3, excess 0) --
`interact::discard_excess_military` correctly opened NO pending at all, by
this binary's own (apparently accurate) reconstruction. The journal's own
announcement AND resolution AGREE with each other (`"Discard Phase 1
military card must be discarded"` then `"Orange discards 1 card"`, a
TRUSTED `discard_phase_oracle` entry, not a mismatched one like 7522640's
round 4) -- yet this binary's own excess was still 0. This is the MIRROR
IMAGE of the 7522640 shape: here this binary's hand is already SHORT one
card from some earlier, untraced upstream gap (a missing draw or gain, nine
rounds back), not carrying a phantom EXCESS card. Forcing an extra discard
in this case removes a real card that was never surplus, actively
DEEPENING the shortfall and corrupting downstream legality (the game stops
two lines later: `StuckPending("decider 1 != expected actor 0, phase
Actions, no pending")`).

The deciding fact: "this binary's own reconstructed discard count is
smaller than the journal's own observed count" is STRUCTURALLY AMBIGUOUS
between two opposite root causes -- (a) this binary is carrying a phantom
EXCESS card and should discard more (the 7522640 shape), or (b) this binary
is already SHORT a card from an unrelated earlier gap and the journal's
discard is confirming a debt that was never phantom to begin with (the
7523818 shape) -- and no purely LOCAL, per-line signal available at the
`Discard` dispatch site (hand contents, this round's own before/after
delta, whether the announcement was itself trusted) reliably distinguishes
them: shape (b) outnumbers shape (a) by roughly 456:1 in the corpus, going
by the regression-to-win ratio of the narrowed attempt.

RULED OUT

- Checkpoint-TIMING artifact for the 7522640 repro: ruled out first, per
  the task's own named risk -- the game completes end-to-end with an exact
  score match, and the 1-card gap is a real, permanent `hand_military`
  COUNT divergence that survives unchanged from round 4 through round 6,

  site: ruled out by an exhaustive read of every remaining direct
  `apply::apply` call site in `replay_common.rs` (see MECHANISM 1 above) --
  none can grow `hand_military`.
- "Force the discard shortfall, unconditionally": measured, 0/1011
  completed. Catastrophic, reverted.
- "Force the discard shortfall, but only when the engine's own pending
  already drained something (`engine_discarded > 0`)": measured, 196/1011
  completed, 456 regressions to 1 win. Still catastrophic, reverted.
- Did NOT attempt restricting the fix to ONLY rounds where
  `prescan_discard_phase_oracle` itself dropped the entry (announced !=
  actual, like 7522640's round 4, excluding 7523818's round 8 which WAS a
  trusted, agreeing entry) -- plausible as a narrower, safer guard in
  principle, but given the regression:win ratio already measured (456:1)
  even under the closest analogue guard, and that this would still not
  address the STRUCTURAL ambiguity (an untrusted announcement can ALSO
  reflect this binary being short, not carrying an excess -- nothing rules
  that out for the 7522640 case itself; see HANDOFF below), did not spend
  further budget chasing a guard with no principled reason to expect a
  better ratio than the one already measured.

HANDOFF
-------
1. The dominant failure mode discovered here (this binary's own
   `hand_military` already SHORT a card from an untraced EARLIER gap,
   which then legitimately fails to trigger a discard pending the real
   player's own bigger hand required) is the SAME open question
   `decider3__DECIDER3.txt` already flagged and could not close within its
   own budget for game `7523376`'s round-9 deficit ("is this deficit
   genuine, or a symptom of an earlier rounds-1-to-9 tracking gap").
   `7523818`'s round-8 shortfall (traced this session) is a SECOND,
   independent instance of the identical open question. A fix for either
   the Draw or Discard bucket's dominant "+1" shape most likely needs to
   chase THIS thread -- an earlier, untraced draw/gain undercount -- rather
   than anything at the discard-resolution call site itself.
2. Even the 7522640 repro (this session's own "safe-looking" case) is NOT
   independently confirmed to be a genuine phantom-excess case rather than
   ALSO an earlier-shortfall case that merely happens to not manifest until
   round 4's discard -- this session did not hand-trace Orange's hand back
   to round 1 for `7522640` to rule that out (the same caution
   `decider3__DECIDER3.txt`'s own NEXT STEPS #1 already recommended for its
   own repro). Given the corpus-wide measurement already shows the
   opposite shape dominates by ~456:1, this was not pursued further, but a
   future session chasing this bucket should not assume 7522640's round 4
   is safe ground without first closing that question.
3. A structurally different approach -- rather than reacting locally at
   the `Discard` dispatch site -- would need to distinguish "phantom
   excess" from "earlier shortfall" using INFORMATION FROM EARLIER IN THE
   GAME (e.g. a full ledger-vs-simulator cross-check at every draw/gain
   checkpoint, not just the existing discard-phase oracle's own coarse
   per-round excess), which is a materially larger undertaking than this
   session's budget allowed and was not attempted.
