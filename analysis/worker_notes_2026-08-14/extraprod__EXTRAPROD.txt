STEP 3: implementation measured (round-start snapshot)
====================================================================

Implemented exactly the previous worker's proposed rule: priced ONLY the
corruption step's blue-token math against each player's resources/food AS
OF THE START OF THE CURRENT ROUND, instead of their live totals (every
other step of `extra_production` -- paying the corruption, gaining food,
consumption, gaining resources -- stayed on the real live `PlayerState`,
unchanged).

Added (all reverted, see CLEANUP):
  - `GameState::round_start_resources`/`round_start_food: [u16; MAX_PLAYERS]`
    (state.rs), captured for EVERY player at once in `game::advance_turn`
    right when `state.round` wraps to a new round (before anyone in the new
    round has acted -- exactly "this round's starting bank" for every
    player, valid all round even for a player who acts first and finishes
    before a later same-round event fires).
  - `economy::blue_available_for(p, resources, food)`, a refactor of
    `blue_available`/`blue_used` that takes the two STORED amounts as
    parameters while still reading `blue_total`/denominations/wonder-steps
    live from `p` (DRY: `blue_used`/`blue_available` became thin callers of
    the new `_for` variants with `p.resources`/`p.food`).
  - `events::extra_production` now computes `corr` via
    `economy::blue_available_for(p, round_start_resources[idx],
    round_start_food[idx])` instead of `economy::blue_available(p)`.
  - Unit test (events.rs):
    `extra_production_prices_corruption_against_the_round_start_bank_not_the_live_one`,
    a synthetic worked example (denom [1], blue_total=15, live
    resources=13/food=2, round-start resources=8/food=5) asserting
    corruption=4 (round-start-priced), not 6 (live-priced). Confirmed RED
    (assertion failed, corr=6 not 4, resources landed at 7 not 9) by
    reverting ONLY the `blue_available_for` substitution back to
    `blue_available`, then GREEN again after restoring it. Full
    `cargo test --profile fasttest --lib`: 1409/1409 passed (including this
    one). `cargo clippy --all-targets --profile fasttest -- -D warnings`:
    clean.

WHY it regresses -- traced one concretely (game 7522009, a 2-PLAYER game,
round 14, REPLAY_DEBUG_ALL=1 against a single-game index/journal pair):
Purple reveals Economic Progress; order = [Purple, Orange] (Purple is
revealer). By the time the event reaches Orange, Orange had ALREADY run
their own round-14 end-of-turn Production Phase earlier in the SAME round
(same shape as 7522652's Orange -- acts first, already produced). The
journal's own text for this event lists NO "CORRUPTION!" line for Orange at
all (corruption = 0). Baseline (LIVE state: resources=6, food=8 -- Orange's
POST-round-14-own-production bank) computes corr=0, matching the journal
exactly. The round-start-snapshot patch instead used Orange's PRE-round-14
(round-13-end) bank and computed corr=2 -- WRONG, a direct contradiction of
the journal. This 2-resource drift compounds turn over turn until round 18,
where it produces `IllegalMove: WonderStep` ("Orange builds 2 stages of
Hollywood Orange spends 11 resources") and the game stops completing
entirely. This is a genuine, real counter-example in the SAME shape as
7522652's Orange (a player who already acted this round when a same-round
event fires) where LIVE, not round-start, is the rule the journal actually
follows -- exactly the disambiguator the previous worker said was missing,
and it comes out the OPPOSITE way from the one example that motivated this
whole investigation.

Read differently: `blue_available`/corruption in this engine is not a
"per-round" quantity that resets or needs a baseline at all in the general
case -- it is a pure function of a player's CURRENT bank at the instant
evaluated, exactly like the rulebook's physical blue-token mechanic (see
`economy::blue_used`'s own doc comment: resources/food ARE literally
represented by blue tokens placed on the tracks, so "tokens used" is
directly, losslessly recoverable from the current stored totals, not an
approximation). Economic Progress firing a SECOND production this round for
a player who already took their turn does not get to pretend that
player's own first production didn't happen -- their blue tokens are
genuinely, physically already spent. 7522652's Orange remains a real,
unexplained 8-resource gap (see below), but "round-start snapshot" is now
proven NOT to be its explanation.
