FINDING 1 -- 7522277, FULLY DIAGNOSED, FIX ATTEMPTED THEN REVERTED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Purple completes Library of Alexandria round 3, elects Homer round 4, then
in round 6 completes St. Peter's Basilica AND replaces Homer with Genghis
Khan in the same turn ("Purple builds 1 stage of St. Peter's Basilica ...
Wonder completed" immediately followed by "Purple elects Genghis Khan Homer
dies", journal lines 84-85). At that instant Purple has TWO completed
wonders. Homer's own printed text (Code of Laws errata, confirmed via
`pdftotext -layout cge_code_of_laws.pdf`): "When you replace Homer, you may
slide this card under ONE of your completed wonders to give it an extra 1
happy face." -- a genuine PLAYER CHOICE. `apply.rs::h_play_leader` (~line
880) never modelled the choice: it always tucks under
`completed_wonders.as_slice()[0]`, i.e. the FIRST-built wonder (Library of
Alexandria here).

Because Purple also holds St. Peter's Basilica ("each OTHER card or worker
that gives you at least one happy face now gives you an extra 1 happy
face"), and a Homer-boosted printed-happy-0 wonder DOES count as a
qualifying source for that bonus (already correctly ported and regression-
tested -- see effects.rs's `homer_happy_face_survives_ravages_of_time` /
`st_peters_basilica_still_counts_a_homer_tucked_wonder_after_it_is_ravaged`
tests, traced to BGO game 7522616), tucking under Library of Alexandria
(the code's fixed choice) makes Library count as an EXTRA qualifying
source, worth 2 extra happy faces total (Homer's own +1, plus St. Peter's
+1 bonus for Library newly qualifying). Tucking under St. Peter's Basilica
itself instead would net only 1 extra face (self-exclusion: "each OTHER
card" -- St. Peter's cannot bonus itself), for a total 1 LOWER than
tucking under Library. Hand-verified: tucking under Library gives raw_happy
7 (score 14, matches our WRONG output); tucking under St. Peter's gives
raw_happy 6 (score 12, matches the journal EXACTLY).

I changed `h_play_leader` to tuck under the LAST (`completed_wonders`'s own
doc comment: "append-only in both engines", so last = most recently
completed) instead of the first, added a full-sentence regression test
(`h_play_leader_tucks_homer_under_the_most_recently_completed_wonder` in
apply.rs's `#[cfg(test)] mod tests`), confirmed RED by reverting to `[0]`
(engine picked Library, test asserted St. Peter's, failed as expected),
then GREEN again with the fix restored. `cargo test --profile fasttest
--lib`: 1411/1411 passed. `cargo clippy --all-targets --profile fasttest --
-D warnings`: clean.

WHY: root-caused both regressions to the IDENTICAL Homer/multiple-
completed-wonders-at-replacement-instant shape as 7522277, but wanting the
OPPOSITE answer:

  7522796 (Purple): Pyramids completed round 3, St. Peter's Basilica
  completed round 7 THE SAME TURN Homer is replaced by Genghis Khan
  ("Purple plays Engineering Genius Purple builds 1 stage of St. Peter's
  Basilica; ... Wonder completed" then "Purple elects Genghis Khan Homer

  this game demonstrably wants Homer tucked under the FIRST-built wonder
  (Pyramids): the "last" fix makes downstream replay reconstruction
  diverge from the journal's own recorded actions badly enough that the
  binary no longer reaches `state.game_over` at all (a stronger signal
  than a mere score mismatch -- something the "last" choice unlocks or

  completed round 6 (a full turn BEFORE Homer is replaced by Michelangelo

that BGO's journal format never records (only "X elects Y; Homer dies",

of one win for two regressions. Per the task's own explicit allowance ("A

reverted.
