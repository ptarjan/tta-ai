PART 1 -- A REAL BUG FOUND AND FIXED (does not resolve 7522967 itself)
===========================================================================

Root cause: rust/src/replay_common.rs, `Replayer::resolve_political_decision`.

This function grounds a player's about-to-be-prepared event/territory card into
their simulated hand_military (evicting a filler "victim" card, or recording a
signed `military_hand_deficit` debt when no disposable filler exists), pushes
the card, then calls `apply::apply` DIRECTLY (not through `Self::try_apply`)
because `events::reveal_current_event` can resolve the newly revealed card's
own immediate effect *synchronously*, inside that same apply call -- for an
`AllPlayers`/`StrongestPlayer` "drawMilitaryCards" block (Development of
Politics, Politics of Strength, ...) this can grow the DECIDER's own hand a
second time in the same call.

To let that immediate draw repay any `military_hand_deficit` debt, the
function snapshots every seat's hand_military length, calls `apply_move`, and
repays `after - before` growth per seat. The bug: the snapshot
(`hand_military_len_before`) was taken AFTER the grounding push of the
prepared card, not before it. For every OTHER seat this is harmless (their
hand isn't touched by the decider's own grounding push), but for the DECIDER
itself, the snapshot baseline already includes the +1 phantom push -- which
`apply_move`'s own `h_prepare_event` cancels right back out a few lines later.
Net effect: the decider's own measured "growth" for this call is undercounted
by exactly 1 whenever their own revealed card's immediate effect ALSO draws
for them. `repay_military_hand_deficit` then credits one fewer unit of debt
than the decider actually just received, deferring (not permanently losing --
see below) the correction to whatever pays down the remainder.

Fix: moved the `hand_military_len_before` snapshot to the top of the function,
before ANY mutation (before the victim-eviction/deficit-increment/push
sequence), so it captures every seat's TRUE pre-preparation length, decider
included.

File:line changed: rust/src/replay_common.rs, `Replayer::resolve_political_decision`
(the `hand_military_len_before` snapshot moved from just before `self.apply_move(mv)`
to just after the `on_top` pile-consistency check, before the grounding/push block).

Test: `a_preexisting_deficit_is_fully_repaid_by_a_preparations_own_immediate_all_players_draw`
(rust/src/replay_common.rs, #[cfg(test)] mod tests). Gives the decider a
pre-existing debt of 2 (on top of the 1 fresh unit this call's own empty-hand
grounding adds -- total owed 3) plus a "Development of Politics"-shaped
preparation that draws 3 for everyone including the decider (net growth for
the decider is +3 raw draws - 1 net spent grounding the preparation = +2...
correction: net growth actually works out to 3 with the fix applied, since the
grounding's own -1(evict)+1(push) washes to 0 when a filler IS available, but
with an EMPTY hand (no filler) it's 0(no evict)+1(push)-1(apply's removal)+3(draw)=+3
from the call's true start; the buggy code measures growth from a baseline
that already includes the +1 push, giving 3-1=2 instead of 3-0=3). Asserts the
debt clears to 0 and the hand ends up empty (all 3 drawn cards spent repaying
debt), not the buggy 1-remaining-debt / 1-card-left outcome.

RED/GREEN confirmation: manually reverted the snapshot back to its old
(post-push) position, reran `cargo test --profile fasttest --lib
a_preexisting_deficit_is_fully_repaid_by_a_preparations_own_immediate_all_players_draw`,
confirmed it FAILS (left: 1, right: 0 -- one unit of debt stranded, exactly as
predicted). Restored the fix, reran, confirmed it PASSES.

Full test suite: `cargo test --profile fasttest --lib` -> 1445 passed, 0
failed (base was 1444 + this 1 new test).
Clippy: `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` -> clean, no warnings.
