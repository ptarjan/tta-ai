0. WHAT WAS BUILT
===========================================================================
A new, PER-PLAYER (deliberately, per the task's own methodological warning
about `DRIFT_AUDIT`'s per-game "first actor wins" convention burying the
actually-relevant actor -- see `resdrift__RESDRIFT.txt` section 3) oracle,
modelled on `Replayer::check_discard_phase_oracle`:

- `economy::pop_cost_base_band_low(base_cost: u8) -> Option<u8>`
  (`rust/src/economy.rs`) -- the literal inverse of the existing, already
  spec-verified `pop_cost_base` band table (§6.1): given an UNCLAMPED base
  food cost, returns the LOW edge of the `yellow_bank` band that must have
  produced it (2->17, 3->13, 4->9, 5->5, 7->1). Unit-tested
  (`pop_cost_base_band_low_inverts_the_cost_table`).
- `Replayer::pop_drift_first: [Option<PopDivergence>; MAX_PLAYERS]`
  (`rust/src/replay_common.rs`) -- one slot PER SEAT, first-wins, never
  overwritten, independent of every other seat's own divergence. Recorded
  by `maybe_record_pop_drift`, called from the `ActionClass::
  IncreasePopulation` arm BEFORE any move mutates state, so the compared
  `p.yellow_bank`/`p.food` are exactly what they were when the real human
  made the choice.
- THE GUARD (mirroring the `discard_blocked` guard the task's brief called
  out by name): only recorded when the journal's own stated cost is
  UNCLAMPED (`journal_cost > 0`). `pop_food_cost`'s formula floors negative
  results at zero, so a clamped 0 could come from ANY yellow_bank band once
  a big enough discount is subtracted -- inverting it would be exactly the
  kind of manufactured, ambiguous "signal" `drift__DRIFT.txt`'s own
  `discard_blocked` false-start warned about. Skipped, not force-recorded.
  A second guard: the recovered pre-discount base cost must be one
  `pop_cost_base` can actually return, or the record is skipped (protects
  against a bad discount read producing a nonsense "band").
- `POP_AUDIT=1` env flag (`debugflags.rs`), inert otherwise -- costs one
  static bool read on the hot Pop path when off, same shape as
  `DRIFT_AUDIT`.
- `replaystats.rs` "## Population audit" report section: delta histogram,
  age/round distribution, and the completion cross-tab against
  `/private/tmp/guard_ids.txt` (the task's own frozen 644-id reference set
  -- NOT `/tmp/rd_ids.txt`, a different, drift-pass-specific file this
  clone does not even populate).

===========================================================================

--- 7522614 (delta +4, round 12, line 200, actor Purple/seat1) -- TRACED ---
Full per-checkpoint trace (temporary `POP_TRACE_DUMP` instrumentation, not
kept in the final patch -- see FILES section) showed every single Pop
checkpoint for Purple agreeing with the journal EXACTLY up through line 130
(round 8): yellow_bank 18 -> 17 -> 16(after age advance) -> 15 -> ... -- six
Pop actions and one `advance_age` §12.2.4 "-2" all matching. Immediately
before line 130, between rounds 7 and 8, Purple ran TWO colonizations back
to back:
  - "Purple colonizes a Strategic Territory Sacrificed Units:; 1 Knights;
    1 Colonization card +1; 1 Colonization card +1; ..." (line ~112)
  - "Purple colonizes a Historic Territory Sacrificed Units:; 1 Warrior;
    1 Colonization card +2; ..." (line ~124)
Ground truth: exactly TWO military UNITS sacrificed total (1 Knights, 1
Warrior) -- the two "Colonization card" clauses are BONUS cards from the
military hand, not deployed units, and `interact.rs`'s own `SendBonus`
handler correctly does NOT touch `yellow_bank` for those (verified: no
`p.yellow_bank` change on that path). But the trace showed THREE
`Move::SendUnit` calls fired for these two colonizations -- "Warriors",
"Warriors", "Knights" -- one MORE than the two the journal's own text
names, and in the WRONG order (Warriors first, twice, then Knights; the
journal's own order is Knights first, once, then Warrior).
`this binary's own reconstructed hand/tableau state did not offer
`Move::SendUnit { card: Knights }` as a legal continuation of
`Replayer::drain_colonize` at the moment it was needed (a hidden-information
gap -- WHICH exact unit card the human held vs. what this binary's
imperfect hand reconstruction believes at that point), so `drain_colonize`
fell through to `Replayer::approximate_colonize` (`rust/src/
replay_common.rs:2312`) -- confirmed directly: `./target/difftest/replay
... 7522614` prints "STOPPED after 225 actions (colonize approximated)".
`approximate_colonize`'s own doc is explicit about what it does: "Repeatedly
pick the engine's own first-offered continuation... until it clears" --
i.e. it abandons trying to match the journal's own named units at all once
the exact match fails, and just grabs whatever `legal_moves` offers first,
however many units that takes to satisfy the SAME total FORCE requirement.
Substituting weaker units (Warriors) for a stronger one (Knights) needs
MORE of them to reach the same force total -- mechanically explaining BOTH
the extra `SendUnit` call and its direction (systematically MORE units
sacrificed than truth, hence systematically HIGHER reconstructed
`yellow_bank` than truth, matching section 2's positive skew exactly).
Every individual `Move::SendUnit` call is itself rules-correct
(`p.yellow_bank += 1` per unit, §11.4's "the token goes to the yellow bank,
not to free workers" -- `interact.rs:1578`, and this formula's correctness
is independently reconfirmed by EVERY OTHER Pop-cost checkpoint in this
exact game matching the journal exactly, both before and after this
episode, until the drift crosses a cost-band boundary at line 200 and
becomes visible). The bug, if it is one, is entirely in the FIDELITY of
`approximate_colonize`'s fallback under hidden information, not in any
rule this binary enforces.

===========================================================================
CONCLUSION / NEXT SUSPECT
===========================================================================
The DOMINANT, cleanly-verified mechanism (at least 3 of 6 non-completing
games directly checked, likely more across the 29) is `Replayer::
approximate_colonize`'s hidden-information fallback substituting a
DIFFERENT, usually WEAKER set of sacrificed military units than the human
actually sacrificed, while still hitting the same total colonization FORCE
-- each substitute unit is individually priced correctly (`interact.rs`'s
`SendUnit` handler, §11.4, verified rules-correct at every other
checkpoint), so the resulting `yellow_bank` inflation is a RECONSTRUCTION
FIDELITY gap under hidden information, not a rules violation. This is
squarely the class of gap the engine's own `colonize_approximated` field
already exists to flag -- not a new bug, and not something a "fix the
engine" patch can close without first improving the colonization hidden-
hand solver's own fidelity (a materially bigger undertaking than this
pass's population-oracle mandate, and out of scope here per "don't force a
patch").
A SECOND, distinct, not-yet-pinned mechanism (7520718) exists for games
that never hit the colonize approximation at all -- named above as the
concrete next step (full `yellow_bank` action ledger, not just Pop
checkpoints).
