WHY I DID NOT LAND A FIX THAT CLOSES 7523376 ITSELF
-------------------------------------------------------
I tested the hypothesis "if step 2's repay had preferred a non-Event/
Territory victim (`Infiltrate` or `Raid(II)`) over `Ravages of Time`, step
3's preparation-cost eviction would then have found `Ravages of Time`
still available (the rule-correct, type-matching choice) and the whole
before/after trajectory would re-converge." This is TRUE for card IDENTITY
(confirmed: with the landed fix, step 2 evicts `Infiltrate` instead of
`Ravages`) but FALSE for card COUNT: step 3's eviction still has to remove
ONE card regardless of which type it prefers, because by round 11 there is
still exactly one genuine debt (from round 9) PLUS one genuine new cost
(International Agreement's own preparation) needing to be paid, i.e. TWO
total real-card losses between round 9 and round 11 in "after" vs only ONE
in "before" (before never pays back round 9's debt at all). Reassigning
WHICH card gets evicted cannot fix a COUNT mismatch -- confirmed by
rerunning 7523376 with the landed fix: identical stop line, identical
error, `discards=18 solved=1` (byte-identical to the unpatched `2bc2e4c`
run) both times I checked.

The real open question, which I could not resolve within budget, is
whether the round-9 deficit is a GENUINE debt (in which case "after"'s
2-card round-11 hand is the historically correct one, and it's actually
"before" that's silently 1-too-many for the rest of the game, coincidentally
never crossing a discard limit or otherwise becoming visible before the
game completes) or a FALSE POSITIVE (in which case Orange's TRUE hand at
round 9 held a 3rd real spendable card this reconstruction's OWN, entirely
separate, upstream draw/discard tracking from rounds 2-8 already fails to
credit it with -- unrelated to anything in today's commits -- and
`ground_auction_winner_hand`'s "no filler" detection is correctly reporting
a shortfall in an already-wrong model, not creating one). The strongest
evidence available points to the latter: "before" (WITHOUT ever paying the
round-9 debt) reconstructs the ENTIRE REST OF THIS GAME through round 18
with an exact final-score match (`engine=[141,175] index=[175,141]
match=true`), and the corpus-wide "Discard-phase hand-size oracle" cross-
check (26 checkpoints game-wide, 24/26 exact) shows only ONE mismatch for
this game and it is for PURPLE at round 11, not Orange, and is IDENTICAL in
both before/after (pre-existing, unrelated). I could not, within budget,
trace Orange's hand all the way back to round 1 by hand to find whether an
earlier draw/discard is undercounted; that is the concrete next step for
whoever picks this up (see below).

MECHANISM LANDED AS A FIX (small, zero-regression, does not close the bucket)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`repay_military_hand_deficit` (replay_common.rs) now prefers a NON-
Event/Territory victim over an Event/Territory one when both are equally
unprotected, falling back to Event/Territory only if that is the sole
option -- mirroring RULES_SPEC 5.2's restriction on what
`resolve_political_decision`'s own preparation-cost eviction is allowed to
spend, so a repay never pre-empts a nearby real preparation's only
rule-legal card. This is directionally correct and provably safe (see
"WHY I DID NOT LAND A FIX THAT CLOSES 7523376 ITSELF" above for why it does
NOT, by itself, fix the count divergence) but is included because it is a
genuine, tested, zero-risk improvement in rules fidelity, not because it
moves the bucket.
