Built rust/target/difftest/{replaystats,cardblame}. Ran

Of the 27 diverging games, checked each journal for a REAL pact mention
(grep -iE on the 9 pact card names, NOT a bare "pact" substring -- that
false-positives on every "Impact of ..." final-event card name, which is
how a naive first pass over-counted 27/27 as "pact games"). Real count:
11/27 diverging games mention an actual pact card.

Of those 11, split by SCOREDIV_DETAIL's own culture_drifted_in_play flag
(distinguishes an in-play accumulated-culture bug from a final-event-only

  - 3 games (7523087, 7523162, 7523253) have culture_drifted_in_play=false,
    i.e. their divergence is END-GAME "Impact of ..." scoring only --
    unrelated to pacts by construction (pacts don't touch final-event
    formulas).
  - 8 games (7523341, 7520722, 7522866, 7521141, 7522473, 7522446, 7522672,
    7521695) have culture_drifted_in_play=true, an in-play checkpoint
    divergence.

Traced each of the 8 in-play-divergent games' journal against its
first_culture_divergence lineno/actor:
  - 7523341: divergence actor Purple, lineno 388 (round 12). Purple is NOT
    a party to ANY pact at that point (Peace Treaty in this game is
    proposed only at line 522, round 15 -- after the divergence). Pacts
    ruled out for this game's divergence.
  - 7520722: divergence actor Orange, lineno 675 (round 18, wonder-build
    turn). Orange IS party to Trade Routes Agreement (accepted round 5),
    but Trade Routes grants no culture at all (food_as_resource/
    resource_as_food only) -- can't be the direct cause of a CULTURE
    divergence. The +4/+8 pattern across two different players (net
    nonzero, not a transfer) points at end-of-turn wonder-completion
    culture math, not a pact.
  - 7522473: divergence actor Green, lineno 617 (round 17). Green's
    Promise of Military Protection (culture-granting pact) with Grey was
    CANCELLED at round 13 (War over Culture declared) -- 4 rounds before
    the divergence. Checked the cancellation code path
    (combat.rs::cancel_attack_pacts, apply.rs::h_cancel_pact) -- both are
    well-tested (h_war_cancels_a_pact_that_ends_on_mutual_attack,
    cancel_attack_pacts_removes_only_the_matching_pact, etc.) and correct
    by inspection; the intervening 4 rounds carry other large culture
    swings (an Impact/event card) that better explain the timing. Not
    attributable to the pact-cancellation path.
  - The remaining 5 (7522866, 7521141, 7522446, 7522672, 7521695): the
    first-divergence actor at the flagged checkpoint is not a party to any
    culture-granting pact at that moment either (their real pacts are
    Open Borders/Trade Routes/Scientific Cooperation/International Trade
    Agreement -- none of which grant culture directly -- or Peace Treaty
    proposed strictly AFTER the flagged lineno).

CONCLUSION ON THE ORIGINAL LEAD: negative. In every one of the 8 in-play
divergent, pact-involving games, the diverging player is either not a
party to any culture-granting pact at the moment of divergence, or the
pact in question grants no culture at all. The pact enrichment in
cardblame's score-mismatch cut looks like a confound of GAME COMPLEXITY
(games that reach pact negotiation are long, multi-mechanic, wonder- and
event-heavy games -- exactly the kind of game where an unrelated culture-
accounting bug is more likely to manifest), not a causal pact mechanism.
This is DISTINCT from the round-length confound cardblame's own 4-cut
analysis already ruled out -- this is a same-length-bucket, different-axis
confound (mechanic density / turn count of decisions), and it stands.

A REAL BUG FOUND, BUT NOT LANDED (regressions)
===========================================================================
While tracing Scientific Cooperation's mechanics I found a genuine,
unrelated-to-mismatches implementation gap: `effects::Stats::
science_partners` (the bitmask marking which OTHER civilization must pay 1
science, per the card's own text: "it pays 2 science less and the other
civilization pays 1 science... if the other cannot pay 1 science, the
technology cannot be developed") is COMPUTED (effects.rs:922,
apply_pact_block) but had NO CONSUMER anywhere in the crate -- grep
confirms its only prior read sites were unit-test assertions of the
bitmask itself. The `tech_discount` (2 off the developer's own cost) side
IS applied (costs.rs:582); the partner-charge side never was.

I implemented the missing half: a `science_pact_partners_can_pay` legality
gate (all 3 DevelopTechnology legality sites in legal.rs) plus the actual
1-science charge to the partner in `h_develop` (apply.rs), with 5 new
tests (both RED-confirmed by reverting the fix and re-running). Lib tests:
1424 passed (1419 baseline + 5 new). Clippy: clean.

BUT the full-corpus guard sweep regressed:
  completed 698 -> 690 (-8), exact 671 -> 665 (-6), 0 wins.
  Regressed IDs: 7521790 7522473 7522512 7522940 7523162 7523404 7523464
  7523510

Traced the regression in 7523162 (game had Scientific Cooperation between
Orange/Grey, accepted round 9). The replay now stops at journal line 324
("Grey discovers Scientific Method Grey loses 4 science; Orange loses 1
science") with IllegalMove -- Grey's own tracked `science` is 3, one short
of the discounted cost of 4, even after correctly reconstructing 6 at the
round-9 End Turn checkpoint (verified against the journal's own "3 science
(now 6)" line by hand: 8 pre-Riflemen-charge - 1 (Riflemen, a REAL
Scientific-Cooperation charge my fix correctly applies) - 4 (Grey's own
revolution cost) + 3 (production) = 6, exactly matching the journal).

Between that verified-correct checkpoint (line 296, round 9 end) and the
next relevant event (line 301, round 10, Orange discovers Strategy -- the
next legitimate Scientific-Cooperation charge my fix applies), there is
NO journal event that should touch Grey's science at all (only Orange's
own Political Phase / plays Reserves / takes Strategy). Yet the engine's
own stored `Grey.science` had already silently dropped from 6 to 4 by the
time my code read it -- a 2-point drop with no corresponding journal
event, entirely INSIDE the round 9->10 turn-boundary transition. This is
a PRE-EXISTING, unrelated bug (present before my change too -- my fix
didn't create it, it only exposed it by being the first code to ever
check a partner's science against a real threshold). It was invisible
before because nothing in the corpus-wide oracles checks SCIENCE in-play
(only culture and final-award culture are cross-validated by
replaystats' existing oracles).

Because this masked bug makes the "partner cannot pay" legality gate (and
even just the raw charge, tested standalone) trip on a false floor for
several games, landing my rules-correct fix regresses the guard. Per this
project's rule ("no regressions may land"), I REVERTED the fix entirely
(rust/ has zero diff vs 570479a; pacts.patch alongside this file is
therefore empty). The attempted fix (~15 net lines: a new
`effects::science_pact_partners_can_pay` helper, its 3 call sites in
legal.rs's DevelopTechnology arms, and a charge block in
apply.rs::h_develop, plus 5 tests) is described in full above so a future
worker can reproduce it without re-deriving the design once the blocking
science-drift bug is fixed.

NEW LEAD FOR A FUTURE WORKER: find why a player's in-play `science` field
can silently drop by a small amount (2, here) across a round boundary with
