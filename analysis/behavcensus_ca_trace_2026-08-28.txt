WHAT `ca_unused_r3` ACTUALLY COUNTS (the "why" of it being wrong)
=====================================================================
It is "proven wrong" not because the trace and the counter disagree (they
agree), but because the QUANTITY IT MEASURES IS NOT THE ONE THE NAME AND THE
FIELD DOC PROMISE:

  1. NAME/DOC: the field is `ca_unused_r3` and the field doc
     (behavcensus.rs:1179-1198) says it mirrors `bin/humanopenings.rs`'s
     `ca_unused_by_r3` column "exactly" -- a per-seat OPENING metric, i.e.
     "civil actions left unused BY (at the end of) round 3".

  2. WHAT IT IS: a CUMULATIVE SUM over EVERY `Move::EndTurn` in rounds
     1-3 (behavcensus.rs:1817 gate `round_before <= 3`, arm `Move::EndTurn`,
     line 1840 `t.ca_unused_r3 += ca_before_move`). It is not a snapshot of
     the pool at the end of round 3; it is "how many CA the player let lapse
     across the three rounds, summed turn by turn". A player who wastes 1 CA
     in r1, 1 in r2 and 1 in r3 scores 3, not 1.

  3. UNIT MISMATCH: it is accumulated as an `i32` PER PLAYER-GAME and
     reported as a HISTOGRAM `opening_ca_unused: HashMap<i32,u64>` --
     "count -> player-games" (behavcensus.rs:1949, 2387-2389), i.e. "N
     player-games had exactly K unused CAs in the window". It is not a
     per-turn or per-round average, and it is not "CA unused at the end of
     round 3".

So the number the counter reports (a per-player-game histogram of a
three-round cumulative lapse total, here concentrated at 0 because the bot
spends to 0 before every r1-3 EndTurn) is a DIFFERENT quantity from the
"unused by round 3" the column name and the field doc describe. That is the
diagnosis: the trace agrees with the counter's arithmetic; the counter's
ARITHMETIC is the wrong instrument for the metric the field claims to be.
