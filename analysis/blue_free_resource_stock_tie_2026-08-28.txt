4. THE STRUCTURAL TIE (the interesting explanation, confirmed)
================================================================================
eval.rs:811-814:
    pub const DOMINATES: &[(WeightKey, WeightKey)] = &[
        (WeightKey::ResourceStock, WeightKey::BlueFree),
        (WeightKey::WonderPotential, WeightKey::WonderPromise),
    ];

eval.rs:934-943 (dominance_repair, ORDERINGS LAST):
    for &(hi, lo) in DOMINATES {
        let a = out.get(hi);
        let b = out.get(lo);
        if b > a + 1e-12 {
            viol.push(...);
            out.set(hi, b);    <-- ResourceStock is RAISED to BlueFree's value
        }
    }

So whenever BlueFree > ResourceStock by more than 1e-12, dominance_repair
COPIES BlueFree's value onto ResourceStock. The two become bit-identical.
This runs on every load and on every accepted mutation (bin/climb.rs:269, 401,
and repair_to_bounds at line ~270). The climb cannot separate them: any
mutation that would make ResourceStock < BlueFree is immediately repaired
back to ResourceStock = BlueFree. The two coordinates are locked together
at the value the DOMINANT side (BlueFree) holds.

bin/climb.rs:209-216 (effective_bound) tightens the DOMINATED side's clamp
to the dominator's bound:
    fn effective_bound(key: WeightKey, players: u8) -> f64 {
        let own = key.clamp_bound(players);
        DOMINATES
            .iter()
            .filter(|(_, dominated)| *dominated == key)
            .fold(own, |acc, (dominator, _)| acc.min(dominator.clamp_bound(players)))
    }
So ResourceStock's effective 3p bound is min(96.757479, 69.112485) = 69.112485.
The value 45.74514020088546 is interior to that -- it is not clamped, it is
REPAIRED.

COMPARISON TO THE 2p card_board_credit/card_board_leader SHAPE:
The 2p bug (coordinator's known bug, fixed in code not yet live): card_board_leader
was a COMPOSITE MULTIPLIER GATED ON card_board_credit -- the climb could not move
them independently because one was computed as a function of the other in the
EVALUATION (not in a repair step). The fix made CardBoardLeader an absolute
multiplier (commit 83d1c2e, tests in 1ed6d38).

The 3p blue_free/resource_stock tie is the SAME CLASS of defect -- two keys the
climb sees as one coordinate -- but the MECHANISM is different:
  2p card_board pair:  tied by EVALUATION (leader was a multiplier of credit).
  3p blue_free pair:   tied by REPAIR (dominance_repair copies BlueFree onto
                       ResourceStock whenever the ordering is violated).
In both cases the hill-climb cannot move the two independently, so they
converge to a single value. In the 2p case the tie was in the score function
itself; in the 3p case the tie is in the constraint repair that runs after
every mutation. The 3p case is a DESIGN INTENT (the DOMINATES pair was added
deliberately to enforce ResourceStock >= BlueFree as a gameplay invariant)
that has the SIDE EFFECT of making the two coordinates non-independent from
the optimizer's perspective. The optimizer sees one coordinate, not two.

WHY 2p AND 4p DO NOT SHOW THE SAME NUMBERS:
2p:  blue_free -0.142264, resource_stock +0.644464.
     Here ResourceStock (+0.644) > BlueFree (-0.142), so the DOMINATES repair
     does NOT fire (b > a + 1e-12 is false: -0.142 > 0.644 + 1e-12 is false).
     The two are free to differ. The structural tie EXISTS (the repair is
     armed) but does not activate because the ordering is already satisfied.
     The 2p pair that IS tied (card_board_credit/leader) is a different bug.
4p:  blue_free -0.520785, resource_stock -0.013431.
     a = ResourceStock = -0.013431, b = BlueFree = -0.520785.
     Is b > a + 1e-12? -0.520785 > -0.013431? No. The repair does NOT fire.
     The two are free. The structural tie exists but does not activate.
3p:  blue_free 45.745140, resource_stock 45.745140.
     The repair HAS fired and locked them together. At some point during the
     climb BlueFree was > ResourceStock, the repair copied BlueFree onto
     ResourceStock, and now they are equal. Any future mutation that would
     lower ResourceStock below BlueFree is immediately repaired back. They are
     stuck at the same value.

So: the structural tie (DOMINATES pair) EXISTS on all three arms. It only
PRODUCES EQUAL NUMBERS on 3p because that is the only arm where the repair
has actually fired and locked the two together. On 2p and 4p the ordering is
satisfied (ResourceStock >= BlueFree) so the repair is dormant and the two
can (and do) differ.
