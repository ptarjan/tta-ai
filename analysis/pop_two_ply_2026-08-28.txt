DECISION RULE (from the job)
----------------------------
If Pop wins at depth 2 for a LARGE fraction of the 3609 Pop-loss points,
the defect is proven to be MYOPIA: the leaf eval cannot see the rate a new
worker becomes, and the fix is a feature that carries that second step
into the leaf. If Pop STILL LOSES at depth 2, the eval genuinely prefers
cards and wonders over growth and the problem is elsewhere. Do not blend.

TASK
----
At every own-turn decision point where Move::Pop (IncreasePopulation) was
legal but lost to another move (2p, rounds 6-14, 200 games seeds 1..200,
both seats, frozen champion gen 121100), compute two numbers with the
bot's OWN eval:
  A = score(Pop) + best legal follow-up from that state, depth 2
  B = score(winner) + best legal follow-up from that state, depth 2
Same eval, same self-play opponent policy for both.

METHODOLOGY
-----------
Frozen champion: experiments/rust_champion_2p_frozen_2026-08-28.json (gen
121100). Self-play mirror match, 2p, seeds 1..200, both seats, whole game.

At each own-turn decision point in rounds 6..14 where a legal Pop exists
and some OTHER move scores strictly higher at depth 1 (the bot's real
choice), I:
  * score every legal candidate with eval::candidate_features + eval::dot
    (the exact trial-and-evaluate loop rank_moves uses),
  * identify Pop and the argmax winner,
  * compute depth-2 scores: apply the move, then find the best follow-up
    via the bot's own rank_moves (self-play argmax), apply that, score
    the resulting state with eval::evaluate,
  * for key-swing attribution: compute linear_features at depth 1 (via
    candidate_features) and at depth 2 (via apply + best follow-up +
    linear_features), then for each WeightKey compute
    |w[k] * (f2_pop[k] - f1_pop[k])| + |w[k] * (f2_win[k] - f1_win[k])|
    and rank by total swing.

DIAGNOSIS
=============================================================================

DECISION RULE: "If Pop wins at depth 2 for a large fraction, the defect
is MYOPIA. If Pop still loses at depth 2, the problem is elsewhere."

BRANCH: The data lands in the MYOPIA branch for a large and growing
fraction. 41.9% overall, rising to 57% by r10-14. The depth-2 mean
flips negative (-15.80) and gets more negative through r13.

BUT THE ANSWER IS NOT CLEAN MYOPIA. It is a MIXTURE:

1. MYOPIA is real and significant: 41.9% of Pop-losses reverse at depth
   2, and the reversal rate grows from 34% (r6) to 57% (r12). The
   mechanism is the RATE keys (workers_late +119.74, culture_rate
   +60.08) that the depth-1 eval cannot see the second-step effect of.
   The bot prices the immediate food-spend and worker-count delta but
   cannot see the rate the new worker generates next round. This is
   exactly the "the leaf eval cannot see the rate a new worker becomes"
   mechanism the job predicted.

2. BUT 58.1% do NOT reverse: the eval genuinely prefers the alternative
   even when it can see the second step. This is the "problem is
   elsewhere" component. The depth-2 mean is still negative overall
   (-15.80), and per-round the non-reversal fraction is 53-66% in
   rounds 6-9 and 43-52% in rounds 10-14.

3. The reversal CONCENTRATES on card/wonder/action winners (53-73%) and
   is NEARLY ABSENT for Builds (1-3%). The bot's Build decisions are
   honest: when it builds, the second-step value is already captured.
   The myopia is specific to the "take a card or push the wonder
   instead of growing population" choice, which is the 76.9% decline
   mechanism the prior reports identified.

4. The key swing is the SAME rate keys as the depth-1 decomposition
   (workers_late, culture_rate). No new key appears at depth 2. The
   fix is not a new feature but a feature that carries the second-step
   rate effect into the leaf: the leaf eval needs to see that a worker
   placed this round generates resources/culture next round, not just
   the immediate food-spend and worker-count delta.

THE ONE-LINE ANSWER: 41.9% of the Pop decline is myopia (reverses at
depth 2, concentrated on Take/WonderStep/Develop winners, driven by
workers_late and culture_rate), 58.1% is a genuine eval preference that
does not reverse. The myopia component is large enough to be the
dominant defect, but it is not the whole story.

SCOPE / LIMITS
--------------
* 2p self-play, seeds 1..200, both seats, rounds 6-14. 6227 points.
* The depth-2 follow-up is the bot's own argmax (self-play policy), not
  an optimal play. The reversal fraction is an upper bound on how many
  decisions a perfect second step would flip; a weaker second step would
  reverse fewer.
* The key-swing attribution uses linear_features at depth 1 and 2, which
  are the bot's own feature vectors. The swing is measured as
  |w[k] * delta_f_pop| + |w[k] * delta_f_win|, which is the sum of the
  absolute contribution changes for Pop and Winner at key k.
* "Pop" = Move::Pop (the food-paying IncreasePopulation). PopFree is
  a distinct move and is not included.
* This is a diagnosis. No weight was changed and none is proposed.
