DECISION RULE (from the job)
----------------------------
If Pop wins at depth 2 for a LARGE fraction of the 3609 Pop-loss points,
the defect is proven to be MYOPIA: the leaf eval cannot see the rate a new
worker becomes, and the fix is a feature that carries that second step
into the leaf. If Pop STILL LOSES at depth 2, the eval genuinely prefers
cards and wonders over growth and the problem is elsewhere. Do not blend.

TASK
----
At every own-turn decision point where Move::Pop (food-paying
IncreasePopulation) was legal but LOST to another move at depth 1
(2p, rounds 6-14, 200 games seeds 1..200, both seats, frozen champion
gen 121100), compute two numbers with the bot's OWN eval:
  A = score(Pop applied) + best legal follow-up at depth 2
  B = score(winner applied) + best legal follow-up at depth 2
Same eval, same follow-up rule on both sides, same self-play opponent
policy for the whole game. Do not hand-derive anything.

METHODOLOGY
-----------
- Self-play 200 games, seeds 1..200, 2 players, frozen champion
  gen 121100 (/Users/pt/tta-ai/experiments/rust_champion_2p_frozen_
  2026-08-28.json).
- At each decision point where the decider's turn is their OWN (EndTurn
  is in the legal set), rank all legal moves via bot.rank_moves.
- Pop = the move whose decision_kind is Pop AND whose variant is
  Move::Pop { full } (the food-paying IncreasePopulation). PopFree is a
  distinct variant and is NOT counted as "Pop" here — the prior
  decomposition census's 3609 points are all Move::Pop { full } points.
  (The filter makes the re-run's total match the census exactly; the
  original run's 6227 excess is the round-label bug, not PopFree
  inclusion, but the filter is applied regardless.)
- The "winner" is ranked[0] (the depth-1 argmax). If winner_kind == Pop,
  the point is not a Pop-loss and is skipped.
- Depth-2 score: apply the move to a cloned state, evaluate with
  eval::evaluate, then take the best legal follow-up (per the same
  bot.rank_moves), apply it, evaluate again. EndTurn as the first move
  is scored on the un-moved root (same convention as rank_moves);
  EndTurn as the follow-up is scored on the trial without a second
  application.
- Key swing per point: |w[k] * (f2_pop[k] - f1_pop[k])| + |w[k] *
  (f2_win[k] - f1_win[k])| summed over all keys, where f1 features come
  from eval::candidate_features (the bot's own scoring path, determinize
  included) and f2 features are rebuilt from the same two depth-2 leaves
  score_depth2 used.
- Tie: m2 == 0.0 exactly (f64 equality). Verified by re-running both
  branches and comparing the full Debug rendering of the two leaf states
  (no state-equality API is exposed; Debug covers every field).
- 6 threads, work-stealing over game indices.

SCOPE AND LIMITS
----------------
- 200 games, seeds 1..200, 2 players, frozen champion gen 121100.
- Follow-up depth is exactly 1 (one more move after Pop/winner). The
  "second step" the job asks about is the best single move after Pop,
  not a full search.
- The tie verification compares Debug renderings of the full state.

  contribution change. It is a proxy for "which key's price moved",
  not "which key caused the flip".
