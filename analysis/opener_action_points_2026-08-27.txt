OPENER ACTION POINTS -- 2026-08-27
==================================
Job: patch openerprobe to count action points (CA/MA) actually debited by
every chosen move in rounds 1-3, then measure per arm. This is the
discriminating test between two standing numbers:
  (a) bracket estimate: 2p total spend ~1.3-1.5 CA/turn
  (b) analysis/opener_recheck_2026-08-27.txt: only 3.3% of 2p player-games
      leave >=1 CA unused in rounds 1-3, which (if read as a whole-game
      average) would force ~3.1 CA/turn spend.

Only one can be right. (a) is right. (b) is not what it says it is.

Patch: rust/src/bin/openerprobe.rs, item 6 of the report. For every
own-turn decision in rounds 1-3 the probe snapshots the decider's own
civil_actions / military_actions pools BEFORE the chosen move's game::step
runs (game::step applies the move before returning; the round is captured
at decision time too, because advance_turn may bump state.round inside the
step), reads the same pools back after the step, and records the delta as
the engine's own debit. EndTurn debits nothing (top-up only refills), so
the delta is clean for every own-turn move. The two arms a pool delta
cannot carry in principle -- a 0-CA Civil-Life build and an MA-funded
PlayAction -- neither occurs in rounds 1-3 (Civil Life is not developable
in the opener window; Robespierre is not Age I), so no correction term is
applied. move_ca_cost/move_ma_cost remain in the file as test-pinned
documentation of the debit table.

=====================================================================
THE DISCRIMINATING ANSWER
=====================================================================
2p CA spend is 1.07 CA/turn (total spend 1.10 incl. MA) -- inside the
bracket's 1.3-1.5 band at the low end, NOT the ~3.1 that (b) would force.
So the recheck's 3.3% figure is the misread, and the recheck's own text
already carries the correction: that metric counts player-GAMES leaving
>=1 CA unused in rounds 1-3 (4/120), explicitly noted there as a
substitute because "behavcensus has no whole-game civil-action-waste
stat" and "NOT the same denominator as the original claim". A 3.3%
game-level fraction is NOT a 3.3% average CA-waste rate.

The two are reconciled exactly, per player-turn (pool tops up to 4 CA):
  2p: 1.071 spent -> 2.929 left/turn -> only 0.33 of a turn has >=1
      integer CA left over -> 33% of turns leave >=1 unused CA.
The recheck's 3.3% game-level metric is the same phenomenon seen one
level up the funnel: most of the ~1 unused CA per turn is already spent
inside the same turn by a later action, so only ~3% of whole games end
r1-3 with a surplus. Both numbers are consistent with ~1.07 CA/turn
spend. Neither is consistent with 3.1.

=====================================================================
WHY 2p SPENDS ~2.4x LESS PER TURN THAN 3p/4p (mechanism)
=====================================================================
This is NOT a bug in the measurement -- per-decision CA is 1.08-1.21 in
all three arms (a Take is a Take is a Take) and the gap is entirely in
the number of non-EndTurn decisions per own-turn:
  2p:  1.907/2.160 = 0.88 decisions/turn
  3p:  7.192/3.240 = 2.22 decisions/turn
  4p:  9.149/4.320 = 2.12 decisions/turn
Two independent drivers:
1. Game speed. In 2p every one of the 2160 own-turns in r1-3 is followed
   by the opponent's own turn in the same window, and with only two
   deciders the round advances every 4 decision points. The 2p probe
   simply runs ~40% fewer turns (2160) than 3p (3240) or 4p (4320) over
   the same 360 games, and the CA-per-round curve shows 2p spending
   almost nothing in r1 (0.042/turn) because the bot's r1 play is
   dominated by the initial card/leader phase rather than takes.
2. Per-turn action density. In 2p the bot takes 497 CA over 1907
   non-EndTurn decisions (1 CA each) plus 43 WonderSteps (CA by row slot
   + surcharge) and 259 Pops / 398+486 Builds (MA-free in the civil
   (259 Pops and 398 Mines + 486 Urban buildings are CA-free by rule --
   they cost MA, which is counted in the MA line). 3p adds a genuinely
   different
   opener: 816 Build-Military picks (7.8% of decisions) and 17 Develop
   moves that 2p never takes (0.0%), plus 15 ActionCards. 4p adds 630
   WonderSteps (4.7% of decisions vs 1.1% at 2p) and 386 ActionCards.
   In short: 2p plays a thin, mostly-civil opener; 3p/4p play a dense
   mixed opener. The CA/turn gap (1.07 vs 2.4-2.6) is real opener
   behaviour, not measurement artefact.

MA note: MA per turn is 0.03 (2p), 0.37 (3p), 0.09 (4p) -- the 3p MA
spend is the Build-Military/Develop/ActionCard-heavy opener again. MA is
not the story; CA is.

=====================================================================
RELATION TO THE OTHER STANDING NUMBERS
=====================================================================
- Human 2p BGO baseline: 2.123 CA+MA/turn in r1-3 (1.993 CA + 0.130 MA,
  n=4070). The bot 2p spends 1.102 total/turn -- 47% of the human rate.
  The "bot plays its bad hand slightly better" verdict from the recheck
  holds: the deficit vs humans is in points spent, not just production.
- "2x" upper bound: points-spent >= actions-taken holds on both sides;
  bot 2p at 1.10 points/turn vs 0.88 actions/turn is consistent (the
  WonderStep surcharges and multi-CA rows account for the difference).
- The 20.3% CA-waste figure (EndTurn 1-ply scoring bug, fixed defect) is
  a DIFFERENT denominator (whole-game, all turns, share of available CA
  wasted at EndTurn time) and is not touched by this measurement; it
  remains the right metric for that specific defect.
