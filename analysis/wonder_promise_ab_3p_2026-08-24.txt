A/B: raising wonder_promise at 3p to cure the late-wonder pathology. REJECTED.
=============================================================================
Measured 2026-08-24. Prerequisites: analysis/wonder_tempo_2026-08-24.txt and
analysis/wonder_tempo_3p_cause_2026-08-24.txt, which established that 3p late
starters are NOT resource-constrained and spend the wonder-step budget on takes.

The four keys that price a wonder
---------------------------------
  WonderProgress   features.rs:639   stages paid    champ3p =  6.375
  WonderRemaining  features.rs:640                  champ3p = -1.155
  WonderInProgress features.rs:671   flat cliff     champ3p =  0.661
  WonderPromise    cards.rs:2197, eval.rs:344-347   champ3p =  0.568

WonderPotential (cards.rs:2148) is the only identity-aware wonder-value
channel, but its `paid_fraction` is exactly 0 on the move that TAKES a wonder
(cards.rs:2159-2162). So at the take decision, WonderPromise is the only term
active, and 0.568 sits well under the DOMINATES ceiling of wonder_potential
(1.938, enforced eval.rs:855).

Verdict and mechanism
---------------------
REJECTED on every measure, and it moved the target metric the WRONG WAY.

Raising the reward for TAKING a wonder without raising the reward for PAYING
one off teaches a one-ply evaluator to grab wonders it never follows through
on. Starts stayed flat while abandonments nearly tripled - a strictly worse
pathology than the late start it was meant to cure.

This reproduces a warning the codebase already carries: eval.rs:836-850's
DOMINATES doc comment says raising wonder_promise relative to wonder_potential
produces exactly this take-but-never-build behaviour. The numeric invariant
(wonder_potential 1.938 >= wonder_promise 1.938) was still satisfied - so
satisfying DOMINATES is necessary, not sufficient, and the doc comment's prose
is the binding constraint, not its inequality.

Next lever, NOT yet tried: move wonder_progress (the key that rewards paying
stages), or a joint move of wonder_progress and wonder_potential together.
Do not retry wonder_promise alone in either direction.
