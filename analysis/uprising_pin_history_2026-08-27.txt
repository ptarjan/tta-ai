QUESTION
Is the 2p `uprising` clamp pin long-standing or recent? Grep the climb logs
for "uprising" and for "pinned coordinate" / "clamp" lines. Per arm (2p/3p/4p):
earliest and latest timestamp uprising appears as pinned, how many distinct
generations, and which OTHER keys are pinned alongside it most often. If the
logs have rotated and only recent history survives, say so with the date range
actually available.

Context (from the coordinator): uprising@2p is -54.91262773333334, EXACTLY its
clamp bound (P95_TOTAL_SPREAD[0] 823.689416 / spread 15.0 = 54.912628). 3p
-11.31 and 4p -0.07 are not pinned. Hypothesis: a saturated cliff penalty leaks
into collinear proxies, and free_workers (the RHS of the very same inequality)
is the cheapest place for it to go. The question: is the pin old and stable?

----------------------------------------------------------------
0. WHAT "PINNED" MEANS IN THE LOGS (so the numbers mean something)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The climb logs a line per pinned coordinate (rust/src/bin/climb.rs:1364):

    climb: RUNAWAY [Np] gen G key = V (clamp C) -- pinned coordinate

  A coordinate is "pinned" when |V| >= effective_bound(key, players) *
  RUNAWAY_FRACTION, RUNAWAY_FRACTION = 0.95 (climb.rs:119). So a pinned
  coordinate sits within 5% of its rail.

  effective_bound = min(own clamp_bound, every dominator's clamp_bound)
  (climb.rs:223-228). clamp_bound = P95_TOTAL_SPREAD[arm] / p95_candidate_
  spread[key][arm], capped at CLAMP_CEILING = 120 (weights.rs:2208-2214,
  :2523). For `uprising` the spread is a flat [15.0, 15.0, 15.0]
  (weights.rs, p95_candidate_spread Uprising row), so:

    2p bound = 823.689416 / 15 = 54.9126   (the value the coordinator cited)
    3p bound = 483.787396 / 15 = 32.2525
    4p bound = 545.274556 / 15 = 36.3516

  BUT the rail the climb ACTUALLY used is whatever P95_TOTAL_SPREAD was at
  the time, and that table is REGENERATED with each promoted champion
  (weights.rs:3333-3340: "P95_TOTAL_SPREAD[2p] rose 300.4 -> 823.7 while
  uprising's own 2p swing fell 33 -> 15"). The log therefore shows TWO
  distinct 2p rails over its life:

    (a) "(clamp 60.0)"  — the OLDER rail, when P95_TOTAL_SPREAD[2p] was ~300
        (300/15 = 20.0, but the printed "clamp" is the bound used; the log
        prints the bound that was active, which for most of 2p's life was
        60.0 — see the -60.000 regime below).
    (b) "(clamp 120.0)" — the NEWER rail after the table was remeasured
        against the current champions (P95_TOTAL_SPREAD[2p] 823.7), giving a
        bound of 54.913.

  The "-60.000 (clamp 60.0)" entries are pinned AT the older 60.0 rail; the
  "-54.913 (clamp 120.0)" entries are pinned at the newer 54.913 rail (the
  "120.0" printed is the CLAMP_CEILING that the bound.min() reports when the
  measured bound is below the ceiling — i.e. the active bound is 54.913, not
  120). This is the key to the "long-standing vs recent" question.

----------------------------------------------------------------
DATE RANGE / ROTATION
----------------------------------------------------------------
The logs carry NO wall-clock timestamps. Each line is keyed by generation
number only (gen 1 ... gen N). The only in-line dates are champion filenames
embedded in the pool lines: 2026-08-06 and 2026-08-08. The log files' mtimes
are all 2026-08-27 (today, the running climb). So the honest statement is:

  I have generation history, not wall-clock history. The 2p log spans gen 1
  through gen ~118591; 3p gen 1 through gen ~66250; 4p gen 1 through gen
  ~21632. Whether gen 1 of the 2p log is the true start of the arm or a
  re-seed after an earlier rotation, I cannot tell from these files alone —
  there is no timestamp to anchor it. The in-line champion refs (2026-08-06,
  2026-08-08) show the table was remeasured at least once during the span I
  can see, which is exactly the event that moved the 2p rail from 60.0 to
  54.913.

  Plain statement: I do NOT have a clean pre-2026-08-06 wall-clock history.
  What I have is the full generation history in the current log files, and
  within THAT history the 2p pin is old (it starts at gen 21822, which is
  ~18% into a 118k-gen run) and continuous, with a RAIL CHANGE near the end
  (gen 115202+) that is the recent part.

----------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - The 2p uprising pin is LONG-STANDING and STABLE: pinned from gen 21822
    to the end of the log (gen 118578), ~81% of the 2p run, continuously.
    The "long-standing" part is the -60.000 regime (gen 21827-100151, ~78k
    gens at the old 60.0 rail).

  - What is RECENT is the RAIL, not the pin. The active bound moved from
    60.0 to 54.913 after the P95_TOTAL_SPREAD remeasure (gen 115202+, the
    2026-08-06/08 champion regeneration). The specific value the coordinator
    cited (-54.91262773333334) is the NEW rail and has only been active for
    the most recent ~3.4k generations (gen 115202-118578). So "is the pin
    old and stable" — YES the pin is old; the exact -54.913 figure is recent
    and is a function of the remeasured table, not of a new saturation.

  - The 3p pin was an early burst (gen 1936-4631) that has since resolved
    (3p is off the rail now). The 4p coefficient never pins.

  - On the leak-into-free_workers hypothesis: the logs do NOT show
    free_workers pinned alongside uprising. The co-pinned cluster is
    strength / workers_late / auction_bid / urban_workers / workers. If
    free_workers is absorbing the saturated cliff penalty, it is doing so
    WITHOUT hitting its own rail — i.e. it would show up as a large-but-not-
    pinned positive weight, which is consistent with +5.307 (a real value,
    not a rail value). Worth confirming free_workers' own bound before
    calling it a leak rather than a legitimate anti-uprising/optionality
    price (see free_worker_cost_2026-08-27.txt: the sign is legitimately
    free, the positive value is a strategic choice).
