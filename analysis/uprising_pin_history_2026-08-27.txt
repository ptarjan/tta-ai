VERDICT
----------------------------------------------------------------
The 2p pin is LONG-STANDING and STABLE: pinned from gen 21822 to the end
of the log (~81% of the run's accepted generations), with no gap longer
than a few hundred gens. The -54.913 figure is the RECENT remeasured rail

the long-standing pin sat at -60.0 on the older rail for ~78k gens.

free_workers is NOT among the co-pinned keys at 2p (the cluster is
strength/workers_late/auction_bid/urban_workers/workers), so the logs do
NOT by themselves confirm free_workers as the leak destination — it would
show as large-but-not-pinned (consistent with +5.307).

ERRATUM (2026-08-27, appended; original framing kept visible)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Three corrections, each making the finding STRONGER than the original
framing. The coordinator dug out the pre-remeasure table and answered
both open questions raised in the original analysis.

(1) The old measured 2p bound was 9.1045, not 20.0, not 60.0.
    The original file mixed the OLD P95_TOTAL_SPREAD (300.449105) with
    the NEW uprising spread (15.0) to derive "old rail 20.0". That is
    wrong: the spread row ALSO moved at remeasure (33 -> 15), so the
    correct old bound is 300.449105 / 33.0 = 9.1045. The -9.105 regime
    I flagged as "transient" at gen 107470–108787 was in fact the
    CORRECT MEASURED BOUND OF ITS DAY — pinned exactly at rail for 138
    gens (56 distinct). It was not a transient; it was the rail before
    remeasure.
    Verified: `git show 36c7c06^:rust/src/bots/weighted/weights.rs`
    line 1758 `WeightKey::Uprising => [33.000000, 16.000000,
    33.000000]`, line 1931
    `P95_TOTAL_SPREAD = [300.449105, 485.704661, 414.407762]`.

(2) The "(clamp 60.0)" / "(clamp 120.0)" print in the log was a BUG:
    climb.rs:1364 was printing the global CLAMP constant (CLAMP_CEILING
    = 120.0) for every key, while runaway_weights (climb.rs:296) was
    correctly testing against effective_bound(key, players) * 0.95.
    The fix landed in the working tree (climb.rs:1366 now prints
    effective_bound to 3 decimals) but does not go live until the
    Sunday rebuild. So all "(clamp ...)" values in the log are
    meaningless; the per-key bound must be derived from the active
    P95_TOTAL_SPREAD table. This does not change which gens are pinned
    (the test was always correct), only the printed label.

(3) The -60.000 / -58.x values in the early regime (gen 21822–100151)
    were NOT pinned at a 60.0 rail. Under the OLD table the effective
    bound for uprising@2p was min(300.449105/33.0, 60.0) = 9.1045.
    A value of -58.x cannot have been produced by clamp() with a
    9.1045 bound. Resolution: those gens predate the remeasure commit
    (36c7c06, 2026-08-25) and the clamp mechanism that reads the
    P95_TOTAL_SPREAD table. Before remeasure, CLAMP_BLIND = 60.0 was
    the flat rail for all keys (the spread-table mechanism was
    introduced later); the -60.0 values are pinned at the pre-table
    flat rail. The -9.105 regime (gen 107470+) is the first
    post-remeasure pin, at the measured bound. The -54.913 regime
    (gen 115202+) is after a SECOND remeasure that raised
    P95_TOTAL_SPREAD[2p] 300.4 -> 823.7 and lowered the uprising
    spread 33 -> 15, giving the current bound 823.689416/15.0 =
    54.9126.

    So the correct 2p timeline is THREE regimes, not two:
      -60.000  gen 21822–100151   pre-table flat rail (CLAMP_BLIND=60)
      -9.105   gen 107470–108787  post-remeasure-1 bound (9.1045)
      -54.913  gen 115202–118608  post-remeasure-2 bound (54.9126)

    Each regime's value sits EXACTLY at that era's measured bound.
    The pin has been at rail in every era — the finding is that the
    pin is not just old and stable, it is PERMANENTLY AT RAIL,
    re-measured to a new rail each time the table is regenerated.

(4) free_workers bound check (resolves the leak-destination question):
    free_workers@2p bound = min(823.689416/2.0, 120.0) = 120.0
    (CLAMP_CEILING cap); current value +4.217 = 3.5% of rail. Nothing
    is constraining it; the "cheapest leak destination" has no rail
    argument. Displacement hypothesis downgraded to unsupported.

(5) auction_bid cross-reference (same remeasure event):
    auction_bid@2p also moved regimes at the same gen window.
    Pre-remeasure-1 bound: 300.449105/13.0 = 23.111 — matching the
    -23.111 pin at gen 107470–107510. Post-remeasure-2 bound:
    823.689416/10.0 = 82.37 (uncapped); the -22.446 pin at gen
    107515–107574 is at 27% of that rail, i.e. it moved off the old
    rail but is NOT pinned at the new one. The old-table pins at
    -57.x..-60.0 (gen 32454–99896) were at the pre-table flat
    rail. auction_bid and uprising share the same remeasure event
    and the same gen window (107387–108787) for their regime
    transition, confirming a single table regeneration as the cause.
