stockrate playtest 3p 2026-08-24
=================================================
Headline: V1 through V4 all LOSE to champ3p.json. V4 (the full ladder) loses
catastrophically. This closes the naive "just flip stock-vs-rate sign" line
for these eight keys the same way the two prior probes (farm_build_probe,
pop_probe, eightkey_playtest) already closed their own target symptoms.

Hypothesis under test: the frozen 3p champion prices FOOD STOCK at its
positive clamp ceiling (+60) and FOOD RATE near zero (+0.07), the inverse of
what the human-weight comparison (scaled by s=29.7797) suggests; same
inversion on resource_stock/workers_late. Built a 4-step ladder (V1..V4) that
flips these coordinates toward the human-scaled reading and measured each
against the champion.


STEP 0 -- dominance_repair round-trip verdict (READ THIS FIRST)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Source read: rust/src/bots/weighted/eval.rs (dominance_repair, DOMINATES,
non_positive_gates/non_negative_gates) and rust/src/bots/weighted/weights.rs
(WeightKey::sign_intent, lines ~1056-1480).

Per-key verdict for the eight keys in the hypothesis table:

  resource_rate   SignIntent::Free  -- no gate, not touched by any variant
  food_rate       SignIntent::Free  -- no gate; survives negative -> positive flip intact
  food_stock      SignIntent::Free  -- no gate; a NEGATIVE food_stock survives the round trip
  resource_stock  SignIntent::Free  -- BUT see the DOMINATES finding below
  best_farm       SignIntent::Free  -- no gate; survives
  free_workers    SignIntent::Free  -- no gate; survives
  workers         SignIntent::Free  -- no gate; survives
  workers_late    SignIntent::Free  -- no gate; survives

None of the eight keys sit in non_positive_gates()/non_negative_gates(), so a
negative food_stock is NOT repaired back to zero by a sign gate -- the engine
CAN express the hypothesis, on six of the eight keys.

CRITICAL FINDING, not anticipated by the task brief: `DOMINATES` (eval.rs
853-856) contains `(WeightKey::ResourceStock, WeightKey::BlueFree)` --
"resource_stock must be >= blue_free". champ3p.json has `blue_free = 60`.
V4 sets `resource_stock -> 1.538` but (correctly, per the spec: "ONLY the
listed keys changed") does NOT touch `blue_free`. Because blue_free (60) then
exceeds the new resource_stock (1.538), `dominance_repair` silently RAISES
resource_stock back to 60 on every load -- confirmed empirically, not just by
reading the code:

  $ climb --start /private/tmp/step0_probe_v4like.json \
          --out /private/tmp/step0_probe_out.json --players 3 --gens 0 --hours 0
  -> checkpoint written; step0_probe_out.json shows:
       resource_stock: 60        (written as 1.538, silently restored to 60)
       food_stock: -9.504        (written as -9.504, survives intact)
       food_rate: 9.08           (survives intact)
       best_farm: 5.334          (survives intact)
       free_workers: 8.013       (survives intact)
       workers: 3.89             (survives intact)
       workers_late: 0           (survives intact)

kindmatch's `--a/--b weighted` path loads via the identical
`bots::weighted::eval::load_weights` -> `parse_weights` -> `dominance_repair`
chain (kindmatch.rs:116,228-240), so this is not a climb-only artifact: every
duel below that uses V4 has its resource_stock intervention silently voided.
V4 "full" therefore only actually differs from V3 in `workers`/`workers_late`
-- the resource_stock half of the "full stock inversion" story never reaches
the evaluator. Per the task's own instruction ("drop that key from the
variants... a first-class finding"), this is reported here loudly rather than
patched around; the ladder was run exactly as specified below, with this
caveat attached to every V4 number.

CAVEATS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- V4's resource_stock component is INERT (see STEP 0) -- its measured
  catastrophic loss is driven by workers/workers_late alone, not by the
  "full" stock-vs-rate story the ladder name promises. Do not read V4's
  9.17%/71.04% as evidence about resource_stock specifically.
- V3's "best of the ladder" status rests on one arrangement being
  inconclusive (p=0.1374, interval straddles the null), not on clearing the
  null in both arrangements -- it is a smaller loss, not a win, and must not
  be reported as the latter.
- Only V1 and V3 were census-compared in full detail (V3 chosen as the
  best-scoring variant per the task's own selection rule); V2/V4 were not
  separately behavcensus'd -- V2 tracks V1 closely on win rate so little
  additional signal was expected, and V4 was skipped once its win rate showed
  a categorical (not marginal) collapse, plus its resource_stock component
  being inert reduces the value of a dedicated census run.
- All duels used the tool's own default seed (0) and threads=5; no
  seed-sensitivity check was run given the budget -- the margins (esp. V4's
  9.17% vs 33.33% null, p=0.0000) are large enough that this is unlikely to
  matter, but V3's inconclusive arrangement (p=0.1374) could in principle
  flip with a different seed and was not re-run to check.
- No variant was tuned mid-run; the ladder was fixed before any duel ran, per
  the task's instruction.
- This report does not re-litigate whether the human-weight scaling
  (s=29.7797) itself is sound -- that was taken as given from the task brief.
