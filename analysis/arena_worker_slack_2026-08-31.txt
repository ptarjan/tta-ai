# Worker-slack arena, 2p: unmodified champion vs a variant that keeps >= 1 free worker

**Date:** 2026-08-31.  MEASUREMENT ONLY -- no weight was fitted, no evaluator
weight and no climb code was touched.  This file reports numbers and the
did-the-constraint-bind check.  It deliberately does not rank the result or
propose anything.

## Why this arena exists

`analysis/human_vs_bot_curves_2026-08-31.txt` measures
`PlayerState::workers_free` at a matched turn-start instant over 632 human 2p
games and 300 bot 2p games and finds the bot holding far less of it from round 4
onward (r4 1.00 human vs 0.61 bot; r6 1.46 vs 0.46; r18 0.65 vs 0.27), while at
r4 the bot's food CONSUMPTION is HIGHER (0.75 vs 0.44) -- it has taken MORE
population and still holds FEWER workers free.  That table cannot say whether
holding the slack is worth anything.  This arena constrains one side to hold it
and plays it against the unmodified champion.

## Method

Two whole-game 2p replays per seed, both seats playing the SAME champion
weights, greedy 1-ply, identical `game::new_game(2, seed)`:

- **constrained replay:** seat 0 obeys the constraint; seat 1 is the unmodified
  champion.  This is the head-to-head game.
- **champion replay:** neither seat is constrained.  This serves twice: as the
  SEAT-BIAS CONTROL for the head-to-head (same seeds, nobody constrained), and
  as the paired-counterfactual baseline in the shape `--arena-food` and
  `--arena-pop` report.

Nothing else differs between the replays: same weights, same `rank_moves`, same
pending-interaction handling, same seed.

### The constraint, exactly

At every own-turn, non-pending decision point at which the constrained seat
holds at least **1** free worker (`SLACK_FLOOR`), it plays the
**highest-ranked legal move that still leaves it holding at least 1 free worker
after the move is applied**, instead of its own top-ranked move.  Formally, over
the bot's own `rank_moves` order, the first `m` with
`workers_free(apply(state, m)) >= 1`.

- Whether a move satisfies that is read by TRIAL-APPLYING it through the
  engine's own `apply::apply` -- the exact call `game::step` makes -- on a
  discarded clone, and reading `PlayerState::workers_free` off the result.  It is
  never read off a hand-written table of which move kinds take a worker.  This is
  the same clone-and-apply `WeightedBot::rank_moves` already performs on every
  candidate; the live state is untouched (guarded by
  `keeps_worker_slack_reads_the_post_move_state_and_mutates_nothing`).
- If NO legal move qualifies, the seat plays its own top pick.  This case is
  counted, not hidden -- it is the one way the constraint could silently fail to
  bind.  It occurred **0 times in 3000 games** (`EndTurn` always keeps a free
  worker).
- If the seat already holds 0 free workers the constraint is **vacuous** and does
  not fire.  A refusal cannot restore slack that is already spent, and requiring
  a `Move::Pop` there would make this the population arena instead of a slack
  arena.  45750 of the constrained seat's 287944 decision points (15.9%) were in
  this class.
- Rounds: the whole game.  No window.  The human/bot gap is present at every
  round the curve file measures (4 through 18+), and rounds 1-2 are vacuous
  anyway (no legal move in them spends a worker).
- **Move generation is untouched.**  The constraint is a pick from the champion's
  own ranked legal-move list, so the champion's own behaviour, its legal move
  space and its evaluator are all unchanged; nothing in `legal.rs`, `apply.rs`
  or the weights was modified for this.

### Measurement instant

Free workers are sampled at the FIRST move of every turn, off the PRE-move
state, with the same turn-boundary test `behavcensus` uses (`state.current` is
reassigned exactly once per turn, so "the actor changed" is exactly "a new turn
started").  That is the identical instant and identical test as the `freeW`
column of `human_vs_bot_curves_2026-08-31.txt`, so the numbers below are
directly comparable to its 1.00 / 0.61.

### How often the constraint actually intervened

| | constrained seat 0 | unmodified champion seat 0 |
|---|---|---|
| own-turn, non-pending decision points | 287944 | 291773 |
| ...holding >= 1 free worker (constraint live) | 242194 | 94436 |
| ...holding 0 free workers (constraint vacuous) | 45750 | 197337 |
| own top pick would spend the last worker | 111382 (46.0% of live) | 29106 (30.8% of live) |
| compliant lower-ranked move substituted | 111382 | n/a |
| NO compliant move existed (fell back to top pick) | **0** | n/a |
| games where the constraint never substituted once | **0 of 3000** | n/a |

Both columns are produced by the identical code path, on both replays and both
seats, so a difference between them cannot be an artifact of measuring the two
sides differently.  The two totals are not expected to match: the champion
spends its way to zero free workers and stays there (197337 of its 291773
decision points are in the vacuous class, against 45750 for the constrained
seat).

Because the constraint substituted at least once in every one of the 3000 games,
there are no by-construction ties in the paired counterfactual -- unlike
`--arena-pop`, where 53.9% of games were byte-identical between the replays.

## (b) HEAD-TO-HEAD win rate, with a seat-bias control

Constrained seat 0 vs the unmodified champion in seat 1, in the same game.
A win is strictly higher final culture (final culture IS final score in this
engine -- `game::scores` reads `p.culture` after `finish_game` writes the
end-game bonus into it).

| | wins / decided | rate | 95% Wilson CI | ties |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| constrained seat 0 vs champion seat 1 | 1450 / 2987 | **0.485** | [0.468, 0.503] | 13 |
| CONTROL: same seeds, NOBODY constrained | 1543 / 2980 | **0.518** | [0.500, 0.536] | 20 |

Difference: **-3.3 percentage points** against the same-seed control.

The control is reported because a flat 50% null assumes the two seats are
interchangeable, and they are not: section 1.9's round-1 civil-action grant is
seat-dependent.  This run measures that seat effect at +1.8pp for seat 0 rather
than assuming it away.  (Note that both existing arenas state a flat "2p null is
50% (symmetric seats, same weights)"; on this evidence that null is off by about
2 points.  Nothing in this report depends on it -- the paired counterfactual in
(c) is immune to seat bias by construction, since it compares seat 0 to seat 0.)

The printed VERDICT line applies an overlapping-CI test, which is conservative
and is not a test of the difference; the two intervals abut at 0.500-0.503.  The
paired comparison in (c) is the seat-controlled one.

CAVEAT, mirrored from `print_forced_pop_arena` (`economy.rs::increase_population`):
a `Move::Pop` moves ONE token from `yellow_bank` to `workers_free`, so this SUM
