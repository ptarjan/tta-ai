CEILING UP SWEEP -- 3-PLAYER -- 2026-08-24

QUESTION
Champion /private/tmp/opencensus/champ3p.json has five weights pinned at
exactly 60.000 (CLAMP_BLIND): blue_free, culture_rate, food_stock,
resource_stock, workers_late. A prior worker (analysis/
ceiling_playtest_3p_2026-08-24.txt) moved all five UP to 120 as one bundle
and it lost decisively (18.54% as A; champion 49.17% as A). That same
worker's per-key DOWN sweep (to 30) showed the bundle hides huge variation
by key: blue_free 3.33% (catastrophic), culture_rate 27.71%, food_stock
23.12%, workers_late 32.92% (exactly neutral), resource_stock inexpressible
(dominance_repair silently reverts it to 60 whenever blue_free stays at 60).
Because the bundled UP result mixes five keys of very different
sensitivity, it tells us nothing about any individual key's UP direction.
This sweep isolates the UP direction per key (per group, for the
blue_free/resource_stock pair, which cannot be split -- see below) to ask:
does any one of the five pinned keys want to be ABOVE 60?

METHOD
Binary freshness check (not rebuilt):
  target/release/kindmatch mtime 1787622407
  src/bin/kindmatch.rs mtime  1786824906
  binary is newer -> proceeded without rebuilding.

All duels: 240 games, 3 players, seed 1001, 5 threads, from
/Users/pt/tta-ai/rust, run in both seat arrangements (variant as A, one
rotating seat vs two champion seats; then champion as A, mirrored, variant
holding two seats). The tool's own printed null for 3p is 33.33% every
time -- quoted verbatim below, never assumed.

DOMINANCE_REPAIR REASONING (rust/src/bots/weighted/eval.rs:853-856,1008-1020)
Read directly from source before running anything:
  DOMINATES = [(hi=ResourceStock, lo=BlueFree), (hi=WonderPotential,
  lo=WonderPromise)]. The repair loop: for each (hi, lo) pair, if
  lo's value > hi's value, hi is silently raised to match lo. So if
  blue_free (lo) exceeds resource_stock (hi), resource_stock gets dragged
  up to blue_free's value -- raising blue_free alone above resource_stock
  is inexpressible by construction, matching what the DOWN-sweep worker
  already found in the opposite direction. This is why blue_free and
  resource_stock are moved TOGETHER in this sweep as a single "stockpair"
  variant rather than tested individually.

Four variants built from champ3p.json, changing ONLY what is named:

  1. stockpair_up: blue_free 60->120 AND resource_stock 60->120 together.
     Repair check: a=120 (hi=resource_stock), b=120 (lo=blue_free).
     b > a + 1e-12 is FALSE (equal) -- NO repair fires. Both values stand
     exactly as written.
  2. culture_rate_up: culture_rate 60->120 only. blue_free and
     resource_stock stay untouched at 60==60 (no repair). culture_rate is
     not a member of DOMINATES at all, so it could never trigger the rule
     regardless of its own value.
  3. food_stock_up: food_stock 60->120 only. Same reasoning as #2 --
     blue_free/resource_stock untouched at 60==60, food_stock not in
     DOMINATES.
  4. workers_late_up: workers_late 60->120 only. Same reasoning as #2/#3.

  In all four variants dominance_repair is a confirmed no-op: each plays
  exactly the written value, with no silent alteration by the repair pass.
  (WonderPotential/WonderPromise are also untouched in every variant, so
  that DOMINATES pair is likewise never engaged here.)

VERDICT
None of the four UP variants beat the champion in both arrangements, so
this sweep does not overturn the prior report's headline: nothing tested
so far shows a key that wants to be above 60. But "all four lose" is not
quite what happened, and the bundle-hides-variation finding from the DOWN
sweep repeats here in the UP direction too:

  - stockpair_up (blue_free+resource_stock) and food_stock_up both lose
    decisively and by a wide margin in both arrangements -- for these,
    120 is clearly worse than 60. Combined with the DOWN sweep (blue_free
    alone at 30 was catastrophic at 3.33%; food_stock alone at 30 lost
    23.12%/39.17%), both directions away from 60 lose for these two
    coordinates. That is evidence 60 sits near a genuine optimum for
    blue_free/resource_stock and for food_stock, not evidence of a
    ceiling suppressing them.

  - culture_rate_up and workers_late_up are BOTH inconclusive in both
    arrangements -- moving them to 120 is statistically indistinguishable
    from leaving them at 60. This is a real asymmetry for culture_rate:
    the DOWN sweep found culture_rate at 30 to be a clear, significant
    loss (27.71%/41.46%, both p<0.05), while UP to 120 here shows no
    significant effect either way. That pattern -- clear loss below 60,
    no measurable change above 60 -- is consistent with 60 sitting at or
    near the top of a plateau for culture_rate, not with 60 suppressing a
    higher optimum. It is NOT proof the key is capped; a single point at
    120 cannot distinguish "60 is optimal and 120 is an unpunished
    plateau" from "the true optimum is somewhere between 60 and 120 and
    this test lacks the power to see it." workers_late is flat in both
    directions (30 and 120 both neutral against 60), the same conclusion
    the DOWN sweep already reached for that key on its own.

  Plainly: no key in this sweep shows a statistically significant WIN from
  going above 60, so nothing here demonstrates that any of the five pinned
  keys is pinned below its optimum. Two keys (the blue_free/resource_stock
  pair, food_stock) actively want to stay at or near 60 in both tested
  directions. Two keys (culture_rate, workers_late) show no measurable
  preference for 120 over 60 -- flat, not winning -- which for culture_rate
  in particular leaves open (but does not establish) that the optimum
  could sit somewhere in the untested 60-120 interior. Combined with the
  original bundled-UP loss and the full DOWN sweep, this closes the
  "ceiling is suppressing something" question for blue_free,
  resource_stock, food_stock and (with lower confidence) workers_late;
  culture_rate is the one key left with a genuinely open, not closed,
  question about values strictly between 60 and 120.

CAVEATS
- Single fixed seed (1001) per duel throughout, same as the prior report;
  no seed-repeat variance estimate exists beyond the tool's own printed
  binomial CI, so a seed-dependent artifact cannot be ruled out for any
  individual number above.
- blue_free and resource_stock could only be tested as a bundled pair at
  120, never in isolation: dominance_repair makes "blue_free above
  resource_stock" inexpressible by construction (see reasoning above), the
  same limitation the prior DOWN sweep hit in the opposite direction for
  resource_stock alone. Whether blue_free specifically (as opposed to the
  pair) wants to be above 60 remains untested and untestable by this
  method without also raising resource_stock.
- 120 is a single point, not a curve -- for culture_rate and workers_late,
  "inconclusive at 120" does not rule out a real, measurable optimum
  strictly between 60 and 120 (or beyond 120 for culture_rate specifically,
  though that direction was not probed and is not suggested by any result
  here). No intermediate values were tested.
- All duels used --players 3 only, matching the assignment; nothing here
  speaks to 2p or 4p behavior of these five keys.
- Games-per-duel was 240, matching the assignment and the prior report;
  culture_rate_up's A-as-variant result (p=0.0906) sits close enough to the
  conventional 0.05 line that more games could tip it either way -- it is
  reported here as inconclusive, not as a confirmed null result.
- This sweep only tested +100% (60->120) as the UP magnitude, matching the
  prior report's bundle. Smaller or larger UP steps were not tried.
