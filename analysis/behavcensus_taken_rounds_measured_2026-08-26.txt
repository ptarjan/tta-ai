VERDICT
-------
Hypothesis (a) from the earlier file (two physical copies of the same
government / the Revolution path) is NOT supported: Government is only
2 of 14 PLAYED mismatches, and the dominant card is Reserves (III), an
Action, not a government.

The actual pattern is a single mechanism, visible in the presence
column:

  (1) 16/17 `absent` = the card left hand_civil (played or antiquated)
      with NO take ever recorded for that CardId under that player.
      The recorder's universe is "non-wonder cards observed leaving
      the row INTO hand_civil via Move::Take" (behavcensus.rs:1589-
      1604); the played/antiquated universe is "cards removed from
      hand_civil" (PlayAction/Develop/Revolution/PlayLeader for
      PLAYED, the age-transition hand diff for ANTIQUATED). A card
      that entered hand_civil without a Move::Take the recorder saw
      falls between the two universes.

  (2) 1/17 `key-present-empty` (Reserves (III), actor 2, round 15)
      = a take WAS recorded but its queue entry was already popped.
      Reserves (III) has count 3 in 3p (data/cards_military_actions.
      json: cards[141]); three physical copies under one CardId share
      one queue. The pop order and the play order disagree when a copy
      is played after an earlier pop consumed the only recorded entry
      -- i.e. one of the three copies entered hand without a recorded
      take, so two plays of the CardId find only one queue entry.
      Same root cause as (1), one step downstream.

Where do the unrecorded takes come from? The candidates, in order of
how well the sample fits:

  (i)  Age-transition hand refill / re-take paths that bypass
       Move::Take. The mismatches are ALL rounds 14-20, i.e. at or
       after the Age III transition (Age III starts ~round 14-16 in 3p
       self-play), and the dominant cards are Age-III cards (Reserves
       III, Military Build-Up, Rockets, the round-18 governments
       Democracy/Fundamentalism) plus late-age cards from earlier
       ages (Military Theory, Justice System, Riflemen, Churchill).
       A card taken in Age I/II and still in hand at the transition
       should have its take recorded in that earlier age -- so the
       age-III concentration says the unrecorded entries are cards
       that ENTERED hand late, through a path the recorder does not
       see. The recorder only sees Move::Take (behavcensus.rs:1456-
       1459: taken_card is non-None for Move::Take only). Any other
       path into hand_civil -- a card returned to hand, a re-take
       after antiquation within the same age, an engine path that
       pushes to hand_civil without a Take move -- is invisible to
       it.

  (ii) The 17 are 17 individual card instances; the census cannot
       distinguish "taken in a round the recorder was not sampling"
       from "entered hand without a take" because taken_rounds has no
       per-round presence log. A follow-up that records, per player,
       the hand_civil membership at every round (a pre/post diff like
       the antiquation branch already does, behavcensus.rs:1634-
       1644) would separate the two: a card that APPEARS in the
       membership diff without a Move::Take in that round is a
       non-Take entry; a card that was present for rounds before its
       play but whose take round predates the recorder's window is a
       windowing artifact.

What the measurement does NOT show:
  - No merge effect (single-threaded, still 17 in 200 games).
  - No cross-player misattribution (every mismatch's actor/player
    matches the hand the card left; the recorder records under
    state.current, the player who took, and a player only takes into
    their own hand).
  - No Wonder involvement (wonders are excluded from both sides, as
    the earlier file established: never in hand_civil, never in
    played_civil_card).

RECOMMENDED FOLLOW-UP (not done in this session -- read-write scope
here was the two instrumentation sites only)
--------------------------------------------
Add a per-round hand_civil membership diff (pre/post, like the
antiquation branch) that fires on EVERY move, not only age
transitions, and logs cards that enter hand_civil without a Move::Take
in that round. That log, cross-referenced against the 17-mismatch
sample, will name the exact entry path (and the exact engine function
that performs it) instead of the class of paths in (i). The fix,
whichever path it is, belongs in the recorder (record the non-Take
entry as a take with the round it was observed), not in the
mismatch counter.
