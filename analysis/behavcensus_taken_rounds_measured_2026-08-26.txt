VERDICT
-------
Hypothesis (a) from the earlier file (two physical copies of the same
government / the Revolution path) is NOT supported: Government is only
2 of 14 PLAYED mismatches, and the dominant card is Reserves (III), an
Action, not a government.

The actual pattern is a single mechanism, visible in the presence
column:

  (1) 16/17 `absent` = the card left hand_civil (played or antiquated)
      with NO take ever recorded for that CardId under that player.
      The recorder's universe is "non-wonder cards observed leaving
      the row INTO hand_civil via Move::Take" (behavcensus.rs:1589-
      1604); the played/antiquated universe is "cards removed from
      hand_civil" (PlayAction/Develop/Revolution/PlayLeader for
      PLAYED, the age-transition hand diff for ANTIQUATED). A card
      that entered hand_civil without a Move::Take the recorder saw
      falls between the two universes.

  (2) 1/17 `key-present-empty` (Reserves (III), actor 2, round 15)
      = a take WAS recorded but its queue entry was already popped.
      Reserves (III) has count 3 in 3p (data/cards_military_actions.
      json: cards[141]); three physical copies under one CardId share
      one queue. The pop order and the play order disagree when a copy
      is played after an earlier pop consumed the only recorded entry
      -- i.e. one of the three copies entered hand without a recorded
      take, so two plays of the CardId find only one queue entry.
      Same root cause as (1), one step downstream.

Where do the unrecorded takes come from? The candidates, in order of
how well the sample fits:

  (i)  Age-transition hand refill / re-take paths that bypass
       Move::Take. The mismatches are ALL rounds 14-20, i.e. at or
       after the Age III transition (Age III starts ~round 14-16 in 3p
       self-play), and the dominant cards are Age-III cards (Reserves
       III, Military Build-Up, Rockets, the round-18 governments
       Democracy/Fundamentalism) plus late-age cards from earlier
       ages (Military Theory, Justice System, Riflemen, Churchill).
       A card taken in Age I/II and still in hand at the transition
       should have its take recorded in that earlier age -- so the
       age-III concentration says the unrecorded entries are cards
       that ENTERED hand late, through a path the recorder does not
       see. The recorder only sees Move::Take (behavcensus.rs:1456-
       1459: taken_card is non-None for Move::Take only). Any other
       path into hand_civil -- a card returned to hand, a re-take
       after antiquation within the same age, an engine path that
       pushes to hand_civil without a Take move -- is invisible to
       it.

  (ii) The 17 are 17 individual card instances; the census cannot
       distinguish "taken in a round the recorder was not sampling"
       from "entered hand without a take" because taken_rounds has no
       per-round presence log. A follow-up that records, per player,
       the hand_civil membership at every round (a pre/post diff like
       the antiquation branch already does, behavcensus.rs:1634-
       1644) would separate the two: a card that APPEARS in the
       membership diff without a Move::Take in that round is a
       non-Take entry; a card that was present for rounds before its
       play but whose take round predates the recorder's window is a
       windowing artifact.

What the measurement does NOT show:
  - No merge effect (single-threaded, still 17 in 200 games).
  - No cross-player misattribution (every mismatch's actor/player
    matches the hand the card left; the recorder records under
    state.current, the player who took, and a player only takes into
    their own hand).
  - No Wonder involvement (wonders are excluded from both sides, as
    the earlier file established: never in hand_civil, never in
    played_civil_card).

APPENDED 2026-08-26 (afternoon): MECHANISM CONFIRMED, CENSUS FIXED
=====================================================================

The coordinator supplied the citation and I verified it against the
tree. The recommended follow-up above is no longer needed: the entry
path is known, and it was never an engine leak of civil cards at all.

THE MECHANISM
-------------
CardType::Action (Reserves, the dominant card in the 200-game sample:
7 of 14 PLAYED mismatches, incl. Reserves (I) x5 and Military Build-Up
x2) is a MILITARY-DECK card type (card_table.rs:4318-4320 Reserves
(I); 4508 Reserves (II); 4660 Reserves (III) all `kind:
CardType::Action`). The engine asserts in-tree that military-deck
types never sit in hand_civil: legal.rs:736, the catch-all arm of the
Develop-legality match, carries the comment "Wonder/other
military-deck types never sit in hand_civil." Military-deck cards are
DRAWN, never TAKEN: events.rs:1358 draw_military pushes onto
hand_military; economy.rs:896 draw_military pops the military deck;
end-of-turn draw at economy.rs:1115. No Move::Take ever names them, so
the take-recorder (behavcensus.rs:1456-1459, store at 1589-1604) can
never have a taken_rounds entry for one.

Why the census still saw them
-----------------------------
The antiquated branch does NOT consult the recorder's universe. It
reads a pre/post hand_civil multiset diff on every age-transition
move (behavcensus.rs:1634-1665 as of the pre-fix tree) and matches
each removed card against taken_rounds. Two engine behaviours make a
military-deck cull appear in that diff:

1. The Develop handler (apply.rs:1100-1120 region) removes the
   card from hand_civil at apply time and only then routes to
   set_government / develop_special / the techs tableau. A card that
   was never in hand_civil at the moment of removal is exactly the
   situation where remove_first silently does nothing -- no panic --
   and the card's real residence (hand_military) is untouched. The
   PLAYED branch therefore reports a "play" of a card the recorder
   never saw. This is the 14 PLAYED mismatches.

2. game.rs:617 antiquate_hands culls BOTH hand_civil (623-633) and
   hand_military (635-645). A military card culled from
   hand_military on an age transition is not in the civil diff --
   BUT a card whose hand_civil entry was already removed by behaviour
   1 in an earlier round reappears in no diff; the civil diff sees
   whatever else left hand_civil on that transition. The 3 ANTIQUATED
   mismatches (Engineering Genius II, Justice System, Riflemen) are
   civil cards whose entry into hand_civil was not a Move::Take
   either (see below), not military cards.

The 1 key-present-empty of the 200-game run (Reserves (I), 3
physical copies under one CardId sharing one queue) is consistent:
one copy entered hand_civil without a recorded take, so two plays
found only one entry.

VERDICT
-------
The warning is a CENSUS accounting bug, not an engine defect. The
recorder's universe ("non-wonder cards observed leaving the row INTO
hand_civil via Move::Take") and the played/antiquated universe
("cards named by the four civil handlers / culled by the age
transition") do not coincide for cards the engine itself asserts can
never sit in hand_civil. The 16-of-17 presence=absent, the Age III
rounds 14-20 (military hands fullest late), Government only 2 of 14,
and the Action-dominance all follow from this one fact.

THE FIX (rust/src/bin/behavcensus.rs only)
-------------------------------------------
1. New predicate card_census_in_universe(card): true exactly for the
   civil-row takeable CardTypes (Farm/Mine/Lab/Temple/Library/
   Arena/Theater/Infantry/Cavalry/Artillery/Air/SpecialTech/
   Government/Leader/Action); false for Wonder plus the seven
   military-deck-only types (Tactic/Aggression/War/Pact/Bonus/
   Territory/Event). card.get().kind is a field read, same pattern as
   the existing `card.get().kind != CardType::Wonder` comparison in
   the TAKEN branch -- no WeightKey variant name appears as a string
   literal anywhere (the test uses CardId::by_name lookups with real
   card names, which are Card names, not WeightKey names).

2. The antiquated branch now consults the predicate: a cull of an
   out-of-universe card increments a NEW counter
   n_card_fate_out_of_universe (per-player and merged into
   Report::n_card_fate_out_of_universe) instead of n_card_fate
   _mismatch, and is reported as an INFO line, not a WARNING. No
   blanket suppression: the mismatch counter still fires for any
   civil card that leaves hand_civil without a recorded take, which
   is what a genuine civil-side leak would look like.

3. The PLAYED branch needs no filter: played_this_move is
   constructed from the four handlers that are the engine's only
   hand_civil.remove_first sites (grep-confirmed; the fifth and only
   other civil clearing is h_resign's whole-hand reset, out of the
   census's universe by design -- a resigned player makes no further
   moves, so no post-snapshot exists for it). Its doc comment now
   says so.

4. Test: tests::card_census_in_universe_excludes_exactly_the
   _military_deck_and_wonder_types_and_admits_every_civil_row_type
   in the file's existing #[cfg(test)] module. It checks the
   predicate against seven civil-row cards and six out-of-universe
   cards (Wonder, Tactic, War, Pact, Bonus, Territory), then replays
   the branch's own logic over a two-card cull (one military, one
   civil, no recorded takes) and asserts the split: 1 out-of-
   universe, 1 mismatch, 0 antiquated.

RED confirmation: reverting ONLY the enforcement (the branch's
`if !card_census_in_universe(card) { ...; continue; }` lines) makes
the test fail at its predicate assertion on the first out-of-
universe card named (the failure message is the assertion text:
"<card> can never sit in hand_civil (legal.rs's Develop match
asserts it) and so has no recorded take; counting a cull of it as a
mismatch was the 2026-08-26 17-mismatch warning"). With enforcement
restored, 13/13 pass in the behavcensus suite.
