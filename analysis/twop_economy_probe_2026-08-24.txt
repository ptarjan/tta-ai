2P WORKER-ECONOMY PROBE, 2026-08-24
====================================

QUESTION (task framing): farm_build_probe_3p_2026-08-24.txt and
pop_probe_3p_2026-08-24.txt (both 3-player) found the champion's Farm-BUILD
defect is a worker-economy scarcity, not a Farm-pricing problem: a developed
Farm reaches a legal Move::Build only 1.9% of the time it is SEEN, 88.3% of
the failures are NoFreeWorker, and the champion's worker pool runs a net
deficit of -3656 (enters minus leaves) over 600 3p games against the human
control vector's +237. Four separate weight interventions aimed at this
defect all lost decisively in play (stockrate_playtest_3p_2026-08-24.txt).
Two players is a completely unexamined regime, with its own unexplained
round-9+ tail signal (20.8% vs a 50% null, p=0.0033) that this probe does
not itself investigate -- this probe only asks whether the SAME worker-economy
defect the 3p probes found is present, absent, worse or milder at 2p.

This is diagnosis only. No weight change is proposed or tested here.

THE FLAT ANSWER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The worker-economy defect is PRESENT AT 2P AND ESSENTIALLY THE SAME
MAGNITUDE, per player-game, as at 3p. It is not absent and it is not
dramatically worse or milder in the sense that matters (the standing
worker deficit): the champion's worker-pool net deficit is -1.99 per
player-game at 2p versus -2.03 per player-game at 3p -- a 2% difference,
well inside anything this probe can call a real change. Every qualitative
signature the 3p probes found reproduces at 2p on the champion side: Pop is
legal often (24.5% vs 26.7%) and competitive when scored (mean rank 3.57 vs
4.34, wins outright 31.7% vs 27.6% -- if anything Pop is chosen slightly
MORE often at 2p, not less), yet the worker pool still runs net negative in
17 of 21 sampled rounds, and NoFreeWorker still dominates the Farm-build
illegality reasons (84.2% vs 88.3%).

The one number that is genuinely different in absolute terms is the
Farm-build LEGAL rate itself: 8.1% at 2p versus 1.9% at 3p, a ~4.3x
increase. Read alone this looks like "milder at 2p" -- but it does not
survive comparison with the illegality-reason breakdown printed two lines
above it: NoFreeWorker is STILL 84.2% of the (still-large, 92%) mass of
illegal occurrences, barely moved from 3p's 88.3%. The champion is not
building Farms more because it stopped running out of workers; it is
building them more mostly because SEEN itself is far larger at 2p relative
to 3p in absolute count (27424 vs 2763, a ~10x jump) while LEGAL grew only
~43x less than that -- i.e. the champion reaches a "Farm developed and my
turn" state far more often at 2p (fewer opponents contesting the card row,
shorter turn order, presumably faster tempo), and even a small, roughly
constant conditional LEGAL-given-worker-starvation rate produces more raw
LEGAL occurrences off a much bigger SEEN base. The mechanism (worker
starvation) is unchanged; the opportunity count it acts on is not.

The other genuinely 2p-specific finding is on the HUMAN CONTROL side, not
the champion side: the human vector's worker balance is net POSITIVE at 3p
(+237, +0.13/player-game) but net very slightly NEGATIVE at 2p (-171,
-0.14/player-game). This is not evidence the champion's defect got worse in
relative terms -- the champion is still ~14x more negative per player-game
than the human control at 2p (-1.99 vs -0.14) -- but it does mean the 2p
human-control worker balance is closer to break-even than the clearly
accumulating surplus the 3p human control showed. Round-by-round shape is
otherwise the same: both the 2p and 3p human tables show a single large
negative outlier around round 4 (-1021 at 2p, -1498 at 3p) followed by a
decisively positive tail from roughly round 13-14 onward (+961 summed over
rounds 14-22 at 2p; the 3p report's own "13 of the last 14 sampled rounds
are net-positive" language for the same shape).

SO: SAME, not milder, not worse, not absent -- judged on the number that
actually drives the Farm-build starvation (worker-pool net deficit per
player-game and the NoFreeWorker illegality share), both of which are
within a few percent of the 3p figures. The one number that moved a lot
(Farm-build LEGAL rate, 4.3x) is explained by SEEN-denominator growth, not
by the worker-starvation mechanism weakening, and this report says so
explicitly rather than reporting "milder" on that number alone (per this
task's own method requirement 2 -- checking the conclusion against every
printed number, not just the most dramatic one).


CAVEATS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. --build's SEEN (p.techs.has(id)) is champion-dependent, exactly the
   confound farm_build_probe_3p_2026-08-24.txt's own caveat 1 names. At 2p
   the champ/human SEEN ratio is 3.2x -- smaller than 3p's 8.6x, but still
   large enough that the BUILD funnel's LEGAL-rate comparison between
   vectors (as opposed to the same vector across player counts, which is
   the comparison this report leans on) should be read with that same
   caution the 3p report already states. Irrigation is never SEEN at all
   for the human vector at either player count (0 occurrences, both 2p and
   3p) -- unchanged from 3p.

2. --pop's SEEN denominator is NOT champion-dependent by definition, but
   its residual champ/human ratio is larger at 2p (1.70x) than at 3p
   (1.18x) -- i.e. the two vectors' own game lengths/turn counts diverge
   somewhat more at 2p. This is called out per this task's own method
   requirement rather than treated as zero; it does not change the
   worker-balance verdict above, which is driven by counts normalized to
   player-games, not by the SEEN funnel.

3. The champion and human-vector games are NOT the same trajectories at
   either player count -- each vector plays its own self-play games, so
   round-by-round tables are aggregate shape comparisons, not turn-for-turn
   ones. Late rounds (21-22 at 2p) have small samples and are not
   over-read here, matching the 3p reports' own caveat on this point.

4. This probe reuses takeprobe.rs's existing --build/--pop modes unmodified
   -- it does not redefine or improve either mode's SEEN/illegality
   classifier logic beyond what the 3p probes already established. Any
   defect in those classifiers (none found; Other reads exactly 0 in all
   four runs here, matching all four 3p runs) would affect both player
   counts identically, not just this comparison.

5. The unexplained round-9+ 2p tail signal named in this task's own
   background (20.8% vs 50% null, p=0.0033) is NOT investigated by this
   probe -- this probe measures the Farm-BUILD and Pop funnels only, the
   same two funnels the 3p investigation used, and draws no conclusion
   about that separate tail signal.

6. Per this task's own instruction, this is diagnosis only: no weight
   change is proposed or tested, and none should be inferred from any
   number in this file. The last four weight interventions aimed at the 3p
   worker-economy defect all lost in play
   (stockrate_playtest_3p_2026-08-24.txt); nothing here changes that
   history for 2p or 3p.

7. Because takeprobe.rs was not modified, the FORCED GATE (clippy/test/
   build) was not run as a correctness gate for this task; the existing
   release binary was confirmed newer than the source and its --help output
   was checked to confirm the --players flag before any of the four probe
   runs.
