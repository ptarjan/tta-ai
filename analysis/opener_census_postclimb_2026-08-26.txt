DEFECT 3 (61% of taken cards ever played; humans ~78%)
  Reported split by EARLY/LATE wonder tempo, but BOTH groups agree closely, so the
  excluded middle cannot move the estimate much:
    3p EARLY: 250419 taken -> played 143106 (57.1%), antiquated 50559 (20.2%),
              still in hand at game end 56754 (22.7%)
    3p LATE:   55695 taken -> played  32414 (58.2%), antiquated 11509 (20.7%),
              still in hand  11772 (21.1%)
  VERDICT: ~57-58%, i.e. NOT IMPROVED (slightly below the old 61%), far under 78%.
  CAUSE, and this is the most useful thing in the whole census: of 107313
  never-played cards, 73776 (68.7%) were NEVER LEGAL to play again after the take.
  Of those cards' blocked decision points, 68.9% are "nothing affordable" (an action
  WAS available and nothing in hand could be paid for) against only 17.5% "no civil
  action". The bot is not short of civil actions. It is buying cards it cannot pay
  for. That is a PRODUCTION defect presenting as a card-play defect, and it is the
  same root as defect 2.

  CA unused across rounds 1-3, not the whole-game share of actions.
