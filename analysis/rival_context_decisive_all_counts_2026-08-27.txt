capable_frac IS THE TIE FLOOR, NOT INFLUENCE
--------------------------------------------
Every one of these six keys has swing exactly 0 wherever its spread is 0,
so its capable_frac (2p 0.0380, 3p 0.0526, 4p 0.0600 for the spread-zero
rows) is ENTIRELY decisions whose winning margin is <= 0 -- exact ties
among the top candidates. It is the floor every zero-swing key reports at
that count and must never be read as influence (it is not, e.g., 3.8%
influence at 2p). The per-count margin distributions the floor is
computed against:

well (for the four original keys), with the rival_free_ca 4p exception
