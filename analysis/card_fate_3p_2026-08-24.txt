Card fate at 3p: the "late starters hoard cards" story is FALSIFIED.
But 39% of every card the bot takes is never played.
====================================================================
Measured 2026-08-24, 200 games of 3p self-play against the frozen
/private/tmp/opencensus/champ3p.json. Prerequisites:
analysis/wonder_tempo_3p_cause_2026-08-24.txt (the EARLY/LATE split and the
civil-spend crosstab) and analysis/wonder_progress_ab_3p_2026-08-24.txt (which
closed the wonder-pricing subspace and pointed at the take side).

The hypothesis under test
-------------------------
The late-wonder group reallocates its civil-action budget to `take` and ends
round 6 holding median 2 civil cards against the early group's 1. If those
extra cards are never played, the take is overpriced and the reallocation is
waste. If they are played, it is a real investment and the tempo difference is
a plan, not a defect.

Definitions
-----------
PLAYED     = the card left hand_civil via Develop / PlayLeader / Revolution /
             PlayAction. Move::Build and Move::Upgrade never touch hand_civil,
             so they cannot be this event.
ANTIQUATED = culled from hand at an age transition (RULES_SPEC.md §12.2).
Dwell is tracked per CARD IDENTITY through a FIFO queue of take-rounds, because
a hand can hold more than one copy of a card and the engine removes the
earliest first. Played and never-played dwell are separate populations; the
never-played one is censored at game end and is never blended with the other.

There is NO hand-limit forced discard in this engine to measure. civil_hand_limit
only blocks an illegal Take; it never forces a discard. That half of the
question does not exist, rather than having been skipped.

Result
------
                                        EARLY (n=192)   LATE (n=157)
  civil cards taken, whole game, mean       17.49          17.95
  ... median                                   18             18
  of every taken card: played               61.0%          61.7%
                       antiquated           16.9%          18.8%
                       in hand at end       22.1%          19.5%
  cards in hand at game end, mean            3.87           3.50
  dwell, PLAYED, median / mean              1 / 1.48       1 / 1.50
  dwell, NEVER played, median / mean        5 / 5.19       5 / 5.32

Finding 1: THE HYPOTHESIS IS FALSIFIED. The two groups are indistinguishable on
every card-fate measure. Over a whole game LATE takes 0.46 more cards than
EARLY - and plays the same fraction of them, at the same dwell, with the same
antiquation rate. The higher LATE take SHARE in rounds 3-9 is therefore a
TIMING difference, not a volume difference: LATE front-loads the same total
number of takes into the early rounds. Card hoarding does not discriminate the
two groups and is not the mechanism behind the late wonder start.

Finding 2, and it is the bigger one: 39% OF EVERY CARD THE BOT TAKES IS NEVER
PLAYED, in BOTH groups. Per player-game that is ~17.5 cards taken, ~10.7
played, ~3.0 destroyed by antiquation and ~3.7 stranded in hand at game end.
A take costs a civil action, and civil actions are the currency of this game.
The ~3 antiquated cards per player-game are unambiguous: a CA was paid and the
card was destroyed having done nothing.

This is a UNIVERSAL defect, not a property of the late-wonder group, which is
exactly why it did not show up in a conditional split. A within-population
crosstab can only find within-population variation.

What this does NOT establish
----------------------------
Whether 39% is actually bad. Human players also take cards they never play -
you take to deny a rival, or to hold an option. The number is meaningless until
it is compared against the same statistic computed over the 1011-game BGO human
corpus. THAT is the next measurement, and it is a diagnostic mirror only: a
divergence from humans is evidence about where to look, never a licence to fit
weights to human play.

Known defect in this census's own tracking
------------------------------------------
3 cards out of 6177 were played or antiquated with no matching take recorded
(0.05%). The census reports this as a WARNING rather than swallowing it. It
means at least one path puts a civil card into hand without a Move::Take. It is
too small to move any number above, but it should be found before this
instrumentation is trusted for anything finer-grained.
