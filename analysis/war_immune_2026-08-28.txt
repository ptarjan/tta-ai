1. THE EMITTING PREDICATE (features.rs:949-954)
-----------------------------------------------
  // RULES_SPEC 5.6 cliff, not a slope -- see `WeightKey::WarImmune`'s own
  // doc comment in `weights.rs`. `combat::war_forbidden` ORs `s.war_immune`
  // (a pact side printing `cannotBeDeclaredWarOnByAnyone`) alongside
  // `pact_forbids_attack` (already `blocks_attack` above); this reads the
  // same `Stats` field `s` already carries for this player.
  f.set(WeightKey::WarImmune, if s.war_immune { 1.0 } else { 0.0 });

Exact predicate: `s.war_immune` -- the player's `Stats` field, set by a pact
side printing `cannotBeDeclaredWarOnByAnyone` (a `PactBlock.war_immune` flag,
e.g. "Loss of Sovereignty"'s B side, per the weights.rs:944-959 doc). It
counts nothing: it is a pure 0/1 boolean. 1.0 iff the player holds (as a
party) a pact side that makes War against them categorically illegal; 0.0
otherwise. No which-pact, which-rival, or board information enters. The
weights.rs doc (944-959) is explicit that this was a deliberate choice:
`cards.rs::pact_value` and `rivals.rs::add_pact_block` both name `war_immune`
as one of the `PactBlock` fields deliberately left OUT of the RATE-shaped
decomposition -- "not a per-turn yield, a boolean gate on legality".

So the flatness is not an accident of the fitter: the coordinate is
structurally a 0/1 flag. The only thing the league can fit is the price of
being on the protected side of that gate.

4. COMPARE AGAINST PACTBLOCKSATTACK
-----------------------------------
The two keys are adjacent in features.rs (948 vs 954) and price two
different halves of the same RULES_SPEC 5.6 legality gate.

PactBlocksAttack (features.rs:555-569, 948) is a COUNT: one per pact the
player is a party to that prints `Special::NoAttacksBetweenParties`, plus
the deferred-credit contribution from a pending offered pact
(`dc.blocks_attack`). It prices "how many of my attack options are
mutually locked with a named rival right now" -- a rival-specific,
board-specific quantity, and it goes both ways: the same pact that protects
me from my partner also bars my attacks against them. That is why its sign
is arm-split (2p -0.122, 3p -2.768, 4p +8.287): the same fact is a net cost
on the two converged arms and a net protection on the under-converged 4p
arm, and weights.rs:1738-1739 records exactly this ("symmetric and costs
the holder its own attack option") as the CITED counterexample keeping it
Free.

WarImmune prices the other gate: a boolean, rival-independent, count-free.
It says "War against me is categorically illegal" -- the value of holding a
pact side that removes the War option from EVERY rival at once, with no
corresponding loss of the holder's own options (it only ever enters combat
legality on the attacker's side, weights.rs:1780-1789). What it cannot
express -- and cannot, because the emitting predicate is a single Stats
boolean -- is which rival is barred (all of them), through which pact
(whatever carries `cannotBeDeclaredWarOnByAnyone`), or how the protection
interacts with the board (e.g. how threatening War is right now). A flat
constant is the most the coordinate's information content allows.

5. IS THE FLAT CONSTANT DEFENSIBLE?
-----------------------------------
Defensible, with the usual caveats, and the gates are right:

- The flatness is structural, not a fitter artifact. The predicate is a
  0/1 Stats boolean; there is no finer fact in the coordinate. If the eval
  cannot express "protection against rival X via pact Y worth Z", that is
  not this key's defect -- the key is honestly priced as the all-or-nothing
  gate it is, exactly the RULES_SPEC 5.6 cliff shape ("declare a war ...
  illegal if a pact forbids it").
- The sign gate is NonNegative (weights.rs:1780-1790): "a protective flag
  that removes no option from its holder", paired with AttackCostDoubled.
  That gate is consistent with the rules: holding the immunity never costs
  the holder anything (the pact's `cultureProduction: -2` is priced by the
  culture coordinates, not here).
- The magnitudes are honest about themselves: 2p is drifting under the
  blind rail (spread 0.0 -> bound 60), 3p/4p are interior under a ceiling
  that the measurement alone would have set far higher. None of the three
  values is parked on a wall (clamp-wall sweep, same day: no WarImmune cell
  walled on any arm).
- The 4p-vs-rest gap (24.15 vs 0.10/0.18) is the same shape as the Tactic
  story: 4p is the least-converged arm (gen 21875) and its protective
  booleans are where it is still pricing large. Worth re-checking at the
  next 4p read; the prediction is that 4p comes down toward the converged
  arms' ~0.1 as it converges, not that the others go up.

Verdict: the flat constant is the correct price for the fact the coordinate
carries (a boolean legality gate), not a flag standing in for unexpressed
structure. If finer structure ever becomes needed -- e.g. "protection
matters more when a rival is strong" -- that is a new coordinate (a
rival-indexed War-threat x immunity product, say), not a widening of this
one.
