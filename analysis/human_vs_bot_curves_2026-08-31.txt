Sample instant  (identical on both sides -- this is the point of the exercise)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The FIRST move of every new turn, read from the PRE-move GameState, before
  that move is applied.  Turn boundaries are detected the same way on both
  sides: state.current is reassigned exactly once per turn (game.rs's
  end_turn), so "the actor differs from the last one sampled" is exactly "a
  new turn started".
    bot    bin/behavcensus.rs, play_one's `prev_actor` block -- the same block
           that already feeds Report::production_by_round and
           Report::alloc_by_round.
    human  bin/humanopenings.rs, the new "food and famine curve" walk over
           replay_game's recorded pre-move Decision states, same prev_actor
           test, same economy::production_this_turn call.
  One sample per player-round.  No half-turn offset exists between the two
  populations.

Column definitions
------------------
  freeW        mean PlayerState::workers_free at the sample instant.
  foodProd     mean economy::production_this_turn(state, actor).0 -- the
               worker-capped food this tableau would produce.
  consum       mean economy::consumption(yellow_bank) (RULES_SPEC 6.6 step 3d).
  net          foodProd - consum.
  foodStock    mean PlayerState::food.
  famine%      share of player-rounds whose PROJECTED shortfall is strictly
               positive.  There is no starvation flag in the engine: the
               penalty (4 culture per missing food) is computed inline in
               economy::end_of_turn and leaves no event.  The projection used
               is the SAME formula shape the evaluator's own
               WeightKey::FamineDeficit uses (features.rs:752,
               (-(FoodStock + FoodRate)).max(0)), with the deferred-gain terms
               dropped because a census sample has no trial move pending:
                   deficit = max(0, consumption(yellow_bank)
                                    - (food_stock + food_prod))
               so this is what the BOT can see, not a post-hoc replay of the
               penalty.  meanDeficit (in the per-side sections of the raw
               outputs) is the mean of that same quantity.
  n            player-ROUNDS that reached this round.  It falls late because
               games end at different rounds; a thin tail is a survivorship
               artifact and is shown rather than smoothed.

Reconstruction coverage
-----------------------
  All FOUR requested quantities reconstruct from the human journals.  Free
  workers, food stock and yellow bank are ordinary replayed PlayerState
  fields, and food production / consumption are pure functions of that state
  (economy::production_this_turn, economy::consumption), so nothing here
  depends on the journal printing a number.  Nothing was dropped.
  The one honest limit is the famine COLUMN's meaning, stated above: it is a
  projected deficit at turn start, not an observed starvation event, because
  the engine records no such event on either side.  It is computed
  identically for humans and bot.
  Human n is flat at 1264 (100% of player-games) from round 1 through round
  17, so nothing in rounds 1-17 is survivorship-thinned.  It drops at 18
  (1162), 19 (542), 20 (92), 21 (4) as real games end.  Bot n is flat at 600
  through round 18, then 598 / 502 / 70.
