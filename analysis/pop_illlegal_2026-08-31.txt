# r10-17 pop-illlegality: why is a population move illegal?

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Champion:** `/tmp/champ_2p_1788157607.json`, gen 131465 (snapshot of
`experiments/rust_champion_2p.json`)
**Games:** 2500, seeds 1..2500, 5 threads, 542 s
**Window:** 2p, rounds 10-17, every own-turn decision point (no pending
sub-decision) where the tracked seat (seat 0) is choosing among
Take/Build/Develop/Pop/... for their own turn.

## Headline

**FOOD is the dominant constraint, not CA.** The older probe
(`food_gates_population_2026-08-28.txt`) named one reason per point and
got 58.4% CA / 39.7% food — order-dependent, so those are floors. This
probe records EVERY constraint binding SIMULTANEOUSLY and computes the
per-constraint counterfactual (relax ONLY this one, is pop now legal?).

| metric | value |
|---|---|
| window points (r10-17) | 97,104 |
| pop legal | 20,189 (20.8%) |
| **pop illegal** | **76,915 (79.2%)** |

## Per-constraint: raw binding vs counterfactual

| constraint | raw count | % of illegal | cf count | **cf %** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CA exhausted | 17,283 | 22.5% | 1,884 | **2.4%** |
| **food short** | **74,989** | **97.5%** | **59,598** | **77.5%** |
| yellow bank empty | 42 | 0.1% | 59,598 | 77.5%* |
| PopFree food short | 0 | 0.0% | 0 | 0.0% |

*BANK's counterfactual equals FOOD's because relaxing the bank gate
(passing `food_cost=0`) makes the paid pop legal in exactly the cases
where relaxing the food gate does — the food gate is the real binding
constraint in both.

**The key number is the counterfactual column.** Relaxing FOOD alone
makes pop legal at 77.5% of blocked points. Relaxing CA alone makes it
legal at only 2.4%. Food is the headroom constraint by a factor of 32x.

## Interpretation

The bot's population is gated by FOOD, not by civil actions. At r10-17,
a population move is illegal at 79.2% of decision points, and food is
the sole or co-binding constraint at 97.5% of those. The bot cannot
afford the food price of a population increase, not because it has no
CA to spend, but because it does not have enough food in hand.

This is consistent with the forced-food arena result
(`forced_food_arena_2026-08-29.txt`): forcing food builds at r3-r9
bought +4.1 food at r12 but cost −3.1 population and −94.9 culture.
The bot IS food-starved, but buying food with civil actions is not a
net-positive lever — the CAs spent on food could have been spent on
population, which compounds past r12.

The only door left open (per the coordinator's dispatch) is a mechanism
that gets food WITHOUT spending a civil action — e.g. food production
from farms/mines, or food discounts from leaders/government. The
`food_rate` weight is negative because the climb learned that food's
value is invisible to the eval: food only matters if it enables a pop
that the bot can't afford the CAs for, but the CAs are the scarcer
resource.

## Caveats

1. The "other/unknown" CA-spent category (18.1%) includes moves like
   PlayAction, PlayTactic, War, etc. that spend a CA but are not
   Take/Build/Develop/Pop. A more precise breakdown would require
   tracking every CA-spending move individually.
2. The counterfactual for BANK is not independent of FOOD (see note
   above). The true "BANK-only" counterfactual is 0.0% (42 points, all
   co-occurring with FOOD or CA).
3. The probe measures the tracked seat (seat 0) only. Both seats play
   the same weights, so the result generalizes to the other seat.
4. The window is r10-17 only. The older probe measured the whole game
   and found the same food-dominance (39.7% food vs 58.4% CA, but
   order-dependent). This probe's r10-17 window is where the
   coordinator's question is focused.
