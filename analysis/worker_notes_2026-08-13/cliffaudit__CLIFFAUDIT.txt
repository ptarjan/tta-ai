CLIFF AUDIT -- legality-cliff sweep of rust/src/legal.rs and rust/src/combat.rs
================================================================================
Scope: every boolean fact about a player's OWN board that changes what
OPPONENTS may legally do to them (a legal-target gate, or a cost/strength
MULTIPLIER on an opponent's move). Not card value. Not an effect. Must be
priced as a 0/1 cliff, sign_intent left Free.

Two cliffs already existed as the template:
  - WeightKey::HasUnit    (RULES_SPEC 11.3) -- rust/src/bots/weighted/weights.rs:558
  - WeightKey::HasColony  (RULES_SPEC 5.4)  -- rust/src/bots/weighted/weights.rs:576
    prices legal.rs:170-179 (aggression_target_qualifies, Annex/StealColony
    half: q.colonies.is_empty())


1. VERDICT ON WeightKey::NoAggression
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
NoAggression is NOT a legality cliff on opponents. It is the mirror image.

  s.no_aggression comes from Special::CannotPlayAggressionOrWar
  (rust/src/effects.rs:1150, rust/src/card_table.rs:1926 -- Mahatma Gandhi's
  leader card). It is read at rust/src/legal.rs:301 and :339, where `s` is
  ALWAYS `effects::state_stats(state, p)` for the ACTING player `p` who is
  generating their OWN legal moves (legal.rs:253/406/787/973). So
  no_aggression gates whether the bearer (Gandhi's own holder) may play
  Aggression/War cards THEMSELVES -- it restricts what its holder can do TO
  opponents, the opposite direction from "what opponents can do to you".

  SHAPE: correct. It is inherently a single bit (has the flag / does not),
  computed as a boolean 0.0/1.0 in features.rs:616 already -- there is no
  slope version of it to confuse it with, so the HasColony-style "cliff vs
  slope" defect does not apply here at all.

  SIGN: correctly Free (weights.rs:617, group Board) -- restricting your own
  aggression is not unconditionally bad (it comes bundled with Gandhi's
  culture and the attack-cost-doubling below), so an unsigned coordinate is
  right, same reasoning as the rest of this table.

  ACTION: none. NoAggression is fine as-is. It is a normal card-effect flag
  that happens to already have the right (trivial) shape; it simply does not
  belong to the "legality cliff on opponents" family this audit is chasing.
  Mahatma Gandhi's card in fact carries BOTH kinds of flag on one leader:
  CannotPlayAggressionOrWar (self-restriction, priced by NoAggression,
  already fine) and OpponentsPayDoubleMilitaryActionsToAttackYou (the real,
  unpriced, opponent-facing cliff -- see #2 below).


2. COMPLETE ENUMERATION OF LEGALITY CLIFFS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every condition in legal.rs/combat.rs gating an opponent's legal move
against a player, or a cost/strength multiplier on it, cross-checked
against docs/RULES_SPEC.md.

(a) PRICED, no action needed
  - Annex target gate: q.colonies.is_empty() [legal.rs:176-179;
    RULES_SPEC.md:123 (5.4)] -> WeightKey::HasColony. Cross-file test
    already existed: legal.rs::has_colony_feature_agrees_with_annex_
    targeting_legality.
  - Colonization-force eligibility (a player at 0 units cannot bid/attack)
    [RULES_SPEC.md:278 (11.3)] -> WeightKey::HasUnit.
  - NoAttacksBetweenParties pact gate: combat::pact_forbids_attack
    [combat.rs:145-154; RULES_SPEC.md:131 (5.6, "illegal if a pact forbids
    it")] -> WeightKey::PactBlocksAttack (features.rs:312-326,564),
    verified to read the identical Special::NoAttacksBetweenParties check.
  - Infiltrate target gate, LEADER half: q.leader.is_none() [legal.rs:187;
    RULES_SPEC.md:129 (5.5, "remove rival's leader ... from game")] ->
    WeightKey::Leader (features.rs:615, `p.leader.is_none()`, same check).
  - Pact strength modifiers feeding attack/defense strength
    (pact_attack_bonus, doomed_pact_strength, combat.rs:169-214) -> the
    PactBlock.strength field flowing into WeightKey::Strength/Pacts/
    PactBlocksAttack via rivals.rs::add_pact_block -- rate-shaped, not a
    cliff, already the established home for this field.

(b) NEWLY PRICED BY THIS AUDIT (3 new WeightKeys)
  - War-immunity gate: combat::war_forbidden ORs in
    effects::state_stats(.., defender).war_immune [combat.rs:162-163;
    RULES_SPEC.md:131 (5.6)], set by a pact side printing
    cannotBeDeclaredWarOnByAnyone (PactBlock.war_immune, e.g. "Loss of
    Sovereignty"'s B side). cards.rs:1559-1564 and rivals.rs:264-266
    explicitly name war_immune as one of the PactBlock fields left OUT of
    the rate-shaped decomposition on purpose -- exactly the effect-
    decomposition-cannot-produce-it gap this audit exists to close.
    -> NEW WeightKey::WarImmune ("war_immune", default 0.0, group Military,
    sign_intent Free). Computed at features.rs:570 off `s.war_immune`
    (the same Stats struct `s` already computed for this player).

  - Attack-cost-doubling: legal.rs:308 and :348 (Aggression and War move
    generation) and combat.rs:262 (start_aggression) all double the
    military-action cost an OPPONENT pays to attack a player whose leader
    is Mahatma Gandhi [RULES_SPEC.md 5.4/5.6 MA-cost clauses], via
    Special::OpponentsPayDoubleMilitaryActionsToAttackYou (card_table.rs,
    currently only Gandhi's leader card). This is Mahatma Gandhi's OTHER
    special (see NoAggression verdict above) and was priced nowhere.
    -> NEW WeightKey::AttackCostDoubled ("attack_cost_doubled", default
    0.0, group Military, sign_intent Free). Computed at features.rs:576-580
    by checking whether the player's own leader card carries the Special
    (not by hardcoding the leader's name the way the engine's `leader_is`
    shortcut does), so a future card sharing the special prices correctly
    without a code change.

  - Infiltrate target gate, WONDER half: q.wonder.is_none() [legal.rs:187;
    RULES_SPEC.md:129 (5.5)]. The leader half was already priced (Leader,
    see 2a) but the wonder half was not: WonderProgress is a RATE-shaped
    sunk-cost magnitude (horizon::wonder_outlook), tuned to value how much
    a wonder is worth finishing, not whether TAKING one at all made its
    owner Infiltrate-able -- the identical "linear count can't express the
    first step" gap Colonies alone left for HasColony.
    -> NEW WeightKey::WonderInProgress ("wonder_in_progress", default 0.0,
    group Wonders, sign_intent Free). Computed at features.rs:616-622 off
    `p.wonder.is_none()`, the exact field legal.rs's own gate reads.

(c) DELIBERATELY NOT PRICED (not a legality cliff -- value fields, out of
    scope for this audit even though also currently unpriced)
  - PactBlock.attacker_strength (Special::OnAttackBetweenParties) -- a
    conditional STRENGTH MAGNITUDE (continuous), not a boolean gate;
    cards.rs:1562-1564 names it explicitly as unpriced but it belongs to
    pact-offer valuation, not this audit.
  - PactBlock.tech_discount / food_as_resource / resource_as_food /
    other_party_pays_science / culture_per_wonder_of_other_party -- all
    magnitude/value fields, same reasoning, not legality booleans.
  - p.war_declared_by_me (legal.rs:339) -- restricts the ACTOR's own next
    War declaration (can't declare a second war while one is pending), the
    same self-facing direction as NoAggression, not opponent-facing.
  - state.last_round (legal.rs:339) -- global game-clock fact, not a
    per-player board predicate.
