Add rowscale: instrument row_last_copy's per-slot magnitude on real Takes

Companion to takeworth.rs. Decomposes row_last_copy's raw feature into
its two factors (card_potential and P-no-further-copy) plus the row-sum
context, on 4952 real chosen Take decisions from 200 self-play 2p games.
Confirms the feature's large magnitude comes from card_potential itself
being an already fully-weighted, unbounded eval-scale quantity (max
678.256 on a single card here) rather than from summing many row slots
(avg 2.8 qualifying slots; taken card is ~80-90% of the row-sum) or from
a near-zero division (there is none). Cost-1-to-cost-3 scaling is
dominantly a row-position/card-quality effect (card_potential grows
~4.7x) rather than a scarcity effect (P-no-further-copy grows ~1.55x).
Measurement only; no pricing or evaluation logic changed.
