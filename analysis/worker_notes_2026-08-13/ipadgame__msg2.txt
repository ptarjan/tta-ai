Document the two protocol gaps the first live game exposed

Two things the doc got wrong, both found within three rounds of the
first game against the app AI.

The reported row is now applied AFTER the turn is handed back, so what
the doc calls "the row" is unambiguously the row Paul is looking at when
he is about to act. Record that, and record the reconstruction shortcut
(2p sweeps 3 leftmost, survivors slide, gaps deal on the right) with the
arithmetic check that makes it safe: new cards reported must equal gaps
opened, or ask for the whole row.

And add a fourth reported observable: the military cards Paul draws.
The "five observables, everything else is inert" measurement was about
what moves the EVALUATION, and it was taken over self-play, where the
engine deals its own cards and can never notice. The advisor plays
Paul's civil cards so it tracks that hand exactly, but military cards are
drawn at end of turn and the mirror invents them -- it recommended
preparing an event card he did not hold. That failure does not degrade
gracefully the way a stale rival strength does.
