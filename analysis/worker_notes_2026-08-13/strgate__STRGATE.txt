1. THE PROBLEM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Commit 578ee9e fixed a real bug (military built first in ~97% of games) by
gating a new, less-aggressive StrengthRel phase blend on a literal
`state.round <= 3`, repeated in eval.rs::evaluate, eval.rs::linear_features,
and rivals.rs::strength_marginal. Per EARLYMIL.txt section 3b, `3` was
chosen by trying an ungated version (27.7% win rate, n=3000), diagnosing it
as too broad, and narrowing until the number recovered most of the loss
(44.1%). That is a fitted parameter, selected by measuring outcomes, sitting
as a bare literal in engine code. This project's rule: the ENGINE computes
rulebook facts; the LEARNER learns worth. `3` must go.


2. DERIVING THE PREDICATE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
### 2a. What actually gates a political action
RULES_SPEC 5.1 ("In the Politics Phase you may perform AT MOST ONE political
action") and 5.4/5.6 ("Reveal [...] pay its military action cost" / "reveal;
pay its MA cost") both name a card that is already IN HAND -- an aggression
or war card can only ever be played from a hand, and a hand can only ever
gain such a card via a military-deck draw. Two more RULES_SPEC facts jointly
bound when that first happens:

- 1.4: "Current age military deck space is empty in Age A [...] players
  never draw Age A military cards." Age A's own 10-card military deck is
  instead shuffled once, at setup, straight into the current-events deck
  (1.6), and whatever is left over "return[s] the rest to the box unseen" --
  no Age A military card is EVER dealt to any hand, at any point in the
  game, confirmed directly in code: `game.rs`'s card-row `replenish` drops
  the whole remaining Age A civil deck the same way, and nothing in
  `economy.rs`'s draw step (`draw_military_step`) ever reads from an Age A
  deck at all.
- 6.6.4 (draw military cards, "1 per unused [...] military action, max 3
  [...] none on round 1"): confirmed in code at `economy.rs:734`,
  `if state.age_military != Age::IV && state.round > 1`.

So nobody can hold a combat-capable card before at least the first
non-round-1, non-Age-A military draw. Read directly off the one quantity
that actually tracks the consequence of this (hand size) rather than
re-deriving "when" from round/age arithmetic a second time (a second copy
of the same fact is exactly the shape of bug this project's own
`card_board_leader` precedent warns about, cited in 578ee9e's own commit
message).

### 2b. `EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND`: a proven lower bound, not a second literal
Measurement (section 7 below) found that hand size ALONE is not sufficient
-- see section 3. The fix needed a genuinely rules-derived floor,
`horizon::EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND`, chained from the same two RULES_SPEC facts
6.6.4 already gave: `FIRST_DRAW_ROUND = 2` (6.6.4, "none on round 1") and a
card gained at end-of-turn cannot be played until that SAME player's own
NEXT turn -- the Politics Phase where it could have been played already
happened earlier in the SAME turn's own sequence (5.0: Start-of-Turn ->
Politics Phase -> Action Phase -> End-of-Turn, and drawing is an End-of-Turn
step) -- one round later under RULES_SPEC 5.0's fixed one-turn-per-player-
per-round cadence. `EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND = FIRST_DRAW_ROUND + 1 = 3`. This
number is IDENTICAL to 578ee9e's own `3`, which is not a coincidence to be
suspicious of: it confirms the empirical win-rate search in 578ee9e
independently rediscovered the true structural boundary. The difference
that matters for this task is HOW the number is obtained: `578ee9e` got `3`
by trying values and reading off which one won more; this pass gets `3` by
chaining two already-cited, unrelated rules facts (draw timing, turn
cadence) that were never searched over at all -- changing either one (a
different draw-timing rule, a different-length turn) would change this
constant WITHOUT touching a single measured win rate, which is exactly what
distinguishes a rules constant (see `horizon.rs`'s own `AGE_IV_ROUNDS`,
labelled "RULE-DERIVED" the same way) from a fitted parameter.


3. THE REACTIVE-EQUILIBRIUM TRAP -- addressed explicitly
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The obvious predicate, "no rival currently has nonzero strength," is
REACTIVE: in self-play, if nobody ever builds military, no rival ever shows
strength, so the gate never closes, so strength never gets valued, so
nobody ever builds it -- a degenerate equilibrium that is also exploitable
by any opponent who does invest. `combat_unreachable` does not read built
units or strength at all, only hand-card COUNTS and `state.round`. Military
card draws are NOT a strategic choice -- 6.6.4's draw step fires
automatically off UNUSED military actions at the end of every turn,
regardless of what a player intends to do with them (Despotism alone grants
2 MA before anyone builds a single Warriors unit -- confirmed in
`economy.rs`'s own test fixture comment, "Despotism: 4 civil actions, 2
military actions"). So even a fully pacifist self-play population keeps
drawing military cards on a fixed schedule and the gate closes on time
regardless of anyone's actual strategy -- the same property `lateness`/
`rounds_left` already have. `EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND` is a compile-time
constant, not a per-game observation, so it cannot be gamed by any strategy
either.

A residual, honestly-flagged limitation: the hand-size half of the
predicate, once past the round floor, is a conservative UPPER BOUND on
combat-readiness (a nonempty military hand does not guarantee it contains
an aggression or war card specifically, rather than a bonus/event card --
the engine has no cheaper public signal that distinguishes them without
reading hand identity, which would be illegal). This can only make the gate
close a little EARLIER than strictly necessary in some games (pricing
strength slightly sooner than truly required), never later -- i.e. it is
conservative in the SAFE direction for the original bug (overvaluing
strength too early), not the trap's direction.


4. LEGALITY -- public information only
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
`combat_unreachable` reads exactly two things:
- `state.round` -- global public state.
- `PlayerState::hand_size_military()` (`hand_military.len() + hidden_military`)
  -- a COUNT, never `hand_military`'s actual contents. This is not a new
  public/private boundary invented for this task: `state.rs`'s own
  `hidden_military: u8` field exists specifically because "the app harness
  [...] mirrors a rival whose hand SIZE is public [...] but whose contents
  were not transcribed. Without them such a rival reads as an EMPTY hand,
  which is not 'unknown', it is wrong" (`state.rs`, `PlayerState::
  hidden_civil`/`hidden_military` doc comment) -- i.e. the engine already
  treats hand SIZE, for every player including rivals, as legitimately
  public, the same physical-card convention this task's own brief names
  ("knowing how many cards remain is public; knowing which, is not").
  `hand_size_civil`'s sibling doc comment cites RULES_SPEC 2.5's hand-limit
  accounting as the rule that actually counts a hand this way.

The function never reads `hand_military.as_slice()` or any card's identity,
for any player, including the evaluated player's own -- proven by a
dedicated test (section 6) that also confirms a violation would be caught.


5. THE REFACTOR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
One function, `horizon::combat_unreachable(state) -> bool`
(`rust/src/bots/weighted/horizon.rs`, next to `lateness`/`live_count`, the
module every one of the three call sites already imports):

    const FIRST_DRAW_ROUND: u16 = 2;
    pub const EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND: u16 = FIRST_DRAW_ROUND + 1;

    pub fn combat_unreachable(state: &GameState) -> bool {
        state.round < EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND || state.active().all(|p| p.hand_size_military() == 0)
    }

Called from all three sites that used to repeat `state.round <= 3`:
- `eval.rs::evaluate` (the StrengthRel phase-blend gate)
- `eval.rs::linear_features` (the mirrored `out[StrengthRel] *= late` gate)
- `rivals.rs::strength_marginal` (the same concept applied to card pricing)

No new WeightKey, no new tunable threshold: `EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND` is a
`const`, not read from the weight vector, and is not subject to
`climb.rs`'s mutation or `WeightKey::sign_intent`'s classification (it isn't
a `WeightKey` at all) -- consistent with constraint 4 (a threshold, if truly
unavoidable, belongs in the weight vector; this one avoids needing that by
being a proven structural bound instead of a free dial).

7. MEASUREMENTS -- honest, including the bug this process found
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
### 7a. A real regression found and fixed mid-pass
The FIRST version of `combat_unreachable` (commit 6d26d9d, pure hand-count,
no round floor) passed every unit test but, measured against real self-play,
gave 47.2% military-first at 2p (`behavcensus`, n=300 games / 600
player-games) -- a near-total reopening of the bug this whole task exists to
close. Root-caused, not patched blind: `STRGATE_DEBUG`-instrumented run
showed `combat_unreachable round=2 sizes=[2, 0] -> false` occurring 369/600
times -- seat 0, under Despotism's 2 unused MAs, draws 2 military cards at
the END of its own round-2 turn (6.6.4's automatic draw), making its hand
nonempty BEFORE seat 1 has even made ITS round-2 build decision, even though
seat 0 cannot play either card until round 3. A bare hand-size check calls
this "combat reachable" for seat 1's decision, which is exactly the
overvaluation the fix exists to prevent, just one round-of-the-game earlier
than the original bug. Fixed by `EARLIEST_COMBAT_ROUND` (section 2b/5),
which as a proven LOWER BOUND can only ever protect decisions the raw hand
check mishandled, never weaken protection past it. This finding, its
root-cause, and the fix are recorded here in full rather than only in a
commit message, per this task's "measure honestly" instruction.
