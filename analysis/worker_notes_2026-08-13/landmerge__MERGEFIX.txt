PROOF 6 -- paired 2p A/B vs /private/tmp/strgate's tip (ADAPTED; see caveat)
  CAVEAT, stated up front: this codebase's `arena`/`kindmatch` tools compile ONE
  `evaluate()` implementation into the binary and only vary WEIGHT FILES between the
  A and B sides -- there is no tool here that lets two DIFFERENT COMPILED BINARIES
  (merged code vs strgate-tip code) play moves against each other inside the same game.
  A true interactive head-to-head would require either (a) new plumbing (e.g. an
  external-process move protocol, which does not exist), or (b) a closed-form
  in-binary comparator reproducing the OTHER binary's exact formula (the technique
  STRGATE.txt's own `strengthrelab --opponent roundgated/legacy` used for ITS
  comparison) -- writing a new comparator for the phasecut mechanism specifically was
  judged out of scope/budget for this merge task (risk of introducing a NEW bug into
  the very proof meant to catch merge bugs, and the coordinator's own instruction to
  prioritize the deployment manifest over this proof if budget is tight).
  ADAPTED METHOD (same technique PHASECUT.txt itself used for its own cross-code
  comparison, Section 7b, just at 25x the scale: n=500 instead of n=20): ran the SAME
  weight file, SAME seed, as an A=B self-play mirror through BOTH binaries independently
  -- this clone's own newly-built `mergefix/rust/target/difftest/arena`, and
  `/private/tmp/strgate/rust/target/difftest/arena` (that clone's OWN prebuilt binary,
  run read-only -- not rebuilt, not modified).
    mergefix binary: `--a champ_backup/rust_champion_2p.json --b <same> --games 500 --players 2 --seed 42 --threads 2`
      -> mean moves 216.5, culture A 35.1
    strgate binary (same file, same seed, same game count):
      -> mean moves 216.7, culture A 35.5
  Both are effectively a coin flip in the ONLY sense a mirror match can measure (each
  reports win rate 50.0% +/- 0.0 by construction, since A and B are literally the same
  weight vector -- not informative on its own). The INFORMATIVE number is the near-
  equality of the two INDEPENDENT runs' aggregate stats: mean moves differ by 0.2 (0.1%),
  culture by 0.4 (1.1%) at n=500 -- small, and consistent with the SAME bounded,
  non-systematic floating-point divergence PHASECUT.txt's own Section 7c already
  root-caused and measured (worst-case absolute eval-score gap ~4.5e-13, occasionally
  flipping an exact tie between two candidate moves, cascading into one different-but-
  still-fully-deterministic game trajectory). No sign of a LARGE, systematic gap that
  would indicate the merge broke something beyond the sum of its parts.
  "Seats rotated, identical shuffles": satisfied by construction -- `arena` itself
