================================================================================
STEP 2 -- WHAT WAS PRICED, AND HOW
================================================================================

Added a Hammurabi rider to board_yields.rs::rider_of (the SAME dispatch table
Genghis Khan's strength-conditional culture and Winston Churchill's floor
culture already use), so it flows through card_potential's existing swap-diff
scaling (card_board_leader * sum_board_triples) exactly like every other
leader value -- no new WeightKey, no flat per-card weight.

fn hammurabi(state, p, out):
  if effects::state_stats(state, p).military_actions >= 1 {
      out.push((Feature::CivilActions, 1.0, Kind::Gain));
  }

Board fact used: `effects::state_stats(state, p).military_actions` -- the
GOVERNMENT'S per-turn grant (Stats field, default 2 under Despotism), the same
kind of production-level quantity Genghis Khan's strength check reads. NOT
`p.military_actions` (the actual remaining-this-turn pool), which a fresh
game's first-turn start-player handicap sets to 0 and would misprice Hammurabi
as worthless on exactly the turn a player is deciding whether to take him.

Documented assumption: whenever the government grants >=1 MA/turn, we price
the FULL printed cap (1 CA) as realized every turn -- not a fitted usage
fraction. This leans generous (an army rarely spends 100% of its granted MA
every single turn, especially pre-Age-I), but no measured firing rate exists
to discount it by, and inventing one would violate "compute over inference".

RESULT: Hammurabi does NOT beat Cultural Heritage. The two runs are
byte-identical to 6 decimal places. Saying so plainly, as asked, with the
mechanism nailed down (not a guess):

ROOT CAUSE, CONFIRMED BY INSTRUMENTATION (added and removed a temporary
eprintln inside board_yields::board_yields and cards::card_potential_core
to trace the live run; both showed the fix firing correctly, so the search
below is not "does the code run", it is "why doesn't it move the score"):

  1. The rider DOES fire and DOES compute the right board fact: the traced
     swap for Hammurabi was `[(Leader, 1.0, Gain), (CivilActions, 1.0, Gain)]`
     -- exactly the intended +1 civil action, correctly gated on
     Despotism's Stats.military_actions == 2.

  2. But `eval::parse_weights` (`rust/src/bots/weighted/eval.rs:990-1016`,
     used by every `--weights` load, including this one) unconditionally
     calls `dominance_repair` on the way out. `civil_actions` is a member of
     `STOCK_NONNEG_GATES` (eval.rs ~707-741: "nothing in the rules costs you
     for holding a civil action you have not spent yet"), and the frozen
     champion's RAW trained value is -0.5196954234135993 (negative --
     already flagged by eval.rs's own doc comment as a known, pre-existing
     bug: "the live 2p champion priced both negative ... which is exactly
     what makes overpaying for cards look like an improvement"). The repair
     clamps it UP to exactly 0.0 on every load.

  3. `WeightKey::CivilActions` (0.0, post-repair) is the SAME key
     `sum_board_triples` uses to price my new `(Feature::CivilActions, 1.0,
     Gain)` triple. `0.0 * 1.0 = 0.0`. The channel my fix feeds is real and
     correctly wired, but the weight sitting at the end of it has been
     silently zeroed by a repair that predates this task and is doing
     exactly its documented job (suppressing a bug that would otherwise
     reward the bot for OVERPAYING civil actions) -- it just also happens to
     zero out the one coordinate a correct Hammurabi valuation needs.

  DEMONSTRATION that the wiring itself is sound (diagnostic only, NOT a
  change to the real champion -- explicitly not "tuning numbers until it
  wins"): scratch/champion_2p_civilnonzero.json is the same frozen champion
  with ONLY "civil_actions" hand-edited to +0.5 outside dominance_repair's
  reach (i.e. edited AFTER loading, not by feeding a different raw value
  through the same repair). Under that vector every action-card take price
  shifts by a large, uniform amount (civil_actions is also read directly as
  a linear stock feature in `features::features`, not only through my
  rider), and Hammurabi's OWN score moves too (-9.38 -> -11.61) --
  confirming the CivilActions channel is live end to end. The DIRECTION
  moved the wrong way in this particular demo because `card_board_leader`
  (-1.0158, see below) is ALSO still negative and flips the sign of
  whatever comes through it; fixing one miscalibrated coordinate while a
  second one it multiplies against is still broken does not produce a clean
  result, which is itself the finding, not a bug in the rider.

  So: two independent, pre-existing calibration problems in THIS SPECIFIC
  frozen champion -- `civil_actions` repaired to 0.0, and `card_board_leader`
  still negative -- together prevent a correct per-turn action-economy
  valuation from being visible in this champion's decisions, no matter how
  correctly the valuation itself is computed. Retraining after this fix
  (letting the hill-climb re-learn `civil_actions` now that its floor is 0,
  and separately revisiting why `card_board_leader` trained negative) is
  the next step, and is outside this task's scope (a code/valuation fix,
  not a training run).

(d) ARISTOTLE and CARD_BOARD_LEADER, as asked:
  Aristotle (`scienceOnTechCardTake`, still and correctly on
  DELIBERATELY_UNPRICED -- out of this task's action-economy scope) scored
  -9.4 in the identical slot-2 substitution test, matching Hammurabi's own
  before-fix and after-fix score almost exactly (both leaders reduce, on
  this champion, to the SAME generic "it is a leader" (Feature::Leader,
  1.0, Gain) term with nothing else surviving the repaired weight) --
  confirming Aristotle is worth ~nothing to the evaluator too, as predicted.

  card_board_leader = -1.0158366438730428 in the frozen champion: LARGE and
  NEGATIVE, confirmed. Leaders ARE being suppressed/miscalibrated by this
  second, independent mechanism, exactly as flagged as a risk. Its practical
  effect is not simply "leaders always score worse than they should" --
  because it multiplies the WHOLE swap-diff sum, and several per-feature
  weights it multiplies against (`leader`: -0.445, `civil_actions`: 0.0
  post-repair) are also negative or zero, the double negative on the
  generic Leader term (`leader` * `card_board_leader` = -0.445 * -1.0158 =
  +0.452, positive) currently makes EVERY leader look mildly positive by
  accident, not suppressed -- but it will flip any POSITIVE-per-feature
  leader rider (Genghis Khan's/Winston Churchill's culture_rate, weight
  +0.00305) NEGATIVE, and will flip my new CivilActions rider negative too
  the moment civil_actions is retrained back to a real positive value
  (since -1.0158 * (positive) < 0). This is a live landmine for the NEXT
  training run and should be looked at alongside re-learning civil_actions,
  not left as-is.
