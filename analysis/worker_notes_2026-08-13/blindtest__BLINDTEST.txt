BLINDTEST -- can the 2p hill-climb's fitness signal see individual weight coordinates?
========================================================================================
Setup:
  Base vector: /private/tmp/rowdig/frozen_champion_2p.json (real 2p champion, gen 42825, 162 weights)
  Harness: rust/target/difftest/bin/arena (existing seat-paired A/B duel binary; see src/arena.rs)
  Command shape: arena --a <modified.json> --b <base.json> --games 200 --players 2 --seed 1 --threads 2
  200 games = 100 deals x 2 seats (A rotates seats to cancel the seat-order advantage), seed 1..100 (deal seeds), which is the "seeds 1..200" paired-game run requested.
  Statistical power note: with 200 paired 2p games, the interval half-width is roughly
  +/-7-9 points at these observed rho/deff values. A win rate landing within ~7-9pts of
  50% and with p > 0.05 is being called INVISIBLE AT THIS SAMPLE SIZE, not proven zero-effect.
  Bigger true effects would need fewer games to detect; a true effect under ~5-7pts could
  easily hide inside this noise floor. Treat every INVISIBLE verdict as "undetectable here",
  not "provably irrelevant to the evaluator".

dominance_repair check (source read, not just runtime): load_weights -> parse_weights calls
dominance_repair on every load. Checked NON_POSITIVE_GATES, NON_NEGATIVE_GATES, DOMINATES,
and NET_NONNEG_PHASE (src/bots/weighted/eval.rs) against all 6 target keys:
  - card_board_leader: NOT in any gate table (source comment explicitly says the
    card_board_* siblings are "deliberately left ungated"). No repair possible.
  - row_last_copy: NOT in any gate table. No repair possible.
  - ca_left: NOT in any gate table (control). No repair possible.
  - card_board_wonder: NOT in any gate table (explicitly called out as excluded from
    STOCK_NONNEG_GATES). No repair possible.
  - special_techs: NOT in any gate table.
  - uprising: IS in LOSS_GATES (non-positive gate: value must be <= 0, repaired to 0.0 if
    a positive slips in). Base -60 and tested value 0.0 are BOTH <= 0, so the gate never
    fires for this experiment; the requested value is played as-is. (A positive uprising
    test would have been silently zeroed -- not attempted here since the task only asked
    for 0.0.)
No repairs fire for any of the 8 tested vectors; the values below are exactly what gets played.

All 6 requested coordinates were tested (8 rows, including the two-value rows 1 and 2 and
the ca_left positive control). Nothing was dropped -- budget and time were both ample.

Command used for every row:
  arena --a <modified.json> --b base.json --games 200 --players 2 --seed 1 --threads 2
(200 games = 100 deals x 2 seats, A rotated through both seats per deal to cancel the
seating-order bias -- see src/arena.rs's doc comment on why this design is seat-paired.)

Notable details beyond the headline numbers:

- Positive control (row 3, ca_left -2.1058 -> -8.0): 25.0% win rate, p=0.0000. This
  matches the task's stated expectation that ca_left is visible (ca_left=0.0 is known to
  lose at 38.2%; an even larger swing to -8.0 loses harder still, consistent direction).
  Harness validated -- trust the other rows.

- Row 1 (card_board_leader) is VISIBLE, and clearly so, in BOTH directions tested. This
  is a genuine surprise given the task's framing: card_board_leader was cited as the
  headline example of instability (-1.02, then -40.70, then -15.00 across recent
  champions), which reads like it's being blindly diffused. The A/B data says otherwise --
  moving it by +15 or all the way to 0 both make the vector measurably WORSE at n=200
  (p<0.002 in both directions). That instability is NOT explained by fitness blindness;
  something else is producing the swings (e.g. interaction with a correlated coordinate,
  the climb overshooting/oscillating around a moving optimum, or genuine non-stationarity
  in what "board credit for a leader" should be worth as other weights move around it).
  This coordinate should be taken OFF the "diffusing blind" list.

- Row 5 (uprising) is also clearly VISIBLE: forcing it to 0.0 (instead of its penalty
  value -60) costs ~24 points of win rate, p=0.0000. Its sitting AT the clamp is
  therefore plausibly a real fitted response to a genuinely large, real penalty -- the
  clamp may be under-sized for how much this coordinate actually matters, rather than
  evidence the climb can't see it. (This experiment cannot distinguish "-60 is optimal"
  from "the true optimum is more negative than -60 and the clamp is truncating it" --
  both look identical from a single 0.0-vs-base comparison. It only rules out "the climb
  can't tell -60 from 0", which it clearly can.)

- Row 6 (card_board_wonder) produced an unusually clean result: win rate EXACTLY 50.0%,
  SE 0.0, rho -1.00, deff 0.00. That is not merely "not statistically different from
  50%" -- it means every one of the 100 seat-paired deals landed on an EXACT tie (A's
  seat-0 and seat-1 results canceled perfectly), i.e. flipping this coordinate's sign
  changed not a single recorded win/loss outcome in 200 games. This is the strongest
  single piece of evidence in this run for real fitness-blindness on a coordinate: it
  isn't just underpowered, the observed effect is indistinguishable from a coordinate
  that has zero causal influence on any decision actually taken in these games.

- Row 4 (special_techs) also came back tight (+/-2.0, not the usual +/-6-7), because its
  paired variance was unusually small (deff 0.08) -- so this INVISIBLE verdict has more
  power behind it than the nominal "200 games detects ~7 points" rule of thumb suggests;
  an effect bigger than roughly 2 points would likely have shown up here and didn't.

- Row 2 (row_last_copy) is INVISIBLE under both a small (-1.0) and a large (-10.0, more
  than 3x the base magnitude) perturbation, at the ordinary ~7-point power level for this
  harness.

STATISTICAL POWER, STATED EXPLICITLY: at 200 paired 2p games with the rho/deff seen here,
the harness reliably detects win-rate swings of roughly 6-8 points (see the +/- SE column
above, which is per-run since deff varies row to row). It does NOT reliably detect a
1-2 point swing except where deff happened to come in very low (rows 4 and 6 above, where
the achieved precision was much tighter). So "INVISIBLE" in this report means "no
detectable effect at this sample size and this run's paired variance," not "provably zero
effect on the true evaluator." Two of the three INVISIBLE rows (special_techs,
card_board_wonder) got unusually strong power for free via low deff; row_last_copy is the
one INVISIBLE verdict where a true small-but-real effect (a few points) could still be
hiding under the ordinary ~7-point noise floor.

BOTTOM LINE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Of the 5 substantive (non-control) coordinates tested, the fitness signal clearly SEES
2 of them (card_board_leader, uprising) and shows no detectable effect for 3
(row_last_copy, special_techs, card_board_wonder) -- one of those three (card_board_wonder)
with unusually strong, near-airtight evidence of zero causal effect on outcomes in this
sample. That is a mixed result, not a clean confirmation of "most coordinates are blind":
the coordinate offered as the headline example of instability (card_board_leader) turned
out to be visible and reactive to large perturbations, which argues its drift across
champions is being driven by something other than fitness blindness (likely a moving
optimum from interaction with other weights, or climb dynamics/overshoot) -- a genuinely
invisible coordinate should NOT reliably punish a champion for moving it, and this one
does, hard, in both directions. Meanwhile row_last_copy and card_board_wonder behave
exactly as the blind-diffusion hypothesis predicts: large, deliberate swings produce no
measurable win-rate change, so the climb has no gradient to work with there and whatever
values those two coordinates carry are effectively arbitrary from the fitness function's
point of view -- hill-climbing cannot ever set them "correctly" because it cannot tell
right from wrong on them. special_techs is in the same boat, with the caveat that its
base value in this particular champion (+40.05) is not actually at the clamp the task
described, so its instability story (if any) is not the "pinned at the wall" pattern
the other two exhibit. Net effect: this run neither confirms nor refutes "most of the 162
coordinates are blind" as a global claim -- it only speaks to these 6 -- but it does show
the phenomenon is real and severe for at least card_board_wonder and row_last_copy, while
showing card_board_leader specifically needs a different explanation than blindness.
