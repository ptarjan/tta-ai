THE TURN-4 "REVOLUTION TO THEOCRACY" BLUNDER
================================================================================
It does NOT happen under (b). It also does NOT happen under (a). On the corrected
board it is the #2 candidate at move 2 in both runs, never the pick:
    (a) REVOLUTION to 'Theocracy' [1 science, all civil actions]   -24.1
    (b) REVOLUTION to 'Theocracy' [1 science, all civil actions]   -22.9
Note the direction: zeroing the civil-action penalties made the revolution slightly
MORE attractive, not less (gap narrows from 24.1 to 22.9). So the sign gates are not
what suppresses this blunder.

What actually suppressed it was fixing the BOARD, not the weights. Control run with the
old buggy mirror's food value (f=6 instead of Paul's true f=3), same everything else:
    (a) f=6: still increase population #1, revolution #2 (-29.6)
    (b) f=6: still increase population #1, revolution #2 (-28.4)
So even f=6 alone does not reproduce it; the original blunder came from a board that was
wrong in more than food -- consistent with the note that the old mirror applied the
reported row and then ran its own sweep-and-refill on top of it, so it was scoring a row
(and a hand) that was not on the table.

Likewise the "took Pyramids at the expensive slot over Hanging Gardens at the cheap one
purely because it cost more" behaviour does NOT reproduce on the corrected T2 row: (a)
takes Alexander the Great at slot 4 for 1 CA, and ranks Hanging Gardens (slot 1) #2 at
move 5. So that specific accusation against the champion is not confirmed by this replay.

CRASHES / ANOMALIES
================================================================================
No crashes. The advisor accepted every update line and never panicked.

One thing that LOOKED like a bug and is not: at turn 3 the mirror's food jumped 4 -> 6
across PREPARE EVENT 'Scientific Breakthrough'. That is correct rules, not a bug --
apply.rs::h_prepare_event calls events::reveal_current_event, i.e. preparing an event
reveals and resolves the top current-events card. In the mirror that card was
Development of Agriculture (+2 food; it shows up as `pastev Development of Agriculture`
in the saved state). In Paul's real game a different event was on top -- he reported
"Development of Settlement" resolving in that window. LESSON FOR FUTURE REPLAYS: an
`event <card>` line is needed before a turn in which the human prepares an event, not
only before an end-of-turn reveal. This mis-resolved event is very likely where the old
mirror's phantom "food 6" came from.

Residual 1 food still unexplained: Paul's true f=3 at T4 vs 4 by arithmetic
(4 - 2 for the T3 population increase + 2 production). Our card table models
Development of Settlement as a FREE +1 population for every player; the app appears to
have charged food for it (or given a discount rather than a free increase). Recorded,
not chased -- f=3 was fed as authoritative.
