Free Worker Food Cost — Q1 rules, Q2 code, Q3 verdict
2026-08-27
================================================================

QUESTION
Does an UNASSIGNED (free/idle) worker cost food? I.e. is food consumption
computed over ALL yellow tokens a player has taken from the bank (including
workers sitting unassigned in the worker pool), or only over workers placed
on buildings? And, given the answer, is `free_workers` (emitted at
rust/src/bots/weighted/features.rs:840) citable as NonNegative, i.e.
"more of this is never worse for me"?

----------------------------------------------------------------
Q1. RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------
SHORT ANSWER: consumption is keyed to POPULATION TAKEN (tokens removed from
the yellow bank), NOT to how many of those tokens happen to be sitting on
cards. An unassigned worker is part of the population that drives
consumption. An idle worker therefore eats food while producing nothing.

The rule is a single board lookup with no card-placement term:

  RULES_SPEC 6.1 (yellow bank layout):
    "consumption = leftmost uncovered printed negative number (−1 on square
     17, −2 on sq 13, −3 on sq 9, −4 on sq 5, −6 on sq 1)".
    The number is revealed as the bank empties; it is a function of how many
    yellow tokens are LEFT in the bank, i.e. how many have been TAKEN
    (population = 18 − tokens-left-in-bank, modulo grants/losses). There is
    no "per worker on a building" term anywhere in the definition.

  RULES_SPEC 6.6 step 3d (end-of-turn, exact 2015 sequence):
    "d. Food consumption — pay food = consumption (6.1); if short, pay what
     you can and lose 4 culture points per missing food [CoL p.6]".

  CoL p.6 (cge_code_of_laws.pdf, "Food Consumption"):
    "The leftmost square in each section of your yellow bank is marked with
     a negative number. • If all these numbers are covered by yellow tokens,
     you have no consumption. • Otherwise, the leftmost uncovered negative
     number is your consumption. − Lose food equal to your consumption. − If
     you lost all your food, but not enough to account for the entire
     consumption, lose 4 culture points for each unit of food necessary to
     cover the difference."
    This is the canonical wording. The consumption number is determined by
    which yellow-bank squares are COVERED by tokens (i.e. how many are still
    in the bank). A token that has been moved OUT of the bank — whether onto
    a farm, a city, a mine, or simply left unassigned in the worker pool —
    has UNCOVERED its square and contributes to the consumption number. The
    rule never asks "is this worker on a card?"

  CoL p.5 (Workers and Population):
    "Any yellow token in your play area that is not in your yellow bank is a
     worker. An unused worker is one that is not on any card."
    This distinguishes "worker" (any token out of the bank) from "unused
     worker" (a worker not on a card). Consumption is a function of the
     former's count, not the latter's.

CROSS-CHECK — faq_v15.pdf:
  The FAQ does not state a separate consumption formula; it reinforces the
  same board-lookup model:
    "Consumption and Corruption: You do not sum all of the values exposed in
     your two banks when figuring out how much Consumption and Corruption to
     pay--you pay only the leftmost [uncovered value]." (faq_v15, "Consumption
     and Corruption" entry)
  The FAQ's one mention of "unused workers" is about UPRISES, not food:
    "Unused Workers have absolutely nothing to do with the number of Discontent
     Workers you have... Unused Workers merely prevent Uprisings."
    (faq_v15, "Discontent Workers" entry)
  i.e. an unassigned worker's ONLY special role in the rules is offsetting
  discontent workers to prevent an uprising. It is NOT exempted from
  consumption; consumption counts it as population.

CROSS-CHECK — open web (not optional, but blocked in this environment):
  Fandom (HTTP 402), BGG (HTTP 403), Reddit and web.archive.org (refused by
  the fetcher) are all inaccessible here. I cross-checked the in-house
  web-captured sources instead, and they agree with CoL p.6:
    • sources/hypercheat.txt:121 — "Consumption: return food to the supply
      according to your empty population bank spaces." (population-bank
      driven, no card-placement term)
    • sources/namu_main.txt:124 — "you can replenish one lack of happiness
      for each worker who is not working." (the "not working" = unassigned
      workers; their special role is offsetting happiness, matching the FAQ;
      they are not carved out of consumption)
  No in-house web source contradicts the board-lookup rule.

CONCLUSION (Q1): food consumption is computed over ALL yellow tokens taken
from the bank (population), regardless of whether they are assigned to
buildings. An idle worker in the worker pool eats food and produces
nothing.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Q2. CODE
----------------------------------------------------------------
The engine's consumption is a pure function of `p.yellow_bank` (tokens LEFT
in the bank), with no card-placement or worker-on-card term.

  rust/src/economy.rs:65
    pub fn consumption(yellow_bank: u8) -> u8 {
        if yellow_bank >= 17 { 0 }
        else if yellow_bank >= 13 { 1 }
        else if yellow_bank >= 9 { 2 }
        else if yellow_bank >= 5 { 3 }
        else if yellow_bank >= 1 { 4 }
        else { 6 }
    }

  The actual end-of-turn payment (production phase step 3d):
    rust/src/economy.rs:1058
      let need = consumption(p.yellow_bank) as u16;
      if p.food >= need {
          pay_food(p, need);
      } else {
          let missing = need - p.food;
          clear_food(p);
          p.culture = p.culture.saturating_sub(4 * missing);
      }
  The economic-progress event runs the identical computation:
    rust/src/events.rs:1566
      let need = economy::consumption(state.players[idx as usize].yellow_bank)
        as u16;

  `p.yellow_bank` is the number of yellow tokens STILL in the bank
    rust/src/state.rs:492
      pub yellow_bank: u8,   // "Unborn population."
  and it decreases only when a token leaves the bank:
    rust/src/economy.rs:304   p.yellow_bank -= 1;   // increase population
  (plus combat/age-end/loss routes). It is NOT decremented when a worker is
  moved onto a card, and it is NOT incremented when a worker is unassigned
  (that only moves the token within the play area; the bank count is
  unchanged). So `consumption(p.yellow_bank)` depends on population TAKEN —
  exactly the rule in Q1 — and says nothing about card placement.

  The `free_workers` feature is emitted from `p.workers_free`
    rust/src/bots/weighted/features.rs:840
      f.set(WeightKey::FreeWorkers,
            f64::from(p.workers_free) + g(GainFeature::FreeWorkers));
  `p.workers_free` counts the unassigned tokens (state.rs:497
  `pub workers_free: u8`). Note the invariant:
      population taken = workers_on_cards + p.workers_free
  (modulo yellow_granted bookkeeping). Since consumption is a function of
  population taken, it is a function of (workers_on_cards + workers_free).
  An increase in `workers_free` — holding card assignment fixed — IS an
  increase in population taken, and therefore an increase in the
  consumption number (at a band boundary) with NO compensating production.

CONCLUSION (Q2): the engine counts every token taken from the bank toward
consumption; it does not distinguish assigned from unassigned. An idle
worker raises (or holds up) the consumption figure and adds no farm/mine
production.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Q3. VERDICT — is `free_workers` citable as NonNegative?
----------------------------------------------------------------
NOT CITABLE as NonNegative.

Reasoning:
  1. A bigger `free_workers` (holding card assignment fixed) means one more
     population token has been taken from the yellow bank. Per Q1 (CoL p.6,
     RULES_SPEC 6.1/6.6) that raises the consumption number at each band
     boundary (17→13, 13→9, 9→5, 5→1) and costs food every turn.
  2. Per Q2 (economy.rs:1058) the engine charges exactly that food and, on a
     shortfall, loses 4 culture per missing food (economy.rs:1063,
     RULES_SPEC 6.6 step 3d).
  3. An unassigned worker produces no food, no resources, no science, no
     culture. Its only positive rulebook role is offsetting a discontent
     worker to PREVENT an uprising (RULES_SPEC 6.3; faq_v15 "Discontent
     Workers") — which is a floor (prevents a total production skip), not a
     benefit that scales with the count.
  4. "More is never worse" therefore FAILS: above the level needed to cover
     discontent workers, each additional idle worker is pure food cost with
     zero production. A player can have strictly too many free workers.

The clean, useful answer: an idle worker EATS FOOD AND PRODUCES NOTHING, so
the NonNegative reading is dead. `free_workers` is not citable as
NonPositive or NonNegative — it is genuinely two-sided (it prevents
uprising up to the discontent count, then becomes a net cost), which is
exactly why it sits in the Free bucket of sign_intent rather than a signed
one. The +5.145 2p weight is pricing "having a spare worker to place / to
block an uprising" as a benefit; if the coordinator's hypothesis is that the
weight is instead rewarding pure idle food-eating, the rulebook does NOT
support a NonNegative sign — the sign is legitimately free and the positive
value is a strategic (anti-uprising + optionality) choice the league is
fitting, not a rules citation.
