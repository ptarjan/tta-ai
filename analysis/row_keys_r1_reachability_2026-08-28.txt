=== 1. What row_pressure() and row_last_copy() sum, and their zero conditions ===

row_pressure(state, idx, w, ctx) -> (urgency, bargain)

    zero only if no such card is both takeable and has card_potential > 0

  - row.rs:383-384 `let saving = costs::row_cost(i) - costs::row_cost(nxt);
    if saving <= 0 { continue; }` then row.rs:420 `bargain += saving *
    survive;`: every visible, takeable card at index i >= slide whose row

=== 2. Reachability at a round-1 decision (2p, round==1, hand_civil empty,
       1-2 civil actions, 0 military actions, row just dealt for Age A) ===

Checked game.rs's actual game setup, not assumed:
  - game.rs:398 `card_row: [CardId::NONE; ROW_SIZE]` (ROW_SIZE = 13,
    state.rs:16) is the zero-init.
  - game.rs:426-434: `state.civil_deck = build_deck(Age::A, true, n)` (20
    cards, comment at game.rs:428 confirms "That deck is 20 cards at every
    player count"), shuffled, then `deal(&mut state, &mut lazy_rng)` is
    called ONCE, BEFORE `new_game` returns and before any player has acted.
  - `deal()` (game.rs:543-556) fills every NONE slot in card_row from
    civil_deck until either ROW_SIZE is full or the deck is empty. Since
    the Age A deck has 20 cards and ROW_SIZE is 13, this call fills ALL 13
    row slots at game start, every time, at every player count.
  - game.rs:379-381 confirms round=1, and civil_actions = seat_index+1
    (1 for the first player, 2 for the second), military_actions=0 --
    matches the scenario's "1-2 civil actions, 0 military" exactly. Hand is
    empty because no card has been taken yet.

  CONCLUSION: at the very first decision of a 2-player game (round 1,
  before either player has picked), card_row already holds 13 real cards,
  not an empty or partially-empty row. The premise "the row can have empty
  slots in the first age because cards do not slide until everyone has
  picked" does not hold for the OPENING decision -- the opening row is
  dealt in full by game.rs:433-434 before round 1 begins. (It may become
  true again LATER, mid Age A, after cards start being taken/replenished,
  but that is a different decision, not the one in the scenario.)

  row_urgency (row_pressure's `urgency`): for n=2, slide = 2 *
  sweep_count(2) = 2*3 = 6 (game.rs:167). Row positions 0..5 (6 of the 13
  freshly dealt cards) are inside the slide zone on the VERY FIRST decision
  of the game, regardless of round number -- the cutoff (row.rs:328) is
  positional, not round-gated.
  VERDICT: YES, can be non-zero at round 1. Decided by row.rs:328 (slide is
  a pure function of player count) together with game.rs:433-434 (row is
  fully dealt before round 1's first decision) -- there is no round-number
  or "cards have not slid yet" gate anywhere in row_pressure.

  row_bargain_forgone (row_pressure's `bargain`): same fully-dealt row
  applies to the non-slide-zone cards (positions 6..12) via row.rs:383-384
  and 420. Whether `saving` is actually positive at position i=6..12 in the
  opening row is a numeric fact about costs::row_cost's schedule (not
  reverified here), but the code path that could produce a non-zero value
  is unconditionally reachable at round 1: no round-number or empty-row
  gate blocks it. Decided by row.rs:315 (visible is non-empty) and
  row.rs:383-384 (saving > 0 test), both reachable given game.rs:433-434.
  VERDICT: YES, reachable at round 1 (structurally; magnitude not
  reverified here).

  row_last_copy: has no positional/slide gate at all (see part 1). Its only
  zero conditions are "row empty" (row.rs:478, false at round 1 per
  game.rs:433-434) and "every takeable wanted card's outlook >= 1"
  (row.rs:507-509), a numeric fact about counting::civil_outlook on the
  freshly-built 20-card Age A deck, not something the code forces to zero
  by construction at round 1.
  VERDICT: YES, structurally reachable at round 1. Decided by row.rs:478-480
  (row non-empty) and row.rs:507-509 (gone > 0 test).

=== 4. Verdict ===

There is no CLASS (b) site for row_urgency, row_bargain_forgone, or
row_last_copy anywhere in rust/src: all three weight keys are read exactly
once each, only inside eval::evaluate (eval.rs:372-373,385), as plain
linear coefficients multiplying features computed by row::row_pressure /
row::row_last_copy -- functions that never consult these three keys'
values themselves. The falsification hypothesis in the prompt (these
weights acting as w.get(...) multipliers deep inside card valuation, the
way CardBoardCredit/CardBoardLeader/CardRateCredit do) does NOT hold for
this family; that precedent does not apply here.

The round-1 effect is real via CLASS (a) instead, because the premise that
motivated the falsification test is false for the opening decision: the
13-slot row is dealt IN FULL from the Age A deck at game.rs:433-434,
before round 1's first turn -- not progressively/emptily as the "cards do
not slide until everyone has picked" framing assumed. row_pressure's
`urgency` sum is gated by a purely positional "slide zone" (row.rs:328,
a function of player count only, not of round number or turns-taken), so
6 of the 13 freshly-dealt cards (2p: slide = 2*sweep_count(2) = 6) sit in
that zone on the very first decision of the game. row_last_copy has no
positional gate at all and is live across the whole row from turn one.
Reverting the three weights therefore changes the round-1 margin by
rescaling these already non-zero, round-1-reachable features -- an
ordinary linear-coefficient effect, not a hidden multiplier path.
