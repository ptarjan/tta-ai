Leader board pricing decouple survey (2026-08-28)
====================================================

Read-only survey of the four questions posed for decoupling leader board
pricing (follow-up to the card_board_pair finding). No code proposed or
edited. All paths relative to /Users/pt/tta-ai.

(1) What does cards::card_yields return for a Leader id?
--------------------------------------------------------

NOT empty. card_yields (rust/src/bots/weighted/cards.rs:223) has no
Leader-specific branch and no leader exclusion: for a leader id it pushes
the general "effects block" triples from cards.rs:286-333 (the else-branch
at :280): culture/science/food/resource stock gains, civil/military actions
(the kind != Government guard at :296 passes for leaders), short-rate
culture/science, strength, happy, yellow bank, blue free, hand limits,
colonize bonus. How much a given leader actually returns depends on its
printed effects data (a pure rider leader like Moses still pushes whatever
its effects block prints; a leader with no printed fields in those buckets
returns an empty vec). It is therefore data-dependent, not structurally
empty.

Why that does not make the static table the leader's board channel:
card_potential_core (cards.rs:1920-2069) reaches the static-table lines
(:2053-2054, sum_yields over card_yields) only when the swap branch at
:2040-2050 does NOT fire. For a Leader that happens in exactly two cases:
  - board is None (no board passed), or
  - credit_board == 0.0 (the wall / degenerate-pair case).
The swap branch comment at cards.rs:2044-2047 says it explicitly: a swap
card (leader/government/wonder) is priced ONLY by the diff, because
card_yields would count a leader's printed gain a second time on top of
the delta that already contains it. board_yields::board_yields (board_yields.rs:852-896)
builds the diff from swap_stats (board_yields.rs:303) via delta_triples
(board_yields.rs:355) -- a real before/after stat recompute, NOT a call to
card_yields -- plus for a Leader only the generic (Feature::Leader, 1.0)
term when the player has no leader yet (board_yields.rs:876-882) and
rider_delta (board_yields.rs:524).

So: the static card_yields table is NOT a second board-value channel for a
leader. It is only reached as a FALLBACK when board-aware pricing is
unavailable (board None) or gated off (credit_board == 0.0). And when
credit_board == 0.0 AND base != 0.0 the fallback's early-return gate at
cards.rs:2034-2043 is not taken either (it requires base == 0.0 AND
credit_board == 0.0), so a leader at the wall with nonzero base is priced
by the plain sum_yields scratch path at :2053-2055 -- its printed static
effects at the rate credit, with ZERO board awareness. The ONLY board-aware
channel a leader has is the swap diff at cards.rs:2040-2050, and it is
multiplied by exactly credit_board = card_board_credit + card_board_leader
(cards.rs:2033, :2050). Confirmed: the swap diff at :2049-2050 is the only
board-value channel a leader has.

Caveat for the decouple design: the swap diff itself is board-aware in the
strong sense (recomputed stats, rider deltas) and can be NEGATIVE (a
replacement that makes things worse). A decoupling that simply routes
leaders through the static table would turn "replace with a worse leader"
from a negative board term into a positive static term. The wall at
sum == 0.0 is what currently deletes this channel entirely (see (1) of the
card_board_pair file).

(2) Per-type keys in card_board_credit_keys() and wall status
-------------------------------------------------------------

card_board_credit_keys() (rust/src/bots/weighted/eval.rs:823-835) is
derived at runtime from cards::board_credit_key over the card table.
board_credit_key (cards.rs:597-659) has exactly ONE Some arm:

  Leader => Some(WeightKey::CardBoardLeader)   (cards.rs:600)

Every other CardType maps to None (cards.rs:656-659: Farm | Mine | Lab |
Temple | Library | Arena | Theater | Infantry | Cavalry | Artillery | Air
| SpecialTech | Tactic | Aggression | War | Pact | Territory | Event |
Government | Action | Wonder | Bonus).

So card_board_credit_keys() == [WeightKey::CardBoardLeader] and NOTHING
else. The dominance_repair composite loop (eval.rs:962-973) therefore
runs over exactly one key. The retired per-type keys (CardBoardGovernment
2026-08-13, CardBoardAction 2026-08-13, CardBoardWonder 2026-08-13,
CardBoardBonus 2026-08-24; weights.rs:2788-2790, :2837) are NOT members --
they were shadowed by dedicated pricers (gov_value/action_value and the
Wonder branch) before being retired, and board_credit_key was edited to
return None for them.

Wall status of the live champions (card_board_credit + card_board_leader
vs 0; "wall" = sum exactly 0.0, i.e. the degenerate pair):

  2p  rust_champion_2p.json gen 120327
      card_board_credit =  0.1965856875309392
      card_board_leader = -0.1965856875309392
      sum = 0.0  -> AT THE WALL (exact equal-and-opposite)

  3p  rust_champion_3p.json gen 67187
      card_board_credit =  0.20084552274685408
      card_board_leader =  0.009458936652382385
      sum = 0.21030445939923647  -> NOT at the wall (positive sum; the
      leader's board-aware price is active and positive here)

  4p  rust_champion_4p.json gen 21838
      card_board_credit =  0.3444198528104188
      card_board_leader = -0.17919798330183695
      sum = 0.16522186950858185  -> NOT at the wall (positive sum; active
      and positive)

Note: the degenerate equal-and-opposite state is a 2p-only observation in
the live champions. The 3p and 4p vectors have a strictly positive sum, so
their leaders ARE priced board-awar with a positive multiplier. This is a
useful check: the "leader priced at zero on the board axis" pathology from
the card_board_pair finding is specific to the 2p champion (and the 2p
postclimb), not a property of all trained vectors.

(4) Existing emitted feature that already carries leader board value?
---------------------------------------------------------------------

NO. There is no emitted feature in features.rs that carries leader board
value. The only leader-related features features.rs emits are:
  features.rs:1023  WeightKey::Leader = 1.0 if the player has a leader, else
      0.0  (the generic "I own a leader" indicator, not a per-card board
      price)
  features.rs:1031-1032  WeightKey::LeaderReplacement = 1.0 if the player
      holds a leader AND has already taken 2+ leader ages, else 0.0  (a
      replacement-urgency flag)
features.rs has no call to card_yields, card_potential, card_choice,
sum_yields, or board_yields, and no per-card leader price. This is by
design: card_board_leader (and card_board_credit) are NOT_SET_BY_FEATURES
-- registry.rs:323-324 lists both under "Multipliers the card-in-hand
valuation layer (cards.rs) applies to a static/board yield-table ROW --
there is nothing for features() to write". They are pure multipliers the
climb tunes, read only inside card_potential_core.

Implication for the decouple: there is no existing feature the implementer
can reuse to carry a leader's board value as a first-class, board-aware,
per-card quantity. The leader's board value today exists ONLY as the
runtime-computed swap-diff triple list (board_yields::board_yields)
multiplied by credit_board inside card_potential_core. To decouple leader
pricing from the card_board_credit/card_board_leader sum (e.g. so a leader
is not driven to zero on the 2p wall), the options are:
  (a) introduce a brand-new WeightKey dedicated to leader board credit
      (separate from card_board_credit and card_board_leader), wired into
      card_potential_core's leader arm and swap_slot; OR
  (b) keep the existing key but change how it is composed for leaders so
      the dominance_repair composite wall cannot force it to cancel
      card_board_credit.
Either way a new WeightKey (or a re-composition) is required; no feature
exists to fall back on. This is a design decision for the implementer, not
something the survey can settle. Note also that card_board_leader is
SignIntent::Free (weights.rs:1712) and card_board_credit is NonNegative
(weights.rs:1489); the composite gate (eval.rs:961-973) is the ONLY
constraint on card_board_leader, so a new independent key would inherit the
same "only the composite gate touches it" status and the flat-valley
behaviour would recur unless the new key is given a real measured bound
(and is NOT_SET_BY_FEATURES, like its siblings).

Summary of the four answers
---------------------------
(1) card_yields for a Leader is NOT structurally empty (general effects
    table, cards.rs:286-333), but it is NOT a board channel: the swap
    branch (cards.rs:2040-2050) intercepts and returns whenever
    board.is_some() && credit_board != 0.0, and the static table is only
    reached as a fallback (board None or credit_board == 0.0). The swap
    diff at :2049-2050 is the ONLY board-aware value channel a leader has.
    Caveat: that diff is a real before/after stat delta (can be negative),
    not card_yields.
(2) card_board_credit_keys() contains exactly one key: CardBoardLeader
    (board_credit_key has a single Some arm at cards.rs:600; all other
    types return None at :656-659; the four retired per-type keys are not
    members). Wall status of live champions: 2p AT the wall (sum 0.0,
    exact equal-and-opposite), 3p NOT at the wall (sum +0.2103), 4p NOT at
    the wall (sum +0.1652). The degenerate zero-leader-price state is
    2p-specific.
(3) board_credit_key call sites: cards.rs:2033, cards.rs:2099, eval.rs:829,
    bin/leadersign.rs:79. credit_board uses: cards.rs:2033, :2034, :2042,
    :2050, :2064, :2066. Leader-relevant subset: :2033, :2034, :2042,
    :2050, plus swap_slot :2099-2101. Downstream card_potential callers:
    row.rs (six sites) and eval.rs LEADERSIGN tests.
(4) No existing emitted feature carries leader board value. The only
    leader features are the Leader ownership flag (features.rs:1023) and
    the LeaderReplacement flag (features.rs:1031). card_board_leader and
    card_board_credit are NOT_SET_BY_FEATURES (registry.rs:323-324). A
    decouple would need either a brand-new WeightKey or a re-composition of
    the existing key; nothing to reuse.
